# 05 — Drone-sighting cases: ambiguity, amplification, and who amplified what

**Empirical strand for the question whether AI agents could deliberately amplify ambiguous physical threats. Cut-off 25 September 2026. Analytic/defensive only.**

**Grades: A** primary/official/peer-reviewed · **B** transparent independent method · **C** reputable journalism · **D** single-source/trade · **E** weak/unverified. Chain used throughout: **ambiguity → initial reports → amplification → media/social attention → citizen vigilance → further genuine or mistaken reports → institutional response → further perceived validation.**

---

## 0. The structural finding

Six episodes share one architecture — not an information architecture but a **detection architecture**. A sparse, unresolvable substrate exists (lawful drones, aircraft, stars, birds, a balloon, occasionally a hostile airframe). A **credentialled individual** — security officer, contractor, patrol officer, airport employee, sheriff, pilot — reports it, and the *credential* rather than the sensor makes it travel. Actors who **cannot verify it** amplify it: scanner accounts, local officials with a constituent problem and no answer, national media, and most consequentially **the responding institutions themselves**. The response becomes the evidence — closures, scrambled fighters, police helicopters, restricted airspace — and generates the next sighting. Finally **detection counts rise because detection improved**, and the rise is cited as proof of the threat the detection was bought for.

**Central result: in every disputed case the amplifiers were organic, political, media, or the responding state. No deliberate adversarial amplification of a drone-sighting wave was credibly documented (§8).**

## 1. New Jersey / US Northeast, November–December 2024 — the reference case

**Grade A (official) / B (FOIA record). Confidence High.**

**Ambiguity and first reports.** First documented observation: **Picatinny Arsenal, Morris County, 13 November 2024** — a contractor saw "a light rising straight up from the tree line"; the witness was a police/security officer. Picatinny confirmed **11 drone sightings and seven that were aeroplanes**. The airspace is among the world's most congested. Officials eventually confirmed **FAA research drones, hobbyist drones, law-enforcement drones, manned aircraft, helicopters and stars** among reports. Key collapses: the **26 November medevac wave-off** was three airliners aligned on approach to Solberg Airport; the **Salem nuclear-plant** report matched a UH-60, a Cessna C150 and Philadelphia traffic; the **"grey mist" over Clinton** was wingtip vortices from a Beechcraft Baron; the **Coast Guard "swarm"** was jets approaching JFK (A; DHS/TSA FOIA slides, May 2025).

**Amplification.** On **19 November** the account **@OSINTdefender** posted that "upwards of a dozen large drones" were over Morris County, tracked by law enforcement and a State Police helicopter; **The War Zone** published the first Picatinny story the same day. TWZ later recorded "an avalanche of drone reports… and a crush of media coverage from around the globe", with broadcasters airing 737s and helicopters as "mysterious craft" (B/C).

**Political amplification was decisive.** On **8 December** mayors of **21 Morris County townships** wrote to Gov. Murphy, focused on the fact that nobody owned the question. The letter circulated online; within 48 hours ~**185 mayors** attended a closed briefing at the state EOC with State Police, FBI, DHS and DoD, were told officials did not know what the objects were but that there was no threat, and left without an answer; Mayor **Ryan Herd** walked out and gave interviews to "every single media tent". Others: Assemblywoman **Dawn Fantasia** publicised briefing notes describing six-foot drones, coordinated, "not hobbyists"; Sen. **Andy Kim** patrolled and reported objects, later conceding most were aeroplanes; former governor **Larry Hogan** posted video of the constellation **Orion**; Rep. **Jeff Van Drew** claimed an Iranian "mothership" (Pentagon-refuted); Rep. **Michael McCaul** claimed Chinese spy drones; **Trump** suggested shooting them down; five governors and six senators demanded action (B/C).

**Citizen vigilance and harm.** Montclair State analysed **170,000+ posts**: conversation rose **19,000%**, **fear (45%)** dominant, **X carrying 86%** (B). Viral content was often false — recycled Chinese COVID disinfectant drones, an Instagram clip with added gunfire, a state senator's "crashed drone" that was a *Star Wars* TIE-fighter replica (C). New York City logged ~**120 calls in the weekend of 14–15 December**, many resolved as aircraft or **Venus**; copycat hobby flights generated further reports; a "military-grade" crash was a **toy drone** (B). **Laser strikes on aircraft surged**, and police had to warn residents not to shoot at or laser aircraft because most were manned (B/C).

**Response, cost, retraction, sequel.** On **17 December** the joint DHS/FBI/FAA/DoD statement reviewed **5,000+ sightings and ~100 leads** and found "**nothing anomalous**", attributing most to lawful drones, hobbyists, law enforcement, manned aircraft, helicopters and **stars** (A). The FAA restricted airspace over Picatinny, the Bedminster club, **22 NJ communities** and parts of New York; counter-UAS authority was extended to **30 September 2025** and then lapsed; the Peters bill was blocked by **Rand Paul** (A). The **TSA had internally debunked** the worst reports by 17 December but published nothing until FOIA months later — which *Reason* argued prolonged concern while helping justify expanded powers (B/C). Stewart International closed ~1 hour; Trenton-Mercer grounded training flights after ATC radar "primary targets" at up to **150 knots**; a medevac diverted; **6,000+ tips**; a classified congressional briefing. The March 2026 TWZ FOIA cache shows police had been told the drones might be "military grade", might carry explosives, and **could jam police radios and air-traffic radar** (B). On **28 January 2025** the White House said the aircraft were primarily **FAA-authorised research drones** and hobbyist/private craft: "This was not the enemy" (A). **No drones were recovered, no arrests made, no foreign actor identified.** An October 2025 *New York Post* contractor claim rests on **one anonymous source (Grade E)**. On **12 January 2026** New Jersey became the **first US state to fund UAP research** (**$2.5m/yr** plus **$1m** for ATC loan forgiveness) (A/B). Public belief nonetheless **burned out in ~six weeks** — search interest 100 → 2 by 12 January 2025.

## 2. Gatwick, 19–21 December 2018 — the archetype

**Grade A (police/court record) / B (misidentification). Confidence High that no drone was ever evidenced.**

A **security officer at a bus stop reported two drones at about 21:00 on 19 December**; within half an hour **six more sightings followed, five from police officers**. The runway closed **33 hours** (reopened 05:57 on 21 December, shut again 17:30–18:23), affecting ~**1,000 flights and 140,000 passengers**. Police logged **170 reports, 115 "credible"**; the Chief Constable cited **92–93 credible witnesses**. **No photograph or video of a drone ever emerged** (A).

Chief Constable **Giles York** conceded police drones "could have caused some level of confusion", and Det Ch Supt **Jason Tingley** said on 23 December there "may never have been a drone" — before his own chief publicly contradicted him the next day. **Amplification was political, media and institutional**: the PM, Defence and Transport Secretaries, shadow ministers, the MoD and the CAA responded; the Army and RAF Regiment deployed; a **£50,000 reward** was offered. **Paul Gait and Elaine Kirk** were arrested at home by 12 armed officers, held ~36 hours and released without charge; Sussex Police settled in **June 2020 for £200,000** (£55,000 damages + £145,000 costs) (A).

The **18-month, £790,000 investigation across five forces closed on 27 September 2019 with nobody charged** (A). Police and the airport maintained it was a malicious attack and floated an insider theory. The *Guardian*'s 2020 investigation argued mass panic and misidentification; a former DJI executive said in 2021 it was "clear" there had been no drone; a **2024 FOI showed the National Police Air Service logged no drone sightings at Gatwick between 19 and 31 December 2018** (B/C). The independent review remains unpublished on national-security grounds. Permanent counter-drone systems nonetheless followed at Gatwick (Nov 2019) and Heathrow (Jan 2020).

## 3. Copenhagen/Oslo and the September–October 2025 European wave

**Grade A (retraction, official counts) / B (reconstruction). Confidence High.**

**Trigger and collapse.** At about 20:00 on **22 September 2025** an airport employee raised the first alarm: "Der er to droner. De er store. Det er ikke legetøj" ("There are two drones. They are large. It is not a toy"). Copenhagen closed ~4 hours (~100 movements, ~20,000 passengers); Oslo Gardermoen closed briefly the same night. Investigators concluded the large object was a **school training aircraft from Roskilde** cleared to fly there, the fast "zig-zagging" object was **lens flare**, and a colleague's later "drone" was the **police helicopter scrambled to hunt the drones the first report had conjured**. The witness had **never seen a real drone in person**; interviewed four times, she held her account (B; *Frihedsbrevet*, 18 March 2026).

**Amplification, and its sequencing.** On **23 September** a senior chief police inspector described "an actor with the capabilities, the will and the tools… perhaps also to train" — an **institutional** framing, not a media one. Police logged **100+ reports** within a day and **500+ nationally in 24 hours** within four. PM **Mette Frederiksen** called it "the most serious attack on Danish critical infrastructure to date"; the Defence Minister called it a hybrid attack; **NOST** went to its highest readiness level; the Armed Forces confirmed observations at several military sites; Germany's Chancellor **Merz** said he suspected Russia behind "most of these drone flights" — explicitly suspicion, without evidence (A/B). At **Borris on 28 September** the military fired **43 rounds** at a supposed drone; TV 2 later found a message reading "They just shot at a plane over the Borris camp" and flight-radar data showing a Widerøe Dash 8-400 overhead at the time (B).

**Retractions and further false positives.** On **19 June 2026** Denmark said none of the ships investigated — **including Russian shadow-fleet vessels** — could be linked to the observations, undercutting the think-tank "shadow fleet" thesis published a fortnight later. On **25 June 2026**, after a nine-month inquiry, Chief Police Inspector **Søren Thomassen** concluded police could "neither confirm… nor rule out" drone activity at the airport, named **no suspects** and closed the case, while the Armed Forces separately concluded drones *had* overflown Danish military installations (A/B). The widely quoted "we were not clear enough with our reservations" could not be traced to any primary source (**Grade E**). Norway's PST said in February 2026 that it had "not uncovered a single case of a drone being controlled by a foreign state", attributing reports to "stars, weather phenomena, aeroplanes, or helicopters" (B). VRT's *Pano* (15 April 2026) examined **558 Belgian reports**; of ~250 over military areas, ADIV judged **42** to have "no other plausible explanation" — but only physical evidence is conclusive under ADIV's definition, and **none was ever produced**. The "enormous drone" video over Zaventem had been **sent to a newspaper by Defence Minister Francken himself**; it was a **federal police helicopter**. A ~**€50m** counter-drone programme followed (A/B). On **13 September 2026** Vilnius airport closed and NATO scrambled fighters after a radar return; **visual identification found a flock of birds**, reopening after **38 minutes** (B). Spain's Guardia Civil concluded the drone that closed **Madrid-Barajas on 6 November 2024** (~1 hour, ~21 flights diverted) **never existed**, repeating the February 2020 "ghost drone" (*Telemadrid*, 7 October 2025; primary URL unretrievable — **C, Medium**).

**The counter-current and the decisive comparison.** The **Poland incursions of 9–10 September 2025** were real: 19–23 drones from the Belarus direction, four downed, airports closed, NATO Article 4 invoked; prosecutors found unarmed decoy drones (A). **Øresund (26 February 2026)** is confirmed and attributed: a drone from the Russian intelligence ship *Zhigulevsk* was jammed by HSwMS *Rapp* and the launch confirmed by technical data (A). Against that, the **Trouw + Dronewatch** audit of **61 sightings in 11 countries** (9 Sept–27 Nov 2025) found ~**40 with no evidence of any drone**, only **three confirmed Russian** (Poland/Romania/Moldova) and three confirmed hobby flights. Reports "far from always" involve a drone, and "the cause can of course be all the attention, **including in the media**" — a **Koninklijke Marechaussee** spokesperson told *Trouw*: an official statement that salience drove reports. Misidentifications included stars (Zuid-Limburg, Billund), a ship (a Norwegian "oil-platform drone"), a police helicopter (three VRT fact-checks) and a landing DHL cargo plane (B). A natural experiment cuts the same way: after an advanced British detection system was rushed into Belgium, **reports declined rapidly** (B).

## 4. Germany, 2024–2026

**Grade A (aggregates, Bundestag record) / B (case detail). Confidence Medium-High.**

**Counts — and a correction.** DFS logged **151** incidents at German airports in 2023, **161** in 2024 and **144 by August 2025**, with "Hobby-Piloten" frequently the cause; DFS counts reported obstructions, not confirmed drones (A). The confidential **BKA Lagebild** of end-August 2026 covers **2026 year-to-date, not 2025**: **>165 suspected sabotage cases**, **747 suspicious drone incidents involving 1,068 drones**, and **24 espionage proceedings**. The 2025 full-year comparison is **~1,300 drone "Sachverhalte"**, so 2026 is running **below** 2025 (A) — figures circulating as "2025" are mis-dated. BER logged **28 sightings in H1 2026** (seven suspending operations) against **8 in all of 2025**; Frankfurt recorded ~**150 illegal ascents since end-2024 with ~50 pilots identified**, "in most cases plane-spotters, hobby pilots, or people flying drones at family celebrations"; the BDL's own headline damage total is **€160m** (A/B).

**The epistemic baseline — and a claim to reject.** The government's answer to a December 2025 written question (**Drucksache 21/3236**) states that "**no reliable and generally valid statements can be derived**" from the information, "particularly because of the heterogeneity of the sightings", and that involvement of intelligence or foreign state bodies "**is to be presumed** for a non-quantifiable portion." A check of 53 Bundestag papers containing "Drohn" found **no** official finding that German sightings were malfunctions, misperceptions or bird strikes. The "**technische Fehlfunktionen**" phrase is real but refers elsewhere: **Drucksache 21/7725** (25 August 2026) applies it to **Ukrainian drones in Finnish and Baltic airspace** — "verifiably not deliberate Ukrainian attacks… but technical malfunctions, presumably attributable to deliberate Russian GPS interference." Any claim that the Bundestag found German airport sightings to be technical malfunctions is **NOT VERIFIED and should not be cited.**

**Episodes.** Munich closed twice on 3–4 October 2025 after "unconfirmed drone sightings" with Bundeswehr *Amtshilfe*; **30 May 2026** rested on two pilots' reports, closed runways ~1 hour, diverted **26 aircraft** and found nothing; on **6 September 2026** the Bundespolizei **explicitly declined to classify** an alarm as a drone incident. **BER** closed ~2 hours on 31 October 2025, again on 12 March 2026, and ~45 minutes on **23 September 2026** with nothing recovered. **Cologne/Bonn, 12 August 2026**: five sightings, landings blocked ~30 minutes, two Ryanair diversions — and a **drone pilot found near the airport**, the clearest single German attribution. **Leipzig/Halle, 4–5 August 2026** is the only German airport case with material evidence: a **Semtex-armed** drone found at 23:42 beside a Ukrainian Antonov after striking a wing — **which the airport's own sensor had not detected** — a detonation heard at ~19:19 in Kursdorf, burned earth, metal fragments and a **tree-mounted antenna**; DNA secured; a third RDX drone found on 25 August; the **Bundesanwaltschaft** took over and **Germany formally blamed Russia on 1 September 2026** (A). A false positive in the other direction is instructive: **Geilenkirchen NATO base, 8–9 October 2025 — "Drohnen-Detektor löst Fehlalarm aus"** (C).

**Who amplified.** The loudest escalation **preceded** any confirmed German hostile drone. During the **29 September – 7 October 2025** wave, driven substantially by Danish and unconfirmed German sightings, **Kiesewetter (CDU) demanded the "Spannungsfall" be declared**; **Söder** said "**Abschießen statt abwarten!**"; **Merz** suspected Russia. The one German case with a device and payload came **ten months later**. **Dobrindt** then used Leipzig (5 August 2026) to announce a "neue **Gefahrenqualität**" and a contest "possibly involving **foreign powers**" — while cautioning that speculation could mislead — and ordered the federal drone-defence unit from ~130 to **300 specialists at eight sites**; **Merz** called the evidence "gerichtsfest" on 5 September, and by 10 September media reported an open "dispute over the evidence". The Greens' motion **21/7669** (20 August 2026) demanded a "**Drohnengipfel im Bundeskanzleramt**" while accusing Dobrindt of "**Symbolpolitik**". One attribution attempt **failed to cascade**: exact-phrase searching for the ARX Robotics chief's "**Angriff auf kritische Infrastruktur auf NATO-Boden**" line (Merkur, 6 August 2026) returns only the Merkur interview itself.

## 5. US military bases and the UK MoD series

**Grade A (UK primary figures) / B (Langley). Confidence Medium-High.**

**Langley AFB, Virginia.** The Air Force confirmed the installation **first observed UAS activity on the evening of 6 December 2023** and experienced multiple incursions through December, none appearing to "exhibit hostile intent"; the *WSJ* later reported **17 days**. Gen. **Mark Kelly**, an eyewitness, described a drone "roughly 20 feet long… more than 100 miles an hour… 3,000 to 4,000 feet". Jamming and directed energy were considered and **ruled out**. Gen. **Gregory Guillot** gave the first public acknowledgement in March 2024 ("I wasn't prepared for the number of incursions that I see") and said in October 2024 that he knew of no "**organized or unorganized foreign nexus**", could describe only "roughly the number and roughly the altitude", and knew of no recovery point; the FBI had **no suspects and recovered no drones** (B). **No operators and no explanation were ever produced.** The amplification is instructive because **the institutional response leaked first**: plane-spotters using flight-tracking software detected F-16 combat air patrols over Newport News around 12 December and a **NASA WB-57F** orbiting Langley the following week, before any official statement (B).

**US counts and the observer-effect.** Guillot told the Senate in February 2025 of **350 incursions across ~100 installations of all types in 2024**, with only about **half** authorised to counter them. A NORTHCOM spokesperson gave ~**230 reported incursions in the year to September 2024 and ~420 in the year to September 2025**, cautioning that **better sensing may explain part of the rise** (B) — the same counting artifact seen in the UK and Germany. The **DoD Inspector General** (DODIG-2026-045, January 2026), after visiting ten installations and interviewing 31 DoD organisations, found that "**a large percentage of installations do not have the operational approval to use c-UAS capabilities**" (A). The one attributable case differs in kind: **Yinpiao Zhou**, who flew a drone over Vandenberg on 30 November 2024 to photograph a classified launch, **pleaded guilty and was sentenced on 31 March 2025 to time served (~4 months) plus a year's supervised release** (A/B).

**United Kingdom.** The MoD's February 2026 release gives the primary figures: **266 reported UAV incidents near Defence sites in 2025, up from 126 in 2024** — the "doubling" used to justify shoot-down powers in the **Armed Forces Bill** (introduced 15 January 2026). Both are calendar-year, all-UK-Defence-sites counts, and the MoD attributes part of the rise to **better detection and better reporting** (A). The counting problem is severe: an **FOI answer to *The Independent*** put 2025 at **250**; **Lord Coaker told Parliament (HL11210, 4 November 2025)** of **187 "drone sightings"**; DroneXL reported **340 incidents in the year to August 2026**. **No published document defines an "incident"** as distinct from a sighting (B/C). A count that rises because a base installed a new sensor **measures the observer**. The MoD's statement **names no perpetrator**; former defence minister **Tobias Ellwood** names Russia and Iran; **not one of the 340 has been publicly attributed** (B).

The UK series contains the cleanest documentable misidentification: a **USAFE spokesperson confirmed** UAS over Lakenheath, Mildenhall and Feltwell "**between November 20 and 22**" (2024), with ~60 British troops deployed — during which a National Police Air Service **EC135 helicopter reported being "chased off"**, describing "red flashing lights" and "large orbits… in excess of 1,500ft", an object later identified as a **USAF F-15**, likely from the sorties the alarm had prompted; the Airprox Board judged no collision risk resulted (B). The first alarms escalated to **COBR**. The **MoD Police inquiry closed with no suspects** (FOI, 27 January 2026); the MoD refuses FOI requests for the inquiry's name, senior investigating officer, staffing and cost, arguing disclosure would "help our adversaries" (B).

**The one UK attribution claim.** *The i Paper* reported on **21 February 2025** that three individuals with links to Russian military and intelligence sites had stayed near Lakenheath and Mildenhall, and that "**at least two… are thought to have worked as seasonal fruit pickers in close proximity to the airbases — visiting fields close to the airbases at night**" — while stating this is "**not definitive proof of Russian involvement**." It nonetheless triggered investigation demands from **Julian Lewis, Tom Tugendhat, Nick Timothy, Terry Jermy and Alicia Kearns**, with the Commons Defence Committee considering the material in a "grey-zone" inquiry — an unproven attribution acquiring political momentum (**Grade D, Low confidence**). No government body has confirmed Russian involvement; IISS's "highly likely" judgement is a think tank's, and FPRI itself notes the Russia link "was never confirmed".

## 6. El Paso, 10–11 February 2026 — the sharpest case

**Grade A/B. Confidence High.**

At about **23:30 local on 10 February 2026** the FAA announced a **10-day closure of all flights over El Paso** for "special security reasons" — without notice to the airport, the city or the local congressional delegation, and issued from Washington rather than by regional air traffic control. It was lifted shortly before **07:00 on 11 February**. Political framing followed at once: Transportation Secretary **Sean Duffy** posted that the FAA had worked with the Pentagon to "address a cartel drone incursion" and that "the threat has been neutralised"; an administration official said "Mexican cartel drones breached US airspace. The Department of War took action to disable the drones" (A).

Two government officials told *The Intercept* the closure was connected to the Department of War's new **counter-drone laser near Fort Bliss** and a miscommunication with FAA headquarters about the risk it posed to air traffic. One said a cartel drone may have been damaged; another said a **Mylar party balloon** was destroyed — apparently **different events**. Rep. **Veronica Escobar** said flatly: "There was not a threat, which is why the FAA lifted this restriction so quickly." CBS reported the Pentagon had wanted to test the laser sooner than the safety meetings with the FAA allowed, and that a test had shot down what appeared to be a foreign drone but was a party balloon. The Mayor told the BBC the chaos "was completely unnecessary"; Mexican officials reportedly feared the closure signalled a US incursion into Mexico (A/B).

**Amplification here was primarily official.** The trigger was an internal test-scheduling dispute; political framing converted it into a border-security event; the 10-day closure order functioned as public evidence of a threat; the correction took under eight hours — faster than the claim travelled. **The durable consequence is real**: after "back-to-back airspace blunders earlier this year", USNORTHCOM confirmed on **28 August 2026** that Joint Task Force–Southern Border used a high-energy laser to **defeat three cartel-linked drones** (A). The ambiguous February episode became the legitimising frame for a capability that then produced genuine interdictions — the clearest case in this corpus of **an ambiguity-induced response generating its own later validation**.

## 7. Other corroborating episode

**Colorado–Nebraska, December 2019 – January 2020.** Hundreds of reports of drones in grid formations of up to 19 at 200–500ft; **70+ agencies** met at Brush, Colorado on 6 January 2020; Gov. Polis ordered state monitoring after a drone came within 100ft of a medical helicopter; a search for an "unspecified command vehicle" found nothing. The state concluded the **majority of sightings were planets, stars, commercial aircraft and hobbyist drones**; **VICE News reported in January 2020 that the drones "were not real."** The investigation closed after roughly **six weeks** (B/C).

## 8. Was any drone scare deliberately amplified? A documented negative

**Grade B/C. Confidence High for the negative; Medium for the 2026 positives.**

I found **no credible, documented finding that a foreign state, bot network, troll farm or influence operation deliberately amplified any drone-sighting wave** — least of all the September–October 2025 western-European wave (Denmark, Norway, Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands). No Europol, EEAS, EDMO, DFRLab, ISD, EU DisinfoLab or Hybrid CoE report attributing a drone panic to amplification was located, and the EU's own **COM(2026) 81 final** drone Action Plan (11 February 2026) contains **zero** occurrences of *disinformation*, *FIMI*, *information manipulation*, *misinformation* or *amplification* — it is a capability and prevention instrument, not an information one (A, checked in full).

What **is** documented is materially different:

- **Real hostile physical flights, separately proven** — Leipzig/Halle (Aug 2026, attributed), Øresund (Feb 2026), Poland (Sept 2025). Physical acts, not information operations (A).
- **Reactive FIMI accompanying drone incursions, confirmed at EU level** — the **EEAS 4th Report on FIMI Threats** (March 2026) makes drones an explicit FIMI category and states that hybrid actions "including drone incursions… in EU countries such as **Poland, Romania, Lithuania and Estonia**… were accompanied by FIMI designed to manage public perception and test responses." Of 540 incidents in 2025, **29% were attributed to Russia, 6% to China and 65% were unattributed**; **Denmark and Norway are not named.** EUvsDisinfo (18 September 2025) documented the Kremlin's "smokescreen of FIMI over drones in Poland" — reactive denial *after* a confirmed Russian incursion, with EDMO/BROD's 18,316-post analysis showing narrative clusters that **followed** the event (A/B).
- **One documented coordinated amplification of a drone narrative — Romania, May–June 2026.** EDMO/BROD found the Galați strike (29 May 2026) was sustained over **seven consecutive days**: 28–105 accounts and 3,400–6,800 posts per day across 116,014 posts analysed, 10–155m views per network. Constanța, by contrast, "emerged organically during a temporary information vacuum." This is the strongest positive evidence in the corpus — and it amplified a **false-flag claim about a real strike**, not a sighting wave (B).
- **One drone-tied state information-operation accusation — Latvia, 27 March 2026**, in which the Defence Ministry alleged "a massive information operation against the Baltic states" and the MFA summoned the Russian ambassador (**C, Medium**).
- **Amplification of an unproven theory, by a think tank and the press.** IISS's July 2026 report claimed an 18-month Russian campaign using **shadow-fleet ships as launch platforms**, tying 144 incidents in 13 countries to Russian intelligence; the *Guardian* and *Telegraph* amplified it. Dronewatch found no physical evidence linking any specific drone to any specific ship, some named vessels >100km from the sightings, and Dutch ministers attributing Volkel and Eindhoven to **small hobby drones** incapable of flying hundreds of kilometres from North Sea ships. IISS argued the absence of confirmation *fits* a successful deception campaign — which Dronewatch called effectively **unfalsifiable** (B/C).
- **"Hide in the noise": used, but not as an information-operation finding.** FPRI (Stein and Ball, "Small Drones, Big Problems", **2 September 2025**) argues a state sponsor can "**hide in the noise**" of frequent sUAS encounters, and that this "may explain why there was a spike in incidents in December 2024, which coincided with the broader public panic over sUAS sightings in New Jersey." Two caveats: FPRI's **attribution thesis concerns China, not Russia**, and it is offered as a hypothesis "worthy of further study" — an inference from a coincidence of timing, not evidence. The phrase's documented coinage also means close to the opposite: Capt. Matthew Moellering's "Hiding in the Noise" (Modern War Institute, 26 September 2022) describes **US special-operations forces hiding from adversary AI surveillance**.

**Conclusion.** For the wave that matters most, the amplification was **organic, political, media and institutional — and the most consequential amplifier was the responding state itself**. Adversarial amplification of a drone scare is documented only for 2026, only in Romania, and only around a real strike.

## 9. Two direct answers

**(a) Was there any case where the physical event was fabricated entirely and the response was produced by the target society?** **Not by an adversary — no.** No case was found in which a state or influence operation fabricated a drone event that a target society then responded to. But **"no verified physical event at all" is common**, and in four cases the response was produced almost entirely by the reporting-and-response system:

| Case | Physical finding | Response produced |
|---|---|---|
| Gatwick 2018 | No drone evidenced; no image; NPAS logged no sightings | 33-hour closure, ~1,000 flights, arrests, £790k inquiry, £200k settlement |
| Colorado–Nebraska 2019–20 | State: majority planets, stars, aircraft, hobby drones; VICE: "not real" | 70+ agency meeting, state air search, local ordinances |
| Madrid-Barajas, Nov 2024 | Guardia Civil: the drone "never existed" | ~1 hour closure, ~21 flights diverted (repeat of Feb 2020) |
| Copenhagen, 22 Sep 2025 | Police: cannot confirm or rule out; school aircraft + lens flare; police helicopter generated a new sighting | 4-hour closure, national alert, 500+ reports/day, nine-month inquiry |

In each, the event was **constituted by the detection and response architecture**, not by an airframe. Ambiguity alone, with no fabricated object, can produce real closures, real expenditure and real law.

**(b) How often does an ambiguous incident lead to durable belief versus burning out quickly?** **Burnout is the norm; durability is bought institutionally, not informationally.**

| Episode | Public belief | Institutional residue |
|---|---|---|
| Gatwick 2018 | Days–weeks | Permanent counter-drone systems (Nov 2019/Jan 2020); review withheld |
| Colorado 2019–20 | ~6 weeks | State report; local ordinances proposed |
| New Jersey 2024 | ~6 weeks (search 100→2); 78% still believed government was withholding information | FAA restrictions; counter-UAS extension then lapse; **state UAP centre, $2.5m/yr** |
| Copenhagen 2025 | Official narrative **9 months** before retraction; public belief shorter | NOST readiness; Nordic/Baltic air policing; NATO posture |
| Belgium 2025 | Reports fell once detection improved | **€50m** programme; ministerial accountability dispute |
| UK 2024–26 | No attribution, no resolution, no public demand for one | Counts **126→266→340**; shoot-down powers; £750m |
| El Paso Feb 2026 | <8 hours | 10-day closure order; laser programme legitimised; **three real interdictions by Aug 2026** |

Three regularities. **First**, rumour intensity is a product of importance and ambiguity; ambiguity resolves cheaply, so public belief decays in weeks unless an institution keeps supplying it. **Second**, the residue is **institutional**: closures, powers, procurement, restricted airspace, research centres and *counts*; the UK is the pure case — a number that nearly tripled while measuring the observer, driving statute. **Third**, durable *belief* requires a named mechanism plus a victim class plus an institutional owner of a harm question. Drone scares almost never produce a victim class — nobody claims injury — so the FBI's threat question is answered "no threat", the task closes, and the belief dies. Where drones *did* acquire a named perpetrator and a physical fact (Leipzig, Øresund, Poland), the response persisted — because the event was real.

## 10. Synthesis: the causal chain across cases

**The pattern.** In eight cases the first reporter was a **credentialled individual with an institutional role**, not a sensor. The pivotal amplifier was almost never a bot or foreign account — it was a police chief, a mayor, a prime minister, a transport secretary, a think tank, or the mere existence of an official counter-drone programme. Physical ambiguity supplied ignition; institutions supplied oxygen; the institutional response became the evidence sustaining the next round of reports. The adversarial-amplification pathway is on this evidence **the least attested link in the chain**, and the institutional-amplification pathway the most.

## 11. Evidence quality and what would change the assessment

**A:** the joint DHS/FBI/FAA/DoD statement; FAA restrictions; Danish police, Armed Forces and PST statements and the 25 June 2026 closure; GOV.UK 266/126; USNORTHCOM's 28 August 2026 interdiction; NJ UAP legislation; the Sussex Police/court record; the Leipzig attribution; the EEAS FIMI report; Bundestag Drucksachen 21/3236 and 21/7725; COM(2026) 81. **B:** Trouw + Dronewatch; the Dronewatch IISS rebuttal; Montclair State; the TWZ FOIA corpora; Belgian aggregates; Rolling Stone. **C:** *Telemadrid*, *Frihedsbrevet*/*DR*, Metro, *The Independent*/DroneXL, Merkur. **D/E — treat as unverified:** the *New York Post* contractor claim; *The i Paper*'s GRU "fruit pickers"; the "clear enough with our reservations" quotation; the Bundestag "technische Fehlfunktionen" attribution; the shadow-fleet launch-platform thesis.

**What would change the assessment.** (1) A released national attribution naming an actor for any of the 340 UK, 747 German (2026) or 558 Belgian reports. (2) Primary evidence that bots or state accounts amplified the September–October 2025 western wave — none exists, and the EEAS report pointedly excludes Denmark and Norway. (3) A single provable case of a deliberately fabricated drone event — the precondition for the strongest version of the parent hypothesis, and the one thing this corpus does not contain. Absent those, **ambiguous drone incidents consistently produce real disruption through organic, political and institutional amplification, and that disruption is self-limiting in belief but self-reinforcing in institutions.**
