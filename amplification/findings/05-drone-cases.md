# 05 — Drone-sighting cases: ambiguity, amplification and who did the amplifying

### Empirical strand for the hypothesis that AI agents could deliberately amplify ambiguous physical threats

**Research cut-off: 25 September 2026.** Analytic/defensive research only; no operational guidance.

**Grades** (consistent with the parent study): **A** primary/official/peer-reviewed · **B** independent research with transparent method · **C** reputable journalism · **D** single-source/trade/encyclopaedia · **E** weak/unverified/advocacy. Confidence in the *inference* is rated **High / Medium / Low**. The chain reconstructed in every case is: **physical ambiguity → initial reports → amplification → media/social attention → citizen vigilance → additional genuine or mistaken reports → institutional response → further perceived validation.**

---

## 0. The structural finding, stated first

Across six episodes spanning 2019–2026, the same architecture recurs, and it is **not** an information architecture. It is a **detection architecture**:

1. A **real but sparse and unresolvable physical substrate** exists (lawful drones, aircraft, stars, birds, balloons, or a small number of genuinely hostile airframes).
2. **Someone with apparent standing reports it** — a security officer, a police patrol, an airport employee, a mayor, a pilot. The credential, not the sensor, is what makes the report travel.
3. **The report is amplified by actors who cannot verify it**: open-source scanner accounts, local officials with a constituent problem and no answer, national media, and — the most consequential and least examined — **the responding institutions themselves**.
4. The institutional **response becomes the evidence**: FAA closures, scrambled fighters, police helicopters, restricted airspace. People then see the response, and the response generates the next sighting.
5. **Detection counts rise because detection improved**, and the rise is then cited as proof of the threat it was built to measure.

The single most important empirical result in this strand is that **in every disputed case the amplifiers were organic, political, media or the responding state — and in no case was a deliberate adversarial amplification of a drone-sighting wave credibly documented.** The negative finding is reported in §9.

## 1. New Jersey and the US Northeast, November–December 2024 — the reference case

**Grade A/B overall (A for official statements; B for the FOIA record); Confidence High.**

**Timeline and physical ambiguity.** The first documented observation was at **Picatinny Arsenal, Morris County, NJ, on 13 November 2024** — a contractor reported "a light rising straight up from the tree line"; the witness was a police or security officer. Picatinny later confirmed **11 drone sightings and seven sightings that were actually aeroplanes** (A). Patrol officers in Morris County reported more on 18–19 November; the FOIA cache of police reports begins **18 November** (A/B, TWZ).

The ambiguity was genuine and layered. The airspace is one of the most congested in the world — Newark Liberty, Teterboro's private-jet corridor, regional airfields, military and industrial sites. Over the following weeks officials confirmed **authorised FAA research drones, hobbyist drones, law-enforcement drones, manned fixed-wing aircraft, helicopters and stars** among the reports (A). Several of the most alarming incidents were later resolved: the **26 November medical-evacuation helicopter waved off at Raritan Valley Community College** was three commercial aircraft aligned on approach to Solberg Airport, appearing to hover as they flew directly at the observers; the **Salem nuclear-plant** report matched a UH-60 Black Hawk, a Cessna C150 and Philadelphia commercial traffic; the **"grey mist" over Clinton** was a Beechcraft Baron in turbulence producing wingtip vortices; the **Coast Guard "drone swarm"** was commercial jets on approach to JFK (A, DHS/TSA FOIA slides, released May 2025; B).

**Initial reports → amplification.** On **19 November** the open-source account **@OSINTdefender** posted that "upwards of a dozen large drones" had been spotted over Morris County and were being tracked by law enforcement and an NJ State Police helicopter; a fire-scanner account posted a photograph. **The War Zone** published the first Picatinny story the same day. TWZ's own retrospective is unambiguous: "In a very short period of time, there was an avalanche of drone reports from all over the region and a crush of media coverage from around the globe… We saw no visual evidence of any mass drone incursions." Mainstream outlets repeatedly broadcast footage of 737s and helicopters "passed off as mysterious craft" (B/C).

**Political amplification was the decisive step.** On **8 December** the mayors of **21 Morris County townships** sent Gov. Phil Murphy a letter — cautious about origins, focused on the fact that no one could tell them who owned the question. It was circulated on social media and picked up by local then regional media. Within 48 hours roughly **185 mayors** attended a closed briefing at the state emergency operations centre in Ewing with the State Police, FBI, DHS and DoD. They were told officials did not know what the objects were but that there was no threat. Mayor **Ryan Herd** of Pequannock walked out and gave interviews to "every single media tent", saying: "They don't know a fucking thing. And they're telling us there's nothing to worry about" (B, Rolling Stone April 2026, on the record).

Other political amplifiers: NJ Assemblywoman **Dawn Fantasia** publicised her notes from an 11 December briefing — six-foot drones, six to seven hours a night, coordinated, evading detection, "not hobbyists"; Senator **Andy Kim** patrolled with local police and reported objects at Round Valley Reservoir, later conceding most were probably aeroplanes; former Maryland governor **Larry Hogan** posted video of what he took to be drones over his home — the constellation **Orion**, corrected by a Community Note; Rep. **Jeff Van Drew** claimed an Iranian "mothership" (refuted by the Pentagon); Rep. **Michael McCaul** claimed Chinese spy drones; **Trump** suggested shooting them down. Governors Hochul, Lamont, Healey, Youngkin and Shapiro all launched or endorsed state responses. In Congress, **Schumer, Booker, Gillibrand, Kim and Klobuchar** demanded briefings; **Blumenthal** suggested jamming (B/C).

**Social attention and citizen vigilance.** Montclair State University analysed **170,000+ social-media posts**: conversation rose **19,000%** against the prior period, spiked nearly **800% on 11 December**, with **X carrying 86%** of discussion; the dominant emotion was **fear (45%)**, followed by disgust (28%) and anger (15%) (B). Viral content was substantially false: a Threads video of Chinese COVID disinfectant drones; an Instagram "gunfire at a drone" clip with added effects; drone light-show footage; and a Pennsylvania state senator's "crashed drone" that was a *Star Wars* TIE-fighter replica (C). New York City logged about **120 drone calls in the single weekend of 14–15 December** — more than the entire previous month — many resolved as aircraft, meteor showers or **Venus** (B). Hobbyist drones flown *in response to* the rumours generated further reports; a "military-grade" drone that crashed in Pequannock was a **toy drone**, assessed as a copycat (B).

Citizen vigilance produced **real harm**: laser strikes on aircraft in New Jersey **surged** during the episode, with a state counterterrorism bulletin recording green-laser interactions with commercial and cargo pilots; the FBI and State Police had to warn residents not to shoot at or laser aircraft, because most were manned (B/C). The medevac wave-off is the clearest case of a genuine, dangerous operational consequence of a misidentified report.

**Institutional response.** The FBI opened its investigation on **3 December**; a spokesperson told Congress on 10 December that **3,000+** public reports had produced no full explanation. The **16–17 December** joint DoD/DHS/FAA/FBI statement reviewed **over 5,000 reports** and found "nothing anomalous", attributing most to lawful drones, hobbyist drones, law-enforcement drones, manned aircraft, helicopters and **stars mistakenly reported as drones** (A). The FAA imposed temporary flight restrictions over the Trump Bedminster club, Picatinny Arsenal, **22 New Jersey communities** and parts of New York (A). The Senate extended counter-UAS authorities to **30 September 2025**, after which they lapsed; the Peters bill was blocked by **Rand Paul**; the Counter-UAS Authority reauthorisation did not pass (A). The **TSA had internally debunked** the highest-profile incidents by 17 December but did not publish; the material emerged only through a FOIA request months later — a decision that, as *Reason* argued, prolonged public concern while helping justify expanded counter-drone powers (B/C).

**Documented cost.** Stewart International Airport closed about an hour; Trenton-Mercer Airport grounded training flights and issued a NOTAM after ATC reported radar "primary targets" at up to **150 knots**; an Atlantic City alert; a medevac diverted; ~185 mayors' time; FBI/DHS/DoD/NJSP investigative effort across **6,000+ tips** (about 100 warranting further investigation); laser strikes; and a classified congressional briefing (A/B). A US soldier who died in the Las Vegas Cybertruck bombing on 1 January 2025 left a note invoking the drones as Chinese/US "gravitic propulsion systems" (C).

**Retraction and explanation.** On **28 January 2025** the White House press secretary said the aircraft were primarily **FAA-authorised research drones**, hobbyists and private individuals, and that public curiosity had driven the increase: "This was not the enemy" (A). No foreign or malicious actor was identified; **no drones were recovered and no arrests were made** in the investigation (B). In **October 2025** the *New York Post* reported that an unnamed defence contractor had allegedly taken credit at an Army summit for a 20-foot manned aircraft — a single anonymous source, disputed by federal officials and the aircraft's manufacturer. **Grade E; treat as unverified.**

**The 2026 sequel.** On **12 January 2026** Gov. Murphy signed legislation making New Jersey the **first US state to fund UAP research**: **$2.5m annually** for a university-based centre plus **$1m** for an air-traffic-controller loan-forgiveness programme. Connecticut, New Mexico and others followed with study bills (A/B). In March 2026 TWZ obtained thousands of pages of police records showing the internal conviction set: officers were told drones might be "military grade", might carry explosives, should be treated as bomb-squad matters if landed, and **could jam police radios and air-traffic radar**. No perpetrator was ever named.

**Verdict.** Burnout, not durable belief: Google search interest fell from **100 (mid-December) to 2 (12 January 2025)**; the panic was over in about six weeks (B). What persisted was not a belief but **a procurement and legislative posture**.

## 2. Gatwick, 19–21 December 2018 — the archetype

**Grade A for the police/court record; B for the misidentification finding; Confidence High that the drone was never evidenced.**

A **security officer at a bus stop reported two drones at about 21:00 on 19 December**; within half an hour **six more sightings** followed, **five from police officers**. The runway closed for **33 hours**, reopened 05:57 on 21 December, and shut again 17:30–18:23. About **1,000 flights and 140,000 passengers** were affected. Police logged **170 reports, 115 "credible"**; the Chief Constable cited **92–93 credible witnesses**. **No photograph or video of any drone ever emerged** (A).

The false-confirmation loop was explicit: **Chief Constable Giles York** conceded police drones "could have caused some level of confusion", and later sightings may have been of a Sussex Police drone. Det Ch Supt **Jason Tingley** said on 23 December that there "may never have been a drone" — then was publicly contradicted by his own chief the next day.

**Amplification** was political, media and institutional: the Prime Minister, the Defence and Transport Secretaries, the shadow transport and shadow attorney-general, the MoD and the CAA all responded publicly; the Army and RAF Regiment deployed; a **£50,000 reward** was offered. **Paul Gait and Elaine Kirk** were arrested at their home by 12 armed officers on 21 December, held ~36 hours, named in some outlets, and released without charge. In **June 2020** Sussex Police settled for **£200,000** (£55,000 damages + £145,000 costs) for wrongful arrest and false imprisonment (A).

The **18-month, £790,000 investigation across five forces closed on 27 September 2019 with nobody charged** (A). Police and the airport maintained it was a malicious attack; an insider theory was floated. The **Guardian's** 2020 investigation argued mass panic and misidentification; a former DJI executive said in 2021 it was "clear" there had been no drone; and a **2024 FOI showed the National Police Air Service logged no drone sightings at Gatwick between 19 and 31 December 2018** (B/C). The independent review remains **unpublished** on national-security grounds. Institutional consequence was durable regardless of the evidence: Gatwick installed a permanent counter-drone system in November 2019 and Heathrow in January 2020.

## 3. Copenhagen/Oslo and the September–October 2025 European wave

**Grade A for the retraction and official counts; B for the reconstruction; Confidence High.**

**The trigger and its collapse.** At about 20:00 on **22 September 2025** an airport employee raised the first alarm: "Der er to droner. De er store. Det er ikke legetøj" ("There are two drones. They are large. It is not a toy"). Copenhagen closed for about four hours (~100 movements, ~20,000 passengers); Oslo Gardermoen closed briefly the same night. Danish police reached the opposite conclusion: the large object was a **school training aircraft from Roskilde** cleared to fly in the area, the fast "zig-zagging" object was **lens flare**, and a colleague's later "drone" was the **police helicopter scrambled to hunt for the drones the first report had conjured**. The witness had **never seen a real drone in person**; she was interviewed four times and held her account (B, *Frihedsbrevet*, 18 March 2026).

**Amplification, in order.** On **23 September** the senior chief police inspector characterised "an actor with the capabilities, the will and the tools… perhaps also to train" — an institutional framing, not a media one. Within a day police logged **100+ reports**; within four days the National Police logged **500+ nationally in 24 hours**. PM **Mette Frederiksen** called it "the most serious attack on Danish critical infrastructure to date"; the Defence Minister called it a hybrid attack; **NOST** went to its highest readiness level. The Danish Armed Forces confirmed drones had been observed at several military sites. **Germany's Chancellor Merz** said "our suspicion is that Russia is behind most of these drone flights" — explicitly suspicion, without evidence (A/B).

**The most dangerous false positive.** At **Borris on 28 September** the military fired **43 rounds** at a supposed drone; TV 2 later reported a Messenger message at 20:09 reading "They just shot at a plane over the Borris camp" and flight-radar data showing a Widerøe Dash 8-400 over the camp at the time. The Defence Minister ordered a review (B).

**Retractions.** On **19 June 2026** Danish authorities said none of the ships investigated — **including Russian shadow-fleet vessels** — could be linked to the observations, undercutting the think-tank "shadow fleet" thesis published a fortnight later. On **25 June 2026**, after a nine-month investigation, Chief Police Inspector **Søren Thomassen** concluded police could "neither confirm… nor rule out" drone activity at the airport, named **no suspects**, and closed the case. Separately, the Armed Forces concluded that drones *had* overflown Danish military installations (A/B). The widely quoted line "we were not clear enough with our reservations" could **not** be traced to any primary source — **treat as unverified (E)**.

**Norway.** In February 2026 PST's Inger Haugland said: "We have not uncovered a single case of a drone being controlled by a foreign state," attributing reports to "various celestial bodies such as stars, weather phenomena, aeroplanes, or helicopters" (B).

**Belgium.** VRT's *Pano* (15 April 2026) examined **558 reports**; of about 250 over military areas, ADIV judged **42** to have "no other plausible explanation" — but under ADIV's own definition only physical evidence (an intercepted drone or a detained operator) is conclusive, and **none was ever produced**. The "enormous drone" video over Zaventem had been **sent to a newspaper by Defence Minister Francken himself**; it was a **federal police helicopter** (A/B). Defence conceded it lacked the means to detect or intervene. Opposition MPs later alleged the minister had been warned before publication and had trimmed ten seconds from the clip — an allegation he denies (C). A **£/€50m counter-drone programme** followed.

**Other 2026 retractions and false positives.** On **13 September 2026** Vilnius airport closed and NATO scrambled fighters from Šiauliai after a radar return assessed as a possible drone; **visual identification established a flock of birds**, and the airport reopened after **38 minutes**. A near-identical Polish track from the Belarus direction the previous week was also birds (B). Spain's Guardia Civil concluded the drone that closed **Madrid-Barajas on 6 November 2024** (about an hour, ~21 flights diverted) **never existed**; the February 2020 closure produced a comparable "ghost drone" story — Barajas replicating Gatwick's evidentiary collapse **twice** (*Telemadrid*, 7 October 2025; primary URL not retrievable — **C, Medium**).

**The strongest counter-current.** The **Poland incursions of 9–10 September 2025** were real: 19–23 drones entered from the Belarus direction, four were downed, airports closed and NATO invoked Article 4. Lublin prosecutors found unarmed decoy drones (A). And two European cases are confirmed and attributed: **Leipzig/Halle (4–5 August 2026)**, with explosives and a formal German attribution to Russia on 1 September 2026, and **Øresund (26 February 2026)**, where a drone launched from the Russian intelligence ship *Zhigulevsk* was jammed by HSwMS *Rapp* and the launch confirmed by technical data (A).

**The decisive comparative result.** The **Trouw + Dronewatch** investigation (November 2025) examined **~60 incidents in 11 countries** and found "much confusion and ambiguity and regularly false alarms"; in the vast majority of cases **no hard evidence of Russian involvement**; and roughly **40** with no evidence of any drone at all. A **Koninklijke Marechaussee** spokesperson told *Trouw* that reports "far from always" involve a drone and that "the cause can of course be all the attention, **including in the media**" — an official statement that salience drove reports (B). Specific misidentifications: stars (Zuid-Limburg; Billund), a ship (a Norwegian "oil-platform drone"), a police helicopter (three separate VRT fact-checks) and a landing DHL cargo plane (B). A natural experiment cuts the same way: after an advanced British detection system was rushed into Belgium, **reports declined rapidly** (B).

## 4. Germany, 2024–2026

**Grade A for aggregate official figures; B for case detail; Confidence Medium-High.**

DFS logged **151** drone incidents at German airports in 2023, **161** in 2024 and **144 by August 2025**, with hobby pilots "frequently assumed" and perpetrators "usually unknown" (A). A confidential **BKA Lagebild** (late August 2026) counted **165+ suspected sabotage cases**, **~747 suspicious drone incidents (1,068 drones)** and **~24 espionage investigations** in 2025 (A/B). The airport association BDL put disruption at **€60m in 2025, €160m including follow-up** (B).

The amplification pattern is the same as elsewhere. **Munich** closed twice on 3–4 October 2025 after "unconfirmed drone sightings", with Bundeswehr *Amtshilfe* and thousands affected; the **30 May 2026** event rested on two pilots' report, closed runways about an hour, diverted **26 aircraft**, and found nothing. On **6 September 2026** the Bundespolizei **explicitly declined to classify** an alarm as a drone incident at all. **Berlin-BER** closed ~2 hours on 31 October 2025, again on 12 March 2026, and ~45 minutes on **23 September 2026** with nothing recovered; H1 2026 brought 28 sightings, seven suspending operations — the most of any German airport (B). **Cologne/Bonn, 12 August 2026**: five sightings, landings blocked ~30 minutes, two Ryanair diversions, and — unusually — **a drone pilot found near the airport** and placed under criminal investigation (B). **Bremen**, 2–3 November 2025: police explicitly did **not** assume espionage (B).

**Who amplified.** Interior Minister **Dobrindt** used the Leipzig attack to announce a "new danger quality" and professional perpetrators implying "foreign powers", and directed the Bundespolizei drone-defence unit to grow from ~130 to **300 specialists at eight sites**; the Greens demanded a **"Drohnengipfel"** in the Chancellery; the ARX Robotics CEO's "das war ein Angriff auf NATO-Boden" interview travelled widely; Hesse's premier promised tougher defence. Interior ministers agreed to equip eight airports; **€4.2m** went to a Hamburg interception system and a German Centre for Drone Security opened at Cochstedt (B/C). The framing shifted from "hobby pilots" to "fremde Mächte" largely **after** Leipzig, and was then applied backwards to unresolved earlier sightings.

**A methodological caution (and a partially supported claim).** I could **not** verify a specific Bundestag document concluding that a significant share of German sightings were "technische Fehlfunktionen" — **NOT VERIFIED**. What is verified is the direction of travel: the BKA found that where pilots *were* identified, espionage usually proved absent, and **Leipzig/Halle (4–5 August 2026)** is the only German airport case with material evidence (explosive FPV drone, RDX, two SIM cards, a 5G router readable as a "phone", a relay antenna in a tree, a strike on an An-124's wing, a second drone hit at ~400m by a Boeing 757) (A).

## 5. US military bases and the UK MoD series

**Grade A for the UK primary figures; B for Langley; Confidence Medium-High.**

**Langley AFB, Virginia.** The Air Force confirmed to TWZ that the installation **first observed UAS activity on the evening of 6 December 2023** and experienced **multiple incursions throughout December**, of fluctuating number and varied size and configuration, adding that none "appeared to exhibit hostile intent" and that the FAA was notified. The *Wall Street Journal* later reported **17 days** of incursions, disrupting training missions. Gen. **Mark Kelly**, then Air Combat Command commander and an eyewitness, described a drone "roughly 20 feet long and flying at more than 100 miles an hour, at an altitude of roughly 3,000 to 4,000 feet… sounding in the distance like a parade of lawn mowers." Jamming and directed energy were considered and **ruled out** (B/C).

Gen. **Gregory Guillot** gave the first public acknowledgement at his March 2024 SASC hearing — "I wasn't prepared for the number of incursions that I see… I'm using that as the centerpiece of my 90-day assessment" — and stated in October 2024 that he knew of no "**organized or unorganized foreign nexus**", that he could describe only "roughly the number and roughly the altitude," and that no recovery point was known. The FBI had **no suspects and recovered no drones** (B). **No operators and no explanation were ever produced.**

The Langley amplification is instructive because **the institutional response leaked first**: plane-spotters using flight-tracking software detected F-16 combat air patrols over Newport News around 12 December and a **NASA WB-57F** flying orbits centred on Langley the following week, before any official statement (B). In a detection-poor environment, the *observable response* becomes the evidence that something is happening.

**US counts and the same observer-effect.** Guillot told the Senate in February 2025 that the military detected **350 drone incursions across ~100 installations of all types in 2024** — and that only about **half** of installations had the authority to counter them. A NORTHCOM spokesperson gave TWZ approximately **230 reported incursions in the year to September 2024 and ~420 in the year to September 2025**, while cautioning that **better sensing may explain part of the rise** (B) — the same counting artifact documented for the UK and Germany. A **counter-drone quick reaction force** was announced at Falcon Peak 25.2 in September 2025 (A/B). The **DoD Inspector General** (DODIG-2026-045, January 2026) found after visiting ten installations and interviewing 31 DoD organisations that policy for designating "covered" assets was unclear and that "**a large percentage of installations do not have the operational approval to use c-UAS capabilities**" (A). The one attributable case in this cluster is different in kind: **Yinpiao Zhou**, a Chinese citizen who flew a drone over Vandenberg Space Force Base on 30 November 2024 to photograph a classified launch, **pleaded guilty and was sentenced on 31 March 2025 to time served (~4 months) plus a year's supervised release** (A/B).

**United Kingdom.** The MoD's own February 2026 release gives the clean primary figures: **266 reported UAV incidents near Defence sites in 2025, up from 126 in 2024** — the "doubling" used to justify granting Defence personnel shoot-down powers in the Armed Forces Bill (introduced 15 January 2026) (A). Both figures are **calendar-year, all-UK-Defence-sites** counts, and the MoD attributes part of the rise to **better detection equipment and better reporting** (A).

The counting problem is severe. An **FOI answer to *The Independent*** put 2025 at **250**; **Lord Coaker told Parliament on 4 November 2025 (HL11210)** of **187 "drone sightings"** near military establishments since 1 January; the February release said **266**; and DroneXL reported **340 incidents in the year to August 2026**. **No published document defines an "incident"** as distinct from a sighting — so 187, 250 and 266 are three different 2025 numbers under at least two definitions (B/C). A count that rises because a base installed a new sensor **measures the observer**. USNORTHCOM's parallel figures make the same point: **~230 reported incursions in the year to September 2024 against ~420 in the year to September 2025**, with better sensing acknowledged as part of the explanation (B). The MoD's statement **names no perpetrator**; former defence minister **Tobias Ellwood** names Russia and Iran; **not one of the 340 has been publicly attributed** (B).

The UK case also contains the cleanest documentable misidentification of the whole series. A **USAFE spokesperson confirmed** UAS over Lakenheath, Mildenhall and Feltwell "**between November 20 and 22**" (2024), with ~60 British troops deployed. During it, a National Police Air Service **EC135 helicopter reported being "chased off", describing an object with "red flashing lights" carrying out "large orbits… in excess of 1,500ft"** — an object subsequently identified as a **USAF F-15**, likely from the very sorties the alarm had prompted. The Airprox Board judged that no collision risk resulted (B, Airprox Board via Metro). The retired detective who assembled the record notes the first alarms escalated to **COBR** and produced warnings to eight other bases including Menwith Hill and Fylingdales. The MoD Police inquiry closed with **no suspects** (FOI response, 27 January 2026; BBC, 3 February 2026); the MoD refuses FOI requests for the inquiry's name, senior investigating officer, staffing and cost, arguing disclosure would "help our adversaries" (B).

**The one attribution claim for the UK.** *The i Paper* reported on **21 February 2025** that three individuals with links to Russian military and intelligence sites had travelled to the UK and stayed near Lakenheath and Mildenhall, and that "**at least two… are thought to have worked as seasonal fruit pickers in close proximity to the airbases — visiting fields close to the airbases at night**." The paper was explicit that the evidence "**is not definitive proof of Russian involvement**" and that it "cannot be confirmed that the individuals were employed to pick fruit." The claim nonetheless triggered investigation demands from **Julian Lewis, Tom Tugendhat, Nick Timothy, Terry Jermy and Alicia Kearns**, and the Commons Defence Committee said it would consider the material in a "grey-zone" inquiry — a textbook case of an unproven attribution acquiring political momentum (D, Low confidence). **No government body has confirmed Russian involvement** in the UK sightings; IISS's July 2026 "highly likely" finding and FPRI's assessment are think-tank judgements, and FPRI itself notes the Russia link "was never confirmed" (B/C).

**The strongest fact in this section cuts against the apparatus**: the UK sent RAF counter-drone specialists and equipment to Belgium in November 2025 to defend Belgian bases **with equipment its own soldiers could not lawfully use to bring down a drone at home** — a capability driven by the count, not by any attribution.

## 6. El Paso, 10–11 February 2026 — the 2026 sequel and the sharpest case

**Grade A/B; Confidence High.**

At about **23:30 local on Tuesday 10 February 2026** the FAA announced a **10-day closure of all flights over El Paso** for "special security reasons" — without notice to the airport, the city, or the local congressional delegation, and from Washington rather than regional air traffic control. It was lifted shortly before **07:00 on Wednesday 11 February**. SECDEF-level political framing followed: Transportation Secretary **Sean Duffy** posted that the FAA had worked with the Pentagon to "address a cartel drone incursion" and that "the threat has been neutralised"; an administration official said "Mexican cartel drones breached US airspace. The Department of War took action to disable the drones" (A).

**The retraction.** Two government officials told *The Intercept* the closure was connected to the Department of War's new **counter-drone laser near Fort Bliss** and a miscommunication with FAA headquarters about the risk it posed to air traffic. One said a cartel drone may have been damaged; another said a **Mylar party balloon** was destroyed — apparently **different events**. Rep. **Veronica Escobar**, whose district includes El Paso, said flatly: "There was not a threat, which is why the FAA lifted this restriction so quickly… There was nothing extraordinary about any drone incursion into the U.S. that I'm aware of" (A/B). CBS reported the Pentagon had wanted to test the laser sooner than the safety meetings with the FAA allowed, and that a test had shot down what appeared to be a foreign drone but was a party balloon (B). The Mayor told the BBC the chaos "was completely unnecessary"; Mexican officials reportedly feared the closure signalled a US incursion into Mexico (B).

**Amplification here was primarily official.** The technical trigger was an internal test-scheduling dispute; the political framing ("cartel drone incursion", "the threat has been neutralised") converted it into a border-security event; the 10-day closure order then functioned as public evidence of a threat; and the correction took under eight hours, far faster than the original claim travelled.

**The durable consequence is real, not rhetorical.** After "back-to-back airspace blunders earlier this year", USNORTHCOM confirmed on **28 August 2026** that Joint Task Force–Southern Border used a high-energy laser to **defeat three cartel-linked drones** at the southern border — a primary-source confirmation, with the Army publicising the system at about "$3 a shot" (A). The ambiguous February episode became the legitimising frame for a capability that then produced genuine interdictions. This is the clearest example in the corpus of **an ambiguity-induced response generating its own later validation.**

## 7. Two further corroborating episodes

**Colorado–Nebraska, December 2019 – January 2020.** Hundreds of reports of drones flying in grid formations of up to 19, 200–500ft, involving **70+ federal, state and local agencies** at a Brush, Colorado meeting on 6 January 2020; Gov. Polis ordered state monitoring after a drone came within 100ft of a medical helicopter; a search for an "unspecified command vehicle" found nothing; the Air Force neither claimed nor denied ownership of counter-drone test flights from F.E. Warren AFB. The Colorado Department of Public Safety concluded the **majority of sightings were planets, stars, commercial aircraft and hobbyist drones**; a WOWT "drone" was a FedEx Cessna Caravan; **VICE News reported in January 2020 that the drones "were not real."** Sightings tapered off by end-January and the state closed the investigation — roughly **six weeks** (B/C). This is the strongest "no verified physical event, full institutional response" case outside Gatwick.

**Belgium's November 2025 wave and the VRT/Pano review** are covered in §3 and belong here analytically: **558 reports**, no conclusive evidence, a ministerial video that was a police helicopter, and a €50m procurement programme that followed regardless.

## 8. Was any drone scare deliberately amplified? A documented negative

**Overall grade B/C; Confidence High for the negative; Medium for the positive 2026 cases.**

I found **no credible, documented finding that a foreign state, bot network, troll farm or influence operation deliberately amplified any drone-sighting wave** — least of all the September–October 2025 western-European wave (Denmark, Norway, Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands). No Europol, EEAS, EDMO, DFRLab, ISD, EU DisinfoLab or Hybrid CoE report attributing a drone panic to amplification was located, and the EU's own **COM(2026) 81 final** drone Action Plan (11 February 2026) contains **zero** occurrences of *disinformation*, *FIMI*, *information manipulation*, *misinformation* or *amplification* — it is a capability and prevention instrument, not an information one (A, checked in full).

What **is** documented is materially different:

- **Real hostile physical flights, separately proven** — Leipzig/Halle (August 2026, attributed to Russia), Øresund (February 2026), Poland (9–10 September 2025). Physical acts, not information operations (A).
- **Reactive FIMI accompanying drone incursions, confirmed at EU level** — the **EEAS 4th Report on FIMI Threats** (March 2026) makes drones an explicit FIMI category and states that escalatory hybrid actions "including drone incursions… in EU countries such as **Poland, Romania, Lithuania and Estonia**… were accompanied by FIMI designed to manage public perception and test responses." Of 540 incidents in 2025, **29% were attributed to Russia, 6% to China and 65% were unattributed**. **Denmark and Norway are not named.** EUvsDisinfo (18 September 2025) documented the Kremlin "smokescreen of FIMI over drones in Poland" — reactive denial *after* a confirmed Russian incursion; EDMO/BROD analysed 18,316 posts whose narrative clusters ("Ukraine Behind Drones", "NATO Drone Hysteria") **followed** the event (A/B). The pattern is opportunistic narrative capture, not orchestration of a sighting wave.
- **One documented coordinated amplification of a drone narrative — Romania, May–June 2026.** EDMO/BROD found the Galați strike (29 May 2026) was sustained by a coordinated campaign over **seven consecutive days**: 28–105 accounts per day, 3,400–6,800 posts per day, 116,014 posts analysed, 10–155m views per network. The Constanța incident, by contrast, "emerged organically during a temporary information vacuum." This is the strongest positive evidence anywhere in the corpus — and it amplified a **false-flag claim about a real strike**, not a sighting wave (B).
- **One drone-tied state information-operation accusation — Latvia, 27 March 2026**, in which the Defence Ministry alleged "a massive information operation against the Baltic states" and the MFA summoned the Russian ambassador. Grade **C, Medium** (B/C).
- **One amplification of an unproven drone-scare *theory*, by a think tank and the press — the IISS report of July 2026.** IISS claimed an 18-month Russian campaign with **shadow-fleet ships as launch platforms**, tying 144 incidents in 13 countries to Russian intelligence; the *Guardian* and *Telegraph* amplified it. Dronewatch's rebuttal found no physical evidence linking any specific drone to any specific ship; some named vessels were >100km from the sightings; and Dutch ministers said the Volkel and Eindhoven cases involved **small hobby drones** that lack the range to fly hundreds of kilometres from North Sea ships. IISS discussed the absence of confirmation and argued it *fits* a successful deception campaign — which Dronewatch called effectively **unfalsifiable** (B/C). **The amplifiers were a think tank and the press, not a hostile state.**
- **A concept correction.** The "hide in the noise" framing sometimes attached to drone waves traces to Capt. Matthew Moellering, "Hiding in the Noise", Modern War Institute, 26 September 2022 — and it describes **US special-operations forces hiding from adversary AI surveillance**, the opposite of a state concealing action inside a noisy information environment. The concept is **not verified** in any EU/NATO drone document (A).

**Conclusion for this strand's central question.** For the wave that matters most, the amplification was **organic, political, media and institutional — and the most consequential amplifier was the responding state itself.** Adversarial amplification of a drone scare is documented only for 2026, only in Romania, and only around a real strike.

## 9. Two direct answers

**(a) Was there any case where the physical event was fabricated entirely and the response was produced by the target society?**
**Not by an adversary — no.** No case was found in which a state or influence operation fabricated a drone event that a target society then responded to. But **"no verified physical event at all" is common**, and in four cases the response was produced almost entirely by the reporting-and-response system:

| Case | Physical finding | Response produced |
|---|---|---|
| Gatwick 2018 | No drone ever evidenced; no image; NPAS logged no sightings; police drone/helicopter implicated in later reports | 33-hour closure, ~1,000 flights, arrests, £790k investigation, £200k settlement |
| Colorado–Nebraska 2019–20 | DPS: majority were planets, stars, aircraft, hobby drones; VICE: "not real" | 70+ agency meeting, state aircraft search, "command vehicle" hunt, mayoral law proposals |
| Madrid-Barajas, Nov 2024 | Guardia Civil: the drone "never existed" | ~1 hour closure, ~21 flights diverted (a repeat of Feb 2020) |
| Copenhagen, 22 Sep 2025 | Police: cannot confirm or rule out; school aircraft + lens flare; police helicopter generated a new sighting | 4-hour closure, national alert level, 500+ reports/day, nine-month investigation |

In each, the event was **constituted by the detection and response architecture**, not by an airframe. That is the empirical core the parent hypothesis needs: ambiguity alone, with no fabricated physical object, can produce real closures, real resource expenditure and real law.

**(b) How often does an ambiguous incident lead to durable belief versus burning out quickly?**
**Burnout is the norm; durability is the exception and is bought institutionally, not informationally.**

| Episode | Public belief | Institutional residue |
|---|---|---|
| Gatwick 2018 | Days–weeks | Permanent counter-drone systems at Gatwick (Nov 2019) and Heathrow (Jan 2020); review still withheld |
| Colorado 2019–20 | ~6 weeks | State report; local drone ordinances proposed |
| New Jersey 2024 | ~6 weeks (search 100→2 in a month); 78% nonetheless believed government was withholding information | FAA restrictions; counter-UAS authority extension (then lapse); **state UAP research centre, $2.5m/yr (Jan 2026)** |
| Copenhagen 2025 | Official narrative **9 months** before retraction; public belief dissipated sooner | NOST readiness; Nordic/Baltic air policing; NATO Baltic posture |
| Belgium 2025 | Reports fell after detection improved | **€50m** counter-drone programme; ministerial accountability dispute |
| UK 2024–26 | No attribution, no resolution, no public demand for one | **266→340 counts**, armed-forces shoot-down powers, £750m, restricted airspace over 44+ sites |
| El Paso Feb 2026 | <8 hours | 10-day closure order; laser programme legitimised; **three real interdictions by Aug 2026** |

Three regularities follow. **First**, rumour intensity is a product of importance and ambiguity; ambiguity resolves cheaply, so public belief decays in weeks unless an institution keeps supplying it. **Second**, the residue is **institutional**: closures, powers, procurement, restricted airspace, research centres and *counts*. The UK is the pure case — a number that nearly tripled while measuring the observer, driving statute. **Third**, durability of *belief* requires a named mechanism plus a victim class plus an institutional owner of a harm question (the Havana pattern). Drone scares almost never produce a victim class — nobody claims injury — so the FBI's "threat" question is answered "no threat", the task closes, and the belief dies. Where drones *did* acquire a named perpetrator and a genuine physical fact (Leipzig 2026, Øresund 2026, Poland 2025), the response persisted — because the event was real.

## 10. Synthesis: the causal chain across cases

| Case | Physical ambiguity | First reporter | Principal amplifier | Type | Cost / consequence | Attribution | Grade / Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gatwick 2018 | Two "drones" at 21:00; no drone ever evidenced | Airport security officer | Chief Constable's certainty + national political response | Political + media + institutional | 33h closure, ~1,000 flights, £790k, £200k settlement | None; contested | A/B · High |
| Colorado 2019–20 | Grid formations, 19 objects | County sheriffs / residents | Sheriffs + local media + state | Political + media | 70+ agency meeting, state air search | None; majority misidentified | B/C · High |
| NJ/Northeast 2024 | 11 Picatinny sightings; genuine incursion pattern unexplained | Picatinny contractor (13 Nov) | OSINT accounts (19 Nov) → 21→185 mayors → national media | Organic + political + media | ~1,000 flight effects, 6,000 tips, laser strikes, UAP centre | None; hostile claims refuted | A/B · High |
| Copenhagen 2025 | Two objects; school aircraft + lens flare | Airport employee | Police "capable actor" + PM + NOST | **Institutional** + political + media | 4h closure, 500 reports/day, 9-month inquiry, 43 rounds at Borris | None; case closed | A/B · High |
| Germany 2024–26 | Mostly unresolved sightings | Airport staff / pilots | Ministerial framing after Leipzig; industry voices | Political + media | €60m/€160m; 8 airports equipped; 300 specialists | Leipzig only | A/B · Med-High |
| UK bases 2024–26 | 126→266→340 counts, no attribution | Base personnel; a civilian following rumours (Lakenheath) | MoD count itself + former ministers + press | **Institutional** + political | Shoot-down powers, £750m, 44+ restricted zones | None of 340 | A/B · Med-High |
| Langley 2023 | UAS incursions from 6 Dec | Base sensors, then flight-trackers | OSINT flight-tracking of the **response** | Organic + media | F-16 CAPs, NASA WB-57F, 90-day assessment | None | B · Medium |
| El Paso Feb 2026 | Test shot at a party balloon; possible cartel drone | Pentagon test / FAA | **Transportation Secretary and White House** | **Institutional/political** | 10-day closure order (~8h actual), border-security framing, later real interdictions | Contested; balloon | A/B · High |

**The pattern.** In eight cases the first reporter was a **credentialled individual with an institutional role**, not a sensor: security officer, contractor, patrol officer, airport employee, sheriff, pilot. The pivotal amplification was **almost never a bot or a foreign account** — it was a **police chief, a mayor, a prime minister, a transport secretary, a think tank, or the mere existence of an official counter-drone programme**. Physical ambiguity supplied the ignition; institutions supplied the oxygen; and the institutional response then became the evidence that sustained the next round of reports. The adversarial-amplification pathway is, on this evidence, **the least attested link in the chain** — and the institutional-amplification pathway the most.

## 11. Evidence-quality summary and what would change the assessment

**Grade A** (primary/official): the FAA/DoD/DHS/FBI joint statement; FAA flight restrictions; Danish police and Armed Forces statements and the 25 June 2026 closure; PST February 2026; GOV.UK 266/126; USNORTHCOM's 28 August 2026 laser interdiction; NJ UAP legislation; Sussex Police/court record; Leipzig attribution; EEAS FIMI report; COM(2026) 81.

**Grade B** (transparent independent method): Trouw + Dronewatch (60 incidents/11 countries); Dronewatch's IISS rebuttal; Montclair State social-media analysis; the TWZ FOIA/OPRA corpus; German and Belgian aggregate figures; Rolling Stone's on-the-record reconstruction.

**Grade C** (reputable journalism, single outlet): *Telemadrid* on Barajas; *Frihedsbrevet* and DR on Copenhagen; Metro on Lakenheath; the *Independent*/DroneXL FOI figures.

**Grade D/E** (weak, treat as unverified): the *New York Post* anonymous contractor claim (Oct 2025); *The i Paper*'s GRU "fruit pickers"; the "we were not clear enough with our reservations" quotation; the Bundestag "technische Fehlfunktionen" finding; the widely repeated "shadow fleet launch platform" thesis.

**What would change the assessment.** (1) A released national attribution naming an actor for any of the 340 UK, 747 German or 558 Belgian reports. (2) Primary evidence that bots or state accounts amplified the September–October 2025 western wave — none exists today, and the EEAS report pointedly excludes Denmark and Norway. (3) Discovery of a single, provable case of a deliberately fabricated drone event — the precondition for the strongest version of the parent hypothesis, and the one thing this corpus does not contain. Absent those, the correct reading is that **ambiguous drone incidents consistently produce real disruption through organic, political and institutional amplification, and that the disruption is self-limiting in belief but self-reinforcing in institutions.**
