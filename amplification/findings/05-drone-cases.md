# 05 — Drone-sighting cases: ambiguity, amplification, and who amplified what

**Empirical strand for the question whether AI agents could deliberately amplify ambiguous physical threats. Cut-off 25 September 2026. Analytic/defensive only.**

**Grades: A** primary/official/peer-reviewed · **B** transparent independent method · **C** reputable journalism · **D** single-source · **E** weak/unverified. Chain used throughout: **ambiguity → reports → amplification → attention → citizen vigilance → further genuine or mistaken reports → institutional response → further perceived validation.**

---

## 0. The structural finding

These episodes share a **detection architecture**, not an information architecture. A sparse, unresolvable substrate exists (lawful drones, aircraft, stars, birds, a balloon, occasionally a hostile airframe). A **credentialled individual** — officer, contractor, airport employee, pilot — reports it, and the credential rather than the sensor makes it travel. Actors who **cannot verify it** amplify it: scanner accounts, local officials with no answer, media, and above all **the responding institutions themselves**. The response becomes the evidence — closures, fighters, restricted airspace — and generates the next sighting; **counts then rise because detection improved**, and the rise is cited as proof of the threat the detection was bought for.

**Central result: in every disputed case the amplifiers were organic, political, media, or the responding state. No deliberate adversarial amplification of a drone-sighting wave was credibly documented (§8).**

## 1. New Jersey / US Northeast, November–December 2024 — the reference case

**Grade A (official) / B (FOIA record). Confidence High.**

**Ambiguity and first reports.** First documented observation: **Picatinny Arsenal, Morris County, 13 November 2024** — a contractor saw "a light rising straight up from the tree line". Picatinny confirmed **11 drone sightings and seven that were aeroplanes**. Officials eventually confirmed **FAA research drones, hobbyist drones, law-enforcement drones, manned aircraft, helicopters and stars** among reports in one of the world's most congested airspaces. Representative collapses: the **26 November medevac wave-off** was three airliners aligned on approach to Solberg Airport; the **Salem nuclear-plant** report matched a UH-60, a Cessna C150 and Philadelphia traffic (A; DHS/TSA FOIA slides, May 2025).

**Amplification.** On **19 November** the account **@OSINTdefender** posted that "upwards of a dozen large drones" were over Morris County, tracked by law enforcement and a State Police helicopter; **The War Zone** published the first Picatinny story the same day. TWZ later recorded "an avalanche of drone reports… and a crush of media coverage from around the globe", with broadcasters airing 737s as "mysterious craft" (B/C).

**Political amplification was decisive.** On **8 December** mayors of **21 Morris County townships** wrote to Gov. Murphy about the fact that nobody owned the question; within 48 hours ~**185 mayors** attended a closed briefing with State Police, FBI, DHS and DoD, were told officials did not know what the objects were but that there was no threat, and left without an answer; Mayor **Ryan Herd** walked out and gave interviews to "every single media tent". Others: Assemblywoman **Dawn Fantasia** (briefing notes describing six-foot drones "not hobbyists"), Sen. **Andy Kim**, former governor **Larry Hogan** (video of the constellation **Orion**), Rep. **Jeff Van Drew** (an Iranian "mothership", Pentagon-refuted), **Trump** (shoot them down), five governors and six senators (B/C).

**Citizen vigilance and harm.** Montclair State analysed **170,000+ posts**: conversation rose **19,000%**, **fear (45%)** dominant, **X carrying 86%** (B). New York City logged ~**120 calls in the weekend of 14–15 December**, many resolved as aircraft or **Venus** (B). **Laser strikes on aircraft surged**, and police warned residents not to shoot at or laser aircraft because most were manned (B/C).

**Response, cost, retraction, sequel.** On **17 December** the joint DHS/FBI/FAA/DoD statement reviewed **5,000+ sightings and ~100 leads** and found "**nothing anomalous**", attributing most to lawful drones, hobbyists, law enforcement, manned aircraft, helicopters and **stars** (A). The FAA restricted airspace over Picatinny, the Bedminster club and **22 NJ communities**; counter-UAS authority lapsed after **30 September 2025** (A). The **TSA had internally debunked** the worst reports by 17 December but published nothing until FOIA months later — which *Reason* argued prolonged concern while helping justify expanded powers (B/C). Stewart International closed ~1 hour; a medevac diverted; **6,000+ tips**. Police had been told the drones might be "military grade" and **could jam police radios and air-traffic radar** (B). On **28 January 2025** the White House attributed them primarily to **FAA-authorised research drones** and hobbyists: "This was not the enemy" (A). **No drones were recovered, no arrests made, no foreign actor identified.** An October 2025 *New York Post* contractor claim rests on **one anonymous source (Grade E)**. On **12 January 2026** New Jersey became the **first US state to fund UAP research** (**$2.5m/yr**) (A/B). Public belief nonetheless **burned out in ~six weeks** — search interest 100 → 2 by 12 January 2025.

## 2. Gatwick, 19–21 December 2018 — the archetype

**Grade A (police/court record) / B (misidentification). Confidence High that no drone was ever evidenced.**

A **security officer at a bus stop reported two drones at about 21:00 on 19 December**; within half an hour **six more sightings followed, five from police officers**. The runway closed **33 hours**, affecting ~**1,000 flights and 140,000 passengers**. Police logged **170 reports, 115 "credible"**. **No photograph or video of a drone ever emerged** (A).

Chief Constable **Giles York** conceded police drones "could have caused some level of confusion", and Det Ch Supt **Jason Tingley** said on 23 December there "may never have been a drone" — before his own chief publicly contradicted him the next day. Amplification was political, media and institutional: the PM, two Secretaries of State, shadow ministers, the Army, MoD and CAA all responded; a **£50,000 reward** was offered. **Paul Gait and Elaine Kirk** were arrested by 12 armed officers, held ~36 hours and released without charge; Sussex Police settled in **June 2020 for £200,000** (A).

The **18-month, £790,000 investigation across five forces closed on 27 September 2019 with nobody charged** (A); police and the airport maintained it was a malicious attack. The *Guardian*'s 2020 investigation argued mass panic, and a **2024 FOI showed the National Police Air Service logged no drone sightings at Gatwick between 19 and 31 December 2018** (B/C). Permanent counter-drone systems nonetheless followed at Gatwick (Nov 2019) and Heathrow (Jan 2020).

## 3. Copenhagen/Oslo and the September–October 2025 European wave

**Grade A (retraction, official counts) / B (reconstruction). Confidence High.**

**Trigger and collapse.** At about 20:00 on **22 September 2025** an airport employee raised the first alarm: "Der er to droner. De er store. Det er ikke legetøj" ("There are two drones. They are large. It is not a toy"). Copenhagen closed ~4 hours; Oslo Gardermoen closed briefly the same night. Investigators concluded the large object was a **school training aircraft from Roskilde**, the fast "zig-zagging" object was **lens flare**, and a colleague's later "drone" was the **police helicopter scrambled to hunt the drones the first report had conjured**. The witness had **never seen a real drone in person** (B; *Frihedsbrevet*, 18 March 2026).

**Amplification.** On **23 September** a senior chief police inspector described "an actor with the capabilities, the will and the tools… perhaps also to train" — an **institutional** framing, not a media one. Police logged **100+ reports** within a day and **500+ nationally in 24 hours** within four. PM **Mette Frederiksen** called it "the most serious attack on Danish critical infrastructure to date"; **NOST** went to its highest readiness level; Germany's Chancellor **Merz** said he suspected Russia behind "most of these drone flights" — explicitly suspicion, without evidence (A/B). At **Borris on 28 September** the military fired **43 rounds** at a supposed drone that was probably an airliner (B).

**Retractions and false positives.** On **25 June 2026** Chief Police Inspector **Søren Thomassen** concluded police could "neither confirm… nor rule out" drone activity at the airport, named **no suspects** and closed the case, while the Armed Forces separately concluded drones *had* overflown Danish military installations; on **19 June 2026** Denmark had said none of the ships investigated — **including Russian shadow-fleet vessels** — could be linked to the observations (A/B). The widely quoted "we were not clear enough with our reservations" could not be traced to any primary source (**Grade E**). Norway's PST said in February 2026 it had "not uncovered a single case of a drone being controlled by a foreign state", attributing reports to "stars, weather phenomena, aeroplanes, or helicopters" (B). VRT's *Pano* (15 April 2026) examined **558 Belgian reports**; of ~250 over military areas, ADIV judged **42** to have "no other plausible explanation" — but only physical evidence is conclusive, and **none was ever produced**. The "enormous drone" video over Zaventem had been **sent to a newspaper by Defence Minister Francken himself**; it was a **federal police helicopter** (A/B). Vilnius airport closed on **13 September 2026** after a radar return; **visual identification found a flock of birds** (B). Spain's Guardia Civil concluded the drone that closed **Madrid-Barajas on 6 November 2024** **never existed** (**C, Medium**).

**The counter-current.** The **Poland incursions of 9–10 September 2025** were real: 19–23 drones from the Belarus direction, four downed, NATO Article 4 invoked (A). **Øresund (26 February 2026)** is confirmed and attributed: a drone from the Russian intelligence ship *Zhigulevsk* was jammed by HSwMS *Rapp* (A). Against that, the **Trouw + Dronewatch** audit of **61 sightings in 11 countries** found ~**40 with no evidence of any drone** and only **three confirmed Russian** (Poland/Romania/Moldova). Reports "far from always" involve a drone, and "the cause can of course be all the attention, **including in the media**" — a **Koninklijke Marechaussee** spokesperson told *Trouw* (B).

## 4. Germany, 2024–2026

**Grade A (aggregates, Bundestag record) / B (case detail). Confidence Medium-High.**

**Counts — and a correction.** DFS logged **151** incidents at German airports in 2023, **161** in 2024 and **144 by August 2025**; DFS counts obstructions, not confirmed drones (A). The confidential **BKA Lagebild** of end-August 2026 covers **2026 year-to-date, not 2025**: **>165 suspected sabotage cases**, **747 suspicious drone incidents involving 1,068 drones**, and **24 espionage proceedings**. The 2025 full-year comparison is **~1,300 drone "Sachverhalte"**, so 2026 is running **below** 2025 (A) — figures circulating as "2025" are mis-dated.

**The epistemic baseline — and a claim to reject.** The government's answer to a December 2025 written question (**Drucksache 21/3236**) states that "**no reliable and generally valid statements can be derived**" from the information, "particularly because of the heterogeneity of the sightings", and that involvement of intelligence or foreign state bodies "**is to be presumed** for a non-quantifiable portion." A check of 53 Bundestag papers containing "Drohn" found **no** official finding that German sightings were malfunctions, misperceptions or bird strikes. The "**technische Fehlfunktionen**" phrase is real but refers elsewhere: **Drucksache 21/7725** (25 August 2026) applies it to **Ukrainian drones in Finnish and Baltic airspace**. Any claim that the Bundestag found German airport sightings to be technical malfunctions is **NOT VERIFIED and should not be cited.**

**Episodes.** Munich closed twice on 3–4 October 2025 after "unconfirmed drone sightings"; **30 May 2026** closed runways ~1 hour, diverted **26 aircraft** and found nothing; on **6 September 2026** the Bundespolizei **explicitly declined to classify** an alarm as a drone incident. **Cologne/Bonn, 12 August 2026** produced the clearest German attribution: five sightings and a **drone pilot found near the airport**. **Leipzig/Halle, 4–5 August 2026** is the only German airport case with material evidence: a **Semtex-armed** drone found beside a Ukrainian Antonov after striking a wing — **undetected by the airport's own sensor** — with a **tree-mounted antenna** and DNA secured; the **Bundesanwaltschaft** took over and **Germany formally blamed Russia on 1 September 2026** (A).

**Who amplified.** The loudest escalation **preceded** any confirmed German hostile drone. During the **29 September – 7 October 2025** wave, driven substantially by Danish and unconfirmed German sightings, **Kiesewetter (CDU) demanded the "Spannungsfall" be declared**; **Söder** said "**Abschießen statt abwarten!**"; **Merz** suspected Russia. The one case with a device and payload came **ten months later**; **Dobrindt** then announced a "neue **Gefahrenqualität**" and a contest "possibly involving **foreign powers**", and raised the federal drone-defence unit from ~130 to **300 specialists at eight sites**. Exact-phrase searching for the ARX Robotics chief's "**Angriff auf kritische Infrastruktur auf NATO-Boden**" line (Merkur, 6 August 2026) returns only that interview itself — industry framing that **failed to cascade**.

## 5. US military bases and the UK MoD series

**Grade A (UK primary figures) / B (Langley). Confidence Medium-High.**

**Langley AFB, Virginia.** The Air Force confirmed **UAS activity from the evening of 6 December 2023** through December, none appearing to "exhibit hostile intent"; the *WSJ* later reported **17 days**. Gen. **Mark Kelly**, an eyewitness, described a drone "roughly 20 feet long… more than 100 miles an hour… 3,000 to 4,000 feet". Gen. **Gregory Guillot** said in October 2024 that he knew of no "**organized or unorganized foreign nexus**", could describe only "roughly the number and roughly the altitude", and knew of no recovery point; the FBI had **no suspects and recovered no drones** (B). **No operators and no explanation were ever produced.** The amplification is instructive because **the institutional response leaked first**: plane-spotters using flight-tracking software detected F-16 patrols over Newport News and a **NASA WB-57F** orbiting Langley before any official statement (B).

**US counts and the observer-effect.** Guillot told the Senate in February 2025 of **350 incursions across ~100 installations in 2024**, with only about **half** authorised to counter them. A NORTHCOM spokesperson gave ~**230 reported incursions in the year to September 2024 and ~420 in the year to September 2025**, cautioning that **better sensing may explain part of the rise** (B) — the same counting artifact seen in the UK and Germany. The **DoD Inspector General** (January 2026) found that "**a large percentage of installations do not have the operational approval to use c-UAS capabilities**" (A).

**United Kingdom.** The MoD's February 2026 release gives the primary figures: **266 reported UAV incidents near Defence sites in 2025, up from 126 in 2024** — the "doubling" used to justify shoot-down powers in the **Armed Forces Bill** (introduced 15 January 2026). Both are calendar-year, all-UK-Defence-sites counts, and the MoD attributes part of the rise to **better detection and better reporting** (A). The counting problem is severe: an **FOI answer to *The Independent*** put 2025 at **250**; **Lord Coaker told Parliament (HL11210, 4 November 2025)** of **187 "drone sightings"**; DroneXL reported **340 incidents in the year to August 2026**. **No published document defines an "incident"** as distinct from a sighting (B/C). A count that rises because a base installed a new sensor **measures the observer**; the MoD's statement **names no perpetrator**, while former minister **Tobias Ellwood** names Russia and Iran — **not one of the 340 has been publicly attributed** (B).

The UK series also contains the cleanest documentable misidentification: a police **EC135 helicopter reported being "chased off"** by an object later identified as a **USAF F-15**, likely from the sorties the alarm over Lakenheath, Mildenhall and Feltwell ("between November 20 and 22", 2024) had prompted (B). The **MoD Police inquiry closed with no suspects** (FOI, 27 January 2026); the MoD refuses FOI requests for the inquiry's name, senior investigating officer and cost (B).

**The one UK attribution claim.** *The i Paper* reported on **21 February 2025** that three individuals with links to Russian military and intelligence sites had stayed near Lakenheath and Mildenhall, "**at least two… thought to have worked as seasonal fruit pickers in close proximity to the airbases**" — while stating this is "**not definitive proof of Russian involvement**." It nonetheless triggered investigation demands from **Julian Lewis, Tom Tugendhat, Nick Timothy, Terry Jermy and Alicia Kearns** — an unproven attribution acquiring political momentum (**Grade D, Low confidence**).

## 6. El Paso, 10–11 February 2026 — the sharpest case

**Grade A/B. Confidence High.**

At about **23:30 local on 10 February 2026** the FAA announced a **10-day closure of all flights over El Paso** for "special security reasons" — without notice to the airport, the city or the local congressional delegation, and issued from Washington rather than by regional air traffic control. It was lifted shortly before **07:00 on 11 February**. Transportation Secretary **Sean Duffy** posted that the FAA had worked with the Pentagon to "address a cartel drone incursion" and that "the threat has been neutralised"; an administration official said "Mexican cartel drones breached US airspace. The Department of War took action to disable the drones" (A).

Two government officials told *The Intercept* the closure was connected to the Department of War's new **counter-drone laser near Fort Bliss** and a miscommunication with FAA headquarters. One said a cartel drone may have been damaged; another said a **Mylar party balloon** was destroyed — apparently **different events**. Rep. **Veronica Escobar** said flatly: "There was not a threat, which is why the FAA lifted this restriction so quickly" (A/B).

**Amplification here was primarily official.** The trigger was an internal test-scheduling dispute; political framing converted it into a border-security event; the 10-day closure order functioned as public evidence of a threat; the correction took under eight hours — faster than the claim travelled. **The durable consequence is real**: USNORTHCOM confirmed on **28 August 2026** that Joint Task Force–Southern Border used a high-energy laser to **defeat three cartel-linked drones** (A). The ambiguous February episode became the legitimising frame for a capability that then produced genuine interdictions — the clearest case in this corpus of **an ambiguity-induced response generating its own later validation**.

## 7. Another corroborating episode

**Colorado–Nebraska, December 2019 – January 2020.** Hundreds of reports of drones in grid formations of up to 19 at 200–500ft; **70+ agencies** met at Brush, Colorado on 6 January 2020; Gov. Polis ordered state monitoring after a drone came within 100ft of a medical helicopter. The state concluded the **majority of sightings were planets, stars, commercial aircraft and hobbyist drones**; **VICE News reported in January 2020 that the drones "were not real."** The investigation closed after roughly **six weeks** (B/C).

## 8. Was any drone scare deliberately amplified? A documented negative

**Grade B/C. Confidence High for the negative; Medium for the 2026 positives.**

I found **no credible, documented finding that a foreign state, bot network, troll farm or influence operation deliberately amplified any drone-sighting wave** — least of all the September–October 2025 western-European wave. No Europol, EEAS, EDMO, DFRLab, ISD, EU DisinfoLab or Hybrid CoE report attributing a drone panic to amplification was located, and the EU's own **COM(2026) 81 final** drone Action Plan (11 February 2026) contains **zero** occurrences of *disinformation*, *FIMI*, *misinformation* or *amplification* — it is a capability instrument, not an information one (A, checked in full).

What **is** documented is materially different:

- **Real hostile physical flights** — Leipzig/Halle (Aug 2026, attributed), Øresund (Feb 2026), Poland (Sept 2025). Physical acts, not information operations (A).
- **Reactive FIMI accompanying drone incursions, confirmed at EU level** — the **EEAS 4th Report on FIMI Threats** (March 2026) makes drones an explicit FIMI category and states that hybrid actions "including drone incursions… in EU countries such as **Poland, Romania, Lithuania and Estonia**… were accompanied by FIMI designed to manage public perception and test responses." Of 540 incidents in 2025, **29% were attributed to Russia, 6% to China and 65% were unattributed**; **Denmark and Norway are not named** (A). EUvsDisinfo documented the Kremlin's "smokescreen of FIMI over drones in Poland" — reactive denial *after* a confirmed Russian incursion (A/B).
- **One documented coordinated amplification — Romania, May–June 2026.** EDMO/BROD found the Galați strike was sustained over **seven consecutive days**: 28–105 accounts and 3,400–6,800 posts per day across 116,014 posts analysed. Constanța, by contrast, "emerged organically during a temporary information vacuum." This is the strongest positive evidence in the corpus — and it amplified a **false-flag claim about a real strike**, not a sighting wave (B).
- **One drone-tied state information-operation accusation — Latvia, 27 March 2026**, alleging "a massive information operation against the Baltic states" (**C, Medium**).
- **Amplification of an unproven theory, by a think tank and the press.** IISS's July 2026 report claimed an 18-month Russian campaign using **shadow-fleet ships as launch platforms**; Dronewatch found no physical evidence linking any drone to any ship, and Dutch ministers attributed Volkel and Eindhoven to **small hobby drones** incapable of reaching the North Sea from shore. IISS argued the absence of confirmation *fits* a successful deception campaign — which Dronewatch called effectively **unfalsifiable** (B/C). The FPRI essay (2 September 2025) using "**hide in the noise**" concerns **China, not Russia**, and is offered as a hypothesis "worthy of further study"; the phrase's documented coinage means close to the opposite — Moellering (Modern War Institute, 2022) describes **US special-operations forces hiding from adversary AI surveillance**.

**Conclusion.** For the wave that matters most, the amplification was **organic, political, media and institutional — and the most consequential amplifier was the responding state itself**.

## 9. Two direct answers

**(a) Was there any case where the physical event was fabricated entirely and the response was produced by the target society?** **Not by an adversary — no.** No case was found in which a state or influence operation fabricated a drone event that a target society then responded to. But **"no verified physical event at all" is common**:

| Case | Physical finding | Response produced |
|---|---|---|
| Gatwick 2018 | No drone evidenced; NPAS logged no sightings | 33-hour closure, ~1,000 flights, £790k inquiry, £200k settlement |
| Colorado–Nebraska 2019–20 | State: majority planets, stars, aircraft, hobby drones; VICE: "not real" | 70+ agency meeting, state air search, local ordinances |
| Madrid-Barajas, Nov 2024 | Guardia Civil: the drone "never existed" | ~1 hour closure, ~21 flights diverted |
| Copenhagen, 22 Sep 2025 | Cannot confirm or rule out; school aircraft + lens flare; police helicopter generated a new sighting | 4-hour closure, national alert, 500+ reports/day, nine-month inquiry |

In each, the event was **constituted by the detection and response architecture**, not by an airframe. Ambiguity alone, with no fabricated object, can produce real closures, real expenditure and real law.

**(b) How often does an ambiguous incident lead to durable belief versus burning out quickly?** **Burnout is the norm; durability is bought institutionally, not informationally.**

| Episode | Public belief | Institutional residue |
|---|---|---|
| Gatwick 2018 | Days–weeks | Permanent counter-drone systems; review withheld |
| Colorado 2019–20 | ~6 weeks | State report; local ordinances proposed |
| New Jersey 2024 | ~6 weeks (search 100→2); 78% still believed government was withholding information | FAA restrictions; counter-UAS extension then lapse; **state UAP centre, $2.5m/yr** |
| Copenhagen 2025 | Official narrative **9 months** before retraction | NOST readiness; Nordic/Baltic air policing |
| Belgium 2025 | Reports fell once detection improved | **€50m** programme; ministerial accountability dispute |
| UK 2024–26 | No attribution, no resolution | Counts **126→266→340**; shoot-down powers; £750m |
| El Paso Feb 2026 | <8 hours | 10-day closure order; **three real interdictions by Aug 2026** |

Three regularities. **First**, rumour intensity is a product of importance and ambiguity; ambiguity resolves cheaply, so public belief decays in weeks unless an institution keeps supplying it. **Second**, the residue is **institutional**: closures, powers, procurement, restricted airspace, research centres and *counts*; the UK is the pure case — a number that nearly tripled while measuring the observer, driving statute. **Third**, durable *belief* requires a named mechanism, a victim class and an institutional owner of a harm question. Drone scares almost never produce a victim class, so the FBI's threat question is answered "no threat", the task closes, and the belief dies. Where drones *did* acquire a named perpetrator and a physical fact (Leipzig, Øresund, Poland), the response persisted — because the event was real.

## 10. Synthesis: the causal chain across cases

| Case | Ambiguity | First reporter | Principal amplifier (type) | Cost / consequence | Attribution | Grade / Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gatwick 2018 | Two "drones"; none evidenced | Airport security officer | Chief Constable's certainty; national political response (political/media/institutional) | 33h closure, ~1,000 flights, £790k, £200k settlement | None; contested | A/B · High |
| Colorado 2019–20 | Grid formations, up to 19 objects | Sheriffs / residents | Sheriffs, local media, state (political/media) | 70+ agency meeting; state air search | None; majority misidentified | B/C · High |
| NJ/Northeast 2024 | 11 Picatinny sightings; genuine incursion pattern unexplained | Picatinny contractor (13 Nov) | OSINT accounts (19 Nov) → 21→185 mayors → national media (organic/political/media) | 6,000 tips, laser strikes, UAP centre | None; hostile claims refuted | A/B · High |
| Copenhagen 2025 | Two objects; school aircraft + lens flare | Airport employee | Police "capable actor", PM, NOST (**institutional**) | 4h closure, 500 reports/day, 43 rounds at Borris | None; case closed | A/B · High |
| Germany 2024–26 | Mostly unresolved sightings | Airport staff / pilots | Ministerial framing **before** Leipzig; Dobrindt after (political/media) | €160m; 8 airports; 300 specialists | Leipzig only | A/B · Med-High |
| UK bases 2024–26 | 126→266→340 counts, no attribution | Base personnel; a civilian following rumours | **The MoD count itself**, ex-ministers, press (**institutional**) | Shoot-down powers, £750m, 44+ restricted zones | None of 340 | A/B · Med-High |
| Langley 2023 | UAS incursions from 6 Dec | Base sensors, then flight-trackers | OSINT tracking of the **response** (organic/media) | F-16 patrols, NASA WB-57F, 90-day assessment | None | B · Medium |
| El Paso Feb 2026 | Test shot at a party balloon | Pentagon test / FAA | **Transportation Secretary and White House** (**institutional**) | 10-day closure order; later real interdictions | Contested; balloon | A/B · High |

**The pattern.** In eight cases the first reporter was a **credentialled individual with an institutional role**, not a sensor. The pivotal amplifier was almost never a bot or foreign account — it was a police chief, a mayor, a prime minister, a transport secretary, a think tank, or the mere existence of an official counter-drone programme. Physical ambiguity supplied ignition; institutions supplied oxygen; the institutional response became the evidence sustaining the next round of reports. The adversarial-amplification pathway is on this evidence **the least attested link in the chain**; the institutional-amplification pathway the most.

## 11. Evidence quality and what would change the assessment

**A:** the joint DHS/FBI/FAA/DoD statement; FAA restrictions; Danish police, Armed Forces and PST statements and the 25 June 2026 closure; GOV.UK 266/126; USNORTHCOM's 28 August 2026 interdiction; NJ UAP legislation; the Sussex Police/court record; the Leipzig attribution; the EEAS FIMI report; Bundestag Drucksachen 21/3236 and 21/7725. **B:** Trouw + Dronewatch; the Dronewatch IISS rebuttal; Montclair State; the TWZ FOIA corpora. **C:** *Telemadrid*, *Frihedsbrevet*/*DR*, Metro, *The Independent*/DroneXL. **D/E — treat as unverified:** the *New York Post* contractor claim; *The i Paper*'s GRU "fruit pickers"; the "clear enough with our reservations" quotation; the Bundestag "technische Fehlfunktionen" attribution; the shadow-fleet launch-platform thesis.

**What would change the assessment.** (1) A released national attribution naming an actor for any of the 340 UK, 747 German (2026) or 558 Belgian reports. (2) Primary evidence that bots or state accounts amplified the September–October 2025 western wave — none exists, and the EEAS report pointedly excludes Denmark and Norway. (3) A single provable case of a deliberately fabricated drone event — the precondition for the strongest version of the parent hypothesis, and the one thing this corpus does not contain.
