# Case evidence: ambiguous incidents producing large, durable disruption without a clear physical cause

### Strand 06b — generalisability test for "agentic amplification of ambiguous physical threats"

**Research cut-off: 25 September 2026.** Eight cases, all *outside* the drone domain. Each is reconstructed
in the parent study's chain — **ambiguity → initial reports → amplification → attention → vigilance → more
reports → institutional response → validation** — with the amplifier classified as **organic, media,
political, adversarial, commercial or mixed**.

**Grades**: **A** primary/official/peer-reviewed primary data; **B** independent research with transparent
method; **C** reputable journalism; **D** single-source/trade/encyclopaedia; **E** advocacy/anonymous.
Confidence is stated per claim where it is not High. URLs marked `[403]` are live but block automated
retrieval; the claim was read from the saved copy in the evidence trail.

## 1. Havana syndrome / anomalous health incidents (2016–2026): the strongest case

**Ambiguity (late 2016).** U.S. and Canadian embassy staff in Havana reported sudden headache, tinnitus,
vertigo, cognitive difficulty and a *perceived* localised sound. No lesion, no sensor reading, no identified
device, no diagnosed disease. The symptoms were real; the cause was unidentified.

**Initial reports → first amplification (2017–18).** The State Department's August 2017 characterisation of
the events as "attacks" was the pivotal step — an *institutional* act, not a media one — converting an open
medical question into a security incident. It triggered the expulsion of two Cuban diplomats, a February
2018 accountability review board, and an indefinite Havana embassy drawdown never reversed; the CIA
recalled officers, Canada halved its Havana presence, and the U.S. extended a health alert to all of
mainland China in June 2018.

**Media and the "sonic weapon" hypothesis (2018–21).** The University of Pennsylvania JAMA study (March
2018) described "a new syndrome … resembles persistent concussion" and diplomats who "appear to have
sustained injury to widespread brain networks" (A for the paper; the finding later failed replication). Its
lead author told the *New York Times* in September 2018 that microwaves were "considered a main suspect".
*The New Yorker*, *60 Minutes*, *Politico*, the *Times* and the *Guardian* sustained the story. The decisive
act was **naming**: "sonic attack", later "directed energy weapon", supplied a causal agent the evidence
never did. A 2021 *60 Minutes* segment was followed within a week by a Senate Intelligence Committee letter
and a DNI public statement.

**Vigilance and the reporting cascade.** Case counts grew with attention — 26 (2017) → 40 (2019) → 130 (May
2021) → 200+ (Sept 2021) → **1,000+ (early 2022)** — and spread to Guangzhou, Berlin, Vienna, Geneva,
Bogotá, Serbia, London and Washington, including at least two incidents on the White House grounds and one
at CIA headquarters (C). Symptom sets that had no name acquired one, and thereafter fitted it.

**Institutional response.** Nine-plus official investigations: State (2018), Penn (2018), FBI Behavioural
Analysis Unit (2018), JASON (2018, 2022), CDC (2019), DoD (2020), CIA (2020), NASEM (2020), Cuban Academy
of Sciences (2021), seven ODNI agencies (2023, updated 2025), NIH (2024), GAO (2024). A State Health
Incident Response Task Force was created in 2021 under two ambassador-rank leads; the CIA created a task
force in 2020 under the officer who had led the bin Laden manhunt; DoD created a task force and later a
**Cross-Functional Team**, moved in 2026 to Research & Engineering and renamed the **Directed Energy
Bio-Effects CFT** (A, [DefenseScoop, 24 Feb 2026](https://defensescoop.com/2026/02/24/havana-syndrome-defense-department-anomalous-health-incidents-ahi-cft/)).

**Validation — and the inversion.** Assessments moved *against* the weapon hypothesis while the
institutional structure kept growing:

| Assessment | Finding |
|---|---|
| **JASON 2018** (A) | Sounds "most likely" insects (high confidence: Indies short-tailed cricket); microwave/ultrasound "highly unlikely"; psychogenic effects "may serve to explain important components" |
| **CIA interim, Jan 2022** (A) | Most of ~1,000 cases explicable by natural causes, undiagnosed conditions or stress; ~24 not ruled out |
| **ODNI, 1 Mar 2023** (A) | Five of seven agencies: adversary involvement "very unlikely"; one "unlikely"; one abstained. **Two dissented at low confidence because they judged RF energy plausible.** Site findings included malfunctioning HVAC systems |
| **NIH, Mar 2024** (A, [link](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-studies-find-severe-symptoms-havana-syndrome-no-evidence-mri-detectable-brain-injury-or-biological-abnormalities) `[403]`) | Two studies (81 and 86 participants): no MRI-detectable brain injury, no significant differences in most clinical/biomarker measures; **the Penn brain-injury findings were not replicated** |
| **ODNI update, Jan 2025** (A) | 2023 judgements held; two of seven shifted slightly on whether a foreign actor has a biological-effect capability |
| **GAO, Jul 2024** (A, [GAO-24-106593](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-106593) `[403]`) | 334 people qualified for care; the finding was *administrative* (poor communication, monitoring), not causal |

**Costs.** HAVANA Act (PL 117-46, 8 Oct 2021): up to one year's full salary, untaxed — **$187,300** for
senior employees — on evidence of neurological injury; passed unanimously (A, [Federal Register rule, 3 Oct
2024](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/10/03/2024-22795/implementation-of-havana-act-of-2021)).
DoD announced the **first payments, totalling nearly $3 million**, in July 2026 (A/B,
[DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/07/13/havana-syndrome-payments-directed-energy-bio-effects/)).
State settled a whistleblower suit for **$460,000**; Canadian diplomats sued for **C$28m** (C). **The scale
inversion is itself a finding**: the *cheapest* component (compensation) totalled ~$3m, while the *most
durable* components — task forces, a statute, a permanent medical pathway, a five-year diplomatic drawdown —
cost far more and persist indefinitely.

**Was there deliberate adversary action?** The IC says no; several journalists and politicians say yes; ten
years have not converged the dispute. *Against*: the ODNI judgements, JASON, NIH, and the **Cuban Academy
of Sciences** report (Sept 2021, 21 scientists) finding prior U.S. analyses biased, cherry-picked and
suppressing psychogenic explanations (A for the document). *For*: a March 2024 *Insider* / *60 Minutes* /
*Der Spiegel* investigation reporting that individuals linked to **GRU Unit 29155** were awarded and
promoted for "non-lethal acoustic weapon" work, and that travel and telephone records matched a few AHI
locations in China and Georgia (B/C — multiple outlets, one category of sourcing, no released records). In
January 2026 CBS/CNN reported that Homeland Security Investigations bought a backpack-sized pulsed-RF
device with Russian-origin components for **$15m** and had tested it on rats and sheep (E — anonymous, no
device shown, no published data); the *Washington Post* reported a **sceptical** Norwegian scientist who
built such a device and tested it on himself in 2024 (C, anonymous). In **June 2026 the outgoing DNI
rescinded the 2023 ICA and its 2025 update** as "ethically flawed" (B/C) — reopening the question on
political rather than scientific grounds.

**How ambiguity itself drove the response.** (i) **No available test**: nothing physical was ever
identified, so no evidence could close the case, and every negative finding could be read as an adversary
covering its tracks. (ii) **Symptom–label feedback**: once a named syndrome existed, common symptoms became
reportable events, so reports rose with publicity — the ODNI attributed part of the pattern to "dynamics
not related to a foreign actor". (iii) **Political irreversibility**: with diplomats, soldiers and spies as
the alleged victims, under-reacting cost more than over-reacting. The 2026 rebranding *towards* directed
energy as the consensus moved *away* from it shows the institutional commitment had detached from the
evidence.

## 2. The 2023 Chinese balloon and the three subsequent shootdowns

A high-altitude balloon crossed Alaska, Canada and the U.S. between 28 January and 4 February 2023. The
ambiguity was not "is there an object?" but "what is it doing, on whose orders, what does it carry?"

**Amplification: political first, media second.** Its track over **Malmstrom AFB** and Minuteman III fields
turned an airspace curiosity into a surveillance story; Republican criticism of the delay ("why not over
Montana?") created an **incentive to escalate** unrelated to the object. The administration, having
declined to shoot over land for safety, downed it on 4 February over the Atlantic with an AIM-9X from an
F-22. The cascade followed within eight days:

| Date | Object | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Feb | Chinese balloon, South Carolina coast | Shot down; debris recovered to FBI Lab |
| 10 Feb | Object over Deadhorse, Alaska | Shot down |
| 11 Feb | "Small, cylindrical object", Yukon | Shot down on Trudeau's order |
| 12 Feb | Object over Lake Huron, Michigan | Shot down |

On **14 February** — ten days after the first shootdown — the U.S. announced the latter three were
**probably private entities with no relation to China**. November 2024 documents showed the Lake Huron
object was "from a company who sells weather monitoring equipment"; the leading candidate for one was the
**Northern Illinois Balloon Experiment** ("Bottlecap Balloon Brigade"), an amateur radio group whose $12
pico-balloon went missing. The four AIM-9X missiles cost **at least $1.5m**; search was abandoned on 17
February (A/B via WSJ figure, [2023 balloon incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Chinese_balloon_incident)).

**The loop breaks.** In February 2024 NORAD intercepted another hobbyist balloon over the western U.S. and
**did not shoot it down**, tracking it until it left airspace; NORAD subsequently established liaison with
balloon hobbyist groups. This is the best-documented case in the set of an institution *learning* and
breaking the amplification loop at the response stage. **Recoverability is a collapse condition**: where
debris can be examined, ambiguity dies in days; where it cannot, it does not.

## 3. UAP, 2023–2026: sustained ambiguity by institutional design

**Scale.** AARO's caseload: 510 reports (Aug 2022) → 1,600+ (late 2024) → "over **2,000** cases" (Feb 2026)
(A, [DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/02/25/hegseth-ufo-disclosure-trump-aaro-uap-caseload/)).

**The hinge.** The House Oversight hearing of **26 July 2023** — Ryan Graves, Cmdr. David Fravor (ret.) and
David Grusch — is where attention, not evidence, was produced. Grusch testified under oath to a
multi-decade retrieval and reverse-engineering programme and recovered "non-human" biologics, based on
interviews rather than direct observation, and produced no documents (A for the hearing; E for the
substantive claims).

**Institutional response.** AARO was established July 2022 and is statutorily mandated by 50 U.S.C.
§3373/§3373a to report quarterly and annually. Its Historical Record Report Vol. I (March 2024) found "no
empirical evidence" of extraterrestrial technology and documented a rejected DHS proposal ("Kona Blue") to
reverse-engineer recovered craft. A House Task Force on Declassification held a further hearing in
September 2025; the 2026 cycle added a Trump executive order, "PURSUE" file releases from May 2026, an
aliens.gov registration, a science advisory council and a Pentagon legal-relief route for UAP
whistleblowers.

**Validation.** AARO's delayed FY2025 report (July 2026): **319 new cases**, **1,870 total**, **114
resolved and 191 archived as insufficient data**; no resolution pointing to advanced foreign technology; no
verified adverse health effects; **no evidence of recovered or exploited non-human materials**; most
space-domain cases attributed with high confidence to **satellite flaring**. It records cases near sensitive
installations where performance "exceed[s] the known state-of-the-art" while conceding "no technical data
accompanied these reports", and a **178% increase** in small-UAS reports near nuclear infrastructure —
which AARO states were *not* genuine UAP (A for the report; B/C, [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/aaros-latest-uap-report-finds-no-evidence-of-exotic-technology-but-mysteries-remain/)).

**Why it persists.** (1) A **statutory reporting channel** — the caseload grows because intake grows. (2) A
**permanent unfalsifiable residual**: resolving 114 of 319 while archiving 191 guarantees a standing
minority of unexplained cases. (3) A **constituency with an interest in non-closure**: advocacy
organisations, sympathetic legislators, a documentary industry and, since 2025, a whistleblower-protection
apparatus. AARO's director stated in 2026 that there "should be no stigma associated with reporting UAP" —
a deliberate effort to raise reporting rates.

## 4. Public-health scares: ambiguity that outran evidence, and ambiguity that did not

**H5N1 in US dairy cattle (2024–26) — alarm without an event.** Late March 2024: USDA/APHIS confirmed H5N1
clade 2.3.4.4b in Texas/Kansas dairy herds, the first known H5N1 in cattle, with very high viral loads in
milk. CDC confirmed the first human case on 1 April 2024 (mild conjunctivitis) and assessed general-public
risk **low**. On 25 April the FDA reported ~**1 in 5** retail milk samples PCR-positive for HPAI fragments —
a milk-supply scare. Amplification was **media plus political, not adversarial**: "next pandemic" framing;
fights over USDA compensation and mandatory testing; the 20–22 January 2025 federal health communications
pause; HHS cancelling mRNA pandemic-flu contracts in May 2025; raw-milk brands marketing against official
warnings (commercial). **No significant state-actor disinformation campaign on H5N1 was documented — a
valuable negative finding.** The milk scare was refuted within a month: of 297 retail samples, 60 (20.2%)
were PCR-positive but **0 of 60 contained viable virus**, and pasteurisation inactivated ≥12 log₁₀. Human
cases rose from 1 to 70 by February 2025, including the **first US H5N1 death on 6 January 2025**; the
**71st** (H5N5, Washington State) died 21 November 2025 with no onward transmission. By 6 March 2026 CDC
still reported **71 cases** and low risk, having ceased H5N1 technical reports after 5 June 2024 and folded
updates into routine influenza reporting on 7 July 2025 (A). Legacy: USDA's National Milk Testing Strategy
(Dec 2024) and a **$1bn** HPAI strategy; FDA added **$8m** and tested 464 pasteurised products, all
negative; CDC monitored **33,200 people** and tested **1,340** (A). BARDA contracts to CSL Seqirus (~$121m)
and Moderna ($176m, then ~$590m) were followed by HHS cancelling **$766m** of pandemic-flu mRNA contracts in
May 2025 (B/C). **Net: durable agricultural and surveillance machinery; no durable public-health
emergency.** Disruption decayed because severity and human transmissibility never moved.

**Mpox clade Ib (2024).** WHO declared a PHEIC on **14 August 2024** for the clade Ib upsurge centred on the
DRC — the second mpox PHEIC in two years (A). A severe but geographically bounded outbreak acquired global
salience because a pre-existing institutional category (PHEIC) and a pre-existing stigmatised label (mpox
2022) were available. Reporting framed it as a vaccine-equity test; critics as profiteering (E). The
emergency was renewed through 2025 rather than resolved — a slow burn, not a spike. Durable legacy: African
CDC capacity funding and a standing response, modest and far below COVID-era spending.

**"Disease X" — ambiguity with no event at all.** WHO's R&D Blueprint placeholder and its Davos 2024 airing
converted a planning heuristic into a durable institutional agenda *and* a durable conspiracy ("WHO power
grab"). The limiting case: **no physical event, no reports, no evidence — yet an established programme and
an established counter-narrative.** Under the right institutional conditions ambiguity need not be
*generated* to be *exploited*; it need only be *named*.

**The DRC's 2024–25 "Disease X", by contrast — collapse.** In December 2024 an unidentified febrile illness
in the Panzi health zone, Kwango province, was reported with death tolls between **67 and 143**. WHO
deployed experts on 6 December; **samples tested positive for malaria within days**, and a 2026 *Nature
Medicine* analysis attributed the outbreak to **known pathogens — principally malaria and respiratory
infection — amplified by acute malnutrition and low vaccination coverage**. No new agency, no new statute;
the only legacy was reinforced surveillance (A). **This is the collapse case by which the persistence
cases should be measured.**

**COVID-19 origins — an unresolvable binary.** The FBI and DOE assessed lab origin at moderate/low
confidence in 2023; the CIA shifted to favouring lab origin with **"low confidence"** in January 2025 (A
for the fact of the assessment; the underlying evidence is not public). Because both hypotheses stay
formally live, neither side can close the issue and each institutional statement re-ignites it.
Amplification is political and media, with adversarial messaging from both Russian and Chinese state
channels in opposite directions. Persistence driver: **a genuinely unresolvable evidentiary question with
high political stakes** — structurally identical to Havana.

**5G and health — a scare that largely burned out.** 5G/COVID claims drove **documented arson and
vandalism against telecom masts**: Mobile UK counted 50 incidents by 15 April 2020 and **77 by 6 May**
(including masts not carrying 5G); BT's CEO reported ~40 abuse incidents including a stabbing; ~120 abuse
cases including murder threats were logged by 23 April; South Africa saw KwaZulu-Natal towers torched in
January 2021 (A for the incident analysis — Langguth et al. 2022 — B/C for the counts). Amplification was
**mixed but overwhelmingly organic**, with celebrity, commercial ("5G Bioshield" USB sticks at ~US$350),
far-right and weak adversarial vectors; **no coordinated Kremlin promotion was established** (A for the
negative finding). *The exact state of the RF evidence, stated carefully*: ICNIRP's 2020 guidelines (100
kHz–300 GHz) derive limits from thermal thresholds and find no established adverse effects below them; the
WHO-commissioned 2024 systematic review (Karipidis et al.) found moderate-certainty evidence that
mobile-phone use **likely does not** increase glioma, meningioma, acoustic neuroma, pituitary,
salivary-gland or paediatric brain tumours; **IARC's 2011 Group 2B ("possibly carcinogenic") classification
is unchanged**, resting on limited human evidence with acknowledged recall and selection bias. The honest
formulation is **"no established effect", not "no effect"**, and electromagnetic hypersensitivity is not a
recognised diagnosis (WHO). Collapse factors: the conspiracy was parasitic on the pandemic, and platforms
acted — YouTube banned 5G–COVID content on 5 April 2020, Twitter banned incitement to attack 5G
infrastructure on 22 April 2020 — though content removal proved largely ineffective while demonetisation
and fraud enforcement worked. Two features separate this from Havana: **no named victim class with
institutional standing** (mast arson was criminal, not a health grievance), and the residual persisted only
where it merged into local identity — **west Belfast saw 17 arson attacks on 5G towers between June 2023
and July 2025**, with prosecutions continuing into April 2026. **The national scare burned out; the local
violence did not.** Same physical medium as the directed-energy hypothesis; radically different durability.

## 5. Product, food and environmental scares

**Tylenol 1982 — a small event with permanent output.** Between 29 September and early October 1982 seven
people died in the Chicago area from cyanide-laced Extra-Strength Tylenol capsules; four of 44 capsules in
one bottle carried roughly three times a fatal cyanide dose, and the tainted capsules had been made in **two
different plants (Pennsylvania and Texas)** — proving post-shelf tampering. The *fact* was certain; the
actor and insertion mechanism never were. The recall went nationwide on 5 October: ~**31 million bottles**.
No one was ever charged with the murders. The legacy is permanent: tamper-resistant packaging across
pharmaceuticals, food and consumer goods; the **Federal Anti-Tampering Act of 1983 (18 U.S.C. §1365)**; the
FDA's tamper-evident packaging rule, **63 FR 59463** (4 November 1998); and the disappearance of the
powder-filled capsule (A for the statute and rule; B for event detail). **A tiny physical event produced
permanent regulatory change across an entire product class without resolving attribution — because the fix
was a *design* change, not a causal finding, so ambiguity never had to be resolved.**

**Strawberry needles (Australia, 2018) — real event, failed attribution, hoax-dominated cascade.** Sewing
needles were found in Queensland strawberries in September 2018, then in Victoria, NSW, the ACT, South
Australia and in exported fruit; copycat reports followed within days involving **apples, bananas and
grapes**, reaching six states. Growers installed metal detectors, punnets were recalled and destroyed, and
the federal government faced lobbying for a **A$6m** grower bailout in an industry worth roughly **A$500m**.
A former Queensland farm supervisor was charged with seven counts of contamination in November 2018 — and
prosecutors **dropped all charges in July 2021**; nobody has been convicted (A/B). The physical event was
real but trivial; the disruption was national. **The scale was generated by the reporting cascade** — the
purest organic "vigilance → more reports" loop in the set, requiring no adversary beyond the initial actor
and none at all for the copycats. Note what did *not* follow: no new agency, no new statute. The legacy was
operational (metal detection, tamper-evident packaging, tighter food-fraud reporting).

**HiPP baby food (Austria/Czechia/Slovakia, 2026) — the counter-model.** In April 2026 a jar of HiPP
"Karotten mit Kartoffeln" bought in Burgenland tested positive for rat poison; HiPP had already withdrawn
its entire baby-jar range from Spar Austria as a precaution, and further jars appeared in Czechia and
Slovakia (five recovered by May, a sixth sought). Austrian authorities confirmed an extortion attempt; a
**39-year-old former HiPP employee** was arrested in early May 2026 on charges of intentional common
endangerment, attempted grievous bodily harm and attempted extortion, with pre-trial detention extended in
September 2026 (A/B). This is what ambiguity's *absence* looks like: confirmed tampering, a named suspect, a
tightly bounded recall, manufacturer-published tamper indicators, and no durable institutional expansion.
Compare the 2025–2026 "metal/glass in food" recall waves — largely **verified** plant contamination,
high event volume, **near-zero political durability**. (The frequently repeated "2024 syringes in baby
food" story could not be traced to any primary report despite repeated searching; it appears to be a
conflation and should not be cited.)

**"Wind turbine syndrome" — a durable claim with no established mechanism.** Nina Pierpont's 2009
self-published book described a symptom cluster (sleep disturbance, headache, tinnitus, dizziness, nausea)
attributed to infrasound and "vibroacoustic disease", the latter imported into anti-wind literature from a
small, unreplicated Portuguese research programme. The evidence is unusually settled for a contested claim:
**25-plus reviews since 2003** found no consistent causal evidence. Chapman & St George (2013) traced the
VAD "factoid" to a single unreplicated study recycled through the internet; Chapman et al. (2013, PLoS ONE)
showed the pattern of Australian complaints **did not match the establishment or distribution of turbines**;
and Crichton et al. (2014) demonstrated experimentally that **expectation**, not exposure, drove reported
symptoms. Systematic reviews by Schmidt & Klokker (2014), Onakpoya et al. (2015) and Liebich et al. (2021)
were negative (A). Amplification was **advocacy-led and partly astroturfed**: the **Waubra Foundation**, an
anti-wind "health promotion" charity linked to fossil-fuel funding, was a principal vector and **lost its
health-promotion charity status**; talk radio, local planning fights and later political figures carried it.
There is no discrete incident at all — only diffuse attribution of ordinary symptoms to a visible new
technology. **The payoff is procedural: a health complaint is a planning objection, and a planning
objection is a delay.** The durable outputs are real — **turbine setback distances and moratoria** (including
Victoria's 2 km residential setback and similar regimes in Denmark, Germany and the UK) and litigation —
while the syndrome appears in **no international disease classification**. Persistence drivers: political
salience, economic interests (property values, land use), and a self-reported, non-specific, **unfalsifiable**
symptom cluster. A renewed push in April 2026, partly routed through the Australian Senate, was described in
trade press as a "scientific dead end".

**"Havana-like" incidents elsewhere.** Suspected cases were reported in Guangzhou, Berlin (2021), Vienna,
Geneva, Bogotá, Serbia, London and the Washington area — including, per 2026 testimony, at least two on the
White House grounds and one at CIA headquarters. All were absorbed into the single Havana-syndrome category
rather than generating independent episodes. **A single named category with an institutional owner
aggregated dozens of otherwise-isolated ambiguous events** — a durability mechanism in itself.

## 6. Deliberate manufacture or exploitation of scares

**Operation "Denver" (popularly INFEKTION), KGB and Stasi, 1983–90s.** *Ambiguity*: from 1983 a terrifying
new syndrome had no known origin. *Injection*: on 17 July 1983 the pro-Soviet Indian daily *Patriot* printed
a front-page anonymous letter, "AIDS May Invade India", attributed to a "well-known American scientist",
asserting the disease was a Pentagon bioweapon made at Fort Detrick. *Adversarial amplification*: on 7
September 1985 the KGB cabled Bulgarian State Security that it was running "a series of [active] measures"
to establish abroad that AIDS resulted from "secret experiments with a new type of biological weapon by the
secret services of the USA and the Pentagon"; on 30 October 1985 *Literaturnaya Gazeta* ran Zapevalov's
"Panic in the West", recycling the *Patriot* letter while hiding its anonymity and date. The Stasi's HVA/X
registered the campaign on 17 July 1986 as Operation **"Denver"**. Boghardt's official CIA history records
coverage in **80 countries, ~200 periodicals and 25 languages** (A,
[Boghardt 2009](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/1-Operation_INFEKTION_Soviet_Bloc_AIDS_Disinformation_Campaign.pdf);
[Selvage & Nehring 2019](https://www.wilsoncenter.org/blog-post/operation-denver-kgb-and-stasi-disinformation-regarding-aids)).

*Measured impact.* This is the strand's only case with quantitative L4 evidence of harm: Chigwedere et al.
estimated ~**330,000 excess deaths in South Africa** from delayed antiretroviral rollout, and Bogart &
Thorburn found ~**50% of African-American respondents** endorsed man-made-AIDS beliefs (A). The template
was recycled: Ebola substituted for HIV in 2014, the 2018 "US biolab in Georgia" story, the 2020–22 "US
bioweapons labs in Ukraine" narrative.

*Contested, and stated as such.* **"Denver" is the archival codename; "INFEKTION" rests on an ex-HVA
officer's recollection and appears nowhere in the files.** Whether the *Patriot* letter was KGB-authored is
**unproven** — the State Department's 1987 report notes its "existence has not been confirmed". Segal's
status (dupe, willing propagandist, or Stasi "contact person") is unresolved; ~90% of HVA files were
destroyed in 1989–90. The 1992 "Primakov admission" is reported second-hand. A Soviet *Agent Orange*
fabrication is **unverified**. **Analytically the case is a partial failure**: it did not change Western
policy, was attributed within four years, and Western and Soviet virologists (Montagnier, Zhdanov, the USSR
Academy of Medical Sciences) dismissed it almost immediately — **the constraint was a credible, independent
scientific validator with access to the same audiences**. The mechanism that made it work is the one this
strand cares about: **the adversary supplied a causal story for an unresolved medical mystery and let target
populations do the rest.** The mechanism that limited it was **attribution plus a trusted validator**. In
Havana the causal story came largely from *domestic* institutions and journalists — which is why it proved
far more durable.

**"Yellow rain" (1975–87, contested).** From 1981 the U.S. alleged that Soviet-backed forces in Laos,
Kampuchea and Afghanistan used trichothecene mycotoxins. The evidence was refugee reports and samples; the
Meselson/Nowicke critique (1983–84, *Nature*) argued the samples were largely **bee faeces and pollen** and
the symptoms inconsistent with trichothecene poisoning; ABC's 1984 documentary amplified the official claim.
The scientific rejection was decisive in the literature — the collapse mechanism was **peer-reviewed
replication** (pollen analysis reproducible from the samples), not institutional authority — but the
political claim persisted for years and neither the claim nor the refutation is universally accepted. A
related Iraqi case is a rare instance of ambiguity resolved *against* the amplifying party: the pre-2003
"yellow rain" chemical-weapon claim failed because Iraq had purified only ~20 ml of trichothecene and never
weaponised it (A/B). **The underlying yellow-rain question is treated here as open**; what is not contested
is the amplification structure (underlying question **open**).

**The 2001 anthrax letters.** *The real attack*: four letters with Trenton postmarks of 18 September and 9
October 2001 to NBC News, the *New York Post* and Senators Daschle and Leahy; American Media Inc.
contaminated. CDC/DOJ figures: **22 cases (11 inhalational, 11 cutaneous) and 5 deaths**; Brentwood and
Hamilton postal facilities closed; the FBI named Bruce Ivins in 2010, a conclusion the 2011 National
Research Council review criticised as over-claiming forensic certainty (A,
[Amerithrax summary](https://www.justice.gov/archive/amerithrax/docs/amx-investigative-summary.pdf);
[NRC 2011](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/catalog/13098/)). *The hoax-amplified response*: white-powder
and anthrax-threat incidents ran into the thousands globally, with **published totals inconsistent (roughly
2,500 to 30,000 depending on jurisdiction, definition and window) — no authoritative count could be
verified in this session, and the analytic point does not depend on it.** Hoaxes outnumbered real letters by
orders of magnitude, and each consumed hazmat response, building closure, prophylactic-antibiotic decisions
and laboratory capacity. *Durable legacy*: the Public Health Security and Bioterrorism Preparedness and
Response Act (PL 107-188, **12 June 2002**), BioWatch from **2003**, Project BioShield (**2004**) and the
USPS irradiation programme. **This is a canonical case of the response being calibrated to the ambiguity
rather than the event** — and the closest historical analogue to the hypothesised mechanism. It is also a
**weak test of the agentic addition**, because in 2001 the marginal cost of a credible-sounding false report
was already near zero.

**Radiological and chemical scares.** *Litvinenko (2006)* is the inverse case: a real polonium-210 poisoning
that killed one man and contaminated ~40 London sites, validated by the Owen inquiry (21 January 2016) and
*Carter v. Russia* (ECtHR, 2021) — a **real event that validated an institutional narrative**; no documented
mass "polonium" hoax wave followed (A). *Padilla (2002)* is the scare case: arrested at O'Hare on 8 May
2002, publicly framed around an alleged radiological dispersal device in June 2002, **never charged with a
dirty-bomb plot**, convicted in 2007 on unrelated conspiracy charges and sentenced to 17 years — yet the
case fed a durable RDD-vigilance architecture (radiation portal monitors, the May 2003 TOPOFF 2 exercise)
whose central premise the case never evidenced (A for the court record). *Hoax "radioactive package"
incidents* are, by frequency, the **modal** radiological event for first responders, with the dominant cost
being evacuation, hazmat deployment and laboratory time rather than exposure — a defensible total could not
be aggregated here (**flagged gap**). *The October 2022 Russian "Ukrainian dirty bomb" claim* is the
cleanest failed adversarial amplification in the set: despite being raised at the UN Security Council by a
permanent member, IAEA inspections of the named facilities found **no undeclared nuclear activity**, and the
claim was read in the West as a Russian pretext signal — **the amplification boomeranged into vigilance
about Russia rather than about Ukraine** (A). *Syria/Ghouta (2013)* shows a fourth mechanism: the UN mission
(S/2013/553, 13 September 2013) confirmed sarin use but had no attribution mandate, the U.S. and Russia
published competing assessments, and the OPCW-UN Joint Investigative Mechanism later attributed Khan
Sheikhoun (2017) and Ltamenah (2018) to Syrian government forces. **Ambiguity there was sustained not by
fabrication but by adversarial contestation of evidence chains** (A).

**2022–2026: exploiting ambiguity without fabricating events.** The Baltic cable incidents, the July 2024 DHL
parcel-incendiary plot traced to Russian intelligence, and Baltic **GPS-jamming** reporting together produced
a generalised "hybrid warfare" narrative in which *unexplained* incidents are increasingly read as hostile
even absent attribution. In each, the operative ambiguity is not the event but **the absence of a benign
explanation**.

## 7. Synthesis: durable response versus rapid burnout

Durability is **not** explained by the size of the physical event, the strength of the evidence, or the
intensity of media attention. Five variables explain it, in rough order of power.

**1. Whether an institutional owner exists for a harm question, or can be created cheaply.** Havana produced
nine investigations, a permanent DoD CFT and HAVANA Act machinery because the alleged victims — diplomats,
soldiers, spies — already had institutional owners with budgets, statutory duties of care and political
exposure. UAP produced AARO and a statutory reporting regime because Congress had already created the
channel. Wind-turbine syndrome produced planning law because local government must decide. **5G produced
almost nothing durable because no institution owned mast safety as a health question**, and EHS has no
institutional sponsor. The HiPP case shows the bound: a confirmed tampering with a named suspect and a
bounded recall produces no institutional expansion at all.

**2. Whether a decisive evidentiary test is available — and run.** Recoverability collapses ambiguity. The
2023 balloon objects were recovered and identified within ten days; H5N1's milk scare collapsed when 0 of
60 PCR-positive samples had viable virus; the DRC "Disease X" collapsed to malaria and malnutrition within
weeks. Havana and UAP cannot close because the objects — a neurological syndrome, degraded sensor imagery —
cannot be recovered or reproduced. **Persistence tracks the unavailability of a decisive test.**

**3. Whether a plausible named mechanism exists that cannot be refuted.** "Sonic weapon" and "directed
energy" gave Havana a causal agent; "insidious infrasound" gave wind-turbine syndrome one; "Disease X" gave
preparedness one. A named mechanism converts diffuse symptoms into a *category*, and a category makes
reports aggregable, countable and politically usable. Where the mechanism cannot be tested (infrasound on
self-reported symptoms; pulsed RF on a subjective syndrome), refutation is effectively impossible —
Havana's 2023 dissent came precisely from agencies that judged RF "plausible".

**4. Whether an interested constituency exists for non-closure.** Havana is the decisive demonstration: five
of seven agencies said adversary involvement was "very unlikely", NIH failed to replicate the brain-injury
findings, and yet in 2026 the DoD team was **rebranded towards directed energy** and the incoming DNI
**rescinded the sceptical assessments**. Advocacy groups, veterans' and diplomats' associations,
congressional subcommittees, a documentary industry and a whistleblower-protection apparatus all benefit
from the question staying open. Wind-turbine opposition had property values and land use. No comparable
constituency existed for 5G mast claims.

**5. Whether ambiguity aggregates into one category or fragments.** Havana absorbed Berlin, Vienna, Geneva,
Bogotá, Guangzhou, Serbia and Washington into a single tracked phenomenon whose case count grew from 26 to
1,000+. The 2023 balloon objects fragmented and were resolved individually, so nothing accumulated.
**Aggregation is an amplification technology**: a shared label turns isolated reports into a trend, and a
trend is what institutions respond to.

### 7.1 The strongest counterexample: New Jersey, late 2024

If the hypothesis were that ambiguity *reliably* produces durable institutional response, this episode
falsifies it. Every condition was present: **sustained ambiguity** over ~two months with spread to New York,
Maryland and Virginia; **more than 5,000 sightings** reported to the FBI; **maximum political attention** (a
President-elect, a national security adviser, governors, senators, the FBI Director, White House briefings);
**real institutional machinery engaged** (DHS, FBI, FAA, DoD, state police, a joint federal statement,
temporary FAA flight restrictions); a **genuine physical substrate** (lawful commercial, hobbyist and
law-enforcement drones plus misidentified aircraft); and a **pre-existing adversary narrative** available for
grafting. And yet the joint DHS/FBI/FAA/DoD statement of **17 December 2024** assessed the sightings as "a
combination of lawful commercial drones, hobbyist drones, and law enforcement drones, as well as manned
fixed-wing aircraft, helicopters, and stars mistakenly reported as drones", adding "we have not identified
anything anomalous" (A,
[FAA joint statement](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/dhs-fbi-faa-dod-joint-statement-ongoing-response-reported-drone-sightings)).
The episode **burned out within about six weeks**; the durable legacy is thin — some tightening of detection
authorities and reporting, no new agency, no compensation regime, no HAVANA Act equivalent.

**Why it burned out.** Three of the five conditions were absent: **no named victim class** (nobody claimed
injury, so no duty-of-care machinery activated); reports were **resolvable in principle and resolved in
practice**; and there was **no institutional owner of a harm question** — the FBI owned a *threat* question,
and once it answered "no threat", its task was complete. Media attention alone could not create permanence.

**Two supporting counterexamples.** (i) *INFEKTION in the West*: the same campaign that transformed belief
in parts of Africa, South Asia and Latin America was dismissed almost immediately by Western and Soviet
virologists — the constraint was the presence of a **credible, independent scientific validator** with
access to the same audiences (A). (ii) *The October 2022 Russian "Ukrainian dirty bomb" claim*: raised at
the UN Security Council by a permanent member, yet IAEA inspections found no undeclared activity and the
claim **boomeranged into Western vigilance about Russia**. **Ambiguity is not a free asset; it can be
turned against the party supplying it.**

**Third counterexample, of a different type: the 2025–2026 acetaminophen–autism controversy.** A causal
claim with weak scientific support acquired the highest possible political sponsorship (a September 2025
White House statement and FDA label action), saturation coverage and litigation. It produced **political and
commercial** effects — label changes, lawsuits, changed consumer behaviour — but **no scientific consensus**
and no durable public-health institution. The reason is diagnostic: the institutions that could have adopted
the claim were the institutions the claim was attacking, so the response was **resistance, not adoption**.
*Ambiguity empowers an institution only when that institution is being asked to protect someone; it does
nothing when the institution is being accused.*

### 7.2 Implications for the AI-amplification hypothesis

1. **The mechanism generalises, but the binding constraint is institutional, not informational.** Every
   durable case persisted because an institution had a reason to keep working. An agent supplying more
   reports supplies raw material; the conversion step — audit, compensation, task force, statute — remains
   human and institutional.
2. **The cheapest route to durability is not more reports but a named mechanism plus a victim class.**
   "Sonic weapon" plus "diplomats with brain injuries" did more work than a thousand uncorroborated
   sightings — and both are *narrative*, not physical, inputs, trivially producible by a text-generating
   agent. This is the narrowest and most credible agentic threat in this strand.
3. **Institutional learning works, and it breaks the loop** — NORAD's hobbyist liaison, the FDA inoculating
   milk samples, the NIH publishing a failed replication, AARO archiving cases. Where a test exists and is
   run, ambiguity collapses in weeks to months; Havana shows what happens when it does not.
4. **Attribution and validators cut against adversarial operations specifically.** INFEKTION was dismissed
   where a trusted validator was present, and attributed within four years; the 2023 balloons were resolved
   and closed; the 2022 dirty-bomb claim backfired. Havana shows that *domestically generated* causal
   narratives are more durable than *adversarially planted* ones because they cannot be dismissed as foreign
   disinformation — exactly what the parent study's "the amplifier is the institution" finding predicts.

### 7.3 Gaps and limits

- The acetaminophen–autism case and the 2025–2026 food-recall wave are covered at a lower evidentiary
  standard; see the delegated raw evidence files.
- **Hoax incident totals are unreliable**: anthrax-letter hoax counts range from ~2,500 to 30,000 across
  jurisdictions, and no defensible aggregate exists for "radioactive package" hoaxes. Both are high-value
  gaps.
- No published quantified **total cost** exists for Havana syndrome comparable to the anthrax cleanup
  figures; the ~$3m HAVANA Act disbursement is the only hard number and almost certainly understates
  institutional cost by an order of magnitude.
- The 2026 Havana developments (the DNI rescission, the reported $15m device purchase) rest substantially on
  anonymous sourcing and should be treated as E-grade until documents or peer-reviewed testing appear.
- Excluded by design: any case inside the drone domain (the parent study's own strand).
