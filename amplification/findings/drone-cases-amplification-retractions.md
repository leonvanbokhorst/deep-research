# European drone-scare cases: amplification chains and retractions

**Compiled 25 September 2026.** **[P]** primary/official · **[J]** journalism · **[T]** think-tank/analytical · **[NV]** not verified this session. Non-English quotes translated.

## A) Copenhagen / Oslo, 22–30 Sept 2025, and the June 2026 retraction

**Trigger (22 Sept).** CPH suspended flights ~4 hours after 2–3 large objects entered controlled airspace; ~20,000 passengers, ~100 movements affected; Oslo Gardermoen closed briefly the same night. The first alarm came from an airport security employee: *"Der er to droner. De er store. Det er ikke legetøj"* ("There are two drones. They are large. It is not a toy"). [J: Frihedsbrevet, 18 Mar 2026]

**"Capable actor" (23 Sept press conference).** **Jens Jespersen**, *ledende politiinspektør* (senior chief police inspector — **not** police chief) at Københavns Politi: *"Det er en aktør, der har nogle kapaciteter, vilje og redskaber til at vise sig frem på den her måde – måske også for at øve sig"* ("It is an actor with the capabilities, the will and the tools to show themselves this way – perhaps also to train"). Basis: number and size; flight pattern; *"tændte og slukkede projektører"* (switching spotlights on and off); arrival from "quite far away", "many kilometres". He said nothing indicated intent to harm, only to "show off and train". [P: DR, 23 Sep 2025]

**Counts.** 24 Sept — Københavns Politi (politiinspektør **Poul Kjeldsen**): "more than 100 reports about drones and drone-like objects" in 24h. [P: politi.dk] 26 Sept — **Rigspolitiet**: "more than 500 reports … across all police districts" in 24h; added *"Politiet vurderer ikke, at der på noget tidspunkt i forløbet har været risiko for civilbefolkningen"* ("Police do not assess that at any point was there a risk to the civilian population") and warned private operators to consider whether it was "a good time to fly at all". [P: politi.dk]

**Escalation and framing.** 25 Sept — **NOST** to *operationsberedskab* (level 3, highest), round-the-clock staffing; national police readiness raised to a level last used after the 2015 Copenhagen terror attacks. NOST's intent assessment was narrow: flyovers *"primært har til hensigt at skabe utryghed og forstyrre vores infrastruktur"* ("primarily intend to create insecurity and disrupt our infrastructure"). PM **Mette Frederiksen**: *"det mest alvorlige angreb på dansk kritisk infrastruktur hidtil"* ("the most serious attack on Danish critical infrastructure to date", 23 Sept) and *"Droner en dag, sabotage en anden, cyberangreb en tredje"* ("Drones one day, sabotage another, cyberattack a third"). Defence Minister **Troels Lund Poulsen**: "a hybrid attack"; *"det ligner bestemt ikke en tilfældighed, men ser systematisk ud"* ("it certainly doesn't look like coincidence; it looks systematic"). [P/J: DR, TV 2] PET chief **Finn Borch Andersen**'s "model of hybrid warfare" line is **[NV]** — no primary text reached.

**Defence Command.** 25 Sept: drones observed at several military installations, including Flyvestation Skrydstrup and Jydske Dragonregiment's barracks at Holstebro. 27 Sept: *"Forsvaret kan bekræfte, at der er blevet observeret droner ved flere af Forsvarets lokaliteter i nat."* [P: forsvaret.dk]

**Borris (28 Sept) — the panic's most dangerous false positive.** Soldiers fired **43 rounds** at a supposed drone. Chief of Defence **Michael Hyldgaard** and Defence Minister **Jeppe Bruus** denied a manned aircraft was targeted. TV 2 then found witnesses heard gunfire ≥11 minutes before the official time, a Messenger message at **20:09** reading *"They just shot at a plane over the Borris camp"*, and flight-radar data showing a Widerøe Dash 8-400 over the camp at **20:06–20:07** at 2,800 m. Bruus ordered a review. [J: TV 2 via sUAS News, 2 Jul 2026]

**Retraction, stage 1 — 18 March 2026.** The crown witness told Frihedsbrevet that police said **repeatedly and firmly** that she had not seen drones. Experts assessed her colleague's video as *"genskær … fra lyskegler eller fra fly"* (lens flare from light cones or aircraft); other outlets' flight data pointed to a **Roskilde school aircraft** cleared to fly over the airport at that time. She was interviewed four times, held her account: *"Jeg sagde til dem, at de må kalde det en UFO, hvis de vil. Jeg ved, hvad jeg har set."* Police told another colleague his later "drone" was a helicopter — probably the police counter-terror helicopter hunting the drones. The airport then had only a bird radar and a DJI hobby-drone detector. [J: Frihedsbrevet]

**Retraction, stage 2 — 25 June 2026.** After a nine-month investigation, Chief Police Inspector **Søren Thomassen**: *"We cannot confirm that there has been drone activity in and around the airport. At the same time … we cannot rule out the possibility that there has been drone activity."* **No suspects; investigation closed.** Justice Minister **Nicolai Wammen**: "It is part of the nature of hybrid warfare that the adversary does not reveal itself…" [P/J: Reuters wire, via The Straits Times]

**The most damaging admission — 19 June 2026 press conference.** Danish authorities stated that **none of the ships investigated, including Russian shadow-fleet vessels, could be linked to the reported drone observations** — directly undercutting the IISS "shadow fleet" thesis published a fortnight later. A separate **Armed Forces report** (≈18 June 2026) nonetheless concluded that, on soldiers' observations plus technical information, drones **had** overflown Danish military installations several times in Sept 2025. [J: dronewatch.nl] The quotes **"we were not clear enough with our reservations"** and a "confirmed drones vs probable aircraft" breakdown are **[NV]**; so is the claim that report publication was postponed until after the election.

**Norway — PST, Feb 2026.** PST department director **Inger Haugland** (not the director-general, Beate Gangås) told **VG**: *"We have not uncovered a single case of a drone being controlled by a foreign state."* Explanations given: *"various celestial bodies such as stars, weather phenomena, aeroplanes, or helicopters."* She added that some cases led to enforcement for no-fly-zone breaches — "something quite different from a coordinated attempt by a foreign state". [J: VG via sUAS News, 6 Feb 2026]

## B) Belgium — the VRT/Pano review (April 2026)

**Pano (VRT) broadcast 15 April 2026.** Verification:
- **"558 reports, no physical evidence" — confirmed in substance.** Francken told the Chamber Defence Committee (22 Apr) that **558 reports** were made; of ~**250 over military areas**, **ADIV** judged **42** to have *"geen andere plausibele verklaring"* ("no other plausible explanation") than drones. But under ADIV's own definition only **physical evidence** (intercepted drone or detained operator) is conclusive — **never produced in any case**, and **no verifiable imagery has ever been presented**. [P: Francken; J: dronewatch.nl, 22 Apr 2026]
- **Minister's "drone" footage was a police helicopter — confirmed.** The "enormous drone" video over Brussels Airport (Nov 2025) was sent by **Francken himself** to *Het Laatste Nieuws*; OSINT identified a federal police helicopter within hours; VRT's fact-check confirmed it on **19 Nov 2025** and HLN removed it. Francken called it *"bijzonder pijnlijk"* ("particularly painful"). [J: dronewatch.nl, 16 & 22 Apr 2026]

**Defence response (mil.be, 16 Apr 2026) [P].** Defence conceded it *"ne disposait pas des moyens et des possibilités nécessaires pour détecter les drones en survol et … pour intervenir"*. A C-UAS emergency package went through the Council of Ministers via negotiated procedure without publicity (art. 25, 1°, e) crisis and g) exclusivity of the 13 Aug 2011 law); orders to four Belgian firms plus one foreign (COBBS **dronehunter BLAZE**, **Senhive SEN PRO**, SAAB **Giraffe 1X**); all sites equipped by June 2026. It called Pano's price comparisons *"totalement erronées"* and argued *"Le fait que tous les signalements n'aient pas pu être confirmés a posteriori ne témoigne pas d'une surestimation de la menace."* Two admissions matter: *"l'intervention potentielle d'un acteur étatique a été prise en considération, **sans attribution formelle**"*, and security services treated Russia as *"l'hypothèse la plus plausible"*. **No formal attribution was ever made.** [P]

**Political consequences.** Francken was summoned to the Chamber ("We hebben tot vier keer te veel betaald" — "we paid up to four times too much", *De Morgen*, 17 Apr). In July 2026 Groen MP **Staf Aerts**, from FOI documents, alleged the cabinet was warned on **6 Nov 2025** the footage was probably the police helicopter and Defence concluded with "reasonable certainty" on **12 Nov** — while Francken told the committee he learned of it only after 19 Nov. Aerts: *"Dat is geen kwestie van interpretatie. Dat is een minister die het parlement bewust verkeerd inlicht"* ("That is a minister deliberately misinforming parliament"). He also alleged Francken **cut ~10 seconds** from the video pre-publication. Francken denies it, citing skeyes telling his office on 4 Nov the object was "certainly not a helicopter". Opposition suggested the threat was inflated ahead of approval of a **€50m** counter-drone programme. [J: dronewatch.nl, 22 Jul 2026; apache.be, 30 Jul 2026]

**Doel, Kleine Brogel, Zaventem (2–9 Nov 2025).** Zaventem closed **4–5 Nov**; Kleine Brogel (2 Nov); **Doel** nuclear plant (3 drones, 9 Nov); Liège closed 9 Nov; also Antwerp port, Leopoldsburg, Marche-en-Famenne. **None of these November episodes was ever substantiated with physical evidence.** The only incident Defence has publicly confirmed as *observed* is Elsenborn camp, **October 2025**. [P/J]

## C) Vilnius birds (13 Sept 2026) and Madrid-Barajas (7 Oct 2025)

**Vilnius — officially confirmed false positive.** On Sunday **13 Sept 2026** a radar return near Vilnius was assessed as a possible drone; the airport closed, an air-raid alert was activated and **NATO scrambled a fighter from Šiauliai** (Italian Eurofighters). **Visual identification established a flock of birds.** The airport reopened **38 minutes** later; the Vilnius marathon was unaffected. Lithuania's **National Crisis Management Centre** said radar returns from birds and drones can look very similar on first detection, hence the need for verification. A near-identical Polish case the week before — a track from the Belarus direction — was also birds. [J: Reuters via dronewatch.nl, 14 Sep 2026; Sky News, DroneXL]

**Madrid-Barajas.** On **7 Oct 2025 Telemadrid** reported the **Guardia Civil** believes the drone that forced the Barajas closure **never existed** ("La Guardia Civil cree que el dron que obligó a cerrar Barajas nunca existió"), concerning the **6 Nov 2024** closure (~1 hour, ~21 flights diverted). **The URL and body could not be retrieved** (candidate telemadrid.es URLs returned 404; site search and Wayback CDX did not surface it) → **[NV] at primary level**. It matches a documented prior: the **3 Feb 2020** closure also produced a "ghost drone" story (*El Independiente*, 8 Feb 2020). Barajas therefore replicates Gatwick's evidentiary collapse twice.

## D) THE CENTRAL QUESTION — was any drone scare deliberately amplified?

**Clear negative finding, scoped precisely: no credible documented finding was located that bots, a foreign state or an influence operation deliberately amplified the SEPT–OCT 2025 western-European drone-sighting wave (DK/NO/DE/BE/NL).** Nothing in EEAS/EUvsDisinfo, EDMO, Europol, DFRLab, ISD, EU DisinfoLab, Hybrid CoE or academic sources supports it. What *is* documented is materially different: (i) real Russian drone incursions, concentrated in **Poland/Romania/Moldova/the Baltics**; (ii) Russian FIMI issued **reactively, after** real drone events; (iii) from **mid-2026**, a documented **coordinated amplification campaign around a drone narrative** (Romania); and (iv) overwhelming evidence of **organic panic, misidentification and media feedback loops** in the 2025 western wave. Two further honest correctives below: the "hide in the noise" concept resolves to an **inverted meaning**, and the EU's own drone Action Plan **does not address disinformation at all**.

**Searched** (~130 queries across Google News RSS, EDMO/EUvsDisinfo site search, the EDMO WordPress REST API, sitemaps, the Wayback Machine and the EU Publications Office), including all of: drone panic disinformation influence operation Europe · Russian bots drone sightings amplification · EDMO/EUvsDisinfo drone disinformation · DFRLab/ISD/EU DisinfoLab/Hybrid CoE drone · "hide in the noise" hybrid warfare drones · drone sightings psyop psychological operation Europe · drone panic Russian Telegram channels amplification · NATO drone disinformation information warfare · EC drone action plan Feb 2026 · Orion/Venus/Starlink reported as drones · copycat drone sightings false reports after media coverage.

**(i) State-sponsored PHYSICAL flights — real, separately proven.** Leipzig/Halle, 4–5 Aug 2026 (explosive FPV; Germany blamed Russia 1 Sep 2026); Øresund, 26 Feb 2026 (drone from the Russian intelligence ship *Zhigulevsk*, jammed by HSwMS *Rapp*, confirmed by Defence Minister **Pål Jonson**); Poland, 9–10 Sept 2025. These are physical acts, not information operations.

**(ii) State-sponsored DISINFORMATION about drones — documented, reactive, and now confirmed at EU level.** The **EEAS *4th Report on FIMI Threats* (March 2026)** [P] makes drones an explicit FIMI category: *"military/security incidents (such as drones' incursions) supported by information campaigns, based on fabricated content and assets, to alter perceptions of said actions and reality"*, and states that escalatory hybrid actions *"including drone incursions… in EU countries such as **Poland, Romania, Lithuania and Estonia**… were accompanied by FIMI designed to manage public perception and test responses."* Scale reported: 10,500 channels/sites, 540 incidents in 2025, 29% Russia / 6% China / **65% unattributed**. **It does not name Denmark or Norway.** Consistent with this: **EUvsDisinfo (18 Sep 2025)**, "The Kremlin spews a smokescreen of FIMI over drones in Poland" — reactive denial and blame-shift *after* the 9 Sep incursion; **EDMO/BROD (17 Sep 2025)**, 18,316 posts analysed, narrative clusters *"Ukraine Behind Drones"* and *"NATO Drone Hysteria"* — narratives that **followed** a confirmed Russian Geran incursion; **EDMO/Re:Baltica (9 Apr 2026)** on Baltic narratives. The pattern is **opportunistic narrative capture after an event**, not orchestration of a sighting wave. [P/T] The war in Ukraine framing also ran the other way: German Chancellor **Merz** said *"Our suspicion is that Russia is behind most of these drone flights"* — suspicion, explicitly without evidence.

**"Hide in the noise" — PRIMARY SOURCE FOUND, MEANING INVERTED.** The phrase traces to Capt. **Matthew Moellering**, "Hiding in the Noise: Preparing the Irregular Warfare Community for the Age of AI", **Modern War Institute, West Point, 26 Sep 2022**: *"Irregular warfare operators will have to learn to hide in the noise to defeat these new instruments of power."* That is **US special-operations forces hiding from adversary AI surveillance — not a state concealing covert action inside a noisy information environment.** The concept as posed for drones is **not verified in any EU/NATO document**; the EEAS FIMI report contains **zero** instances of "noise". [P] IISS's unfalsifiability move (below) is the closest functional analogue, and it is not what the term means.

**(iii) The one documented COORDINATED AMPLIFICATION of a drone narrative — Romania, May–June 2026.** **EDMO Weekly Pulse / BROD (Funky Citizens), 11 June 2026** [T]: the **Galați** drone strike (29 May 2026) was *"sustained through a coordinated amplification campaign lasting seven consecutive days"* — **28–105 accounts per day**, **3,400–6,800 coordinated posts per day**, **116,014 posts analysed**, with **10–155 million views** and **52,000–1.7 million shares** per network and aggregate follower audiences of **5.5–49 million**. By contrast the **Constanța** incident *"emerged organically during a temporary information vacuum"*. This is the strongest positive evidence anywhere in this corpus that a drone narrative was amplified in a coordinated way — but it is **May–June 2026, not Sept–Oct 2025**, and it amplified a **false-flag claim about a real strike**, not a sighting wave.

**Government accusing an adversary of an INFORMATION OPERATION tied to drones — one case, 2026.** **Latvia, 27 March 2026**: Defence Ministry spokesperson **Guna Gavrilko** said Russia had launched *"a massive information operation against the Baltic states"*, and the MFA handed Russia a formal protest (via EDMO/Re:Baltica, 9 Apr 2026). This is the **only** clear case found of a government explicitly framing drone-related activity as an adversary information operation — and it is **2026, not Sept–Oct 2025**. For Sept–Oct 2025 there is no equivalent: Frederiksen called it *"det mest alvorlige angreb på dansk kritisk infrastruktur hidtil"* and Wammen spoke of hybrid war's opacity, but neither accused Russia of an information operation about drones. [P/J]

**(iv) The IISS case — an unproven theory amplified by a think tank and the press.** The IISS report *Russia's UAV Campaign Over Europe* claimed an 18-month Russian campaign over bases, nuclear sites and infrastructure, with **shadow-fleet ships as launch platforms**, tying **144 incidents in 13 countries** to Russian intelligence; *The Guardian* and *The Telegraph* amplified it. Dronewatch's rebuttal (4 Jul 2026): **no physical evidence links any specific drone to any specific ship** — no observed launches, no intercepted control signals, no wreckage, no convincing video, no telemetry; some named ships were **>100 km** from the sightings. IISS **discusses** Dronewatch/*Trouw*'s finding of no confirmation, then argues the absence of confirmation *fits* a successful Russian deception campaign. Dronewatch: *"Die redenering is lastig te toetsen, omdat de hypothese daarmee in feite niet meer weerlegbaar wordt"* ("the hypothesis thereby becomes effectively unfalsifiable"). **The amplifers here were a think tank and the press, not a hostile state.** Technical falsification: Dutch ministers **Brekelmans**/**Tuinman** said Volkel/Eindhoven involved **small hobby drones**, which lack the range to fly hundreds of km from North Sea ships. Italy: an anti-drone system repeatedly alarmed on a supposed Russian **Zala 421** over the EU Joint Research Centre and a Leonardo site, prompting an espionage investigation; months later **no drone had flown** — a **GSM signal booster** plus system limits. RAF Lakenheath, Nov 2024: the key incident was a **US F-15 mistaken for a drone**, and the **first alarm came from a civilian following online rumours**, not the base. [J: dronewatch.nl, 4 Jul 2026]

**(v) Organic panic and media feedback loops — the best-documented mechanism, with the amplification hypothesis directly tested and rejected.** The **Trouw (NL) + Dronewatch** investigation (Nov 2025) examined **~60 incidents in 11 countries**: *"veel verwarring en onduidelijkheid en regelmatig vals alarm"* ("much confusion and ambiguity and regularly false alarms"), and *"Voor Russische betrokkenheid… is in het overgrote deel van de gevallen geen hard bewijsmateriaal geleverd"* ("in the vast majority of cases no hard evidence of Russian involvement has been produced"). Roughly **40 incidents** were unclear or showed no evidence of any drone; the exceptions with hard evidence were **Poland, Romania and Moldova**. A **Koninklijke Marechaussee** spokesperson told Trouw that reports *"far from always"* involve a drone and that *"Oorzaak hiervan kan natuurlijk alle aandacht zijn, ook in de media"* ("the cause can of course be all the attention, including in the media") — a direct official statement that media salience drove reports. Specific misidentifications: Belgium — aircraft and helicopters; **Zuid-Limburg and Billund (DK) — stars**; Norway — an oil-platform "drone" was probably a **ship**; VRT fact-checks found the "enormous drone above Zaventem" was a **police helicopter**, two further videos a **police helicopter** and a **landing DHL cargo plane**; TV 2 (DK) found many Copenhagen reports were **regular air traffic**. [J]

Dronewatch's one-year retrospective (Wiebe de Jager, 22 Sep 2026) adds the mechanism: the CPH employee who raised one of the first alarms later accepted investigators concluded it was probably not drones. *"Wie regelmatig nieuwsberichten ziet over vijandelijke drones, zal een onbekend lichtpunt eerder als drone interpreteren… Het is een zichzelf versterkend mechanisme"* ("…a self-reinforcing mechanism"). Media entered the observation chain: *"de beelden werden zelf een aanleiding voor nieuwe waarnemingen"* ("the images themselves became a trigger for new observations"). A natural experiment: after an advanced British detection system was rushed into Belgium, *"namen de meldingen van vermeende drones daarna snel af"* ("reports of alleged drones then declined rapidly"); conversely the **Netherlands had no drone-detection capability** during Volkel/Eindhoven because IRIS radars were lent to Ukraine — and no concrete evidence was later found there either. Many alleged drones were *"gewone vliegtuigen, helikopters, sterren, planeten of andere verklaarbare verschijnselen"* ("ordinary aircraft, helicopters, **stars, planets** or other explicable phenomena"). The Orion/Venus claim is thus directionally supported (stars and planets are confirmed explanations) though no source names those objects for Europe — **[NV]** as to Orion/Venus specifically.

**The EU's own drone Action Plan is silent on disinformation — a checked negative.** **COM(2026) 81 final** (11 Feb 2026, full text, 69,910 chars, retrieved via the EU Publications Office Cellar) contains **zero** occurrences of *disinformation, information manipulation, FIMI, psychological, misinformation, amplification* or *information space*; the eight uses of "hybrid" are generic threat framing. It is a prevention/detection/response and counter-drone **capability** plan. Whatever the EU's concern about drone-related FIMI, it is not expressed in this instrument. [P]

**Bottom line, stated precisely.** For the **Sept–Oct 2025 western-European wave** the evidence supports real but largely **unattributed physical incursions**, opportunistic propaganda riding the wave, and — for the flagship Copenhagen and Belgian cases — **organic misidentification amplified by media feedback**, with no documented hostile amplification. Documented **coordinated amplification** exists, but for **2026 Romania**, and documented **FIMI accompanying drone incursions** exists, but for **Poland/Romania/Lithuania/Estonia** — not Denmark or Norway. The nearest thing to a state psychological-operation accusation tied to drones is **Latvia, 27 March 2026**. The nearest thing to amplification of a drone-scare theory without proof is the **IISS report and its press uptake**, and the amplifers there were not a state.
