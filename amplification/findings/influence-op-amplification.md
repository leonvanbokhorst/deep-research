# Amplification / influence-operation evidence

**Question:** Is there a documented, credible finding that bots / a foreign state / an influence operation deliberately **amplified** a European drone-sighting wave (esp. Sept–Oct 2025: DK/NO/DE/BE/NL)?
**Date of research:** 25 Sep 2026. Unretrieved items marked NOT VERIFIED.

## Bottom line (explicit negative)
**No.** No credible documented finding was located that social-media actors, bots, a foreign state or an influence operation deliberately amplified the **Sept–Oct 2025** drone-sighting wave. What *is* documented is a different thing: **Russian FIMI (disinformation) issued *reactively, after* real drone events**, plus (from mid-2026) documented **coordinated amplification of a drone *narrative***. The Sept–Oct 2025 wave itself is best explained by **real incursions in the east + organic panic/misidentification/media feedback loops in the west** (Trouw's 60-incident audit).

## (a) Institutional reports on drone-scare disinformation
- **EEAS, *4th Report on FIMI Threats — Dismantling the FIMI House of Cards*, March 2026 (PRIMARY, EU official).** Frames drones explicitly as a FIMI category: *"FIMI incidents that would imply for example actions of sabotage, or military/security incidents (such as drones' incursions) supported by information campaigns, based on fabricated content and assets, to alter perceptions of said actions and reality."* And: *"Significant FIMI activities were accompanied by escalatory hybrid actions including drone incursions… in EU countries such as Poland, Romania, Lithuania and Estonia. These incidents were accompanied by FIMI designed to manage public perception and test responses."* Scale: 10,500 channels/sites mobilised; 540 FIMI incidents in 2025; 29% Russia, 6% China, 65% unattributed. **It does not name Denmark or Norway.**
- **EUvsDisinfo / EEAS, "The Kremlin spews a smokescreen of FIMI over drones in Poland", 18 Sep 2025 (PRIMARY).** Documents reactive denial/blame-shifting after the 9 Sep 2025 Polish incursion — *"Nothing happened and we didn't do it"*, *"'They' did it"*. Not amplification of a panic.
- **EDMO/– Funky Citizens (BROD), "False Narratives Analysis Of Russian Drone Incidents In Romanian Airspace", 17 Sep 2025 (think-tank/hub, primary-research).** 18,316 posts analysed (Telegram, X, Facebook, Bluesky, news); 326 narrative-bearing posts; four clusters incl. *"Ukraine Behind Drones"* and *"NATO Drone Hysteria"*. Narratives *followed* a confirmed Russian Geran incursion.
- Europol, DFRLab, ISD, EU DisinfoLab, Hybrid CoE: **no drone-scare-specific item retrieved — NOT VERIFIED** (see query list).

## (b) Analyses testing the amplification hypothesis, Sept–Oct 2025
- **Trouw (NL) + Dronewatch, Nov 2025 — "Analyse zestig drone-incidenten in Europa: veel paniek en weinig bewijs" (journalism, investigative).** ~60 incidents, 11 countries, 3 months: *"veel verwarring en onduidelijkheid en regelmatig vals alarm"* ("much confusion and ambiguity and regularly false alarms"). *"Voor Russische betrokkenheid… is in het overgrote deel van de gevallen geen hard bewijsmateriaal geleverd."* ("no hard evidence of Russian involvement in the vast majority of cases"). ~40 incidents: origin unclear or no evidence of any drone. Exceptions with hard evidence: **Poland, Romania, Moldova** (debris found; Dutch F-35s downed Russian drones over Poland).
- **PRIO, "What do the recent drone sightings in Denmark and Norway mean?", 23 Oct 2025 — exists, NOT VERIFIED** (could not retrieve text).

## (c) State-linked amplification
**Positive but different wave:** EDMO Weekly Pulse, **11 Jun 2026**, reporting BROD/Funky Citizens — the Galați drone strike (29 May 2026) was *"sustained through a coordinated amplification campaign lasting seven consecutive days"*: 28–105 accounts/day; 5.5M–49M aggregate follower audiences; 10–155M views and 52,000–1.7M shares per network; 3,400–6,800 extra coordinated posts/day; 116,014 posts analysed across Telegram, X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit. By contrast the Constanța incident *"emerged organically during a temporary information vacuum"*. **This is May–Jun 2026, not Sept–Oct 2025, and amplified a false-flag claim about a real strike.**

## (d) "Hide in the noise"
**Primary source found, but meaning is inverted.** Capt. Matthew Moellering, *"Hiding in the Noise: Preparing the Irregular Warfare Community for the Age of AI"*, **Modern War Institute, West Point, 26 Sep 2022**: *"Irregular warfare operators will have to learn to hide in the noise to defeat these new instruments of power."* This is **US SOF hiding from adversary AI surveillance** — not a state concealing covert action inside a noisy information environment. The "concealment in noise" idea as posed is **NOT VERIFIED** in any EU/NATO document retrieved (EEAS FIMI report contains zero instances of "noise").

## (e) Government accusing an adversary of PSYCHOLOGICAL OPERATIONS tied to drones
**One documented case, from 2026.** Latvia, 27 Mar 2026: Defence Ministry spokesperson **Guna Gavrilko** stated on X that Russia had launched *"a massive information operation against the Baltic states"*; the MFA handed Russia a formal protest. She cited the campaign's coordination and reach across platforms and languages (via EDMO/Re:Baltica, 9 Apr 2026). For **Sept–Oct 2025** there is no equivalent: Danish PM Mette Frederiksen called it *"an attack on Danish critical infrastructure"*; Merz said *"Our suspicion is that Russia is behind most of these drone flights"* — suspicion, explicitly without evidence, and not a psyop accusation.

## (f) Virality driving ADDITIONAL FALSE reports — DOCUMENTED
- **Koninklijke Marechaussee (Dutch military police) spokesperson**, via Trouw: it receives many reports but *"het lang niet altijd om een drone gaat. 'Oorzaak hiervan kan natuurlijk alle aandacht zijn, ook in de media.'"* ("far from always a drone… the cause can of course be all the attention, including in the media").
- **Misidentification, per Trouw:** Belgium — small **aircraft/helicopters** taken for drones; **Zuid-Limburg and Billund (DK) — objects were STARS**; Norwegian police: a suspected "drone" near a North Sea oil platform was probably a **ship**. Some were hobbyists/tourists.
- **VRT fact-check (via Trouw):** the "exclusive images of an enormous drone above Zaventem" was a **police helicopter**; two further "drone" videos near Belgian military bases were a **police helicopter** and a **landing DHL cargo plane**.
- **TV2 (DK) reconstruction (via Trouw):** many Copenhagen reports were attributable to **regular air traffic**.

## (g) NATO / EU statements linking drones to information operations
- **NATO, "Countering hybrid threats" (updated 29 Jan 2026):** hybrid methods include *"propaganda, deception, sabotage"*; *"The Alliance actively counters disinformation and propaganda – not with more propaganda, but with facts."* No drone-amplification claim. Special Coordinator for Hybrid Threats created 2025.
- **EEAS FIMI report (Mar 2026):** *"Incidents involving drones and other incursions into EU territory have contributed to a climate of intimidation and uncertainty. FIMI does not operate in isolation; it forms part of a broader hybrid arsenal combining digital interference with physical signalling and coercive actions."*

## COM(2026) 81 final (11 Feb 2026), "Action Plan on Drone and Counter Drone Security"
**Checked — NEGATIVE.** Full text retrieved (69,910 chars). **Zero** occurrences of *disinformation, information manipulation, FIMI, psychological, misinformation, amplification, information space*. Eight uses of "hybrid", but only as generic threat framing (e.g. drones/balloons *"as a tool of hybrid campaign"*); the plan is prevention/detection/response/counter-drone capability, not information operations.
