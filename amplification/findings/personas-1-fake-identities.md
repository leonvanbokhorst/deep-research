# Long-Running Fake Personas & Fabricated Identities in Influence Operations — Findings 1

**Research cut-off:** 25 September 2026. **Framing:** defensive/capability assessment only.
**Grades:** A = primary/official · B = peer-reviewed/high-quality investigative · C = reputable news · D = vendor/think-tank/NGO · E = anonymous/unverified.
Every factual line below is tagged **[demonstrated]**, **[claimed]** or **[inferred]**. Where a persona name or URL could not be confirmed, that is stated explicitly rather than filled in.

---

## 1. Russia: John Mark Dougan, CopyCop and Storm-1516 — the clearest long-duration case

**Duration: at least 9 years (2017 → 2026), with the disinformation-network phase running Aug 2023 → present.**

Dougan, a US Marine (1996–98) and Palm Beach County (Florida) sheriff's deputy (2005–2008), was dismissed in 2009 after sexual-harassment allegations, which he reframed as whistleblowing. After posting officers'/judges' personal data he was searched by the FBI in 2016; in **2017 he was indicted in the US on 21 state charges of extortion and wiretapping**, having already fled via Canada to Russia. He adopted the cover identity **"BadVolf"** — initially presenting as a Russian hacker. **[demonstrated — court/arrest record reported; NewsGuard, NYT, WaPo]**

**The infrastructure.** France's VIGINUM (SGDSN) technical report of **7 May 2025** — a primary government analysis — attributes at least **293 domains** (active + historical) to the CopyCop / "MAGAstan" / "False Façade" network, *technically linked* by shared registration, configuration and hosting. Anchors include `badvolf.com` (**registered 10 April 2017**), `dcweekly.org`, `clearstory.news`, `newsdesk.press`, `nebraskatruth.com`, and a fake "Syndicate of Independent International Journalists" (`soiij.org`). From January 2024 Dougan registered dozens of typosquatted media brands (`chicagochron.com`, `londoncrier.com`, `infosindependants.fr`); **on 10 May 2024 alone he registered 84 domains**. VIGINUM states it can "confirm the almost systematic exploitation of CopyCop by Storm-1516" and believes Dougan registers/maintains the infrastructure and may help produce narratives. **[demonstrated — VIGINUM, grade A]**

**The operation.** Storm-1516 has been active **since at least August 2023**. VIGINUM documented **77 information operations**; Bloomberg (Apr 2026) counted **more than 190 fabricated stories**; roughly **40% target Ukraine**, about a third target foreign electoral processes. VIGINUM's appendix lists operation-by-operation dates from 20 Aug 2023 (Zelensky's mother-in-law's Egyptian villa) through the 2024 US election. **[demonstrated — A/D]**

**Attribution.** VIGINUM links Dougan to the Moscow think-tank **Centre for Geopolitical Expertise (CGE)** and to **GRU Unit 29155**, naming **Yury Khoroshenky/Khoroshyovsky** as the officer who allegedly funded and coordinated operations and made transfers to Dougan's account **from April 2022**. Unit 29155's involvement is **inferred**, not proven — VIGINUM explicitly says it cannot confirm direct Unit 29155 involvement. Bloomberg (Apr 2026) adds: coordination by **Sergei Kiriyenko**; GRU Unit 29155 head **Oleg Kushnir** paying for servers/AI tools; **Yury Khoroshenky** coordinating; content possibly produced by the sanctioned pseudo-rights group **Foundation to Battle Injustice**; and **Alexander Dugin** coordinating amplification. **[claimed/inferred, multiple intelligence sources — D/C, medium confidence]**

**Official responses.**
- **US:** DOJ on **4 Sept 2024** charged two RT employees, **Kostiantyn Kalashnikov and Elena Afanasyeva**, over a ~$10M scheme funding US influencers (the Tenet Media case). On **31 Dec 2024** Treasury/OFAC sanctioned Russian and Iranian entities for election interference; VIGINUM records that Treasury thereby confirmed GRU coordination of CGE. I did **not** locate a US federal indictment of Dougan himself in this session — his US criminal exposure appears to be the 2017 Florida state charges. **[demonstrated for the charges; "DOJ indicted Dougan" NOT verified]**
- **EU:** Dougan was designated under the EU's hybrid-threats framework (Council Decision (CFSP) 2024/2643), published in **Official Journal L 2025/2568, date of listing 15 December 2025** — asset freeze plus travel ban. The official reason cites his operation of CopyCop and support to Storm-1516, with the "information manipulation and interference" designation. NewsGuard called it the first EU sanctions listing of an American for pro-Kremlin disinformation (**16 Dec 2025**). **[demonstrated — A]**
- NewsGuard named Dougan its **2024 Disinformer of the Year** (6 Jan 2025). **[D]**

## 2. Storm-1516's fabricated "whistleblower" personas — what actually named

VIGINUM found burner accounts on **six platforms** (YouTube, Instagram, X, Facebook, TikTok, Rumble), used in the initial seeding of **45 of 77** operations, with most fake accounts **still not suspended** as of 25 March 2025. Documented specifics:

- **"Mr. James O."** — presented as an eyewitness in the Prince Andrew/Ukraine child-abuse video, seeded by disposable YouTube account **@Ibrahimabiola668** (27 Aug 2023). **[demonstrated — A]**
- **"Jules Vincent"** — a fake French-speaking "freelance journalist" specialising in environment, used in the "Zelensky & Soros plan to bury toxic waste in Ukraine" operation (27 Nov 2023). The X account was **created in September 2018** and was fed genuine environmental content from **15 November 2023** — i.e. a pre-aged persona warmed up over weeks. **[demonstrated — A]**
- **"Alicia Brown" / "Alisha Brown"** — the paid actor in the Kamala Harris hit-and-run video (2 Sept 2024); the accompanying fake outlet **"KBSF-TV"** does not exist, and the persona's name was spelled inconsistently across assets — an exposing error. **[demonstrated — C/D]**
- **"Rick"** — the supposed Kazakh exchange student accusing Tim Walz of sexual abuse (first aired on the "RedPill 78" QAnon radio show, 5 Oct 2024). The State Department said **no Kazakh students attended Mankato West High School 2000–2020**. **[demonstrated — C]**
- Burner handles left live as of 25 Mar 2025: **@ShahzadNasir33**, **@johndoe__2023**. **[demonstrated — A]**
- Paid seeding intermediaries: **Simeon Boykov (@aussiecossack)** paid **@AlphaFox78 $100** to post a fake "Haitians voting illegally" video; French exile **Adrien Bocquet's** "Véritées Cachées" carried the Volkov/prostitution video. **[demonstrated — A]**

**What gave the network away (TTPs):** shared registration dates (three YouTube accounts used in three Sept 2023 ops were **all created 30 Sept 2022**, implying bulk purchase); duplicated assets (an X-ray lifted from a medical journal; a crash photo from a 2018 Guam accident); template scripts; cross-platform redundancy (same video on Rumble + YouTube under near-identical names); and simultaneous publication across **100+ Dougan sites** (e.g. 19 Oct 2024). **[demonstrated — A/C/D]**

**Not verified:** I could not confirm personas named **"Vladimir Z."**, **"Alexei"**, or a Doppelganger/Storm-1099 persona named **"Elise"** in any primary or high-quality source. Doppelganger/RRN (Storm-1099) *is* a separate fake-news-site impersonation operation (VIGINUM op #30, 7 Mar 2024, inverted it as "the RRN/Doppelgänger IMS is led by the US State Department"), but no "Elise" persona is documented here. Treat those names as **unverified**.

## 3. China: Spamouflage / Dragonbridge — personas that persist through takedowns

Graphika uncovered the network in **September 2019**. Its evolution is the best-documented long-run persona programme:

- **2020** — "Spamouflage Goes to America": first substantial English-language video wave (from June 2020), first use of **AI-generated profile pictures** by this network. **[demonstrated — D]**
- **2021** — "Spamouflage Breakout": the operation shifted from hundreds of crude fakes to a **dozen "personality" accounts with developed backstories**. Named personas: **"贺景润 He Jingrun" (@jingrunhe)** — an account created **7 July 2009** whose first tweet appeared only on **7 Jan 2020** (a hijacked/sleeper account) — and **"李若水 Li Ruoshui" (francisw)**, resurrected repeatedly under the same name after suspension. They broke out of the bubble, amplified by Venezuelan FM **Jorge Arreaza**, ministers, a Huawei Europe executive, George Galloway and Chinese diplomats. **[demonstrated — D]**
- **2024** — "The Americans": **15 X accounts + one TikTok account** posing as US citizens/veterans/anti-war activists, plus a **cross-platform persona posing as a US-focused news outlet** (TikTok + X + now-suspended Instagram/YouTube). ISD separately documented four "MAGAflage" pro-Trump personas (**April 2024**). Give-aways: stock/AI profile pictures, bio discrepancies, coordinated resharing, and a staged "depression relapse" to explain a posting gap. **[demonstrated — D]**
- **2026** — In **late August 2026** X announced removal of a claimed **~200,000-account Chinese bot network** pushing anti-AI-data-centre narratives; researchers (Business Insider, san.com, Sept 2026) noted many accounts had **no followers or engagement**, so the headline figure overstates reach. **[claimed, partially contradicted — C, medium/low confidence]**

## 4. Iran

- **Endless Mayfly (2018–19).** Citizen Lab, **14 May 2019**: an "Iran-aligned network of inauthentic personas and social media accounts" that impersonated media outlets then deleted content once it gained traction ("ephemeral disinformation"). Documented persona: **"Mona A. Rahman" (@Mona_ARahman)**, who DM'd Saudi-criticism researcher **Ali Al-Ahmed** on **5 November 2018** with a fabricated Harvard Belfer Center article. **[demonstrated — B]**
- **Liberty Front Press (Aug 2018).** Facebook removed **652 accounts/pages/groups** linked to Iran; FireEye published the accompanying analysis of a network of inauthentic news sites (Liberty Front Press, British Left, US Journal, Ingavere, Quest 4 Truth) targeting US, UK, Latin American and Middle Eastern audiences. **[demonstrated broadly — C; I could not retrieve the FireEye PDF text in-session]**
- **Instituto Manquehue (Chile).** A Chilean outlet documented as amplifying Iranian state narratives (HispanTV/RT-adjacent framing). Attributed to Iran by DFRLab. **[claimed — I could not load the DFRLab article; treat as medium confidence, no URL verified]**
- **"Rafat" / International Union of Virtual Media.** A pro-Iran network of sites and fabricated journalist bylines documented by DFRLab/Clemson. **[claimed — not verified in-session]**
- **2024:** FBI indicted **three IRGC cyber actors** (**27 Sept 2024**) for a hack-and-leak operation against the 2024 US election — a persona-adjacent (hack-and-leak) case. **[A, from fbi.gov via search index]**

## 5. Israel: Team Jorge / AIMS (exposed 15 Feb 2023)

The Guardian/Forbidden Stories "Story Killers" consortium (with Haaretz, Le Monde, Der Spiegel, Radio France, TheMarker, OCCRP) exposed **Tal Hanan**, 50, ex-Israeli special forces, pseudonym **"Jorge"**, operating from Modi'in, Israel, through **Demoman International**.

- **AIMS (Advanced Impact Media Solutions)** controlled **more than 30,000 fake social-media profiles** on Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, Telegram, Gmail, Instagram, YouTube — some with **SMS-verified numbers, credit cards, bitcoin wallets and Amazon/Airbnb accounts**. Reporters identified **~2,000 AIMS-linked bots** on Facebook and Twitter. **[demonstrated — B]**
- **Named avatars:** **"Canaelan"** — a fake Sheffield-based Twitter user whose profile photo was **stolen from real Dutch journalist Tom Van Rooijen**; and **"Alexander"** (beanie, orange tulips, bio: "Fiction has to make sense"). **[demonstrated — B]**
- **Documented operations:** astroturfed outrage against the UK **Information Commissioner's Office** (Oct 2020, PPE procurement); a **staged fake protest on Regent Street, London**, and leafletting near the Eiffel Tower, filmed for amplification; activity across ~20 countries. Hanan **claimed 33 presidential-level campaigns, 27 successful** — the Guardian notes he may have embellished to win business. **[demonstrated for AIMS/bots; claims unverified — B]**
- **Meta removed AIMS-linked bots** on 14–15 Feb 2023, linking them to a firm banned in 2019. **[demonstrated — B]**

## 6. PeaceData (Russia, 2020) — fabricated "left-wing" editors

Graphika's report **"IRA Again: Unlucky Thirteen"** (Sept 2020): the site `peacedata[.]net` was **registered 6 Feb 2020**; Twitter **@peacedata_** created **25 Dec 2019**. The operation built a persona cluster posing as *staff*: **Ali Ahmed Ghanem** (Arabic editor-in-chief), **Jake Sullivan** (English editor-in-chief), **Alex Lacusta** and **Albert Popescu** (associate editors) — all using **AI-generated profile pictures**, while also duping real US and UK journalists into writing for the site. Facebook/Twitter took the network down **1 Sept 2020** after an FBI tip. Meta/Graphika assessed only ~5% of English content concerned the US election but the intent was to build a left-wing audience and steer it from Biden. **[demonstrated — A/D]**

## 7. Philippines — troll farms and the Duterte ecosystem

Reuters/Cyabra (**11 Apr 2025**): of **1,890 X profiles** discussing Duterte's ICC arrest, **~one-third were fake**, generating 1,300+ posts, 7,000+ engagements and **11.8M+ potential views**. Of **2,154** profiles examined 1 Jan–10 Mar 2025, **37% were fake**; of **3,033** profiles on the administration-backed coalition, **~45% were fake**. Cyabra told Reuters **up to 45% of election discussion** was inauthentic, with **~54M potential views**. Detections rested on simultaneous posting, coordinated spikes, identical content and shared hashtags — and fake profiles **interacted with journalists and real users to blend in**. Reuters notes "click armies" credited with helping Duterte win **2016**; Rappler has documented troll farms continuously since 2016 (Mocha Uson network, 2022 and 2025 elections). **[demonstrated — C/D]**

## 8–10. Kenya, India, Brazil — thinner verification this session

- **Kenya.** Cambridge Analytica's 2013/2017 work is documented (NYT, 20 Mar 2018; Privacy International long-read; Nation Africa on Facebook's suspension of a CA-linked firm). AFP Fact Check's **"Hashtags for hire"** (12 Aug 2024) documents paid "digital mercenaries" in Kenyan and Nigerian polls. **"The Real Uhuru" could not be verified as a documented page name** — treat as unverified. **[C, medium confidence]**
- **India.** Cobrapost's sting **"Operation Karaoke"** (19 Feb 2019) is the key documented case of paid-news/"IT cell"-adjacent networks. The EU DisinfoLab **"Indian Chronicles"** report is the strongest documented long-run case: a **15-year operation (2005 → 2020)** by the **Srivastava Group**, resurrecting **10+ dead UN-accredited NGOs** (and even a deceased chairman), running **750+ fake media outlets across 119 countries**, registering **550+ domains**, and launching the fake outlet **"EU Chronicle"** with fictitious journalists — amplified by India's ANI. **[demonstrated — A/B]**
- **Brazil.** Folha de S.Paulo (**18 Oct 2018**) documented companies bankrolling anti-PT mass WhatsApp messaging for the Bolsonaro campaign; the STF's fake-news inquiry and the "gabinete do ódio" investigations followed. **Specific persona names and 2025–26 STF outcomes were not verified in-session.** **[C, low-medium confidence]**

## Evidence gaps / caveats
1. No verified US federal indictment of Dougan was found; the 2017 Florida state indictment is the confirmed US criminal case.
2. "Vladimir Z.", "Alexei" and Doppelganger persona "Elise" — **unverified**.
3. DFRLab URLs for Instituto Manquehue and the Rafat/IUVM network could not be loaded (site reorganisation/404) — claims retained at lower confidence.
