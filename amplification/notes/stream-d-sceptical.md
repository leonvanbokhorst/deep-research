# Stream D — Sceptical assessments of covert influence-operation IMPACT

Cut-off 25 Sep 2026. URLs HTTP-checked 25 Sep 2026 (all 200 unless noted). Grades: A = primary/official · B = peer-reviewed · C = reputable news · D = vendor/think-tank/preprint · E = unverified. Tags: **[D]** demonstrated · **[C]** claimed · **[I]** inferred.

## 1. Breakout Scale — attribution CORRECTED

*The Breakout Scale: Measuring the impact of influence operations*, **Ben Nimmo** (Graphika), **Brookings, September 2020** — NOT Camille François, NOT 2019. https://www.brookings.edu/articles/the-breakout-scale-measuring-the-impact-of-influence-operations/ · PDF https://www.brookings.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Nimmo_influence_operations_PDF.pdf · A · HIGH · **[D]**

Corrections: (1) **Author is Nimmo**; François appears only in acknowledgements (draft feedback) and as co-author of Graphika's *IRA CopyPasta Campaign* (21 Oct 2019) — **[D]**. (2) **Levels are CATEGORY ONE–SIX, not "A to F"** — that labelling appears nowhere — **[D]**. (3) Brookings' *"Adding a 'D' to the ABC disinformation framework"* (27 Apr 2020) is by **Alexandre Alaphilippe** (verified from metadata); the original ABC framework is generally credited to François but no primary publication was verifiable — LOW.
**Levels [D]:** 1 one platform, no breakout · 2 one platform with breakout OR many platforms without · 3 multiple platforms, multiple breakouts · 4 cross-medium (mainstream media) · 5 celebrity amplification · 6 policy response or call for violence.

**Sceptical core [C]:** the scale "does not necessarily equate to actual impact: for example, a policymaker may repeat an IO's message in a debate but have it rejected" (p.5) — an upper bound on effect. Breakout requires *journalists, policymakers and celebrities* to carry content; covert assets rarely break out unaided. Example: the IRA's 2017 "March Against Sharia" drew "under a dozen anti-Muslim protesters… and only an estimated 60 counter-protesters. Nobody was hurt" (p.2). **[D]**

## 2. Takedown arithmetic vs. measured reach

**Meta (A).** Since 2017: "more than **200** covert influence operations", **68 countries**, **≥42 languages**; **two-thirds** wholly or partly *domestic*; ~one-third solely foreign. https://about.fb.com/news/2022/12/metas-2022-coordinated-inauthentic-behavior-enforcements/ · **[D]**. Q1 2023: "**We removed the majority of these operations before they were able to build authentic audiences**"; re China-origin ops, "we continue to find and remove them before they are able to build their audience." https://about.fb.com/news/2023/05/metas-adversarial-threat-report-first-quarter-2023/ · **[C]**, self-reported.

**Best-quantified case — Moldova, 11 Oct 2024 (A):** 7 FB accounts, 23 Pages, 1 Group, 20 IG accounts removed. Reach: **~4,200 Page followers; 0 Group joins; ~335,000 IG followers**, but "the vast majority… were outside of Moldova, which suggests the use of inauthentic engagement tactics"; ad spend **~$4,000**. "**We removed this campaign before they were able to build authentic audiences on our apps.**" https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/taking-action-against-coordinated-inauthentic-behavior-in-moldova/ · **[D]**

## 3. Do takedowns work? Quasi-experimental evidence (mixed)

- **Chandrasekharan et al. 2017**, *PACM HCI* 1(CSCW):1–22, doi:10.1145/3134666 — Reddit r/fatpeoplehate + r/CoonTown ban, 100M posts: "**The ban worked.**" Stayers cut hate speech **≥80%**; absorbing subreddits saw **no significant** change. B · **[D]**.
- **Horta Ribeiro et al. 2023**, *PNAS Nexus* 2(3):pgad035 — Parler deplatforming; panel N=112,705, DiD N=996: daily activity on other fringe platforms **+10.9 pp** (95% CI 5.9–15.9) desktop, **+15.9 pp** (10.2–21.7) mobile, **no decrease** in overall fringe activity. B · **[D]**.
- **Klinenberg 2023**, *J. Conflict Resolution* 68(6):1199–1225, doi:10.1177/00220027231188909 — YouTube vs Bitchute: deplatformed creators **+30%** Bitcoin revenue, **+50%** Bitchute views, but net **fall** in overall views/revenue → "deplatforming works". B · **[D]**.
- **Buntain & Snegovaya 2025**, *PNAS Nexus* 4(11):pgaf333 — Twitter "Great Deplatforming", Jan 6, vs Reddit: immediate **rise** in extremity/polarisation, later moderation; **no uniform flight** — only conservative-leaning users significantly more likely to leave. B · **[D]**.
- **Golovchenko 2022**, *J. Politics* 84(2), doi:10.1086/716949 — Ukraine's 2017 VKontakte ban (natural experiment): **cut activity despite easy circumvention**; pro-Russia-affiliated users affected *at least as much* — access, not attitudes, drove response. B · **[D]**.

**[I] net:** takedowns reliably cut activity *on the targeted surface* but displace it when substitutes exist (Parler→Gab/Rumble). "Whack-a-mole" is a **migration/aggregate-exposure** critique, not proof removals do nothing. Meta's record (>200 operations; six China-origin networks, half in a seven-month window) supports rapid reconstitution — **[D]** counts, **[I]** reconstitution.

## 4. Backfire debate — corrections mostly work

- **Nyhan & Reifler 2010**, *Political Behavior* 32(2):303–330, doi:10.1007/s11109-010-9112-2 — the origin claim. B · HIGH.
- **Nyhan 2021**, *PNAS* 118(15):e1912440117 — "an emerging research consensus finds that corrective information is **typically at least somewhat effective**"; media "distort[ed] its generality". *But* gains "often do not last or accumulate… decay or be overwhelmed by cues from elites and the media". B · HIGH · **[C]**. Double-edged.
- **Wood & Porter**, *Political Behavior* 41(1):135–163, doi:10.1007/s11109-018-9443-y (550 cites). **Date correction:** online 16 Jan 2018, issue 2019. The widely repeated "10,500 subjects / 52 experiments" figure **could not be verified** (paywalled; no OA copy per Unpaywall). MEDIUM for the null finding, **LOW** for any N.
- **Guess & Coppock**, *BJPS* 50(4):1497–1515, doi:10.1017/s0007123418000327 — three survey experiments, "**no evidence of backlash, even under theoretically favorable conditions**". B · HIGH · **[D]**.
- **Swire-Thompson, DeGutis & Lazer 2020**, *JARMAC* 9(3):286–299, doi:10.1016/j.jarmac.2020.06.006 — "widespread failures to replicate"; no subgroup shows consistent backfire. B · HIGH.
- **Ecker, Sharkey & Swire-Thompson 2023**, *PLOS ONE* 18(4):e0281140 — failed to replicate familiarity/fear backfire; myths-vs-facts **reduced** misconceptions. B · HIGH.
- **Effect sizes.** Chan et al. 2017, *Psych. Science* 28(11):1531–1546, doi:10.1177/0956797617714579 — **k=52, N=6,878**; debunking **ds = 1.14–1.33**; residual persistence **ds = 0.75–1.06**. B · HIGH.
- **Walter & Tukachinsky 2019/20**, *Communication Research* 47(2):155–177, doi:10.1177/0093650219854600 — 32 studies, N=6,527; **residual continued influence after correction r = –.05 (p = .045)** — corrections remove nearly all measured influence. B · HIGH · **[D]**.
- **Walter & Murphy 2018**, *Communication Monographs* 85(3):423–441, doi:10.1080/03637751.2018.1467564 — **effect size NOT verified** (no accessible abstract). LOW.
- Lewandowsky et al. 2012, *PSPI* 13(2):106–131, doi:10.1177/1529100612451018 — canonical continued-influence framing later work failed to replicate. B · HIGH.

## 5. Implied truth effect and soft-moderation limits

- **Pennycook, Bear, Collins & Rand 2020**, *Management Science* 66(11):4944–4957, doi:10.1287/mnsc.2019.3478. Study 1 **n=5,271**, Study 2 **n=1,568**: warnings gave only a "**modest reduction**" in perceived accuracy of false headlines, while **untagged headlines were judged more accurate than control**; tagging some *true* headlines "eliminates, and in fact slightly reverses" the effect. B · HIGH · **[D]**.
- **Critique:** Blanchar & Norris 2024, *HKS Misinformation Review*, doi:10.37016/mr-2020-157 — **Trump voters with high political knowledge judged election misinformation MORE truthful with "disputed" tags** than in control; "little to no evidence… for meaningful changes in beliefs about election fraud or fairness". Single non-representative experiment. B · MEDIUM · **[D]**.

## 6. Backlash against the intervener; effective counter-measures

- Roozenbeek et al. 2022, *Science Advances* 8(34):eabo6254, doi:10.1126/sciadv.abo6254 — 6 preregistered RCTs (**n=6,464**) + YouTube field (**n=22,632**); improved technique recognition and sharing decisions, robust across the political spectrum. B · HIGH · **[D]**.
- Biddlestone et al. 2026, *Communications Psychology*, doi:10.1038/s44271-025-00379-3 — 13 surveys, 12 EU states, **N=19,735**; campaign reached **120M+ YouTube users** pre-2024 EU elections; effects **small, ds ≈ 0.08–0.38**. B · HIGH · **[D]**.
- van der Linden et al. 2026, *HKS Misinformation Review*, doi:10.37016/mr-2020-193 — Instagram field study, **N=375,597** reached (quiz n=806): treated users **+21 pp** at identifying manipulation, persisting **5 months**. B · MEDIUM · **[D]**.
- **Counterpoint:** Wang, Phillips, Carley, Lin & Pennycook 2024, preprint doi:10.31234/osf.io/uxp85 — across five preregistered studies the engagement effect "**appeared to nullify**" once real tweets or other manipulation techniques entered the feed. D · MEDIUM.
- **Carnegie** (Bateman & Jackson, *Countering Disinformation Effectively*, 31 Jan 2024, https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2024/01/countering-disinformation-effectively-an-evidence-based-policy-guide): "**None of the interventions considered in this report were simultaneously well-studied, very effective, and easy to scale**"; utility of most "quite uncertain". Includes a case study on removing inauthentic asset networks. D · HIGH · **[C]**.
- Evidence that foreign interference reliably *backfires for the intervener* was **not verified**; closest mechanism is Golovchenko 2022. Treat as an open hypothesis, not a finding. LOW.

## 7. 2025–26: has AI qualitatively changed IO impact?

**Strongest number:** Hölbling, Maier & Feuerriegel, "A meta-analysis of the persuasive power of large language models", *Scientific Reports*, 12 Dec 2025, doi:10.1038/s41598-025-30783-y (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-30783-y) — **7 studies, 17,422 participants, 12 effect sizes: no significant LLM-vs-human difference in persuasiveness, g = 0.02 (p = .530)**; heterogeneity high (**I² = 75.97%**). B · HIGH · **[D]** for the result; **[I]** that this constrains claims AI transformed persuasive *impact* (as opposed to volume, cost and speed).

Supporting: Google's Q1–Q2 2026 bulletins show very large volume removals (15,727 then 18,514 channels) with **zero** audience data; Carnegie (2024) finds the toolbox largely unproven; Wang et al. (2024) find inoculation effects vanish in realistic feeds. **[I]** — the evidence supports "cheaper and more plentiful" far better than "more effective."

**Unverified (LOW):** NBC News, "Some of the largest online propaganda campaigns are using 'AI slop,' researchers say" (19 Nov 2025) — headline from Google News RSS, underlying report/URL unresolved. "18,000+ channels" Q3/Q4 2025 items (The Tech Buzz) are grade D and superseded above. **No Meta Content Library–based measurement of CIB reach was located** — a notable gap, since Meta's 2025–26 CIB reporting is not machine-retrievable.
