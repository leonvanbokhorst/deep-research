# Case C — "Disease X" and the US withdrawal from WHO

## Causal chain (150-350 words narrative)

In February 2018 a WHO R&D Blueprint consultation (Geneva, 6–7 February) added **"Disease X"** to its priority-pathogen list — a placeholder for "a serious international epidemic… caused by a pathogen currently unknown to cause human disease." The ambiguity was **named and given an institutional owner**, with no event behind it. In November 2022 WHO launched a global revision (300+ scientists, 25+ virus families); by 2024 the fixed list had become a *framework* covering 28 viral families and 1,652 pathogens.

On **17 January 2024** the World Economic Forum staged a Davos panel, "Preparing for Disease X." Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus explained the placeholder in real time — "we need to have a placeholder for the diseases we don't know may come… 'Disease X' is a placeholder for unknown diseases" — and repeated "anything happening is a matter of **when, not if**." The panel's actual content was humdrum: vaccine supply chains, epidemiological centres, surge-capable health systems ("You don't need to know the disease — there are common factors"). The reaction inverted the frame: Alex Jones alleged a "Globalist Plan to Launch #DiseaseX to Install World Government"; Donald Trump Jr. told Instagram followers to prepare by buying a sponsor's $299.99 ivermectin-containing "emergency medical kit." "Plandemic" and "WHO power grab" framings went viral; FactCheck.org, PolitiFact and AFP Fact Check documented and debunked them.

Institutionally, the ambiguity kept producing durable output even as the physical threat failed to appear. The **IHR amendments** were adopted by consensus at WHA77 on **1 June 2024** (WHA77.17), creating a "pandemic emergency" tier above a PHEIC; they entered into force **19 September 2025**. The **WHO Pandemic Agreement** was adopted by consensus at WHA78 on **20 May 2025** (committee vote 124–0–11). Then the attack: Trump's **20 January 2025** executive order withdrew the US from WHO, completed **22 January 2026**, and Washington declared itself bound by neither instrument. The one genuine "Disease X" outbreak — Panzi, Kwango, DRC, December 2024 — resolved into malaria, influenza and malnutrition.

## Persistence vs collapse factors

**Persisted:** a named, owned placeholder with no falsifiable claim; funded roadmaps and vaccine platforms; new legal machinery (pandemic-emergency tier, National IHR Authorities, States Parties Committee, PABS); "when, not if" in Tedros's rhetoric (June 2024, May 2026); a standing counter-narrative.
**Fragile:** no pathogen ever materialised; the 2024 revision dissolved the fixed list into a framework; the sole real "Disease X" outbreak resolved to known causes; the PABS annex remains unfinished, so the agreement is **not open for signature and has zero ratifications**; and the largest funder walked out.

## Institutional legacy (dates, dollar figures)

- 6–7 Feb 2018 R&D Blueprint review adds Disease X · Nov 2022 revision launched · 2024 framework (1,652 pathogens).
- 17 Jan 2024 Davos panel · 1 Jun 2024 WHA77 IHR amendments (WHA77.17) · 19 Sep 2025 amended IHR in force (11 of 196 States Parties rejected).
- 20 May 2025 WHA78 adopts the Pandemic Agreement by consensus; PABS annex deferred to an IGWG (WHA78.1); 20% real-time production target; entry into force 30 days after 60 ratifications. IGWG met 1 May, 6–17 July and 14–18 Sept 2026; UNGA high-level pandemic meeting 25 Sept 2026.
- US: EO 20 Jan 2025 (Fed. Reg. 2025-01957); withdrawal complete 22 Jan 2026; 15.6% of WHO revenue in 2022–23 (largest donor); assessed contributions capped at 22% ($109–122m/yr, FY2017–24).
- WHO: 2024–25 budget US$6.83bn; ~3,000 posts (22% of staff) cut Jan–Jun 2025; KFF records a US$6.2bn 2026–27 budget (9% lower), Health Policy Watch a $4.2bn operating target against a $1.7bn gap.
- Aid cuts: *Lancet Global Health* (Feb 2026) projects **22.6m additional deaths in 93 countries by 2030**; CNN and the Washington Post reported a 9.4m figure from the same study.

## COUNTEREXAMPLES

- The conspiracy framing never acquired institutional standing; fact-checks were fast and repeated, and the Wellness-kit grift was commercial, not strategic.
- WHO's own text affirms the Agreement gives the Secretariat **no** power to mandate lockdowns, vaccination or travel measures; the IHR cast WHO as Secretariat "without authority to compel action by countries."
- The single real "Disease X" outbreak (Panzi) resolved to known pathogens (Nature Medicine, 2026) — the placeholder absorbed a false positive without expanding.
- The US-backed alternative is also institutional, not merely destructive: 15 bilateral pathogen-access deals.
