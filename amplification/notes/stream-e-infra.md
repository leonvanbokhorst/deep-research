# Stream E — Infrastructure scare operations and hybrid-sabotage ambiguity, 2022–2026

Cut-off 25 Sep 2026. Analytic notes only. Chain shorthand: ambiguity → reports → amplification (adversarial / mediated / organic / political / media) → attention → vigilance → more reports → institutional response → validation. Each case states whether disruption came from the **physical** act or the **ambiguity/reporting**.

## 1. Nord Stream 1/2, 26 Sep 2022 — ambiguity as the payload

Four of six strings were ruptured in the Danish/Swedish EEZ. The physical act was strategically near-inert: Nord Stream 2 never operated and NS1 was already at zero, so no EU consumer lost gas that winter. Meaning is what moved. No actor claimed responsibility; competing narratives blamed Moscow, Washington (Moscow's "Anglo-Saxon powers") and a Ukrainian team. Sweden closed its probe on 7 Feb 2024 on jurisdictional grounds and Denmark on 26 Feb 2024, finding deliberate sabotage but "not enough grounds" — an outcome that itself fed cover-up narratives. Germany then moved: Serhii Kuznetsov extradited from Italy (handover approved 27 Nov 2025); diver Volodymyr Zhuravlev arrested in Pula, Croatia, on 19 Aug 2026, extradition approved 24 Sep 2026; a July 2026 indictment alleged a Ukrainian state link, which Kyiv denies. Amplification: **adversarial blast + mediated + heavily political/media**. Disruption came overwhelmingly from the **ambiguity/reporting** — it reshaped German energy policy and the Berlin–Kyiv relationship, and with no final conviction the ambiguity self-renews.

## 2. Balticconnector, 8 Oct 2023, and the "drifting" scare

The Finland–Estonia gas pipeline lost pressure and two Estonia–Finland/Sweden telecom cables were damaged. Initial framing was sabotage, suspect the Hong Kong-flagged *Newnew Polar Bear*. Finland's NBI recovered its anchor on 24 Oct 2023, matched paint, and said the mechanism was mechanical anchor drag while noting intent was unknown. In Aug 2024 China circulated a finding that a storm caused an accidental drag; Estonia's prosecutor called the document inadmissible as evidence. The Hong Kong captain was detained from May 2025, charged with causing damage and maritime-safety breaches, appeared in court in July 2025 without counsel, and the case continues (18 witnesses listed for a Jan 2026 hearing; not-guilty plea Feb 2026). Amplification: **mediated/media**, later **political** (JEF deployments). Physical damage was commercially minor; disruption was the **ambiguity** — it normalised "anchor drag" as the Baltic default while fuelling a grey-zone mapping thesis. Windward data, Sept 2026: Baltic drifting fell 5% to 526 vessels in 2025, but North Sea drifting rose 52% to 13,079 and South China Sea 88% to 18,401; ≥11 Baltic cables were cut or damaged Oct 2023–end 2025.

## 3. Nov–Dec 2024: Yi Peng 3 and Eagle S

On 17–18 Nov 2024 the BCS East-West Interlink (Sweden–Lithuania) and C-Lion1 (Finland–Germany) failed; Sky News and others reported "sabotage suspected" within hours. The Chinese bulk carrier *Yi Peng 3* was shadowed by the Danish navy; the WSJ reported (29 Nov 2024) that investigators suspected deliberate anchor dragging over ~100 miles and that China eventually allowed European investigators aboard (19 Dec 2024). The ship was released in Dec 2024; Sweden later concluded it could not rule out sabotage, nobody was charged, and US intelligence assessed the cuts as non-deliberate. Amplification: **media-led and globally contagious**. On 25 Dec 2024 the Cook Islands-flagged *Eagle S* was stopped by the Finnish Border Guard after its port anchor dragged ≥90 km, damaging Estlink 2 and four telecom cables. Helsinki's district court dismissed the case on 3 Oct 2025 for lack of jurisdiction; the Court of Appeal reversed on 27 Aug 2026, finding Finland has jurisdiction and reopening it, with ~€105m in civil claims. A parallel *Fitburg* trial opened 8 Sep 2026. Outages were brief; the **ambiguity** produced NATO's Baltic Sentry (14 Jan 2025), the "shadow fleet" doctrine and a live jurisdictional precedent.

## 4. GNSS jamming and spoofing, 2024–2026

In late April 2024 Estonia blamed Russia for interference that forced Finnair to suspend Tartu flights for about a month — the first visible commercial scheduling change from jamming. Attribution centred on Kaliningrad and Black Sea assets; Lithuania claimed in May 2026 that spoofing reaches ~450 km from Kaliningrad. By 2026 reporting treats it as routine ("daily GPS interference" in the Baltic), with material safety consequences: an Aug 2026 fatal air-ambulance crash was attributed to GPS failure, and in Sep 2026 GPS "instability" grounded part of Azul's Embraer E2 fleet. Amplification: **mediated/organic with strong political uptake**. Disruption is genuinely **physical/electronic** (navigation degradation, schedule and fleet decisions), but the scare is manufactured by attribution ambiguity — Russia denies it, and the interference also affects Russian aircraft. Aggregates must be handled carefully: the widely circulated "1,500 flights a day" figure is single-source and unverified.

## 5. The 2024–2026 sabotage-arson complex

On 19 July 2024 four parcels with explosive-incendiary charges were mailed from Vilnius: one ignited at the Leipzig/Halle DHL hub before loading to a UK-bound aircraft, one detonated on a DPD truck in Poland, one detonated in a DHL warehouse in Birmingham, one malfunctioned; nobody was hurt. Two test packages went to the US and Canada, and two more parcels were seized in Amsterdam. On 6 Mar 2026 Eurojust announced that a joint investigation team (DE, PL, NL, UK, LT) had identified **22 suspects** recruited from Russia, Latvia, Estonia, Lithuania and Ukraine, "often in a vulnerable socio-economic situation", acting for Russian military intelligence; five face terrorism trial in Lithuania (up to 10 years). Lithuania had charged 15 in Sept 2025; a German court convicted a Ukrainian man on 18 Aug 2026. Amplification: **adversarial devices → mediated/media → political**. Physical disruption was trivial; reporting produced airport-security panic, German consulate closures and a drone-defence law. The Leipzig/Halle drone-bomb belongs here: an explosives-laden drone struck an An-124 wing without detonating on **4–5 Aug 2026** (the brief's "Aug 2025" is a date error); Germany blamed Russia on 1 Sep 2026, and Bild-linked DNA matched the 20 July 2024 Leipzig DHL arson. Elsewhere, Warsaw's Marywilska 44 fire (May 2024) was publicly blamed by Tusk on Russian services with charges in Apr 2026; a Vilnius IKEA arson produced a Nov 2025 conviction; and on 24 Sep 2026 Poland called a fire at the Wola Krobowska Starlink ground station serving Ukraine "sabotage". BKA chief Holger Münch cited **over 1,000 suspicious drone flights over Germany in 2025**.

## 6. AI-generated ambiguity and the China dimension

The strongest verified synthetic-media figure is aggregate: the EEAS 4th FIMI report (Mar 2026) recorded **540 FIMI incidents globally in 2025, 27% involving AI-related TTPs — up from 41 cases in 2024 to 147 in 2025 (~+259%)**. Named actors include Storm-1516, Overload, Portal Kombat (suspected LLM "grooming" of training data) and Chinese Spamouflage; the EEAS judges most AI material low-quality with weak organic engagement, producing "cognitive chaos" more than persuasion. Europol's TE-SAT 2026 confirms deepfake audio/image use for propaganda, mostly "awful but lawful"; EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties took effect 2 Aug 2026. No verified case was found of a state using synthetic media to amplify a specific infrastructure incident. On China the same hulls recur (HK-flagged *Newnew Polar Bear*, Chinese-linked *Yi Peng 3*), and Taiwan's 3 Jan 2025 cable cut near Keelung was blamed on a Chinese-linked vessel; Taiwan jailed a Chinese captain in June 2025 — its first such conviction — while an April 2026 review attributed most near-shore cable damage to illegal anchoring. Again, disruption came from **ambiguity** (jurisdictional grey zones, unresolved intent), not demonstrated intent. The Sept 2026 Svalbard disclosure is the inverse: NATO states say they physically confronted Russian GUGI submersibles in spring 2026 and publicised an April 2026 finding to deter — manufacturing *deterrent* ambiguity rather than damage.

**Cross-cutting:** cheap, deniable physical acts (an anchor, a parcel, a jammer, a balloon) become strategic through unresolved attribution. Persistence: no final verdict (Nord Stream, Balticconnector, Yi Peng 3), jurisdictional novelty (Eagle S), political uptake. Collapse: mundane forensics, official contradiction, low organic reach for AI content.
