# Stream D — Radiological and Chemical Scare Campaigns and Hoaxes, 2002–2026

Cut-off 25 September 2026. Grades: **A** primary/official/court/peer-reviewed · **B** high-quality
secondary · **C** single-source/weaker secondary · **D** aggregator · **E** advocacy/vendor/anonymous.
Every URL below was fetched or HTTP-checked; blocked ones are flagged inline.

## 1. Litvinenko / polonium-210, London 2006 (adversarial → mediated → institutional)

A covert state act with no initial public claim: Litvinenko ingested Po-210 in the Pine Bar of the
Millennium Hotel on **1 Nov 2006** and died at University College Hospital on **23 Nov 2006**. The
ambiguity was clinical and forensic, not rhetorical; discovery of Po-210 on the day of death turned a
suspected poisoning into an unprecedented public-health event. The visible "polonium trail" became
the evidentiary substance. Institutional validation was strong but slow: warrants for Lugovoy (2007)
and Kovtun (2011), who remain in Russia; the Owen inquiry (established July 2014) reported **21 Jan
2016** that they killed him and that the FSB operation was "probably approved" by FSB head Patrushev
and President Putin; *Carter v. Russia* (no. 20914/07, **21 Sept 2021**) held Russia responsible.
Attention was mediated and diplomatic, not a mass-report cascade. Persistence: a physical radiological
trace, a named victim, a legal chain that outlived the event by 20 years. Collapse: Russian
non-cooperation, no extradition, no binding finding until 2021.

**Quantified (HPA, primary).** Urine from **753 people** processed (~500 in month one, +250 to
end-May 2007, +3 to Aug 2007); **139** above the 30 mBq d⁻¹ Reporting Level; doses for **36** at
1–6 mSv, **17** at ≥6 mSv, highest ≈**100 mSv**; **664 persons from 52 countries** flagged for
overseas follow-up. Police examined **60+ scenes**, made **40+ mutual legal assistance requests to
15+ countries**, with **200+ officers** involved. Inquiry sat 34 days, 30 days of evidence, 62 oral
witnesses. Note: the popular "40+ contaminated locations" is wrong — the report's verified figure is
**60+ scenes**.

**Copycat afterlife (organic → mediated).** No verified post-2006 "polonium" copycat cluster exists.
Verified later items: (a) **Feb 2026, Mohali, Punjab** — a bomb-threat email to Fortis Hospital
falsely claimed CM Bhagwant Mann would be poisoned "with polonium"; police traced it to Dhaka and the
poison claim was exposed; (b) **Vladimir Kara-Murza**, poisoned 2015 and 2017, with FBI records and
FSB-surveillance evidence indicating intentional poisoning, but **no public confirmation of polonium**;
(c) **Navalny, Aug 2020**, an OPCW-confirmed cholinesterase inhibitor, not radiological. Persistence of
the *trope* is real; persistence of a copycat *event* is not.

## 2. Padilla and the 2002–2005 US dirty-bomb apparatus (political → media → institutional)

Ambiguity was manufactured, not reduced. Padilla was arrested at Chicago O'Hare on **8 May 2002** on a
material-witness warrant; on **10 June 2002** Attorney General Ashcroft announced an al-Qaeda "dirty
bomb" plot and a likely Washington target. Official statements supplied the weapon's name; no
radiological charge ever followed. The grand jury returned an **11-count superseding indictment on
22 Nov 2005** (conspiracy to murder/kidnap/maim overseas; material support). Conviction **16 Aug
2007**; sentenced **17 years 4 months on 22 Jan 2008**; found too lenient on appeal and resentenced to
**21 years on 9 Sept 2014**. Validation inverted the amplification: by **June 2004** Justice was
describing the device as **uranium** wrapped in explosives, which physicists told CBS "cannot" be an
RDD. Persistence: the phrase and the named suspect. Collapse: no charged radiological offence plus
expert refutation. The parallel 2002–2005 build-out (portal monitors, Securing the Cities, TOPOFF 2,
12–16 May 2003, Chicago and Seattle) institutionalised vigilance that long outlived the plot claim.

## 3. Hoax radiological incidents (organic / mediated / political)

Verified exemplars of deliberate radiological hoaxing are sparser than the literature implies:

- **14–15 June 2017, Port of Charleston (Wando Welch terminal, SC).** A caller claimed the *Maersk
  Memphis* carried a "dirty bomb"; the terminal was shut Wednesday night and reopened Thursday within
  hours after scanning; one shift was missed; the FBI opened a false-threat investigation. Traced to a
  conspiracy-theory caller.
- **Feb–Mar 2010, Middleboro, Massachusetts.** Reported "radioactive" canisters declared a hoax.
- **Feb 2026, Mohali, Punjab.** Bomb threat plus false polonium-poisoning claim (case 1).
- **11 June 2026, the Pentagon.** An anthrax/hazmat *sensor* triggered a multi-hour lockdown and
  partial evacuation; air testing found no hazard. Organic false positive, same cascade shape.

Consequence profile is uniform: short evacuation, multi-agency search, no injury, rare prosecution,
almost no aggregate reporting. **No government or peer-reviewed tally of radiological hoaxes was
found.** The nearest quantitative series is the IAEA Incident and Trafficking Database, which counts
trafficking/possession, not hoaxes. Treat any "hoax wave" claim as unsupported.

## 4. The 2022 Russian "Ukrainian dirty bomb" narrative (adversarial → political → mediated → institutional)

Trigger: Russian setbacks in Kharkiv and Kherson. **23 Oct 2022** — Russian state media asserted
Ukraine was near completing a dirty bomb; Shoigu called US, UK, French and Turkish counterparts the
same day. **24 Oct** — Gerasimov called US/UK counterparts; Russia's UN ambassador wrote to the UN
Secretary-General calling Ukrainian use "nuclear terrorism"; Russia's NBC-defence chief briefed that
forces were preparing for a radioactive environment; Lavrov and Peskov insisted the threat was real.
**26 Oct** — Putin repeated the claim while overseeing the Grom nuclear exercise; Shoigu called India
and China. **28 Oct** — Polyansky issued the last explicit warning; the narrative then stopped
abruptly. Western counter was collective and fast: the P3 ministers rejected the claims as a pretext;
Stoltenberg called them "absurd"; State called them a pretext for escalation. The decisive
institutional counter came from inspection: at Ukraine's request, IAEA inspectors visited the
Institute for Nuclear Research (Kyiv), the Eastern Mining and Processing Plant (Zhovti Kody) and P.A.
Pivdennyi Machine-Building Plant (Dnipro), and on **3 Nov 2022** reported "no indications of
undeclared nuclear activities and materials", with unfettered access. CSIS (2024) lists five
non-exclusive motives and records that US officials admitted they did not know the narrative's
purpose. Persistence: fusion with real nuclear rhetoric and the Grom drill. Collapse: the Kherson
withdrawal, IAEA access, and the unexplained stop.

## 5. Zaporizhzhia NPP narratives, 2022–2026 (adversarial ↔ adversarial, mediated, institutional)

The IAEA Support and Assistance Mission reached ZNPP on **1 Sept 2022** and has been continuously
present since — the strongest institutional anchor in any case here. All six units are in cold
shutdown (IAEA welcomed this **13 Apr 2024**). The dominant recurring incident class is **loss of
off-site power**, forcing diesel-generator operation; UN News flagged renewed shelling risk from
28 Aug 2022 and further power losses into Oct 2025. **Arms Control Today (Sept 2026) reports the IAEA
counted 25 complete losses of off-site power since the start of the conflict, 13 of them in the four
months to August 2026**, after the plant went from ten off-site power lines to a single operational
330 kV connection (Ferosplavna-1) once the 750 kV Dniprovska line was lost. A drone struck a turbine
on 30 May 2026 — the IAEA called it the first military activity to affect the plant site directly
since April 2024 — and by 15 July 2026 more than two-thirds of off-site radiation monitoring stations
were not transmitting. Competing claims are structural and mutual: Russia attributes strikes to
Ukraine; Ukraine denies and attributes endangerment to Russian occupation and militarisation. Both
narratives feed state-aligned media and are then relayed by wires — the definitional case of
competing state claims sustaining ambiguity. What demonstrably worked: IAEA-brokered local ceasefires
allowing line repair, with off-site power restored in early Sept 2026. No radiological release or loss
of reactor cooling has occurred.

## 6. Syria: Ghouta 2013, the JIM, and Douma (adversarial → political → mediated → institutional)

**21 Aug 2013 Ghouta:** the UN Mission (Sellström) report **S/2013/553** (13 Sept 2013) found Sarin in
surface-to-surface rockets at Ein Tarma, Moadamiyah and Zamalka — "clear and convincing evidence" of
use, but its mandate **excluded attribution**. That mandate gap is the structural source of a decade
of sustained ambiguity. The US Government Assessment of **30 Aug 2013** asserted government
responsibility and >**1,400** dead; Russia advanced an alternative assessment blaming the opposition.
Competing national assessments, not evidence, drove the political crisis. Attribution came later and
in stages: OPCW Fact-Finding Mission chlorine findings (2014–2015); the OPCW-UN JIM (UNSC res. 2235
(2015)) attributed **Khan Sheikhoun (4 April 2017)** in S/2017/904 and **Ltamenah (March 2017)** in
S/2018/202. Russia vetoed JIM renewal in Nov 2017, ending the mechanism. The function moved to the
OPCW Investigation and Identification Team, which attributed Douma to the Syrian Arab Air Force; a
**fifth IIT report** appeared in Jan 2026. Douma added a second-order information operation — leaks, a
whistleblower dispute, and a "staged attack" narrative — corroding the OPCW's own credibility even as
attributions accumulated. The OPCW suspended Syria's rights in **2021**. After Assad's fall (Dec
2024), the Director-General visited Damascus in **Feb 2025**, the Secretariat deployed repeatedly, and
on **9 July 2026** the Executive Council reinstated Syria's rights by consensus (67 co-sponsors),
approving destruction-verification plans for Al Qutayfah and Homs. Ambiguity collapsed only when the
state sustaining it fell.

## 7. 2023–2026 state information operations (adversarial → mediated)

The pattern is pre-emptive attribution of an incident that has not happened. Verified currents:
(1) Russian messaging, amplified by RT and Lavrov, that Ukraine or its allies will stage a
radiological/nuclear provocation; (2) **2026** warnings by Lithuania (8 July) and by Poland and the
Baltics (10 Aug) that Russia may stage a drone "false flag" against NATO infrastructure using captured
Ukrainian drones; (3) 2026 Russian claims that Ukraine planned to start a Russia–UK war; (4)
continued nuclear-rhetoric signalling tracked by ISW. The May–June 2026 ZNPP strike exchanges are the
live radiological variant. Structural feature: the claim is calibrated to be unfalsifiable inside the
decision window, so the burden falls on third-party inspection (IAEA access) — the same mechanism that
defeated the Oct 2022 dirty-bomb narrative.

## UNSOURCED / UNCERTAIN

- **"40+ contaminated locations"** — wrong. Owen report text says **60+ scenes**; HPA gives no single location count.
- **Post-2006 "polonium copycat wave"** — **not established.** No aggregate study or verified cluster. Individual claims are single-source.
- **Kara-Murza and polonium** — **contested / unsupported.** Poisoning is well evidenced; the **agent is not publicly confirmed as polonium**. Do not merge with Litvinenko.
- **Padilla's 10 June 2002 Ashcroft text** — direct DOJ release not retrievable (archive numbering 404s); corroborated only by CBS/AP 2004 and PBS.
- **Padilla 2007/2008/2014 dates** — corroborated via aggregated headlines (NYT 17 Aug 2007; CBS 22 Jan 2008; NPR/CSM 9 Sept 2014); DOJ 2007–08 releases and 11th Circuit opinions not retrieved.
- **TOPOFF 2 participation/cost** (commonly "8,000+") — **unverified**; gao.gov, defense.gov, hsdl.org were blocked or 404.
- **Radiation portal monitor / Securing the Cities counts** — **unverified**; GAO-04-652, GAO-06-665, GAO-22-105094 all returned 403 and lacked Wayback snapshots. This is a genuine recall gap: GAO is the required source.
- **Charleston 2017** — event verified (FreightWaves full text plus NYT/VOA/WCIV/gCaptain headlines); no prosecution outcome verified; most original URLs dead.
- **ZNPP "24th off-site power loss"** — the Anadolu headline figure was **superseded and corrected**: the Arms Control Association's September 2026 report, citing the IAEA, gives **25 total losses** as of mid-August 2026.
