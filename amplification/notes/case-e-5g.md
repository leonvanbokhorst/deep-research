# Case E — 5G/health claims (non-biological physical exposure)

## Causal chain (150-350 words narrative)

The exposure here — radiofrequency (RF) electromagnetic fields — is real, measurable and ubiquitous, yet physically incapable of transmitting a virus. That gave the ambiguity its hook: a plausible physical mechanism ("radiation weakens immunity") bolted onto a pandemic with no visible cause.

The peer-reviewed trace (Langguth et al. 2022, *Int. J. Data Science and Analytics*) dates the first 5G–COVID tweet to **21 January 2020**. Full Fact also saw the claim emerge in the **second half of January 2020**, before the pandemic was declared, on a substrate built since at least mid-2018 (David Icke) from pre-existing anti-5G, anti-vaxx and wellness communities.

Early framing was mild: 5G "compromised immunity"; the virus was a worse cold. Under lockdown it radicalised into a **Cover-up narrative** — RF is lethal, "COVID-19 deaths" are really radiation injury, and the pandemic was invented to hide the rollout (Langguth et al.). Parallel strands called the virus a hoax and recast the "real reason" as vaccine/eugenics: Bill Gates implanting microchips and controlling populations via 5G (BBC, June 2020). Full Fact records a cruise-ship variant blaming onboard "radiation emitting technology".

Growth was platform-led, not press-led: GDELT analysis found traditional media "played essentially no role in the formation of this digital wildfire". Between **25 March and 2 April 2020**, after UK-sourced YouTube videos, daily 5G tweets **quadrupled**; arson began the weekend of **3 April 2020**.

## Key actors and amplification classification

**Mixed — overwhelmingly organic; commercial and celebrity/media vectors present; state-linked amplification unproven.**

- **Organic:** "Stop 5G International" networks, anti-5G and anti-vaxx/wellness accounts, local Facebook groups urging engineer harassment.
- **Media/celebrity:** David Icke (later deplatformed); Amanda Holden shared a petition blaming 5G for coronavirus symptoms; Anne-Marie and Eamonn Holmes amplified, prompting Ofcom guidance to ITV. Mainstream outlets had covered 5G fears uncritically pre-2020 (Full Fact).
- **Commercial:** "5G Bioshield" USB sticks at ~US$350; demonetising this was among the few effective interventions.
- **Political:** far-right actors "capitalised" on it (GNET, April 2020); US amplifiers skewed Trump/QAnon-adjacent, with no central direction.
- **Adversarial:** weak — RT America ran a 2019 anti-5G segment and Icke clips persisted on Russian platforms (BBC), but no coordinated Kremlin campaign was established.

## Persistence vs collapse factors

Collapse in the West followed the pandemic's end and platform bans: **YouTube, 5 April 2020**; **Twitter, 22 April 2020**; Facebook "aggressive steps". Langguth et al. found removal largely ineffective but demonetisation effective. Volume peaked 4 April 2020 and decayed through 2021, spiking around new attacks (New Zealand, June 2020; South Africa, January 2021).

Persistence was local and identity-linked: the UK, US, Canada, Australia and NZ kept low-level interest, while Nigeria, India and South Africa spiked then fell away. The constituency merged into anti-vax/mRNA and smart-meter/EHS networks — Full Fact notes 5G concern already overlapped with smart meters, street lights and baby monitors. In **west Belfast** it never subsided: **17 arson attacks since June 2023** (BBC, 2 July 2025).

## Institutional legacy (dates, attack counts, laws)

**Attacks.** 3 Apr 2020: ≥10 arson attacks across the UK, NZ and Netherlands, >20 more the next week, 6 in Canada two weeks later. **Mobile UK: ~50 masts by 15 April; 77 by 6 May 2020** (Business Insider); Langguth et al. corroborate **77 by 7 May** and **121 by 2 July 2020**. Mobile UK via Sky News (25 May): ~**90** (87 recorded) during lockdown.

**People.** BBC Newsbeat (23 Apr): the CWU and trade body logged ~**120 abuse cases**, including murder and stabbing threats. BT CEO Philip Jansen (21 Apr): **40 incidents** against BT staff; one Openreach engineer **stabbed**. Guardian (7 May): Openreach recorded ~**50 abuse incidents in April**. Langguth et al. cite **273 UK technician incidents**.

**Abroad.** Netherlands **22 arson + 3 attempted**; Ireland **3**; Cyprus **2**; Belgium/Italy/Sweden/Finland ≥**1** (ETNO/GSMA via POLITICO). New Zealand: **17 towers vandalised**. Australia: pre-emptive police liaison. Peru: **8 technicians kidnapped**, 10 June 2020.

**Convictions.** Michael Whitty, 3 years (Liverpool Crown Court, June 2020); two men charged **April 2026** over 2023–2025 west Belfast arson; Michael and Darren Clarke charged September 2025 (The Register).

**Institutions.** GOV.UK guidance (**6 May 2020**): "Viruses cannot travel on radio waves or mobile networks", citing Ofcom guidance and a joint EE/O2/Three/Vodafone statement; Ofcom's myth-clearing and vandalism statements; WHO's EMF programme; **ICNIRP 2020 RF guidelines (100 kHz–300 GHz)**, finding no established adverse effects below guideline levels (limits derive from thermal thresholds); IARC's unchanged 2011 **Group 2B** ("possibly carcinogenic") classification; and the WHO-commissioned 2024 review (Karipidis et al.) finding moderate-certainty evidence that mobile phone use **likely does not** increase glioma, meningioma, acoustic neuroma, pituitary, salivary-gland or paediatric brain tumours. The boundary to state precisely: **"no established effect", not "no effect"** — 2B persists on limited human evidence with acknowledged recall/selection bias and crude exposure assessment.

## COUNTEREXAMPLES

- **Never mass belief:** ~0.05% of COVID-19 tweets mentioned 5G; a May 2020 UK survey found 1.8% agreed *completely* that coronavirus is 5G radiation poisoning (>20% "at least a little").
- **Media did not create it:** press silence during formation; coverage followed the arson.
- **A real dispute exists:** IARC 2B is unresolved, and the 2024 review's authors flag weak exposure assessment — "settled science" overstates the other way.
- **Moderation barely worked:** removal failed; demonetisation and fraud enforcement worked better.

## UNSOURCED/UNCERTAIN

- **"20 engineers assaulted in April 2020"** — not verified; documented equivalents are ~40 (BT, 21 Apr), ~50 (Openreach, April), ~120 (CWU/trade body, 23 Apr) and 273 (total incidents). Likely a conflation with the 20 Easter mast attacks.
- **The "1982 5G patent" false claim** — no fact-check located; adjacent patent claims (2015 Lieber; 1991) are documented false (AAP FactCheck; USA Today).
- **A specific Diamond Princess–5G claim** — not found; only Full Fact's generic cruise-ship variant.
- **An NCSC statement** on 5G–COVID — claimed, not corroborated.
- **A dedicated UK law or mast-insurance regime** from the scare — not verified; prosecutions used existing arson/criminal-damage law.
- **Coordinated RT/Sputnik amplification** — not established.
- **EHS** is not a recognised diagnosis (WHO; Springer review).
