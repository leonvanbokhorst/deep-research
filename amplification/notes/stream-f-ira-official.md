# Stream F — IRA Reach Figures: Official / Primary Source Base

Research cut-off 25 Sep 2026. Defensive framing only. Grades: **A** primary/official · **B** peer-reviewed · **D** vendor/think-tank · **C** news. Labels: **DEMONSTRATED** · **CLAIMED** · **INFERRED**.
**Method:** `web_search` dead session-wide; discovery via Google News RSS + Bing RSS. `intelligence.senate.gov`/`dni.gov` 403 non-browser clients — all PDFs below text-verified via Wayback (`web.archive.org/web/<ts>id_/…`).

---

## 1. SSCI, *Russian Active Measures Campaigns and Interference in the 2016 U.S. Election* (A)

**Vol. 2, "Russia's Use of Social Media," 8 Oct 2019** — `intelligence.senate.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Report_Volume2.pdf` (403 live; verified Wayback `20191008172455`, 7,490,823 B, 80 pp. + TAG addenda). **Vol. 1** (25 Jul 2019, Wayback `20190725190002`) covers election infrastructure, **not** IRA reach.

**Cleanest primary inventory, Vol. 2 p. 77 (DEMONSTRATED):** "81 Facebook Pages, including approximately **61,500 unique Facebook organic posts** and **3,393 paid advertisements**; … nearly **116,000 Instagram posts across 133 Instagram accounts**; … approximately **10.4 million tweets across [3,]841 Twitter accounts**; and … approximately **1,100 YouTube videos (43 hours of video) across 17 account channels**."

**Authoritative self-caveat, same page (n. 297):** the Committee "has not attempted to make an independent determination as to the accuracy of the social media companies' internal investigations or the true provenance of the accounts," believing the data "almost certainly [is] not the entirety" of IRA activity; p. 3 — "the full scope of this activity remains unknown." **Every figure is a floor.**

**Findings (pp. 4–8):** ads "were **not key**"; ~**$100,000** over two years vs operating costs ~**$1.25M/month** (n. 17); no group targeted more than **African-Americans** (>66% of ad content race-related; **96%** of YouTube content race/police-focused). "**Tens of millions**" reached = CLAIMED, unquantified.

**Vols. 3–5 retrieved and text-verified** (4.5 / 28.2 / 52.4 MB; Vol. 5 = 18 Aug 2020). **Negative finding: they add no new IRA reach numbers and do not revise Vol. 2.**

## 2. Twitter/X (A)

**"Update on Twitter's review of the 2016 US election," 19 Jan 2018** (blog dead; verified via Wayback): **3,814** IRA-linked accounts + **1,062** more; those accounts "posted **175,993 Tweets**, approximately **8.4%** of which were election-related"; **677,775** US users emailed; "Approximately **1.4 million people** have now received a notification"; **50,258** Russian-linked automated accounts = **0.016%** of Twitter accounts.

**"3,841 / ~10M tweets" is primary at SSCI Vol. 2 p. 77** (10.4M tweets / 3,841 accounts) and n. 298 (public archive released 17 Oct 2018) — better provenance than any blog. **Conflict to state:** Twitter told SSCI "nearly **8.5 million** tweets … **72 million** engagements on … original content," while the delivered dataset held **10.4M** tweets; difference is originals (NK: "~10.4 million … of which ~**6 million were original**") vs retweets. **288M views, 1–15 Nov 2016** = CLAIMED (via Eady et al. fn. 23; underlying statement not retrieved).

## 3. Facebook/Meta (A) — **resolves reach-vs-impressions**

**6 Sept 2017** "An Update On Information Operations On Facebook" (about.fb.com, live): ~**$100,000**, June 2015–May 2017, "roughly **3,000 ads**," about **470** inauthentic accounts/Pages. **2 Oct 2017** "Hard Questions: Russian Ads Delivered to Congress" (live): "An estimated **10 million people in the US** saw the ads"; "**5% appeared on Instagram** … **About $6,700** was spent on these ads."

**Decisive document — Facebook's QFR responses to SSCI (Colin Stretch), 8 Jan 2018** (`…/Facebook Response to Committee QFRs.pdf`, verified Wayback, 525,282 B):
> "**Reach** (the number of people who saw a story at least once): We estimate **11.4 million people in the US** saw at least one of these ads between 2015 and 2017." "**44%** of total ad impressions [were] before the US election … **56%** after." "Roughly **25%** of the ads were never shown to anyone."
> "roughly **29 million people were served content in their News Feeds directly** from the IRA's **80,000 posts** over the two years. … **three times more people may have been exposed** to a story that originated from the Russian operation. Our best estimate is that approximately **126 million people may have been served content** from a Page associated with the IRA … about **0.004% of content in News Feed**, or approximately **1 out of 23,000 pieces**."
> "We also deleted roughly **170 Instagram accounts that posted about 120,000 pieces** of content."

**Verdict: 126M is REACH (potential exposure), not impressions, and is INFERRED** — 29M direct × a 3× share/like multiplier, explicitly hedged ("may have been served"). Facebook's counts **conflict** with SSCI (80,000 posts vs 61,500; 170 IG accounts/120,000 posts vs 133/116,000). Note the **10M → 11.4M ad-reach revision**. Facebook events: ≥**130** events, **13** pages, **338,300** accounts engaged, **62,500** intended attendees, **25,800** interested (Vol. 2 p. 46).

## 4. Instagram (A, unresolved dispute)

Vol. 2 pp. 48–50: **133 accounts, >116,000 posts, 3.3M followers, 187M total engagements** — "the most effective tool used by the IRA." Verbatim: "Facebook representatives indicated that Instagram content **reached just 20 million users** … the published findings of the working group led by TAG researcher Renee DiResta **contest that 'the Instagram number is likely lower than it should be'**." **Unresolved official disagreement.** `@Blackstagram_`: 300,000+ followers, 28M+ interactions.

## 5. New Knowledge, *The Tactics & Tropes of the Internet Research Agency* (D)

**17 Dec 2018.** Authors (title page, verified): DiResta, Shaffer, Ruppel, Sullivan, Matney, Fox (New Knowledge); Albright (Tow Center, Columbia); Johnson (Canfield Research). Original URL dead (firm → Yonder). **Verified copy:** `web.archive.org/web/20240913042739id_/https://cdn2.hubspot.net/hubfs/4326998/ira-report-rebrand_FinalJ14.pdf` (13,080,729 B). Same authors/date at Vol. 2 n. 4.

**Numbers (CLAIMED; mostly restatements of platform data):** "reached **126 million** people on Facebook, at least **20 million** users on Instagram, **1.4 million** users on Twitter, and uploaded over **1,000** videos to YouTube"; budget "exceeded **$25 million USD**," 2017 operating budget **$12.2M**; ~**77M** FB / ~**187M** IG / ~**73M** Twitter engagements; total FB+IG **263,769,228**; Twitter original content **72,801,807**.

**Method caveats (verbatim):** "The platforms **didn't include methodology for identifying the accounts; we are assuming**…"; "Since Facebook did not provide data about any sockpuppet accounts … or the existence of 'fake Likes' … **we are operating under the assumption that this engagement was from real people**"; on Instagram, "it is **possible that the IRA's Instagram engagement was the result of click farms**"; "**a couple of real accounts were misidentified as Russian**, while other IRA were missed." **Grade D despite SSCI sponsorship: vendor analysis of a company-curated dataset, attribution and engagement authenticity assumed, not verified.**

## 6. OII CompProp — Howard, Ganesh, Liotsiou, Kelly, François, *The IRA, Social Media and Political Polarization in the US, 2012–2018* (Dec 2018) (D)

PDF (venue page dead): **verified at the mirror SSCI cites** — `int.nyt.com/data/documenthelper/534-oxford-russia-internet-research-agency/c6588b4a7b940c551c38/optimized/full.pdf` (1,260,183 B), Vol. 2 n. 187.

**Numbers:** "reached **tens of millions**"; "**Over 30 million users, between 2015 and 2017, shared** the IRA's Facebook and Instagram posts"; organic FB content "shared … just under **31 million** times, liked almost **39 million** times, … almost **5.4 million** emoji reactions … almost **3.5 million comments**"; Instagram "almost **185 million likes** … about **4 million** comments." Accounts: **3,841 Twitter; 76 FB ad accounts; 81 FB pages; 133 Instagram.** **Load-bearing concentration claim:** "the **top twenty most liked pages received 99%** of all audience engagement" (elsewhere "**99.6%**"); "Forty [Instagram] pages received **99% of all likes**." **Caveat:** ads analysis "is based on a **sample of 3,233 ads (91.9% completion)**"; "**Google chose not to disclose any account data** on ads, YouTube, or Google+."

## 7. Eady, Paskhalis, Zilinsky, Bonneau, Nagler & Tucker (2023), *Nature Communications* 14:62 (B)

DOI **10.1038/s41467-022-35576-9**, PMC9829855, published 6 Jan 2023; full text verified via PMC (nature.com redirects to an IdP). YouGov panel, n=1,496.

- "only **1% of users accounted for 70% of exposures**"; "only **10% of respondents … account for 98%**"; "**1% of Russian accounts** account[ed] for **89%** of the content in individuals' timelines."
- Median weekly exposure in the final month = **0**; ~**4** IRA posts/day vs ~**106** from national news media and ~**35** from politicians (**25×** and **9×**).
- Exposure concentrated among **strong Republicans** (~9× Democrats/Independents).
- **Null effects:** −0.18 pp change in Trump vote (90% CI −1.15, 0.78); "no evidence of a meaningful relationship" with attitudes, polarization or vote.
- **INFERRED aggregate:** "**32 million Americans were potentially exposed**" (0.63 × 51M; Pew Twitter penetration 21%; Census 2016 adult pop. 244,807,000). Platform figures are "**not comparable**."

**Source of the "small share of users received the bulk of exposure" claim; strongest corrective to reach-only framing.**

## 8. Post-2019 official follow-up (A) — quantitatively empty

- **ODNI ICA, "Assessing Russian Activities and Intentions in Recent US Elections," 6 Jan 2017** (`dni.gov/files/documents/ICA_2017_01.pdf`): **no quantified reach claims**; Vol. 4 confirms social-media analysis was handled separately.
- **State Dept GEC, *Russia's Pillars of Disinformation and Propaganda Ecosystem*, Aug 2020** — **verified** (11,859,765 B): `2017-2021.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Pillars-of-Russia's-Disinformation-and-Propaganda-Ecosystem_08-04-20.pdf` (live state.gov 403s). ~40 pp., **qualitative only, zero reach numbers** — an ecosystem map, not a measurement.
- **ODNI Annual Threat Assessments, verified PDFs.** **ATA 2024** (5 Feb 2024): "**Malign Influence Operations**" — "Moscow views U.S. elections as opportunities and has conducted influence operations for decades and as recently as the U.S. midterm elections in 2022"; actors "may use new technologies, such as **generative AI**." **ATA 2025** (Mar 2025): Russia will "deploy … disinformation … influence operations"; "using **AI to create highly-capable deepfakes**." **ATA 2026** (Mar 2026, cut-off 14 Mar 2026; verified Wayback 2,088,258 B): "Russia's gray zone tools include cyber attacks, **disinformation and influence operations**, energy market manipulation, military intimidation, and sabotage."
- **No ATA 2021–2026 repeats or revises any IRA reach figure. The official quantitative record stops at SSCI Vol. 2 (2019).**
- **DOJ, *US v. Internet Research Agency LLC et al.*, No. 1:18-cr-00032-DLF (D.D.C.), 16 Feb 2018** — cited throughout Vol. 2 (nn. 7, 14, 229) for the ~$1.25M/month budget and the SEO department. Not independently retrieved; confidence medium on URL, high on content.

## 9. Unreconciled numbers — flag every one

**126M FB "reached"** = REACH not impressions; 29M × 3 multiplier; **INFERRED** (FB QFR Jan 2018). **11.4M saw an ad**, but FB first said **10M** (Oct 2017). **FB QFR vs SSCI counts conflict:** 80,000 posts vs 61,500; 170 IG accounts/120,000 posts vs 133/116,000. **20M Instagram reach** (Facebook testimony) **contested by New Knowledge**; unresolved. **187M IG engagements** CLAIMED; NK flags possible click-farm inflation. **3,814 accounts / 175,993 tweets** (Twitter Jan 2018) superseded by **3,841 / 10.4M** (SSCI p. 77). **"8.5M" vs "10.4M" tweets** = originals vs total; state both. **1.4M notified (677,775 US)** DEMONSTRATED. **288M views** CLAIMED, 1–15 Nov 2016 only, likely understated. **32M exposed (Twitter)** INFERRED (Eady 2023). **"Tens of millions"** CLAIMED, unquantified (SSCI Vol. 2).

**Cross-cutting caveats:** every figure rests on **platform self-investigation with unpublished method** (NK: "we are assuming"); the Committee **never verified attribution**; platforms had incentives to bound their own exposure; ad data are sample-based; and **reach ≠ exposure ≠ effect** — Eady finds null effects and extreme concentration.

## 10. Could NOT verify (low confidence — re-check before use)

1. **Twitter's Oct 2018 blog text** ("more than 10 million Tweets, 3,841 accounts") — absent from Wayback at every path tried; **numbers remain grade A** via SSCI Vol. 2 p. 77 and n. 298.
2. **Twitter's 288M-views statement** — not retrieved in primary form.
3. **YouTube/Google specifics** — the common **1,108 videos / 43 h / ~309,000 US views / 18 channels** bundle **conflicts with SSCI** ("more than **1,100** videos, or 43 hours … **17** YouTube channels"; **$4,700** search+display) and is untraceable to any primary Google document (Oct 2017 `blog.google` post 404s, unarchived) → **C-grade, unverified**. SSCI separately notes **$53,000** Russian-*connected* (not Kremlin-attributed) ad spend — a different figure, often conflated.
4. **Gmail ads** — Vol. 2 says Gmail accounts were used "to establish accounts on both Facebook and Twitter," **not** that the IRA bought Gmail ads. Any Gmail-ad-spend claim is **unsupported**.
5. **Reddit** — **944** suspicious accounts, ~**14,000** posts, **662** zero-karma; judged "unsuccessful in getting any traction" (pp. 59–60). **Tumblr** — **84** accounts, ~**100,000** posts, **11.7M unique US users**, nearly **30M globally** (pp. 60–61). Both grade A, single-source platform self-report, never replicated.
6. **New Knowledge page numbers** — archived copy paginates differently; cite by section ("Key Takeaways / Statistical Highlights"), not page.
