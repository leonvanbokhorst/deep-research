## (a1) 1982 Chicago Tylenol cyanide murders — mechanism ambiguity → permanent regulation

29 Sep 1982: Mary Kellerman (12) died after Extra-Strength Tylenol; six more followed. The tainted capsules came from **two different plants (Pennsylvania, Texas)**, proving post-shelf insertion. The *fact* was certain; the *actor and insertion mechanism* never were. The recall widened from one lot to all Chicago-area extra-strength capsules, then **nationwide on 5 Oct 1982: ~31 million bottles, retail value >US$100M (~$334M in 2025 dollars)**. J&J halted output and advertising; share fell **35% → 8%**, recovering within a year on a triple-sealed relaunch. **Copycats:** hundreds of incidents; 1986 Diane Elsroth (cyanide Tylenol), the Excedrin case (Stella Nickell, 90 years), the **Encaprin hoax** that forced a real recall and permanent withdrawal, and 1991 Sudafed. Halloween 1982 US candy sales fell **>20%** for a threat never documented, and nobody was ever charged with the murders.

**Amplification:** criminal act → saturation media → consumer self-organising → political demand. **Persistence:** the mechanism was invisible to consumers but the fix could be material (a seal), not causal — ambiguity was designed away, not resolved. **Legacy:** tamper-evident packaging across pharma, food and consumer goods; **Federal Anti-Tampering Act 1983 (18 U.S.C. §1365)**; FDA final rule on tamper-evident OTC packaging, **63 FR 59463 (4 Nov 1998, PMID 10187120)**; powder-filled capsules retired; J&J settled all wrongful-death suits in **1991**. Sept 2026: Idaho detectives named Mathew Francis Betkouski ("Unknown Wanderer", cyanide suicide Dec 1982) as a new lead — explicitly **not** a suspect.

## (a2) 2025–26 acetaminophen/autism — contested causal claim → political but not scientific consensus

**22 Sep 2025:** White House/HHS/FDA event; the President urged pregnant women to avoid Tylenol. FDA said it had *initiated* a pregnancy label change and written to physicians — while stating in the same document that **"a causal relationship has not been established."** Two citizen petitions followed (ICAN, same day; a second in Nov 2025 extending to young children); Kenvue and the Consumer Healthcare Products Association opposed both as bypassing the monograph process. ACOG's Steven Fleischman: *"not backed by the full body of scientific evidence."*

**Evidence asymmetry.** HHS-cited work was small and indirect (an 8,856-child cohort measuring **ADHD only** by recalled self-report; a 996-pair birth cohort). Against it: a **JAMA Internal Medicine** analysis (June 2026) of **708,020 Hong Kong mother–child pairs**, sibling-matched to 124,333 (autism) and 97,285 (ADHD), found **HR 1.00 and 1.01** with no trimester or dose effect; a 2024 Swedish sibling analysis of ~2.5m children agreed. Sibling-controlled evidence now exceeds **2.7m children**; it tests whether the association survives removal of shared family confounding, and it does not.

**Amplification:** political (White House) plus adversarial/legal (plaintiff bar), commercial and media; the *scientific* signal ran the other way and still lost the news cycle. **Behaviour:** *Lancet* found ED acetaminophen orders for pregnant patients **fell ~10%**; leucovorin prescribing for ages 5–17 **rose 71%** (elsewhere sevenfold; ~**50,000 US children**).

**Persistence:** survives because it is legally actionable and politically owned — refuted scientifically, not institutionally. The **label never changed**: as of Sept 2026 the Tylenol Regular Strength record is **v13, revised 27 May 2026**, still saying only "ask a health professional before use." Monograph M013 requires a proposed order, comment and final order; FDA has issued no neurodevelopmental order. **Litigation:** Dec 2024 Judge Denise Cote (SDNY) dismissed **500+** suits, excluding plaintiffs' experts; **13 Jul 2026** the Second Circuit (Judge Guido Calabresi, unanimous) **reversed**, reviving them while stressing it was not deciding causation; **11 Sep 2026** Kenvue and pharmacy chains sought rehearing. FDA approved leucovorin on **10 Mar 2026 for cerebral folate deficiency — not autism**.

## (b1) 2018 Australian strawberry needle crisis — real origin, hoax copycats, no conviction

Sept 2018: needles found in strawberries in Queensland, then Victoria, NSW, ACT, South Australia, and in exported fruit in New Zealand. Copycats within days reported needles in **apples, bananas and grapes**; the scare reached six states. **Outcome:** former Queensland farm supervisor **My Ut Trinh** was charged in Nov 2018 with seven counts, but prosecutors **dropped all charges in July 2021** — no conviction, no attribution. The original contamination was real; the wave was largely opportunistic or hoax.

**Response:** supermarkets pulled sewing needles from shelves; growers installed metal detectors; lobbyists sought a **A$6m** grower bailout; a Queensland inquiry reported in **Feb 2019** that authorities "could do better". It was framed as a **~A$500m industry derailed by the smallest of objects**, with millions wiped from farm-gate value. **Amplification:** media-led and strongly mimetic; adversarial in origin, commercial in consequence. **Persistence:** peaked ~2 weeks, then collapsed once coverage turned to hoaxes and the prosecution failed. Legacy is operational (metal detection, tamper-evident punnets), **not** institutional.

## (b2) 2026 HiPP baby-food rat-poison extortion — confirmed tampering, contained by recall

**17–19 Apr 2026:** a jar of HiPP "Karotten mit Kartoffeln" (190g) bought at a Spar store in Schützen am Gebirge, Burgenland, Austria tested **positive for rat poison** (anticoagulant). HiPP had already withdrawn its **entire jar range from Spar Austria** as a precaution. Austrian Ages confirmed an extortion attempt; one Spar in Eisenstadt and **two Tesco stores in Czechia** were affected, with further jars in **Slovakia**. By May, **five jars** had been recovered with a sixth sought. Police opened investigations for intentional common endangerment, attempted grievous bodily harm and attempted extortion.

A **39-year-old former HiPP employee** at an Austrian site was arrested 2–3 May 2026 and remains in pre-trial detention (extended 14–15 Sept 2026); he denies the charges. HiPP said Germany and unaffected routes were not involved and published tamper indicators (white sticker with red circle on the base, broken vacuum seal, spoiled smell). **Precedent:** the **Sept 2017** Bodensee/Ravensburg baby-food poisoning extortion ended in arrest on 30 Sep 2017 and a **12.5-year sentence** from Landgericht Ravensburg on **22 Oct 2018** — a template that made the 2026 response fast and bounded. **Amplification:** adversarial → commercial precaution → national media. **Persistence:** low; containment worked, so no policy change followed.

## (c) "Wind turbine syndrome" — no mechanism, yet durable in planning law

**Claim cluster:** attributed to infrasound and "vibroacoustic disease" — sleep disruption, headache, dizziness, nausea, tinnitus, cognitive loss, with claims reaching cancer, autism and death. Popularised by **Nina Pierpont's 2009 self-published *Wind Turbine Syndrome*** (case series near Mars Hill, NY); "vibroacoustic disease" was imported from a small Portuguese research programme.

**Evidence:** **25+ reviews since 2003** find no consistent causal evidence. **Chapman & St George (2013)** showed the VAD "factoid" traced to a single unreplicated study recycled through cyberspace, and **Chapman, St George, Waller & Cakic (2013, PLoS ONE)** found Australian complaints **do not match the establishment or distribution of turbines**, supporting "communicated disease". **Crichton et al. (2014)** demonstrated the **nocebo-expectation** effect experimentally: expectations, not exposure, drove symptoms. Systematic reviews from 2014, 2015, 2021 and 2023 converge on noise *annoyance* moderated by attitude, visibility and economic benefit, not disease. Australia's **NHMRC review and statement (Feb 2015)** found no direct evidence of harm; March 2025 studies again found effects no worse than road-traffic noise.

**Amplification:** advocacy-led and **astroturfed** — the **Waubra Foundation**, an anti-wind "health promotion" charity linked to fossil-fuel funding, was a principal vector and lost its health-promotion charity status. **Persistence:** *persists* despite refutation because the payoff is procedural — a health complaint is a planning objection, and an objection is a delay. The symptom cluster is self-reported, non-specific and unfalsifiable: the structure most resistant to collapse. An April 2026 revival push, partly routed through the Australian Senate, was reported by trade press as a "scientific dead end." **Legacy:** durable in *planning law*, not medicine — setback distances and moratoria (Victoria's 2 km regime; similar restrictions in Australia, Denmark, Germany and the UK) plus French nuisance litigation. The syndrome appears in no international disease classification.

## (d1) "Havana syndrome"/anomalous health incidents — political durability without a disease

2016–17 Havana onset of perceived directional sound plus neurological symptoms in US and Canadian diplomats. A 2018 UPenn/JAMA study claimed widespread brain-network injury; it was criticised in the same journal for absent controls, baseline and blinding. **JASON (2018)** concluded the recorded sounds were "most likely" the **Indies short-tailed cricket** and that no plausible single energy source could produce both the recordings and the medical effects. The FBI assessed mass psychogenic illness and found no evidence of a sonic attack. **ODNI, March 2023** (seven agencies): *"no credible evidence that a foreign adversary has a weapon or collection device that is causing AHIs."* **NIH (2024, 86 patients)** found no MRI-detectable brain injury and **failed to replicate** UPenn; NIH cancelled the studies in Sept 2024 after a participant alleged CIA coercion.

**Amplification:** mixed and mutually reinforcing — media, congressional oversight, patient advocacy, unresolvable adversarial ambiguity. Case counts grew from 26 (2017) to >1,000 (early 2022) as the label spread: the signature of a socially transmitted category. **Persistence:** *highly durable*, because the claim never needed a mechanism — compensation and care could be justified by reports alone. **GAO (July 2024):** 334 people qualified for military health-system care. In **March–June 2026** spy chiefs agreed the Biden-era assessments should be retracted and outgoing DNI **Tulsi Gabbard rescinded two ODNI assessments on 11 June 2026**, while *60 Minutes* aired a "CIA cover-up" claim. The fight is now about institutional integrity, not physics.

## (d2) Electromagnetic hypersensitivity — durable accommodation, no accepted diagnosis

EHS has **no case definition, no clinical guideline and no diagnostic test**; WHO and ICNIRP conclude there is no established causal link between EMF exposure and the reported symptoms, and double-blind provocation studies consistently fail to show that self-identified EHS individuals can detect exposure. It nonetheless won **durable institutional accommodation without medical acceptance**: Sweden treats it as a functional impairment rather than a disease, and a **California appellate court held in 2021 that EHS may qualify as a disability under FEHA**. Its legacy attached to *reported suffering*, not a validated mechanism — structurally identical to Havana syndrome, much older and quieter.

## (d3) 2024–25 DRC "Disease X" (Kwango/Panzi) — ambiguity that collapsed fast

Dec 2024: reports of an unidentified febrile illness in the **Panzi health zone, Kwango province, DRC**, with death tolls reported between **67 and 143**. WHO deployed experts on 6 Dec 2024 and **initial samples tested positive for malaria** within days. A **Nature Medicine 2026** analysis attributed the outbreak to **known pathogens — principally malaria and respiratory infection — amplified by acute malnutrition and low vaccination coverage**. No new agency or statute followed; the clearest *collapse* case, with ambiguity resolved by routine laboratory work in weeks.

## COUNTEREXAMPLES — scares that conspicuously failed to take hold

- **1982 Halloween poisoned candy.** Never documented, yet cut US candy sales >20% that October; with no regulatory fix available it burned out in one season.
- **EHS.** Decades of advocacy won accommodations but **never a disease classification**; controlled provocation studies repeatedly falsified detection.
- **Wind turbine syndrome.** 25+ negative reviews over two decades; it survived only by relocating into planning law, and the April 2026 revival was reported as a "scientific dead end."
- **DRC "Disease X" (2024–25).** Unknown pathogen, high child mortality and weak surveillance still collapsed to malaria/malnutrition within weeks, because the diagnostic pathway was fast and the claim falsifiable.
- **Metal/glass-in-food recalls (2025–26).** Verified at the plant, so nothing ambiguous remains to amplify; high volume, near-zero political durability.

**Why scares fail:** the threat is falsifiable by simple observation, the fix is a design change rather than a causal finding, the institutional response is bounded, or no litigation/compensation payoff attaches.
