## A. ACTIVE MEASURES (Soviet practice)

### A1. The term and its canonical Western definition — **H**

**Origin.** Russian *активные мероприятия / aktivnyye meropriyatiya*; term dates to the 1920s; run by **Service A of the KGB First Chief Directorate**, taught at the Andropov Institute (Yasenevo). Western analysts designate it "active measures" / "executive action". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_measures **H** on structure.

**Canonical definition and the propaganda distinction.** U.S. Department of State, *Soviet Influence Activities: A Report on Active Measures and Propaganda, 1986–87* (Aug 1987), "Definition" (https://archive.org/details/dos-report_s-12so-8-12), verbatim: active measures is "a literal translation from Russian, *aktivnyye meropriyatiya*, which denotes **covert or deceptive operations conducted in support of Soviet foreign policy**… distinct both from espionage and counterintelligence, and from traditional diplomatic and informational activities. The goal… is to influence opinions and/or actions… **Deception is the essence**." **Propaganda** is "information that reflects the perceptions or perspectives of a government"; active measures are "deceptive operations… often covert". A forgery is an active measure; a state news agency *repeating* it is propaganda used to sustain it. **H** (primary doc, read in full).

### A2. Mitrokhin Archive — **origin/scope H; individual allegations M; page specifics UNVERIFIED**

**Canonical sources.** Vasili Mitrokhin hand-copied KGB First Chief Directorate files 1972–84 (~25,000 pp, six trunks, defected 1992 after the CIA dismissed the material as possible fakes). Published by **Christopher Andrew & Vasili Mitrokhin**: *The Sword and the Shield* (Basic Books, 1999), https://archive.org/details/swordshieldmitro00andr; *The World Was Going Our Way* (Basic Books, 2005), https://archive.org/details/worldwasgoingour0000andr. Finding aid: Wilson Center Digital Archive, https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/topics/mitrokhin-archive. **H**.

**What it documents.** Industrial-scale **forgeries**; front organisations; agents of influence; penetration of churches/parties; assassination plots; **sabotage preparations** (wartime weapons caches). Two items matter for H1: (i) **Operation PANDORA** — the KGB ordered a delayed-action explosive planted in "the Negro section of New York" (preferred target a Black college), then anonymous calls blaming the **Jewish Defense League** (quoted at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_PANDORA); (ii) the AIDS story was "officially disowned" in Aug 1987. **M** on each (handwritten notes only; no originals; scholarly scepticism).
**Page numbers: UNVERIFIED** (restricted search-inside).

### A3. Thomas Rid, *Active Measures* (2020) — **argument H; "reflexive" framing M**

**Canonical.** Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2020; https://archive.org/details/activemeasuresse0000ridt. Rid published **~90 of his own primary sources, linked to the endnotes**, at https://archive.org/details/active-measures — the best first stop. Reviews read: Small Wars Journal (2025, https://smallwarsjournal.com/2025/07/18/disinformation-as-a-strategic-weapon-book-review/) and *American Diplomacy* (2020, https://americandiplomacy.web.unc.edu/2020/11/active-measures/); also CIA *Studies in Intelligence* 64(1), JSTOR 27087774, TLS, RAF ASPR. Foreign Affairs review exists but its body is paywalled (**UNVERIFIED**).

**What it claims.** (1) **Periodisation = FOUR big waves, each a generation apart** (Rid, p. 6, quoted in SWJ), tracing disinformation from the 1920s through WWII/early Cold War and the 1970s–80s to the digital era. **The "five waves" framing in the brief is not supported by any reachable review; the book says four.** **H**. (2) Continuity, not novelty: today's campaigns are continuous with Soviet active measures. (3) Disinformation is **not merely false**: "some of the most vicious and effective active measures… were designed to deliver entirely accurate information" (p. 10); "almost all disinformation operations are, in fact, imperfect by design" (p. 9); its goal is to "engineer division… emotion over analysis". **H**.

**The "self-fulfilling / reflexive" dynamic.** Rid's own formulation (Hewlett Foundation interview, 11 Aug 2020, https://hewlett.org/the-secret-history-of-disinformation-and-political-warfare-five-questions-for-thomas-rid/) is the key passage for H1: the internet has made active measures **"more active and less measured"**, because it is "even harder than it was in the past to **disentangle the actual effects of a campaign from the perception of their effect**… If you're exacerbating an already existing conflict or trend, how much of it was really you're doing?" — and because "some of the **value-creation of disinformation [is] outsourced to the victim itself**, to activists and researchers and journalists who go in and report out the gems." TLS review: active measures are now "more direct, noisier, faster — and less effective." **H** on the quote; **M** on calling this a formal theory of reflexivity (Rid frames it as *attribution difficulty and outsourced amplification*, not reflexive control).

### A4. Cases: forgeries, a real physical incident, and one ghost — **M–H**

- **Operation Denver / "INFEKTION" (AIDS, 1983–87).** Canonical: Wilson Center (Selvage & Nehring, https://www.wilsoncenter.org/blog-post/operation-denver-kgb-and-stasi-disinformation-regarding-aids); the 7 Sep 1985 KGB telegram to Bulgarian State Security (https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/document/208946); Boghardt, *Studies in Intelligence* 53(4). Sequence: anonymous "American scientist" letter → *Patriot* (India), July 1983 → *Literaturnaya Gazeta*, Oct 1985 → Segal report → Stasi HVA/X "Operation DENVER" plan (1986). Reached 80+ countries in 30+ languages; 15% of Americans in 1992 believed AIDS was lab-made. **H**.
- **The 1980s nuclear-freeze forgery = the "Haig–Luns letter."** U.S. State Dept, *Soviet Active Measures: Focus on Forgeries* (April 1983), Annex — https://archive.org/details/USDepartmentOfStateSovietActiveMeasuresFocusOnForgeries. A professionally made forged 1979 letter attributed to NATO SACEUR **Gen. Alexander Haig**, discussing a "possible nuclear first strike" and "action of a sensitive nature" to "jolt the faint hearted" in Europe; tell-tale error "Dear Joseph" vs Haig's habitual "Dear Joe." Purpose: "to **stimulate the nuclear disarmament campaign** by suggesting a Haig–Luns collusion against opponents to the modernization of nuclear forces in Europe." Surfaced in the Belgian leftist weekly *De Nieuwe*, 22 April 1982, then on Belgian TV/radio and in the Luxembourg Communist paper — timed to spring 1982 anti-nuclear demonstrations. **H** (primary doc).
- **The "Miami virus": UNVERIFIED.** No reachable source attests a Soviet active measure by that name (Wikipedia full-text `insource:"Miami virus"` = zero; Google News = zero). The documented AIDS-disinformation geography is **Fort Detrick, Zaire, Haiti, Pakistan**, plus "AIDS-oiled condoms." Treat the phrase as a garbled reference until a source is produced.
- **KAL 007 (1 Sep 1983) — real physical incident + deliberate disinformation.** The shoot-down was real and Soviet; the *meaning* was engineered. Politburo line: a deliberate US "provocation"/MASINT spy mission; TASS/Novosti spread the claim that Nixon had been booked but the CIA warned him off. Decisively, the Ustinov–Chebrikov memo (Dec 1983) says simulated search operations were run "**in order to dis-inform the U.S. and Japan**", and the flight recorders were concealed for nine years. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Air_Lines_Flight_007 (with primary citations); ICAO 1993 report. **H**.
- **Physical incidents — explicit verdict.** Active measures **did** include physical violence in principle (assassinations; wartime sabotage caches; the PANDORA explosive plot). But the canonical *executed, documented* cases are **informational or forgeries — forgeries about physical things**, not exploitation of a genuinely ambiguous real event, and physical violence ran as a **separate** assassination/sabotage line. **M**.

**Fit: Partial.** It supplies state intent, scale and a documented failure/blowback record. **Where it does NOT fit:** the unit is a **forged artefact with a provenance** (letter, pamphlet, planted story), so the counter is provenance analysis; H1's unit is **an unresolvable real event needing no forgery**, where the counter is decision discipline and base-rate literacy. Active measures were also **labour-intensive and state-owned**; H1's novelty is cheap autonomous scale. And active measures' physical violence was *carried out by the state*, never *outsourced to the target's own reaction*.

## B. HYBRID WARFARE

### B1. Frank Hoffman (2007) — **H**

**Canonical.** Frank G. Hoffman, *Conflict in the 21st Century: The Rise of Hybrid Wars*, Potomac Institute for Policy Studies / CETO, Dec 2007 — https://www.potomacinstitute.org/images/stories/publications/potomac_hybridwar_0108.pdf (read in full). **Definition (Executive Summary/Introduction):** "Hybrid threats incorporate a full range of different modes of warfare including conventional capabilities, irregular tactics and formations, terrorist acts including indiscriminate violence and coercion, and criminal disorder. Hybrid Wars can be conducted by both states and a variety of non-state actors… generally operationally and tactically directed and coordinated within the main battlespace to achieve **synergistic effects in the physical and psychological dimensions** of conflict." Hoffman credits first use to Robert G. Walker's 1998 thesis and to Mattis/Hoffman, USNI *Proceedings*, Nov 2005.
**Deliberate vs emergent: deliberate** (a state/non-state actor fuses modes by design). **Fit: Poor.** Hoffman has no amplification mechanism, no threshold concept, no role for a reactive population; and "hybrid" has become an unfalsifiable umbrella (below).

### B2. Gerasimov 2013 and the "Gerasimov Doctrine" — **H that the label is a Western invention**

**Canonical.** V. Gerasimov, "Ценность науки в предвидении" (*The Value of Science Is in the Foresight*), *Voenno-promyshlennyi kur'er / Military-Industrial Courier* No. 8 (476), 27 Feb 2013 — https://vpk-news.ru/articles/14632. English: Rob Coalson's RFE/RL translation, reproduced with commentary in Galeotti's blog (below); *Military Review*, Jan–Feb 2016. **H**.

**What Gerasimov actually wrote.** "The very 'rules of war' have changed. **The role of nonmilitary means of achieving political and strategic goals has grown and, in many cases, they have exceeded the power of force of weapons in their effectiveness**"; "All this is supplemented by military means of a concealed character"; asymmetrical actions include "special-operations forces and internal opposition to create a permanently operating front through the entire territory of the enemy state, as well as informational actions"; and (relevant to H1) he forecasts "walking, crawling, jumping, and flying robots" and AI. **H**.

**The misnomer — Galeotti's own admission.** Mark Galeotti coined the phrase as a blog title. His own "belated but heart-felt PS": "**I was just going for a snappy title**… (a) Gerasimov didn't invent this; if any CoGS deserves the 'credit' it would be his predecessor **Makarov**… (b) **it's not a doctrine**… So **stop it, please!**" — and the article "is crucially about **catching up with and defeating what he regards as a *Western* innovation**." Source: https://inmoscowsshadows.wordpress.com/2014/07/06/the-gerasimov-doctrine-and-russian-non-linear-war/ **H**. Later: "I'm Sorry for Creating the 'Gerasimov Doctrine'", *Foreign Policy*, 5 Mar 2018, https://foreignpolicy.com/2018/03/05/im-sorry-for-creating-the-gerasimov-doctrine/ (title verified; **body paywalled and unarchived → content UNVERIFIED**); scholarly version: Galeotti, "The mythical 'Gerasimov Doctrine' and the language of threat", *Critical Studies on Security* 7(2), 2019. **H**.

**Did Gerasimov write the "nonlinear war" text?** **No.** "Non-linear war" is not Gerasimov's formulation; it was popularised by **Russian military writers Chekinov & Bogdanov** and adopted by Western analysts (Galeotti: "call it non-linear war, which I prefer"). Bērziņš: "**there isn't a Gerasimov doctrine.** His presentation… just reflected the main points some military thinkers, notably Chekinov and Bogdanov, have been doing for years" (https://www.berzins.eu/russian-warfare-is-not-hybrid/); he also argues Gerasimov was partly describing **American** doctrine and asking Russian military science to study it. Also: McDermott, "Does Russia Have a Gerasimov Doctrine?", *Parameters* 46(1), 2016. **H** that the attribution is wrong; **M** on the precise "he was describing US doctrine" reading.
**"The Gerasimov Doctrine is dead": UNVERIFIED as a Galeotti title.** Verified rebuttals are the 2014 blog, the 2018 FP piece and the 2019 article.

### B3. "Hybrid warfare" as unhelpful; the grey-zone shift; EU/NATO framing — **M–H**

- **Kofman & Rojansky**, Kennan Cable No. 7 (Wilson Center, April 2015): "**hybrid war is merely a label attributed to Russian actions**"; the concept is "well established [and] old as warfare itself"; "Hybrid war has become the **catchall** phrase"; the "domino-like repetition of 'hybrid war' is the **wrong** [model]." https://www.wilsoncenter.org/publication/kennan-cable-no7-closer-look-russias-hybrid-war (PDF read). **H**.
- **Renz & Smith**, *Russia and Hybrid warfare — going beyond the label*, Aleksanteri Papers 1/2016 — https://researchportal.helsinki.fi/en/publications/russia-and-hybrid-warfare-going-beyond-the-label (metadata/conclusion verified; full PDF 403 here). **M**.
- **Echevarria**, "How should we think about 'grey-zone' wars?", *Infinity Journal* 5(1), 2015: labels are repeated "more out of habit than reflection." **M**.
- **Institutional framing.** *Hybrid CoE* (Helsinki, est. 2017; 35 participating states, cooperating with EU and NATO) defines hybrid threats as "**Coordinated and synchronized action that deliberately targets democratic states' and institutions' systemic vulnerabilities**… **Activities that exploit the thresholds of detection and attribution**, as well as the different interfaces (war–peace, internal–external…)" — https://www.hybridcoe.fi/hybrid-threats-as-a-phenomenon/. The EEAS page (EU Hybrid Toolbox, Hybrid Rapid Response Teams, dedicated Russia hybrid sanctions regime) adds that campaigns are "designed to remain **below the threshold** which could constitute or be perceived as an act of war": https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eeas/countering-hybrid-threats_en. **H**.
- **Fit: Poor as an analytical tool, Partial as a vocabulary.** The "thresholds of detection and attribution" clause is the single institutional formulation that *does* match H1's mechanism. Everything else is too elastic to test.

### B4. 2020s drone incursions — attribution and self-generated disruption — **M–H**

- **Gatwick, 19–21 Dec 2018.** 33-hour runway closure, ~1,000 flights / 140,000 passengers affected; 170 reports (115 "credible"), **no photograph or video of a drone ever emerged**; the arrested couple were released without charge; the review is still withheld, and a 2024 FOI showed the National Police Air Service logged **no** Gatwick drone sightings 19–31 Dec 2018. Police/airport maintained "malicious attack"; the Guardian's 2020 investigation argues mass panic/misidentification. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gatwick_Airport_drone_incident; https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2020/dec/01/the-mystery-of-the-gatwick-drone. **H**.
- **The Sept–Nov 2025 European wave.** Copenhagen CPH closed ~4 hours on 22 Sep 2025; Oslo Gardermoen briefly the same night; 500+ national reports in 24h; police spoke of a "capable actor," the PM of "the most serious attack on Danish critical infrastructure to date." **The corrective:** on 25 Jun 2026 Copenhagen Police concluded it could **neither prove nor disprove** drones over CPH and admitted "we were not clear enough with our reservations"; **Norway's PST** found no foreign-state link (Feb 2026), most cases being stars, weather, aircraft or helicopters. In Belgium/Netherlands, VRT *Pano* and NOS found **no physical evidence** and a ministerial "drone" clip was a police helicopter. The UK Nov 2024 RAF-base sightings closed in Feb 2026 with **no suspects**. **H** (from the parent workspace evidence files, each citing official statements: `../../research/06-airports-infrastructure.md`, `../../research/08-hybrid-nato.md`, `../../research/denmark.md`).
- **What *is* attributed.** Leipzig/Halle, 4–5 Aug 2026: an FPV drone with explosive struck an An-124; **Germany formally blamed Russia on 1 Sep 2026.** Øresund, 26 Feb 2026: Sweden confirmed a drone launched from the Russian intelligence ship *Zhigulevsk*. Poland, 9–10 Sep 2025: 19–23 drones and Article 4, but Lublin prosecutors called the recovered devices **unarmed decoys**. Dublin 2023 and Cologne/Bonn 2026 were prosecuted/investigated **careless fliers**. **H**.
- **Institutional attribution.** EPRS PE 777.962 (Oct 2025) records officials "**suggesting** in some cases" drones came from offshore vessels and that many devices were "unarmed and some merely decoys," yet governments "**regard** these intrusions as a form of hybrid threat." IISS's "highly likely Kremlin campaign" (https://www.iiss.org/research-paper/2026/06/russias-uav-campaign-over-europe/) is a **think-tank judgement, not a state attribution**; no consolidated NATO/EU public attribution of the sighting wave existed by 25 Sep 2026. **H** on the absence of attribution.
- **Fit: Partial (mechanism) / Poor (framing).** The **mechanism H1 proposes is already observable without any attacker**: airport closures, diversions and institutional hardening were produced by *reported* sightings that later collapsed or were never attributed. **Where it does NOT fit:** "Russia did it" is a different, weaker claim than "the reaction did it," and treating the wave as a proven hybrid campaign is unsupported — the honest reading is that ambiguity, media and institutional risk-aversion did the work.

**Bottom line for H1.** Active measures supply the closest historical *intent* and Rid supplies the closest historical *statement of the mechanism* (effect-attribution ambiguity; "value-creation outsourced to the victim"). Hybrid warfare supplies almost nothing analytically, and its flagship label is a Western misnomer. The 2025–26 drone wave is better evidence for H1's **emergent** half — disruption generated by reaction to ambiguity — than for any claim of deliberate state engineering.
