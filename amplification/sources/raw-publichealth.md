## Case A — H5N1 clade 2.3.4.4b in US dairy cattle (2024–2026): alarm without an event

### Causal chain (ambiguity → reports → amplification → attention → vigilance → more reports → response → validation/refutation)

Late March 2024 USDA/APHIS confirmed H5N1 clade 2.3.4.4b (genotype B3.13) in Texas/Kansas dairy herds — the first known H5N1 in cattle, with very high viral load in milk and an unknown transmission route. On 1 April 2024 CDC confirmed the first human case: a Texas dairy worker with conjunctivitis, mild illness; CDC assessed general-public risk as **low**. On 25 April 2024 the FDA reported that roughly **1 in 5** retail milk samples were qRT-PCR-positive for HPAI fragments, producing a transient milk-supply scare. That strand was refuted inside a month: of 297 retail samples, 60 (20.2%) were PCR-positive for viral RNA but **0/60 contained viable virus** by egg inoculation (FDA, 10 May 2024), and a commercial continuous-flow HTST study showed pasteurisation inactivated ≥12 log₁₀ (28 June 2024). Media framing ("next pandemic", "bird flu is coming for us") ran well ahead of the human data. Vigilance produced more reports — 1 case in April 2024, 70 by February 2025 — including a severe Louisiana D1.1 infection (December 2024) and the **first US H5N1 death on 6 January 2025**, both in older backyard-flock owners with underlying conditions. The virus then went quiet in humans: the 71st US A(H5) case since early 2024 — the first since February 2025 — was the **H5N5** infection in Washington State, hospitalised early November 2025 and **died 21 November 2025**; contact tracing found no onward transmission and WHO kept the overall A(H5) risk at **low** (low-to-moderate for occupationally exposed). By 6 March 2026 CDC still reported **71 total cases**. Case reporting continued but institutional attention visibly de-escalated: CDC's last H5N1 technical report is dated **5 June 2024**; on **7 July 2025** CDC folded H5N1 updates into routine influenza reporting and stopped mirroring USDA animal data; WHO's last formal A(H5) Disease Outbreak News entry is December 2025. One new human case in thirteen months against a background of >1,000 affected herds worldwide (ABC, August 2026) is the signature of an alarm that did **not** convert into a pandemic or a permanent emergency posture.

### Key actors
USDA/APHIS; FDA; CDC; WHO/PAHO/ECDC; state agencies (California CDPH, Washington DOH, Nevada, Michigan, Colorado); HHS/White House (2025 communications pause); HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.; raw-milk industry (Raw Farm LLC / Mark McAfee); dairy and poultry producers; national media.

### What amplified it, and how
- **Organic:** veterinarians, dairy/poultry workers, state and local health departments, occupational-health clinicians — the surveillance itself was the main report generator.
- **Media:** sustained "next pandemic" framing 2024–2025 (Time, Guardian, NYT); a second wave in 2026 over Australian seal/mammal deaths. Classification: **media/organic**.
- **Political:** fight over USDA compensation and mandatory milk testing; the **20–22 January 2025 federal health communications pause**; HHS's May 2025 cancellation of mRNA pandemic-flu contracts; RFK Jr.'s promoted raw-milk and "let it spread" positions. Classification: **political/ideological**.
- **Commercial:** raw-milk brands marketed against official warnings; vaccine manufacturers (Moderna, CSL Seqirus) and their investors. Classification: **commercial**.
- **Adversarial:** no significant state-actor disinformation campaign on H5N1 was documented in these sources — a **negative finding** worth recording.

### Persistence vs collapse factors
**Persisted:** genuine unresolved biology (cow-to-cow and cow-to-human route still not settled; aerosol vs milk-contact contested — Colorado State's Jason Lombard, quoted ABC, August 2026); avian H5N1's historical severity (973 global human cases and 470 deaths, **48% CFR**, to 22 April 2025, PAHO/WHO); the economic stakes for a $60bn+ dairy sector.
**Collapsed:** the commercial-milk-supply panic (viability data refuted it); the claim that raw milk consumption was infecting people (no human cases linked to raw-milk consumption reported, despite two Raw Farm recalls in November 2024); the "imminent pandemic" frame (no sustained human-to-human transmission in any US case; 71 cases and 2 deaths over 2.5 years).
**Net:** ambiguity persisted biologically but the *disruption* decayed because severity and transmissibility in humans never moved.

### Institutional legacy (dates, figures)
- **USDA:** National Milk Testing Strategy and HPAI dairy producer support (December 2024); **$1 billion** comprehensive HPAI strategy (2025); interstate movement order for lactating dairy cattle, updated **27 April 2026**. APHIS H5N1 hub last modified 20 January 2026.
- **FDA:** **$8 million** added response funding (10 May 2024); 297 retail samples tested; **464** pasteurised dairy products all negative for viable H5N1; Cornell raw-milk-cheese work showing 60-day ageing does **not** eliminate viable virus (14 March 2025); cat/dog food H5N1 hazard rule (17 January 2025).
- **CDC:** at least **33,200 people monitored and 1,340 tested** (Feb 2022–29 Aug 2026), of which ~5,000/156 since 28 Sept 2025; technical reports ceased after 5 June 2024; reporting cadence reduced 7 July 2025.
- **Vaccines (figures, URL unverified — see UNSOURCED):** BARDA contracts to CSL Seqirus (~$121m, Sept 2024) and Moderna ($176m July 2024; ~$590m January 2025); **$766m of Moderna pandemic-flu contracts cancelled by HHS in May 2025**; Moderna raised ~$54.3m privately in December 2025. Net effect: stockpile work proceeded, then was part-cancelled and re-privatised — a **partial** durable legacy.
- **Net assessment:** durable *surveillance and agricultural* machinery (milk testing, biosecurity funding, wastewater/H5 monitoring) survived; the *public-health emergency* framing did not. Durable but de-escalated.

## Case B — Mpox clade Ib PHEIC (Aug 2024 – Jan 2026): a real but regionally bounded outbreak framed globally

### Causal chain
**Ambiguity.** A novel clade Ib MPXV lineage emerged around September 2023 in Kamituga, South Kivu (DRC), transmitting person-to-person, largely sexually. Conflict, displacement and thin laboratory coverage (nine DRC testing sites) left true size and severity unknown; WHO's first IHR Emergency Committee report (19 Aug 2024) recorded "insufficient information" on clade Ib severity and warned against over-reading crude case-fatality rates.
**Reports → amplification (Aug 2024).** DRC reported **>15,600 cases and 537 deaths in 2024**; over 100 laboratory-confirmed clade Ib cases appeared in four previously mpox-free neighbours (Burundi, Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda). **Africa CDC declared a Public Health Emergency of Continental Security (PHECS) on 13 August 2024** — its first ever — and WHO followed with a **PHEIC on 14 August 2024**; Committee Chair Dimie Ogoina framed it as "an emergency, not only for Africa, but for the entire globe."
**Attention.** Media framing outran official assessment: **ECDC (16 Aug 2024) rated overall risk to the EU/EEA general population "low"**, imported cases merely "highly likely". Sweden reported the first clade Ib case outside Africa on 15 Aug 2024; Thailand (22 Aug), Germany, India and the UK followed, with one UK household transmission. **No European or US border, entry/exit screening or travel restrictions were imposed.**
**Institutional response.** A joint Africa CDC–WHO continental plan (6 Sept 2024) was budgeted at **close to US$600 million** for Sept 2024–Feb 2025; Africa CDC's own **US$245 million** ask was reported as only **~10% funded** in late Aug 2024. WHO prequalified MVA-BN on 13 Sept 2024 and EUL-listed LC16m8 on 19 Nov 2024 (Japan donated 3.05m doses). First doses reached DRC on 5 Sept 2024; Gavi's "largest shipment yet" — **200,000 doses** — arrived in May 2025.
**Validation/refutation.** Clade Ib's CFR settled near **0.2–0.5%** — close to clade IIb and roughly 15× lower than clade Ia (2.5–3.6%; historically ~10%). Declines in DRC, Burundi and Uganda followed. **WHO terminated the PHEIC on 5 September 2025**, extending standing recommendations to August 2026 and then **August 2027**; **Africa CDC lifted its PHECS only on 22 January 2026**. The feared Western wave never materialised, but clade Ib resurged in **Madagascar (Dec 2025) and Angola (Apr 2026)**.

### Key actors and amplification classification
- **Africa CDC (Kaseya)** — political/institutional: first-ever PHECS; continental framing; alert held 17 months.
- **WHO (Tedros, IHR EC, Ogoina)** — institutional, organic→political: PHEIC one day after Africa CDC, driven by evidence gaps and regional pressure.
- **Western media (Guardian, Vox and others)** — media: "new mpox pandemic" and vaccine-equity framing.
- **Gavi/UNICEF/WHO Access & Allocation Mechanism** — institutional/commercial-mixed: procurement, allocation, First Response Fund.
- **Bavarian Nordic; KM Biologics/Japan** — commercial (and state-commercial): US$50–75/dose; 55,000 doses donated plus a 500,000-dose Gavi deal; 3.05m LC16m8 doses donated to DRC.
- **Anti-vaccine networks** — adversarial: recycled COVID-vaccine claims that vaccines cause mpox.
- **US government** — political → adversarial: principal donor, then funding freeze and WHO withdrawal.

### Persistence vs collapse factors
**Durable:** standing recommendations to Aug 2027; Africa CDC's PHECS precedent and incident-management model; **>US$1 billion mobilised and >5 million doses deployed across 16 countries**; DRC testing sites expanded **9→28** with >10-fold sequencing growth; a global ICG mpox vaccine stockpile launched **27 August 2026**; a transition roadmap integrating mpox into HIV/STI services. **Fragile:** WHO's Sept 2024 US$87.4m appeal had raised only ~US$65.5m by Feb 2025, and the April 2025 plan's US$145m requirement drew no new commitments by June 2025; of 2.9m doses distributed by June 2025 only **~724,000 were administered**. The US funded one-third of the response; its 2025 freeze stranded US$7.5m and cut DRC specimen transport and testing, and the US completed WHO withdrawal on **22 Jan 2026**. Falling cases ended the emergency — and the 2026 Madagascar/Angola surges show how fast vigilance decays. **Comparison with clade IIb (2022):** that PHEIC (July 2022) was declared after a genuinely global multi-country outbreak and ended in May 2023 after ~90% case decline; WHO's fact sheet gives **>100,000 confirmed cases and >220 deaths across >120 countries (Jan 2022–Aug 2024, all clades)**. Clade Ib produced a comparable institutional response to a far more regional event — the escalation was in *institutions*, not in Western social disruption.

### Institutional legacy (dates, figures)
- 13 Aug 2024 PHECS; 14 Aug 2024 PHEIC; EC meetings 14 Aug 2024, 22 Nov 2024, 25 Feb 2025, 5 Jun 2025, 4 Sep 2025.
- 6 Sept 2024 joint plan ~US$600m; Africa CDC ask US$245m, ~10% funded.
- 13 Sept 2024 MVA-BN prequalification; 19 Nov 2024 LC16m8 EUL; 5 Sept 2024 first DRC doses; 1 May 2025 200,000-dose Gavi shipment; ~1.6m MVA-BN doses to 13 countries and ~1m administered by Sept 2025.
- 5 Sept 2025 PHEIC ended; **22 Jan 2026 PHECS lifted**; **27 Aug 2026 global stockpile**.
- Cumulative **1 Jan 2022 – 31 Jul 2026: 190,683 confirmed cases, 529 deaths, 145 countries** (WHO sit rep #69, 14 Sept 2026).
- Africa CDC: 80,276 suspected cases / 1,340 deaths in 2024; CFR 2.6% → 0.6%.

## Case C — "Disease X" and the US withdrawal from WHO: ambiguity given an institutional owner

### Causal chain
In February 2018 a WHO R&D Blueprint informal consultation (Geneva, 6–7 February) added **"Disease X"** to its priority-pathogen list — a placeholder for "a serious international epidemic… caused by a pathogen currently unknown to cause human disease." The ambiguity was **named and given an institutional owner**, without any event. In November 2022 WHO launched a global revision (300+ scientists, 25+ virus families); by 2024 it had replaced the fixed list with a *framework* covering **28 viral families, one bacterial core group and 1,652 pathogens**. On **17 January 2024** the World Economic Forum staged a Davos panel, "Preparing for Disease X." Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus explained the placeholder in real time — "we need to have a placeholder for the diseases we don't know may come" — and repeated the formula "anything happening is a matter of **when, not if**", while the panel's concrete content was humdrum: vaccine supply chains, epidemiological centres, surge-capable health systems. **The reaction inverted the frame.** Alex Jones alleged a "Globalist Plan to Launch #DiseaseX to Install World Government"; Donald Trump Jr. told Instagram followers to prepare by buying a sponsor's **$299.99** ivermectin-containing "emergency medical kit." "Plandemic" and "WHO power grab" framings went viral; FactCheck.org (26 Jan 2024), PolitiFact (Aug 2023) and AFP Fact Check documented and debunked them. Institutionally, ambiguity kept producing durable output even as the physical threat failed to appear: the **IHR amendments** were adopted by consensus at WHA77 on **1 June 2024** (WHA77.17), creating a new "pandemic emergency" alert tier above a PHEIC, entering into force on **19 September 2025** (11 of 196 States Parties rejected them); and the **WHO Pandemic Agreement** was adopted by consensus at WHA78 on **20 May 2025** (committee vote 124–0–11). Then the counter-attack: Trump's **20 January 2025** executive order withdrew the US from WHO, completed **22 January 2026**; Washington declared itself bound by neither instrument. The one genuine "Disease X" outbreak — Panzi, Kwango, DRC, December 2024 — resolved into malaria, influenza and malnutrition.

### Key actors and amplification classification
- **WHO / Tedros / R&D Blueprint SAG** — institutional-organic: created, then repeatedly defended, the placeholder.
- **WEF (Davos AM24)** — institutional-political: supplied the global stage and the viral clip.
- **CEPI, Gavi, pharma** — commercial-institutional: funded "Disease X" vaccine/platform R&D and the 100 Days Mission.
- **Alex Jones, Trump Jr., anti-vax/wellness networks** — adversarial **plus commercial**: conspiracy plus product sales ($299.99 kit).
- **Fact-checkers (FactCheck.org, PolitiFact, AFP)** — media: rapid, repeated refutation.
- **Trump White House / HHS** — political → adversarial: WHO exit; rejected IHR amendments and the agreement.
- **LMIC vs high-income blocs; US bilateral deals (15 states, single-source)** — political(-commercial): PABS deadlock; aid/commercial access traded for pathogen samples.

### Persistence vs collapse factors
**Persisted:** a named, owned placeholder with no falsifiable claim; funded roadmaps and vaccine platforms; new legal machinery (pandemic-emergency tier, National IHR Authorities, States Parties Committee, PABS); "when, not if" institutionalised in Tedros's rhetoric (June 2024, May 2026); a standing counter-narrative.
**Fragile:** no pathogen ever materialised; the 2024 revision dissolved the fixed list into a framework; the sole real "Disease X" outbreak resolved to known causes; the PABS annex remains unfinished, so the agreement is **not yet open for signature and has zero ratifications**; and the largest funder walked out.

### Institutional legacy (dates, dollar figures)
- 6–7 Feb 2018 Blueprint review adds Disease X; Nov 2022 revision launched; 2024 prioritisation framework (1,652 pathogens).
- 17 Jan 2024 Davos panel; 1 Jun 2024 WHA77 IHR amendments (WHA77.17); 19 Sep 2025 amended IHR in force.
- 20 May 2025 WHA78 adopts the Pandemic Agreement by consensus; PABS annex deferred to an IGWG (Resolution WHA78.1); 20% real-time production target; entry into force 30 days after 60 ratifications. IGWG sixth (resumed) meeting ended 1 May 2026; seventh 6–17 July 2026; eighth 14–18 Sept 2026; UNGA high-level pandemic meeting 25 Sept 2026.
- **US:** EO 20 Jan 2025 (Fed. Reg. doc. 2025-01957); withdrawal complete 22 Jan 2026; the US supplied **15.6% of WHO revenue in 2022–23** (largest donor).
- **WHO:** 2024–25 programme budget **US$6.83bn**; ~**3,000 posts (22% of staff) cut Jan–Jun 2025**; KFF records a US$6.2bn 2026–27 budget (9% lower), while Health Policy Watch reports a $4.2bn operating target against a $1.7bn gap.
- **Aid cuts:** *Lancet Global Health* (Feb 2026) projects **22.6 million additional deaths in 93 countries by 2030**.

## Case E — 5G/health claims: a real physical exposure with no viral mechanism

### Causal chain

RF electromagnetic fields are real, ubiquitous and measurable, but physically cannot transmit a virus. That was the ambiguity hook: a plausible physical mechanism ("radiation weakens immunity") could be bolted onto a pandemic with no visible cause. The peer-reviewed trace (Langguth et al. 2022) dates the first 5G–COVID tweet to **21 January 2020** — 698 tweets and 1,089 retweets that month — on a substrate built since at least mid-2018 by anti-5G, anti-vaccine and wellness communities. Early framing was mild ("5G compromises immunity"); under lockdown it radicalised into a **cover-up narrative**: RF is lethal, "COVID-19 deaths" are radiation injury, and the pandemic was invented to hide the rollout. Growth was platform-led, not press-led — GDELT found traditional media "played essentially no role in the formation of this digital wildfire", engaging only after real-world harm. Between **25 March and 2 April 2020**, after UK-sourced YouTube videos (notably the 28 March "former Vodafone executive" clip), daily 5G tweets **quadrupled**. Arson began the weekend of **3 April 2020**. Mobile UK logged ~**50 masts attacked by 15 April, 77 by 6 May, 121 by 2 July 2020**; the CWU and trade bodies recorded ~**120 abuse cases by 23 April** (BT's CEO cited 40 incidents including a stabbed Openreach engineer); Langguth et al. count **273 UK technician incidents**. Abroad: Netherlands **22 arson + 3 attempts**, Ireland 3, Cyprus 2, New Zealand **17 towers vandalised**, Peru **8 technicians kidnapped** (10 June 2020). Refutation was rapid and official: **GOV.UK guidance of 6 May 2020** ("Viruses cannot travel on radio waves or mobile networks"), Ofcom myth-clearing statements, ICNIRP's 2020 guidelines, and WHO's EMF programme.

### Key actors and amplification classification
**Mixed — overwhelmingly organic, with celebrity, commercial and political vectors; state-linked amplification unproven.**
- **Organic:** "Stop 5G International" networks, anti-5G/anti-vaxx/wellness accounts, local Facebook groups inciting engineer harassment.
- **Media/celebrity:** David Icke (later deplatformed); Amanda Holden, Anne-Marie and Eamonn Holmes amplifying (prompting Ofcom guidance to ITV). Mainstream outlets had covered 5G fears uncritically pre-2020.
- **Commercial:** "5G Bioshield" USB sticks sold for ~US$350; demonetising this was among the few effective interventions.
- **Political:** far-right actors "capitalised" on it (GNET, April 2020); US amplifiers skewed Trump/QAnon-adjacent with no central direction.
- **Adversarial:** weak — RT America ran a 2019 anti-5G segment and Icke clips persisted on Russian-hosted accounts, but no EUvsDisinfo case establishing coordinated Kremlin promotion was retrievable.

### Persistence vs collapse factors
**Collapsed in the West** as the pandemic ended and platforms acted: **YouTube banned 5G–COVID videos on 5 April 2020**; **Twitter banned incitement to attack 5G infrastructure on 22 April 2020**. Content removal was largely ineffective (Langguth et al.); demonetisation and fraud enforcement worked better. **Persistence was local and identity-linked** — the constituency merged into anti-vax/mRNA, smart-meter and EHS networks. **West Belfast never subsided: 17 arson attacks on 5G towers since June 2023** (BBC, 2 July 2025), with the Clarke brothers charged in September 2025 and two further men charged in April 2026.

### Institutional legacy and the exact state of the RF evidence
- **No dedicated UK law** emerged; prosecutions used existing arson/criminal-damage law (Michael Whitty, 3 years, June 2020). New Zealand and Australia saw operator–police liaison rather than legislation.
- **ICNIRP 2020 RF guidelines (100 kHz–300 GHz)** derive limits from thermal thresholds and find no established adverse effects below them.
- **IARC's 2011 Group 2B** ("possibly carcinogenic") classification is **unchanged**.
- The **WHO-commissioned 2024 systematic review** (Karipidis et al., *Environment International*) found moderate-certainty evidence that mobile-phone use **likely does not** increase glioma, meningioma, acoustic neuroma, pituitary, salivary-gland or paediatric brain tumours.
- **Honest boundary:** this is **"no established effect", not "no effect"**. Group 2B persists on limited human evidence with acknowledged recall/selection bias and crude exposure assessment. Electromagnetic hypersensitivity is **not** a recognised diagnosis (WHO).
