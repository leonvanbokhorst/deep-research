## Case A — H5N1 clade 2.3.4.4b in US dairy cattle (2024–2026): alarm without an event

### Causal chain
Late March 2024 USDA/APHIS confirmed H5N1 clade 2.3.4.4b (genotype B3.13) in Texas/Kansas dairy herds — the first known H5N1 in cattle, with very high viral load in milk and an unknown transmission route. On 1 April 2024 CDC confirmed the first human case: a Texas dairy worker with conjunctivitis, mild illness; CDC assessed general-public risk as **low**. On 25 April 2024 the FDA reported that roughly **1 in 5** retail milk samples were qRT-PCR-positive for HPAI fragments, producing a transient milk-supply scare. That strand was refuted inside a month: of 297 retail samples, 60 (20.2%) were PCR-positive for viral RNA but **0/60 contained viable virus** by egg inoculation (FDA, 10 May 2024), and a commercial continuous-flow HTST study showed pasteurisation inactivated ≥12 log₁₀ (28 June 2024). Media framing ("next pandemic") ran well ahead of the human data. Vigilance produced more reports — 1 case in April 2024, 70 by February 2025 — including a severe Louisiana D1.1 infection (December 2024) and the **first US H5N1 death on 6 January 2025**, both in older backyard-flock owners with underlying conditions. The virus then went quiet in humans: the 71st US A(H5) case since early 2024 — the first since February 2025 — was the **H5N5** infection in Washington State, hospitalised early November 2025 and **died 21 November 2025**; contact tracing found no onward transmission and WHO kept overall A(H5) risk at **low** (low-to-moderate for occupationally exposed). By 6 March 2026 CDC still reported **71 total cases**. Institutional attention visibly de-escalated: CDC's last H5N1 technical report is dated **5 June 2024**; on **7 July 2025** CDC folded H5N1 into routine influenza reporting and stopped mirroring USDA animal data. One new human case in thirteen months against >1,000 affected herds worldwide (ABC, August 2026) is the signature of an alarm that did **not** convert into a pandemic.

### Key actors and amplification
USDA/APHIS, FDA, CDC, WHO/PAHO/ECDC, state agencies (California, Washington, Nevada, Michigan, Colorado), HHS/the White House, HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., raw-milk industry (Raw Farm LLC/Mark McAfee), dairy and poultry producers, national media. Classification: **media/organic** (sustained "next pandemic" framing plus genuine occupational surveillance); **political/ideological** (USDA compensation and mandatory-testing fights; the 20–22 Jan 2025 federal health communications pause; HHS's May 2025 cancellation of mRNA pandemic-flu contracts; RFK Jr.'s raw-milk promotion); **commercial** (raw-milk brands marketed against official warnings; vaccine makers). **Negative finding: no significant state-actor disinformation campaign on H5N1 was documented.**

### Persistence vs collapse
**Persisted:** unresolved biology (cow-to-cow and cow-to-human route still not settled; aerosol vs milk-contact contested); avian H5N1's historical severity (**973 global human cases and 470 deaths, 48% CFR**, to 22 April 2025, PAHO/WHO); agri-economic stakes. **Collapsed:** the commercial-milk-supply panic (viability data refuted it); the claim that raw milk consumption was infecting people (no human cases linked to raw-milk consumption reported despite two Raw Farm recalls); the "imminent pandemic" frame. Net: ambiguity persisted biologically, but *disruption* decayed because human severity and transmissibility never moved.

### Institutional legacy
- **USDA:** National Milk Testing Strategy and dairy producer support (Dec 2024); **$1 billion** HPAI strategy (2025); interstate movement order for lactating dairy cattle, updated **27 April 2026**.
- **FDA:** **$8 million** added response funding (10 May 2024); 297 retail samples; **464** pasteurised dairy products all negative for viable H5N1; Cornell work showing 60-day ageing does **not** eliminate viable virus in raw milk cheese (14 March 2025); cat/dog food H5N1 hazard rule (17 January 2025).
- **CDC:** at least **33,200 people monitored and 1,340 tested** (Feb 2022–29 Aug 2026; ~5,000/156 since 28 Sept 2025); technical reports ceased after 5 June 2024; cadence reduced 7 July 2025.
- **Vaccines (reported, URL unverified):** BARDA contracts to CSL Seqirus (~$121m) and Moderna ($176m, then ~$590m); **$766m of Moderna contracts cancelled May 2025**; ~$54.3m private raise Dec 2025.
- **Net:** surveillance and agricultural machinery survived; the public-health emergency framing did not. **Durable but de-escalated.**

## Case B — Mpox clade Ib PHEIC (Aug 2024 – Jan 2026): a bounded outbreak framed globally

### Causal chain
A novel clade Ib MPXV lineage emerged around September 2023 in Kamituga, South Kivu (DRC), transmitting person-to-person, largely sexually. Conflict, displacement and thin laboratory coverage (nine DRC testing sites) left true size and severity unknown; WHO's first IHR Emergency Committee report (19 Aug 2024) recorded "insufficient information" on clade Ib severity and warned against over-reading crude CFRs. DRC reported **>15,600 cases and 537 deaths in 2024**; over 100 laboratory-confirmed clade Ib cases appeared in four previously mpox-free neighbours. **Africa CDC declared a Public Health Emergency of Continental Security (PHECS) on 13 August 2024** — its first ever — and **WHO declared a PHEIC on 14 August 2024**. Media framing outran official assessment: **ECDC rated EU/EEA general-population risk "low" on 16 Aug 2024**, with imported cases merely "highly likely". Sweden reported the first clade Ib case outside Africa on 15 Aug 2024; Thailand (22 Aug), Germany, India and the UK followed, with one UK household transmission. **No European or US border, entry/exit screening or travel restrictions were imposed.** A joint Africa CDC–WHO plan (6 Sept 2024) was budgeted at **close to US$600 million**; Africa CDC's own **US$245 million** ask was reported as only **~10% funded** in late Aug 2024. WHO prequalified MVA-BN on 13 Sept 2024 and EUL-listed LC16m8 on 19 Nov 2024 (Japan donated 3.05m doses); first doses reached DRC on 5 Sept 2024 and Gavi's "largest shipment yet" — **200,000 doses** — arrived in May 2025. Clade Ib's CFR settled near **0.2–0.5%**, close to clade IIb and roughly 15× lower than clade Ia (3.6%). **WHO terminated the PHEIC on 5 September 2025** (standing recommendations first to Aug 2026, then **Aug 2027**) and **Africa CDC lifted its PHECS only on 22 January 2026**. The feared Western wave never materialised; clade Ib resurged in **Madagascar (Dec 2025) and Angola (Apr 2026)**.

### Key actors and amplification
**Africa CDC (Kaseya)** — political/institutional (first-ever PHECS, held 17 months). **WHO (Tedros, IHR EC, Ogoina)** — institutional, organic→political. **Western media** — media ("new mpox pandemic", vaccine-equity framing). **Gavi/UNICEF/WHO AAM** — institutional/commercial-mixed. **Bavarian Nordic; KM Biologics/Japan** — commercial (US$50–75/dose; donations). **Anti-vaccine networks** — adversarial (recycled COVID-vaccine claims causing mpox; ACAM2000 misinformation). **US government** — political → adversarial (principal donor, then funding freeze and WHO withdrawal).

### Persistence vs collapse
**Durable:** standing recommendations to Aug 2027; Africa CDC's PHECS precedent; **>US$1 billion mobilised and >5 million doses deployed across 16 countries**; DRC testing sites **9→28** with >10-fold sequencing growth; a global ICG mpox vaccine stockpile launched **27 Aug 2026**; integration of mpox into HIV/STI services. **Fragile:** WHO's Sept 2024 US$87.4m appeal had raised only ~US$65.5m by Feb 2025; the April 2025 plan's US$145m requirement drew no new commitments by June 2025; of 2.9m doses distributed by June 2025 only **~724,000 were administered**. The US funded one-third of the response; its 2025 freeze stranded US$7.5m and cut DRC specimen transport and testing. **Clade IIb comparison (2022):** that PHEIC (July 2022) followed a genuinely global outbreak and ended May 2023 after ~90% case decline; WHO gives **>100,000 confirmed cases and >220 deaths across >120 countries (Jan 2022–Aug 2024, all clades)**. Clade Ib produced a comparable institutional response to a far more regional event — escalation was in *institutions*, not Western society.

### Institutional legacy
13 Aug 2024 PHECS; 14 Aug 2024 PHEIC; EC meetings 14 Aug 2024, 22 Nov 2024, 25 Feb 2025, 5 Jun 2025, 4 Sep 2025. 6 Sept 2024 joint plan ~US$600m; Africa CDC ask US$245m, ~10% funded. 13 Sept 2024 MVA-BN prequalification; 19 Nov 2024 LC16m8 EUL; 5 Sept 2024 first DRC doses; 1 May 2025 200,000-dose Gavi shipment; ~1.6m MVA-BN doses to 13 countries and ~1m administered by Sept 2025. **5 Sept 2025 PHEIC ended; 22 Jan 2026 PHECS lifted; 27 Aug 2026 global stockpile.** Cumulative **1 Jan 2022 – 31 Jul 2026: 190,683 confirmed cases, 529 deaths, 145 countries** (WHO sit rep #69). Africa CDC: 80,276 suspected cases/1,340 deaths in 2024; CFR 2.6% → 0.6%.

## Case C — "Disease X" and the US withdrawal from WHO: ambiguity given an institutional owner

### Causal chain
In February 2018 a WHO R&D Blueprint informal consultation (Geneva, 6–7 February) added **"Disease X"** to its priority-pathogen list — a placeholder for "a serious international epidemic… caused by a pathogen currently unknown to cause human disease." The ambiguity was **named and given an institutional owner**, without any event. A November 2022 revision (300+ scientists, 25+ virus families) culminated by 2024 in a *framework* covering **28 viral families, one bacterial core group and 1,652 pathogens**. On **17 January 2024** the World Economic Forum staged a Davos panel, "Preparing for Disease X"; Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus explained the placeholder in real time — "we need to have a placeholder for the diseases we don't know may come" — and repeated "a matter of **when, not if**", while the panel's concrete content was humdrum (vaccine supply chains, epidemiological centres, surge capacity). **The reaction inverted the frame.** Alex Jones alleged a "Globalist Plan to Launch #DiseaseX to Install World Government"; Donald Trump Jr. urged Instagram followers to buy a sponsor's **$299.99** ivermectin-containing "emergency medical kit"; "Plandemic" and "WHO power grab" framings went viral; FactCheck.org (26 Jan 2024), PolitiFact (Aug 2023) and AFP documented and debunked them. Institutionally, ambiguity kept producing durable output even as the physical threat failed to appear: the **IHR amendments** were adopted by consensus at WHA77 on **1 June 2024** (WHA77.17), creating a new "pandemic emergency" tier above a PHEIC, in force **19 September 2025** (11 of 196 States Parties rejected them); the **WHO Pandemic Agreement** was adopted by consensus at WHA78 on **20 May 2025** (committee vote 124–0–11). Then the counter-attack: Trump's **20 January 2025** executive order withdrew the US from WHO, completed **22 January 2026**; Washington declared itself bound by neither instrument. The one genuine "Disease X" outbreak — Panzi, Kwango, DRC, December 2024 — resolved into malaria, influenza and malnutrition.

### Key actors and amplification
**WHO / Tedros / R&D Blueprint SAG** — institutional-organic (created then defended the placeholder). **WEF (Davos AM24)** — institutional-political (global stage and viral clip). **CEPI, Gavi, pharma** — commercial-institutional (Disease X vaccine/platform R&D; 100 Days Mission). **Alex Jones, Trump Jr., anti-vax/wellness networks** — adversarial **plus commercial** (conspiracy plus $299.99 product sales). **Fact-checkers** — media. **Trump White House / HHS** — political → adversarial. **LMIC vs high-income blocs; US bilateral deals (15 states, single-source)** — political(-commercial).

### Persistence vs collapse
**Persisted:** a named, owned placeholder with no falsifiable claim; funded roadmaps and vaccine platforms; new legal machinery (pandemic-emergency tier, National IHR Authorities, States Parties Committee, PABS); "when, not if" institutionalised in Tedros's rhetoric (June 2024, May 2026); a standing counter-narrative. **Fragile:** no pathogen ever materialised; the 2024 revision dissolved the fixed list into a framework; the sole real "Disease X" outbreak resolved to known causes; PABS is unfinished, so the agreement is **not open for signature and has zero ratifications**; and the largest funder walked out.

### Institutional legacy
6–7 Feb 2018 Blueprint review adds Disease X; Nov 2022 revision launched; 2024 framework (1,652 pathogens). 17 Jan 2024 Davos panel; 1 Jun 2024 WHA77 IHR amendments (WHA77.17); 19 Sep 2025 amended IHR in force. 20 May 2025 WHA78 adopts the Pandemic Agreement; PABS deferred to an IGWG (Res. WHA78.1); 20% real-time production target; entry into force 30 days after 60 ratifications. IGWG sixth (resumed) meeting ended 1 May 2026; seventh 6–17 July 2026; eighth 14–18 Sept 2026; UNGA high-level pandemic meeting 25 Sept 2026. **US:** EO 20 Jan 2025 (Fed. Reg. 2025-01957); withdrawal complete 22 Jan 2026; the US supplied **15.6% of WHO revenue in 2022–23** (largest donor). **WHO:** 2024–25 programme budget **US$6.83bn**; ~**3,000 posts (22% of staff) cut Jan–Jun 2025**; KFF records a US$6.2bn 2026–27 budget (9% lower), while Health Policy Watch reports a $4.2bn operating target against a $1.7bn gap. **Aid cuts:** *Lancet Global Health* (Feb 2026) projects **22.6 million additional deaths in 93 countries by 2030**.

## Case D — COVID-19 origin ambiguity: the persistence case

### Causal chain
Two hypotheses — zoonotic spillover (probably via the Huanan Seafood Wholesale Market) and a laboratory-related incident in Wuhan — have been live since early 2020, and **neither has met the standard of proof**. The zoonotic case rests on the Feb 2021 WHO-convened joint study (which judged a laboratory incident "extremely unlikely" and intermediate-host introduction "likely to very likely") and on 2022 *Science* papers by Worobey et al. (spatial clustering of early cases at the market) and Pekar et al. (genomic evidence for at least two independent market introductions). The lab-leak case rests on the absence of an identified intermediate host, the WIV's bat-coronavirus work, and the 2018 **DEFUSE** proposal (EcoHealth Alliance/WIV to DARPA, proposing work involving furin cleavage sites) — but **no direct evidence of a lab leak exists either**. The controversy turned political in 2023: the **DOE assessed "low confidence"** that a lab leak was most likely (reported 26 Feb 2023); the **FBI assessed "moderate confidence"** in a lab incident (Wray, Feb/Mar 2023); and the **ODNI declassified report of 23 June 2023** found the IC split — four elements plus the National Intelligence Council leaning zoonotic at **low confidence**, two leaning lab-origin at **low confidence**, two undecided: **no consensus, all low confidence**. On **25 January 2025** the **CIA shifted to assess a lab origin "more likely" than natural origin, but with "low confidence"** — the first public CIA position. The "Proximal Origin" paper (Andersen et al., *Nature Medicine* 2020, arguing against deliberate engineering) became a focal point after 2023 congressional releases of the authors' private messages suggested they had weighed a lab origin more seriously than the published text implied. What sustained the controversy **after the biological evidence stopped moving** was structural: an unresolved hypothesis with no falsifying datum, Chinese refusal of a transparent Phase 2 investigation (WHO SAGO has repeatedly called for data), US congressional politics (House Select Subcommittee final report, December 2024), and media hardening on both sides. By 2026 the question had been *politicised again*: outgoing DNI Tulsi Gabbard released declassified COVID-origin documents on her final day in office (18–19 June 2026) alleging Fauci influenced the origins intelligence and misled Congress — a claim **Lawfare's analysis (23 June 2026) concluded the documents do not prove**. Germany's BND had reportedly leaned lab-origin in 2025 (BBC, 13 Mar 2025).

### Key actors and amplification
**WHO / SAGO / joint study team** — institutional-organic (the "extremely unlikely" formulation and its revision). **EcoHealth Alliance / WIV / Peter Daszak** — scientific-commercial, later adversarial-adjacent (DEFUSE; Proximal Origin messages). **US intelligence community (ODNI, DOE, FBI, CIA)** — political-institutional (successive, mutually inconsistent low/moderate-confidence assessments). **US Congress (House Select Subcommittee; later DNI Gabbard)** — political (hearings, declassifications, 2026 releases). **Media** — mixed (a visible "lab leak" turn 2023–2024 alongside continued zoonosis reporting). **Chinese government** — adversarial-by-opacity. **Social-media ecosystems and advocacy scientists** — organic/adversarial, both directions.

### Persistence vs collapse
**Persisted because:** neither hypothesis is falsifiable with available evidence; no intermediate host was identified; China withheld access; the Proximal Origin affair supplied a *process* scandal independent of the biology; and the question acquired partisan identity, so new evidence is filtered rather than decisive. **Never collapsed:** unlike the milk-supply (Case A) or 5G (Case E) strands there was never a cheap decisive refutation, and unlike mpox (Case B) the asserted outcome had already happened. **Genuine state of the science by 2026:** zoonotic spillover remains the mainstream scientific position with the strongest positive evidence; a lab incident remains unexcluded; the honest summary is **"unresolved", not "settled" in either direction**.

### Institutional legacy
2020 Proximal Origin (*Nature Medicine*); 2021 WHO joint study; 2021 SAGO established; 2022 Worobey and Pekar (*Science*). Feb 2023 DOE "low confidence"; Feb/Mar 2023 FBI "moderate confidence"; **23 June 2023 ODNI declassified report — no consensus, low confidence throughout**. Dec 2024 House Select Subcommittee final report. **25 Jan 2025 CIA "more likely" lab origin, low confidence.** 13 Mar 2025 BBC reports BND leaned lab-origin. **June 2026** Gabbard declassification and Lawfare rebuttal. Durable policy legacy: biosafety/gain-of-function funding restrictions, HHS action against EcoHealth Alliance, and permanent congressional oversight — while the scientific question stays open. Precise funding/debarment figures were not verified this session.

## Case E — 5G/health claims: a real physical exposure with no viral mechanism

### Causal chain
RF electromagnetic fields are real, ubiquitous and measurable, but physically cannot transmit a virus. That was the ambiguity hook: a plausible physical mechanism ("radiation weakens immunity") could be bolted onto a pandemic with no visible cause. The peer-reviewed trace (Langguth et al. 2022) dates the first 5G–COVID tweet to **21 January 2020** — 698 tweets and 1,089 retweets that month — on a substrate built since mid-2018 by anti-5G, anti-vaccine and wellness communities. Early framing was mild ("5G compromises immunity"); under lockdown it radicalised into a **cover-up narrative**: RF is lethal, "COVID-19 deaths" are radiation injury, and the pandemic hides the rollout. Growth was platform-led, not press-led — GDELT found traditional media "played essentially no role in the formation of this digital wildfire". Between **25 March and 2 April 2020**, after UK-sourced YouTube videos (notably the 28 March "former Vodafone executive" clip), daily 5G tweets **quadrupled**. Arson began the weekend of **3 April 2020**. Mobile UK logged ~**50 masts attacked by 15 April, 77 by 6 May and 121 by 2 July 2020**; the CWU and trade bodies recorded ~**120 abuse cases by 23 April** (BT's CEO cited 40 incidents including a stabbed Openreach engineer); Langguth et al. count **273 UK technician incidents**. Abroad: Netherlands **22 arson + 3 attempts**, Ireland 3, Cyprus 2, New Zealand **17 towers vandalised**, Peru **8 technicians kidnapped** (10 June 2020). Refutation was rapid and official: **GOV.UK guidance of 6 May 2020** ("Viruses cannot travel on radio waves or mobile networks"), Ofcom myth-clearing statements, ICNIRP's 2020 guidelines and WHO's EMF programme.

### Key actors and amplification
**Mixed — overwhelmingly organic, with celebrity, commercial and political vectors; state-linked amplification unproven.** Organic: "Stop 5G International", anti-5G/anti-vaxx/wellness accounts, local Facebook groups inciting engineer harassment. Media/celebrity: David Icke (later deplatformed), Amanda Holden, Anne-Marie, Eamonn Holmes (prompting Ofcom guidance to ITV); mainstream outlets had covered 5G fears uncritically pre-2020. Commercial: "5G Bioshield" USB sticks at ~US$350 (demonetising these was among the few effective interventions). Political: far-right actors "capitalised" on it (GNET, April 2020); US amplifiers skewed Trump/QAnon-adjacent with no central direction. Adversarial: weak — RT America ran a 2019 anti-5G segment and Icke clips persisted on Russian-hosted accounts, but no EUvsDisinfo case establishing coordinated Kremlin promotion was retrievable.

### Persistence vs collapse
**Collapsed in the West** as the pandemic ended and platforms acted: **YouTube banned 5G–COVID videos on 5 April 2020**; **Twitter banned incitement to attack 5G infrastructure on 22 April 2020**. Removal was largely ineffective (Langguth et al.); demonetisation and fraud enforcement worked better. **Persistence was local and identity-linked** — the constituency merged into anti-vax/mRNA, smart-meter and EHS networks. **West Belfast never subsided: 17 arson attacks on 5G towers since June 2023** (BBC, 2 July 2025), with the Clarke brothers charged Sept 2025 and two further men charged April 2026.

### Institutional legacy and the exact state of the RF evidence
No dedicated UK law emerged; prosecutions used existing arson/criminal-damage law (Michael Whitty, 3 years, June 2020). **ICNIRP 2020 RF guidelines (100 kHz–300 GHz)** derive limits from thermal thresholds and find no established adverse effects below them. **IARC's 2011 Group 2B** ("possibly carcinogenic") classification is **unchanged**. The **WHO-commissioned 2024 systematic review** (Karipidis et al., *Environment International*) found moderate-certainty evidence that mobile-phone use **likely does not** increase glioma, meningioma, acoustic neuroma, pituitary, salivary-gland or paediatric brain tumours. **Honest boundary: this is "no established effect", not "no effect"** — Group 2B persists on limited human evidence with acknowledged recall/selection bias and crude exposure assessment. Electromagnetic hypersensitivity is **not** a recognised diagnosis (WHO).

## COUNTEREXAMPLES — plausible scares that conspicuously FAILED to take hold

- **The commercial-milk strand (Case A).** "1 in 5 retail milk samples positive for bird flu" looked like a mass-exposure story about a universally consumed product. It collapsed within weeks because the refutation was decisive and fast: **0 of 60 PCR-positive samples contained viable virus**, 464 pasteurised dairy products were all negative, and CDC's flu surveillance showed no human uptick. Refutation arrived *with* the alarm, from the same agency.
- **European/North American mpox disruption (Case B).** Despite a PHEIC and "new mpox pandemic" framing, ECDC rated EU/EEA general-population risk **"low"**; clade Ib produced only sporadic imports and one UK household cluster, and **no Western border or travel measures were imposed**. Real but regionally bounded epidemiology, low CFR, and a pre-existing orthopox toolkit. Escalation happened in *global-health institutions*, not Western society.
- **Burundi's mpox outbreak (Case B).** Controlled largely with non-pharmaceutical measures — sensitive surveillance, contact tracing, decentralised district action — despite minimal resources.
- **Mass 5G belief (Case E).** ~0.05% of COVID-19 tweets mentioned 5G, and a May 2020 UK survey found only **1.8%** agreed *completely* that coronavirus is 5G radiation poisoning (though >20% agreed "at least a little"). Traditional media "played essentially no role" in creating it. Harm came from a tiny, angry minority; no country adopted 5G restrictions on health grounds.
- **The Panzi "Disease X" outbreak (Case C, Dec 2024).** 394 cases and 30 deaths were initially undiagnosed and briefly fitted the placeholder — then resolved to **malaria, influenza and malnutrition**.
- **Case D is the inverted counterexample**: a controversy that persisted *without* any new physical facts, because the decisive evidence never arrived. That contrast — refutation collapses a scare; permanent non-resolution sustains one — is this file's central finding.

## SYNTHESIS — what made ambiguity persist or collapse

| Case | Ambiguity | Persisted because | Collapsed/decayed because | Institutional legacy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — H5N1 dairy | Transmission route; pandemic potential | Unresolved biology; high historical CFR of other clades; agri-economic stakes | No human-to-human spread; 71 cases/2 deaths in 2.5 yrs; milk-supply claim refuted fast | **Durable but de-escalated**: milk testing, $1bn USDA strategy, 33,200 monitored; vaccine contracts part-cancelled |
| B — mpox clade Ib | Severity and reach of a novel clade | Novel clade; African institutional agency; equity politics; COVID-era muscle memory | Low CFR (~0.2%); no Western spread; funded response never fully materialised | **Durable institutional, ephemeral emergency**: PHECS precedent, stockpile (Aug 2026), testing 9→28; PHEIC ended 5 Sep 2025 |
| C — Disease X | A threat that cannot be falsified | Named, owned placeholder; "when, not if" rhetoric; legal machinery | No pathogen; Panzi false positive; PABS deadlock; US exit | **Most durable of all**: IHR amendments in force, pandemic agreement adopted, WHO cut ~22% of posts |
| D — COVID origins | Which of two hypotheses is true | Neither hypothesis falsifiable; Chinese opacity; Proximal Origin process scandal; partisan identity | **Never collapsed** — no decisive refutation available either way | Durable policy (biosafety/gain-of-function restrictions, HHS action, permanent oversight); science stays open |
| E — 5G/health | Whether RF exposure harms health | Real, measurable exposure; unresolved IARC 2B; identity-linked communities | No viral mechanism; official refutation within days; platform action | **Ephemeral**: no law, no agency; residual local arson in west Belfast |

**Recurring mechanism:** ambiguity converts into durable institutions when an institution *owns the placeholder* (Case C) or when the threat is agricultural/economic (Case A). It produces ephemeral disruption and then collapses when a cheap, decisive refutation is available (Case A's milk strand; Case E) or when the feared outcome simply fails to arrive (Case B). It persists indefinitely when neither refutation nor resolution is possible (Case D).
