Improving air safety in the UK by learning from reported Airprox occurrences in UK
The benefits of Airprox reporting
The UK Airprox Board’s primary role is to review reported Airprox incidents to help improve air safety. It does not assign blame or liability. To support open and honest reporting, the Board does not publish the names of organisations or individuals in its reports.
