# Sub-cases: Portal Kombat / "Pravda" network, and Matryoshka + adjacent 2025–26 Russia operations

Cut-off 25 Sep 2026. Grades: **A** primary/official · **B** peer-reviewed/investigative · **C** reputable news · **D** vendor/think-tank · **E** anonymous/unverified. URLs fetched unless marked "(search result only)".

---

## 1. PORTAL KOMBAT / "PRAVDA" NETWORK

**Structure.** **DEMONSTRATED (A, high).** VIGINUM, 12 Feb 2024: **at least 193 "information portals"** analysed Sep–Dec 2023. Sites produce **no original content** — they mass-republish Russian/pro-Russian Telegram accounts, Russian agencies (RIA Novosti, TASS, Izvestia) and official local sites. <https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/publications/portal-kombat-un-reseau-structure-et-coordonne-de-propagande-prorusse> **DEMONSTRATED (A, high).** VIGINUM, 29 Apr 2024: ecosystem "now includes **224** information portals"; new domains included **19 targeting EU states**, 6 non-EU Europe, 3 Africa, 3 Asia — a "quadrillage informationnel" before the June 2024 European elections. <https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/publications/portal-kombat-extension-du-reseau-de-propagande-russe>

**CLAIMED, unresolved (D, medium).** ISD, 18 Nov 2025: the network "originally dubbed Portal Kombat by VIGINUM" runs **~87–90 sites**, **>5.8–6m articles since 2014**, one piece "every few seconds". <https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/link-by-link-hundreds-of-webpages-cite-pro-russia-pravda-network/> The 224-vs-90 gap is a **counting-base difference** (all portals incl. Russian/Ukrainian local outlets vs Western-facing "pravda" sites); which base ISD used is **unverified**. Not a contradiction.

**NOT VERIFIED.** NewsGuard (6 Mar 2025 AI-contamination report; 27 Jan 2026 naming Yevgeny Shevchenko "2025 Disinformer of the Year"), Propastop (4 Aug 2026, Estonia), Maldita.es (30 May 2025, ">150,000 posts" in Spain), the EC Disinfo Bulletin (16 Apr 2024 "Portal Kombat expanding in the EU") and EDMO (24 Apr 2024) all blocked or returned shells — **headlines only**; their numbers are unconfirmed here.

**Localisation/automation.** **DEMONSTRATED (A, high).** pravda-en[.]com published **1,734 items in one day** (18 Aug 2023); Telegram @pravdafrcom (created 3 Jul 2023) posts near-continuously, max ~9 items/hour, same diurnal pattern on @pravdaencom. Visible machine-translation errors RU→FR; untranslated Cyrillic video titles on VK. SEO deliberate — pravda-fr[.]com ranked top of Google for "leader palestine syndicate" (Oct 2023). VIGINUM's account puts the footprint on **Telegram + VK only**; X amplification is Matryoshka's separate vector (§2).

**Reach — epiphenomenal to the public, consequential in the retrieval layer.**
- **DEMONSTRATED (A, high).** VIGINUM/Similarweb, Nov 2023: the five Western-facing "pravda" portals averaged **~31,000 visits/month combined**; the France-targeting site was **the least visited**. Its Telegram channel had **119 subscribers** (26 Dec 2023); VK/Telegram engagement was "extremely low volume, or in some cases, none at all."
- **DEMONSTRATED (D, high).** ISD (Nov 2025): **919 sites** linked to Pravda articles Jul 2024–Jul 2025. Of 303 English-language sites reviewed, **81% treated Pravda as credible**, 11% decontextualised, only **3% properly contextualised** it as an information operation; **34 sites with >1m monthly visits** cited it as reliable. DFRLab separately found heavy Pravda link use in Wikipedia and X Community Notes.
- **CLAIMED, contested (D/C, low-med).** The American Sunlight Project argues Pravda's volume targets LLMs (widely cited **"33%"** repetition figure); ISD found popular LLMs **rarely cite** Pravda links. Unreconciled.
- **DEMONSTRATED (A, high).** VIGINUM on Storm-1516 (6 May 2025): real impact on public debate "remains difficult to estimate". <https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/publications/analyse-du-mode-operatoire-informationnel-russe-storm-1516> Its 11 Jun 2026 Rokh Solis report found "**very low online visibility … despite numerous attempts at artificial amplification**" — a useful benchmark against overstatement. <https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/viginum/publications/rokh-solis-analyse-dun-mode-operatoire-informationnel-ayant-cible-les>
- **INFERRED.** Direct human reach is marginal; the measurable effect is on the **citation/retrieval layer** (search ranking, Wikipedia, LLMs), not persuasion.

**Official action — negative finding.** No confirmed 2025–26 EU DSA proceeding, sanction listing, takedown or deindexing aimed specifically at Pravda/Portal Kombat was found. Treat as **unconfirmed**.

**Do not conflate.** Portal Kombat/Pravda (VIGINUM) ≠ **RRN**/Reliable Recent News (VIGINUM, 13 Jun 2023: **355 typosquatted media domains**, 4 impersonating French outlets, ≥58 articles) ≠ Doppelganger (copycat sites, EU-sanctioned 2023) ≠ **Storm-1516** (Microsoft name; GRU 29155-linked fake-video MO) ≠ **CopyCop** (Recorded Future; John Mark Dougan) ≠ Matryoshka ≠ Rokh Solis. <https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/publications/maj-19062023-rrn-une-campagne-numerique-de-manipulation-de-linformation-complexe-et>

## 2. MATRYOSHKA (2026, GERMANY) AND ADJACENT OPERATIONS

**Matryoshka exists — but was named in 2024, not 2026.** **DEMONSTRATED (A, high).** VIGINUM technical report, **10 Jun 2024**: *Matriochka*, active **since at least Sept 2023** (Check First: from Aug 2023). Method: publish fake content (reports, graffiti, memes) impersonating North American and European media and figures, seed it into the **X reply space** of media, personality and fact-checking accounts in **60+ countries**, then **contact targets directly on X and by email** to ask them to investigate. Most content was **pre-published on Russian Telegram channels**. Aim "most likely" to discredit media/fact-checkers; meets foreign-interference criteria. A VIGINUM footnote records the **name comes from Russian activist group Antibot4Navalny**, and the campaign had been **dubbed "Operation Overload"**. <https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/files/files/20240611_NP_SGDSN_VIGINUM_Matriochka_EN_VF.pdf> 2026 is therefore a **later wave**, not the campaign's origin.
- **CLAIMED (D, medium).** ISD (4 Sep 2026) calls Matryoshka "Operation Overload or **Storm-1679**" in its summary but "**Storm-1670**" in its findings — an internal inconsistency; Microsoft's own page was not retrievable.
- **NEGATIVE FINDING.** No documented Matryoshka-specific activity against the **Baden-Württemberg / Rhineland-Palatinate elections of 8 Mar 2026** was found; the documented 2026 German targeting is the **September 2026** cycle.

**The 2026 German wave.** **DEMONSTRATED (D, high).** ISD, 4 Sep 2026: **269 posts across 21 days (24 Jun–1 Sep 2026)** on X, BlueSky and TikTok. **94 posts** on Saxony-Anhalt, plus Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Lower Saxony municipal and Berlin House of Representatives elections; **103** generic "September elections" posts. Parties attacked: CDU/CSU (90), Greens (20), SPD (18), Die Linke (18), FDP (13). **AfD and BSW conspicuously untouched** — ISD infers intent to damage governing parties and strengthen anti-Ukraine-aid anti-establishment parties. Only **7 of 269 posts were in German** while impersonating 31 media brands (20 German). Strictly **Mon–Fri, 09:00–21:00 CEST**, 61.2% at 15:00–17:59, median 3h09 between posts. Errors (candidate/party mismatches; "SvDP" for FDP ← Russian СвДП) indicate a Russian-language translation pipeline. Engagement shows strong **inauthentic-amplification** signals (one post: 110.2k views, 405 likes, **0 comments**, likes arriving in a single one-hour spike); "direct reach appears limited outside of amplification by media or other actors." ISD adds Matryoshka is "**already beginning to target France's 2027 presidential election**". <https://www.isdglobal.org/digital_dispatches/investigation-an-old-dog-with-no-new-tricks-matryoshka-targets-regional-elections-in-germany/>

**Generative AI — DEMONSTRATED (D, high).** ~**40%** of Matryoshka's 2026 German-election videos use **AI voiceovers**, including cloned celebrity voices built on public Cameo clips (Sarah Jessica Parker, Mike Tyson, Daniel Brühl, Heidi Klum), with real clips cut to stock footage while synthetic audio delivers the false claim (#StopMerzToday, #TimeToDivideGermany).

**France 2026 — the Storm-1516 line, not Matryoshka.** **DEMONSTRATED (A, high).** VIGINUM fiche technique, **6 Feb 2026**: on 4 Feb 2026 a video claimed the US Epstein files held a compromising 2017 message from Jean-Luc Brunel about **Emmanuel Macron** ("Le nouveau président français va organiser une fête avenue Foch…"). First published on **france-soir.net**, **registered 1 Feb 2026**, impersonating france-soir.fr and Le Parisien journalist Victor Cousin. Imputed to **Storm-1516 with "very high confidence"**, with technical support from **CopyCop**, itself imputed to **John Mark Dougan**; Storm-1516 publicly attributed to **GRU unit 29155** with the Moscow "Centre d'expertise géopolitique". Reach: **~1.1m views on X — "an audience below previous operations"**. <https://www.sgdsn.gouv.fr/files/files/Publications/20260206_NP_TLP-CLEAR_SGDSN_VIGINUM_Fiche-Technique-Storm-1516_0.pdf> VIGINUM's 6 May 2025 report analysed **77 Storm-1516 operations**, calling it a mature MO capable of both reactive strikes and long-term pre-election reputation damage.
- **NOT VERIFIED (search-result headlines only).** Reports that **Édouard Philippe** (Meduza, 24 Jul 2026) and **Gabriel Attal** (Le Monde 5 Aug 2026; RFI 5 Aug 2026) were targeted, and Politico (18 Aug 2026) "France probes disinfo campaigns targeting 2027 election candidates", could not be fetched (paywall/bot blocks). Plausible but **unconfirmed**.
- **DEMONSTRATED (A, high).** The March 2026 French municipal interference picture was **not only Russian**: VIGINUM's **Rokh Solis** report (11 Jun 2026) describes four websites with coordinated TikTok/Instagram/X/Facebook relays, hostile to **La France Insoumise**, with technical markers indicating **Israeli actors including the influence firm Blackcore** — again with **very low visibility**.

**Germany 2025–26 — gaps.** **NOT VERIFIED.** No primary **BMI/BfV/Verfassungsschutz** attribution for the **Storm-1516 fake ballot-manipulation video** before the 23 Feb 2025 federal election, or for Sept 2026, was retrievable. ISD's recommendation that German intelligence "initiate proceedings in order to attribute Matryoshka" implies **no public German attribution had been made** by 4 Sep 2026. **(E, low)** Reuters (6 Aug 2026) and FT (6 Aug 2026) on Russian pre-election activity rest on **anonymous security sources (E)**; DW/Euronews fact-checks surfaced repeatedly but were not retrievable in full.

**Bottom line.** Both clusters show **high-volume, low-yield** operations. Portal Kombat/Pravda is structurally enormous but audience-marginal, its measurable effect landing on search/Wikipedia/LLM sourcing rather than voters. Matryoshka is a 2023-origin media-impersonation and fact-checker-flooding operation recycled into Germany's September 2026 state elections with **AI voice cloning**, near-industrial posting discipline, inauthentic engagement and **limited organic reach**. The defensible impact claims concern **contamination of the citation layer and newsroom workload**, not persuasion — and VIGINUM's own hedged language is the best evidence against overstating effects.
