## (a) Operation "Denver" (popularly INFEKTION): AIDS-from-Fort-Detrick, 1983–1990s

*Chain.* **Ambiguity:** a terrifying new syndrome with no known origin. **Injection:** on 17 July 1983 the pro-Soviet Indian daily *Patriot* (New Delhi) front-paged an anonymous letter, "AIDS May Invade India", ascribed to a "well-known American scientist and anthropologist", claiming AIDS was a Pentagon bioweapon made at Fort Detrick that would spread from Pakistan into India. **Adversarial:** on 7 Sept 1985 the KGB told Bulgarian State Security (Information Nr. 2955) it was running "a series of [active] measures" to establish that AIDS came from "secret experiments with a new type of biological weapon by the secret services of the USA and the Pentagon"; on 30 Oct 1985 *Literaturnaya Gazeta* ran Zapevalov's "Panic in the West", recycling *Patriot* while hiding its anonymity and 1983 date. The Stasi HVA/X registered the campaign on 17 July 1986 as Operation "Denver" ("INFEKTION" is ex-officer Bohnsack's recollection, not in the files) and in Sept 1986 planned to hand the Bulgarians a "scientific study" — the Segal Report — issued as the Harare Non-Aligned summit brochure "AIDS: USA home-made evil, NOT out of AFRICA". **Mediated:** TASS/Novosti replay and paid placements; Kuwait, Bahrain, Finland, Sweden and Peru reprinted Zapevalov; State counted **≥32 Soviet media appearances in Jan–Jun 1987 alone**; per CIA history the story reached **80 countries, 200+ periodicals, 25 languages** by late 1987. **Organic:** it fused with earlier Western theories (the *New York Native* sought a congressional inquiry, Nov 1986) and real grievances — Tuskegee, racism, US bases. North Korea ran its own anti-GI AIDS campaign; an "ethnic weapon" variant was grafted on. **Collapse:** 23 Oct 1987 — Shultz told Gorbachev Moscow was peddling "bum dope"; the Soviet Academy of Sciences disavowed the thesis via *Izvestia*; in summer 1988 Academy president Pokrovskiy said "not a single Soviet scientist… shares this position". Yet *Sovetskaya Rossiya* defended "different views" the same day, Radio Moscow aired an uncritical Segal interview on 13 Feb 1988, and 1989 still produced a dozen-plus reports worldwide. In 1992 SVR director Primakov confirmed KGB authorship.

*Actors/type:* KGB Service A, Stasi HVA/X, Bulgarian KDS, Novosti, the Segals, *Patriot* — adversarial-state, laundered via mediated third-country placements, then organic. *Persist:* foreign datelines, a scientific veneer and authentic grievances, with no rebuttal reaching the receiving audiences; it ended as policy only when Moscow needed US scientific cooperation — the audience was never persuaded. *Legacy:* in 2014 Russian outlets substituted Ebola for HIV, claiming the US, Britain and apartheid South Africa engineered Ebola to kill black Africans; the 2018 "US biolab in Georgia" story reused the *Patriot* frame; 2020–22 "US bioweapons labs in Ukraine" extended it. Chigwedere et al. estimated ~330,000 excess South African deaths from delayed ARV rollout; Bogart & Thorburn found ~50% of African-American respondents endorsed man-made-AIDS beliefs. *Contested:* the codename; *Patriot* letter authorship (State 1987: its "existence has not been confirmed"); Segal's agency (Stasi "contact person" "Diagnosis"; ~90% of HVA files destroyed); the Primakov admission is second-hand. **Soviet "Ebola" is documented at grade A/B; an "Agent Orange" fabrication is NOT** — the documented item is the Soviet charge that the US used chemical weapons in Vietnam and supplied the Afghan resistance: amplification of a real dioxin programme, not manufacture.

## (b) "Yellow rain", 1975–present: a contested allegation that outlived its evidence

*Chain.* **Ambiguity:** from 1975 Hmong, then Khmer, refugees reported air-dropped "yellow rain" — sticky liquid, powders, smoke — with seizures, blindness and bleeding; descriptions were heterogeneous, mycotoxicoses were endemic and laboratory chains of custody were weak. **Injection:** on 13 Sept 1981 Secretary of State Haig stated in Berlin that the US had "physical evidence" of "abnormally high levels of three potent mycotoxins… not indigenous to the region"; the evidence went to the UN Group of Experts on 14 Sept with a Stoessel press backgrounder; State's Special Report No. 98 (1982) and its Update formalised the charge that the USSR supplied T-2 toxin to Laos, Kampuchea and Afghanistan. A 1997 US Army Medical Department textbook asserted >10,000 deaths. **Adversarial (mirror-image):** Moscow called it a "big lie" and counter-accused the US of chemical warfare in Vietnam and of supplying chemical weapons to the Afghan resistance. **Scientific contest:** Mirocha (Minnesota) reported T-2/HT-2/DAS in blood, urine, tissue and yellow powder (1983); Rosen & Rosen (1982) found four *Fusarium* mycotoxins plus "synthetic material". Against this, Meselson's Harvard team went to Laos in 1983: Nowicke & Meselson's *Nature* palynology (1984) found the droplets were overwhelmingly pollen with protein digested out and shells intact — honeybee faeces — each droplet carrying a *different* mix of locally typical pollen, as many bees would leave, not a manufactured aerosol. British, French and Swedish labs found pollen and no mycotoxins; trichothecenes clear from blood within hours, making late detections implausible. The 1982 UN inquiry (A/37/259) was inconclusive, finding only the "possible use of some sort of toxic chemical substance in some instances". A 1989 *JAMA* re-analysis found "marked inconsistencies" and leading questions in the refugee interviews; in a 1982 field test most Hmong interviewees misidentified authentic bee droppings as the weapon.

*Actors/type:* Haig and State/ACDA, the UN Group of Experts, Mirocha, Meselson/Nowicke/Guillemin, the Soviet and Vietnamese governments, the *WSJ* editorial page, DTRA — political first, mediated second, organic third. *Persist:* the peer-reviewed literature turned decisively against the claim (Barss 1992; *JAMA* 1997 "widely regarded as erroneous"; Annual Review of Phytopathology 2003 "largely discredited"), but the **political** claim did not collapse — no US retraction; Army textbooks (1997, 2007) kept it; DTRA held it unresolved and that a trichothecene weapon "may never be unequivocally proved"; the *WSJ* in 2003 accused Meselson of "excusing away evidence of Soviet violations" and carried "yellow rain" into the Iraq WMD case. The strongest reverse reading is Katz & Singer (2007): the balance supported *some* chemical/biological attack in Southeast Asia in the late 1970s–early 1980s, but could not identify agent, intent or source. It re-ignited as memory: a bee-pollen release at Sangrampur, India (2002) triggered a chemical-attack scare; pollen fell on Sofia on 23 May 2015 and was read as an attack amid Bulgaria–Russia tension; a 2012 *Radiolab* episode on Hmong testimony produced a backlash and an on-air apology. *Explicitly contested:* whether trichothecene weapons were used (formally unresolved); whether US insistence was sincere assessment or political instrumentation; CS-positive samples suggesting US-origin contamination. **The 1984 ABC documentary could not be verified — do not cite it.**

## (c) 2001 anthrax letters, the hoax wave, and ricin copycats

**Real attack (kept separate from the amplification).** Four letters with Trenton NJ postmarks of 18 Sept and 9 Oct 2001 went to NBC News, the *New York Post*, and Senators Daschle and Leahy; American Media Inc. (Boca Raton) was contaminated. Officially **22 cases (11 inhalational, 11 cutaneous) and 5 deaths** — Stevens (5 Oct), Morris (21 Oct), Curseen (22 Oct), Nguyen (31 Oct), Lundgren (21 Nov). Brentwood and Hamilton facilities closed. Amerithrax concluded in 2010 naming Bruce Ivins; the 2011 National Academy of Sciences review criticised the certainty of the forensic attribution.

**Hoax-amplified response.** Five people died; the *response* ran on fear of a mass mail-borne campaign, and the hoax wave is what scaled vigilance. White-powder/anthrax-threat incidents in Oct–Dec 2001 number in the thousands to tens of thousands globally by commonly quoted FBI-derived figures — but **published totals are inconsistent (roughly 2,500 to 30,000, varying by jurisdiction, definition and window) and no single authoritative count was verified here**. The robust point does not depend on the number: hoaxes exceeded real letters by orders of magnitude, and each consumed hazmat response, building closure, antibiotic-prophylaxis decisions and laboratory capacity. The **response was calibrated to the ambiguity, not the event**. Later cases: the 2003 ricin letter at Greenville SC and the 2004 ricin find in the Dirksen Senate Office Building; the 2013 ricin letters (Richardson; Dutschke); the 2014 letter to Obama; the **2018 ricin letters** to Trump, Pompeo and Mattis (arrest: Navy veteran William Clyde Allen III). None re-scaled the 2001 response — a natural dose-response experiment. *Actors/type:* the (contested) perpetrator, FBI/USPS/CDC/EPA, Congress, media — organic/mass-media plus political. *Legacy (dated):* Public Health Security and Bioterrorism Preparedness and Response Act signed **12 June 2002** (PL 107-188); BioWatch deployment from **2003**; Project BioShield **2004**; USPS irradiation/sanitisation and dispersal of mail processing from Brentwood. *Persist:* the select-agent/BioWatch architecture proved durable and resistant to GAO criticism. *Collapse:* the hoax wave faded as attention shifted to Iraq.

## (d) Radiological and chemical scare campaigns

**Litvinenko (real, adversarial, validated).** Po-210 ingested in the Millennium Hotel's Pine Bar on 1 Nov 2006; death 23 Nov. HPA-RPD-067: **753 people** urine-tested, **139** above the reporting level, **36** dosed 1–6 mSv and **17** ≥6 mSv (highest ≈100 mSv), with **664 people across 52 countries** flagged for follow-up; police examined **60+ scenes** and made 40+ mutual legal assistance requests to 15+ countries. The Owen inquiry (reported **21 Jan 2016**) found Lugovoi and Kovtun killed him and that the FSB operation was "probably approved" by FSB head Patrushev and President Putin; *Carter v. Russia* (no. 20914/07, ECHR, **21 Sept 2021**) held Russia responsible. The popular "40+ contaminated locations" figure understates the verified "60+ scenes". Strongest grade-A radiological-attribution case — validation, not ambiguity. **No verified post-2006 "polonium copycat wave" exists**: a Feb 2026 Punjab bomb-hoax email threatening Chief Minister Mann was exposed as false, and Kara-Murza's 2015/2017 poisonings are well evidenced but the agent is not publicly confirmed as polonium.

**Padilla (2002): the dirty bomb as political amplification.** Arrested at O'Hare on 8 May 2002 on a material-witness warrant; on 10 June 2002 Ashcroft announced from Moscow an al-Qaeda dirty-bomb plot. The **22 Nov 2005 superseding indictment carried 11 counts and no radiological charge**; conviction 2007, sentence 17 years 4 months (Jan 2008), resentenced to **21 years in Sept 2014**. By June 2004 DOJ was describing the device as uranium in explosives, which physicists told CBS could not be an RDD — an official claim **downgraded by later official briefing**. The case underwrote a durable RDD-vigilance build-out (portal monitors, Securing the Cities, TOPOFF 2 in May 2003) whose central premise the case never evidenced.

**Hoax radiological incidents.** Verified exemplars: **14–15 June 2017**, Port of Charleston/Wando terminal — a caller's "dirty bomb" claim evacuated the terminal and the FBI/Coast Guard found nothing aboard the *Maersk Memphis*; **Feb–Mar 2010**, Middleboro MA — "radioactive" canisters declared a hoax; **11 June 2026**, the Pentagon — an anthrax-sensor false alarm caused a multi-hour lockdown and partial evacuation. Consequence profile is consistent: short evacuation, multi-agency search, no injury, rare prosecution. **No government or peer-reviewed aggregate count of radiological hoaxes exists**; the IAEA Incident and Trafficking Database counts trafficking and possession, not hoaxes — treat any "wave" claim as unsupported.

**Zaporizhzhia (2022–26).** The IAEA Support and Assistance Mission arrived on **1 Sept 2022** and has stayed continuously — the strongest institutional anchor. All six units are in cold shutdown (IAEA welcomed this 13 April 2024). The dominant recurring incident class is **loss of off-site power** (diesel-generator operation) from 330 kV line damage; Russia and Ukraine trade attribution for strikes, including a turbine-building drone strike asserted on 31 May 2026. The IAEA brokered local ceasefires to permit line repairs; off-site power was restored in early September 2026. The best available count is **25 complete off-site power losses since the conflict began, 13 of them in the four months to August 2026**, with the plant down from ten off-site lines to a single 330 kV connection (*Arms Control Today*, Sept 2026, citing the IAEA) — this supersedes the "24th loss" press headline.

**Russian "Ukrainian dirty bomb", Oct 2022.** 23 Oct: state media asserted Ukraine was near completing an RDD and Shoigu called US, UK, French and Turkish counterparts; 24 Oct: Gerasimov called US and UK counterparts and Russia's UN ambassador wrote to the Secretary-General calling Ukrainian use "nuclear terrorism"; 26 Oct: Putin repeated the claim while overseeing the Grom nuclear exercise; 28 Oct: the last explicit warning, then the narrative stopped abruptly. The P3 foreign ministers rejected it as a pretext (24 Oct); NATO's Stoltenberg called it "absurd". Ukraine invited inspection, and on **3 Nov 2022** the IAEA reported that inspectors at the Kyiv Institute for Nuclear Research, Zhovti Kody and Dnipro found **no indications of undeclared nuclear activities or materials**, with unfettered access. An adversarial amplification that failed to validate — its main effect was Western vigilance about Russian false-flag intent.

**Syria/Ghouta 2013 and after.** UN Doc **S/2013/553** (13 Sept 2013) found "clear and convincing evidence" of sarin in surface-to-surface rockets at Ein Tarma, Moadamiyah and Zamalka — but its mandate excluded attribution, and that gap is the structural source of the sustained ambiguity. The US Government Assessment of **30 Aug 2013** (≥1,400 killed) blamed the government; Russia advanced an opposition-attribution alternative. The OPCW-UN JIM (UNSC res. 2235, 2015) attributed **Khan Sheikhoun (S/2017/904)** and **Ltamenah (S/2018/202)** to Syrian forces; Russia vetoed JIM renewal in Nov 2017. The OPCW's Investigation and Identification Team then attributed Douma, with a fifth report in Jan 2026. The 2018 Douma leaks and "staged attack" narrative corroded the OPCW's own credibility; the Conference suspended Syria's rights in **2021**, then reinstated them on **9 July 2026** after Assad's fall. **Ambiguity was sustained by adversarial contestation of evidence chains, not fabrication — and it collapsed only when the sponsoring state fell.** Since 2023 the pattern is *pre-emptive attribution of an attack not yet occurred*: Russian messaging that Ukraine will stage a radiological provocation, and 2026 Lithuanian (8 July) and Polish/Baltic (10 Aug) warnings of a Russian drone "false flag" against NATO infrastructure.

*Cross-case pattern:* real act (Litvinenko) → validated; official claim (Padilla) → downgraded by later official briefing; adversarial narrative (dirty bomb 2022) → refuted by third-party inspection; adversarial contestation (Syria) → resolved only by regime change.

## (e) Infrastructure scare operations and hybrid-sabotage ambiguity, 2022–2026

**Nord Stream 1/2, 26 Sept 2022 — ambiguity as the payload.** Four of six strings ruptured in the Danish/Swedish EEZ. The physical act was strategically near-inert: Nord Stream 2 was never operational and Nord Stream 1 had been throttled to zero, so no EU consumer lost gas that winter. What moved was the meaning. No actor claimed it; competing narratives blamed Moscow, Washington and a Ukrainian team. Sweden closed its probe on 7 Feb 2024 (jurisdiction) and Denmark on 26 Feb 2024 (concluding deliberate sabotage but "not enough grounds"), both handing material to Germany — itself feeding a "cover-up" narrative. German prosecutors then moved: Serhii Kuznetsov's extradition was approved 27 Nov 2025, diver Volodymyr Zhuravlev was arrested in Croatia on 19 Aug 2026 with extradition approved 24 Sept 2026, and a July 2026 indictment alleged a Ukrainian state link; Kyiv denies involvement. **The disruption came overwhelmingly from the ambiguity and reporting** — it reshaped German energy policy, Berlin–Kyiv ties and domestic German politics. Persistence factor: no final conviction, so the ambiguity self-renews.

**Baltic cables and pipelines, 2023–2026.** *Balticconnector* (8 Oct 2023): Finnish NBI recovered the *Newnew Polar Bear*'s anchor on 24 Oct 2023, matched paint, and stated the mechanism was mechanical anchor drag — while explicitly saying intent was unknown; in Aug 2024 China circulated an internal finding of storm-caused accidental drag that Estonia's prosecutor called inadmissible; the Hong Kong captain was detained in May 2025 and pleaded not guilty in Feb 2026, the case still running. The Nov 2024 cuts (BCS East-West Interlink and C-Lion1, 17–18 Nov) drew same-day "sabotage suspected" headlines; the *Yi Peng 3* was released in Dec 2024 with no charges and US intelligence assessed the cuts as not deliberate. The *Eagle S* (25 Dec 2024) dragged anchor ≥90 km, damaging Estlink 2 and four telecom cables: Finland's district court dismissed the case on 3 Oct 2025 for lack of jurisdiction, and the Helsinki Court of Appeal reversed on **27 Aug 2026**, holding that Finland is competent and reopening it, with civil claims near €105m. Quantified context (Windward, via Reuters, Sept 2026): Baltic "drifting" fell 5% to 526 vessels in 2025 while North Sea drifting rose 52% and South China Sea 88%; **≥11 Baltic cables were damaged or cut between Oct 2023 and end-2025**. The physical outages were brief; **the disruption was the ambiguity**, which produced NATO's Baltic Sentry (14 Jan 2025) and a "shadow fleet" legal doctrine.

**GNSS jamming and spoofing, 2024–26.** In late April 2024 Estonia blamed Russia for interference that forced Finnair to suspend Tartu flights for about a month — the first time commercial scheduling visibly changed. Lithuania warned in May 2026 that Russian spoofing reaches ~450 km from Kaliningrad. The widely circulated "1,500 flights a day" figure is single-source and unverified.

## COUNTEREXAMPLES — scares that conspicuously failed to take hold

1. **New Jersey drone sightings, Nov–Dec 2024.** The FBI received **>5,000 reported sightings in a few weeks**, with congressional pressure and real public panic. On **17 Dec 2024** DHS/FBI/FAA/DoD jointly assessed them as "lawful commercial drones, hobbyist drones, and law enforcement drones, as well as manned fixed-wing aircraft, helicopters, and stars mistakenly reported as drones", with "nothing anomalous" — a high-report, low-yield ambiguity that collapsed once federal technical collection was applied. **A** https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/dhs-fbi-faa-dod-joint-statement-ongoing-response-reported-drone-sightings
2. **INFEKTION inside the West.** The campaign that reshaped belief in parts of Africa and South Asia was rejected almost immediately by Western *and* Soviet virologists. Collapse mechanism: a credible independent validator with access to the same audiences.
3. **Yellow rain.** Peer-reviewed pollen forensics was reproducible from the samples themselves, so rejection held in the literature even as the political claim survived — replicable technique, not institutional authority.
4. **Alleged Iraqi "yellow rain" (2002–03).** The claim failed because Iraq had purified only ~20 ml of trichothecene and never weaponised it — an ambiguity resolved *against* the amplifying party by post-hoc inspection.
5. **Russian "Ukrainian dirty bomb", Oct 2022.** Raised at the UNSC by a permanent member, yet IAEA inspection found no undeclared activity — the amplification boomeranged into vigilance about Russia.
6. **2013–2018 ricin letters.** Real crimes, real prosecutions, proportionate response; no re-scaling of national mobilisation.

**Pattern.** Scares collapse when (i) an independent, technically credible validator can inspect the physical evidence; (ii) no second, independently generated data stream sustains the ambiguity; (iii) the amplifying actor needs something from the audience it is deceiving. They persist when the claim travels faster than the rebuttal and the rebuttal never reaches the original audience.
