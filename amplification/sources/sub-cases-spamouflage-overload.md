25 Sep 2026. Defensive framing. Grades: **A** primary/official, **B** peer-reviewed/investigative, **C** reputable news, **D** vendor/think-tank, **E** unverified. Tags: DEMONSTRATED / CLAIMED / INFERRED.

## 1. OPERATION OVERLOAD — PRO-RUSSIAN (resolved)

**DEMONSTRATED — naming [A, high].** Check First/Reset.Tech explicitly **renamed** it ("we deemed it necessary to change the name … and called it **Operation Overload**"), previously tracked as **"Matryoshka"** by the Russian activist group **Antibot4Navalny** with AFP; VIGINUM records the same equivalence. `checkfirst.network/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Operation_Overload_WEB.pdf`; `sgdsn.gouv.fr/files/files/20240611_NP_SGDSN_VIGINUM_Matriochka_EN_VF.pdf`.

**DEMONSTRATED — activity [A, high].** Objective: "to target fact-checkers, newsrooms, and researchers globally … to deplete their resources and exploit credible information ecosystems to disseminate the Kremlin's political agenda." On X since at least Aug/Sep 2023; VIGINUM counts **≥90 successive operations**. TTPs: "content amalgamation," impersonating media/individuals, exploiting real events, plus a hidden **email layer**.

**DEMONSTRATED — scale [A, high].** **"Over 800 organisations were targeted"** via fake X accounts; **200+ emails** collected with **20+ media organisations**; coordinated Russian-language **Telegram** seeding channels; **100 inauthentic anonymous accounts** as amplifiers; also VKontakte, Odnoklassniki, Kremlin-aligned sites and the **"Pravda" network**. Main targets **France and Germany**. Check First: **X consistently failed to curb the network**. VIGINUM: **500+ X accounts targeted**, incl. **40+ French**; accounts bought from a vendor; AI-generated profile pictures.

**INFERRED.** Russian origin rests on narrative alignment and infrastructure, not a document proving direction (Check First says "clear indicators of" FIMI). High confidence on Russian origin; *degree* of state control unestablished.

## 3. SPAMOUFLAGE / DRAGONBRIDGE (PRC-linked)

**DEMONSTRATED — attribution [A/D, high].** Google TAG: DRAGONBRIDGE ("Spamouflage Dragon"), a "spammy influence network linked to the … (PRC)" (`blog.google/threat-analysis-group/google-disrupted-dragonbridge-activity-q1-2024/`). Graphika has tracked it since 2019; aliases **Spamouflage, Dragonbridge, Taizi Flood, Empire Dragon** (`graphika.com/reports/ore-else`, 30 Jul 2026).

**DEMONSTRATED — Google takedown scale [A, high].** **2023:** >**65,000** instances; of >**57,000** channels disabled **80% had zero subscribers**; of >**900,000** videos suspended **65%+ had <100 views, 30% zero views**. **Q1 2024:** >**10,000**; lifetime >**175,000**. **Q4 2024:** **10,291 / 6,318 / 5,028** channels + 2 blogs; **206 domains blocked** from Google News/Discover (tied to "a technology and marketing company" pushing pro-PRC content incl. 2024 Taiwan election material). **Q3 2025:** **6,484 / 2,533 / 2,497** + 1 blog + 3 domains. **Q4 2025:** **6,280 / 3,715 / 105**. **Q2 2026:** **1,763** channels (Apr); **22 domains + 12 channels** (May); Google credits an **Atlantic Council DFRLab lead** in a related Bulgaria case. Sources: `blog.google/threat-analysis-group/tag-bulletin-q*-20**/`; `blog.google/security/influence-operations-bulletin-q2-2026/`. Q1 2026 bulletin exists but could not be fetched.

**DEMONSTRATED — cross-platform [A/D, high].** TAG documents the pre-Taiwan-election "secret history" narrative on **X, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, Medium and smaller forums/bulletin boards**, plus comments on legitimate YouTube videos; Graphika adds **Parler and Tumblr** (2026). **Negative finding: no substantial Telegram presence confirmed.**

**DEMONSTRATED — generative AI [A/D, high].** Jan 2024 Taiwan campaign = "the largest observed DRAGONBRIDGE campaign using generative artificial intelligence to date": **AI-generated news hosts, synthetic audio and avatars**, thousands of videos/comments around a false "secret history" document; quality "varied widely," some "appearing realistic at first glance." Earlier: AI footage (Graphika 2022), AI images (Mandiant Mar 2023), AI "news presenter" (May 2023). In 2026, **62 accounts** posted "almost certainly AI-generated text and images" from **25 Jun 2026**, targeting journalists covering Chile's "Operation High Voltage." *INFERRED:* TAG names **no** commercial model — model attribution unproven.

**DEMONSTRATED — reach (key finding) [A/D, high].** TAG: it "achieves practically no organic engagement from real viewers"; engagement "was almost entirely inauthentic, coming from other DRAGONBRIDGE accounts," and AI video "has not resulted in significantly higher engagement." Graphika agrees, with one exception: an inauthentic media-outlet persona on **TikTok** whose July 2024 video had **1.5 million views**; it found **15 Spamouflage accounts on X and one on TikTok** posing as U.S. citizens (`graphika.com/reports/the-americans`). *INFERRED:* that outlier is the clearest documented mass-reach instance; it does not show sustained audience capture.

**CLAIMED — Meta takedowns [C, medium; primary unretrieved].** Meta's Aug 2023 action was reported as its **largest-ever single takedown** of a Chinese operation — thousands of Facebook/Instagram accounts and pages, Chinese law-enforcement-linked [NYT, Time, The Record, CyberScoop, DW, 29–31 Aug 2023]. **Exact counts UNVERIFIED.**

**2025–26 developments [A/D, high].** Anti-journalist harassment (62 accounts, Chile, Jun 2026). New **transnational-repression tactic**: manipulated event flyers on Facebook/X, Jun–Jul 2026, disrupting anti-CPC events in the U.S./Europe — first observed; Graphika assesses it "could likely be used for other attempts at transnational repression or election interference" (`graphika.com/reports/save-the-date-for-spamouflage`, 13 Jul 2026). Quarterly PRC-linked terminations continue through Q2 2026; OpenAI hosts `.../disrupting-malicious-uses-of-ai-spamouflage/` (sitemap-confirmed; body unretrieved).

## 5. Bottom line
- **Keep the two 2024 cases apart.** Overload is pro-Russian and anti-fact-checker (Check First/Reset.Tech; VIGINUM's *Matriochka*), notable for email flooding and platform non-enforcement on X. The Israeli commercial case is OpenAI's **Zero Zeno**, press-attributed to **STOIC**, distinct from OpenAI's **Bad Grammar**.
- Spamouflage is **high-volume, low-reach, persistent**; production scale is a poor proxy for influence. AI raised volume and quality **without** demonstrably raising authentic engagement (one TikTok outlier aside).
- Cross-cutting lesson: **attribution fragility** — firm- and state-level links often rest on vendor disclosures hard to re-verify publicly.
