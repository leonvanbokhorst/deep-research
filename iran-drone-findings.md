# Iranian Drone and Loitering-Munition Proliferation and Proxy Use

**Compiled 25 September 2026.** Tags: **[D]**=DEMONSTRATED (physical/official documentation), **[C]**=CLAIMED (government, belligerent or vendor assertion), **[I]**=INFERRED. Confidence H/M/L. Cost and interception figures are frequently partisan and are flagged.

## 1. Shahed family: cost, range/payload, recipients, Alabuga

**Shahed-131/136 (Geran-1/2)** are delta-wing one-way-attack drones using GNSS/INS; the 136 carries ~40–50 kg and ranges ~1,000–2,500 km [D] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahed_drones ; https://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2025/04/iran-displays-missiles-and-drones-at-baghdad-military-expo.php . **Cost is contested.** The common **$20,000–$50,000** figure is [C] and, per Defence Express, unrealistic: leaked 2022 Russian buys were **$370k/unit (2,000 order), $290k, then $193k for the 6,000-unit order**, with Russian domestic cost ~**$70k** by 2025 — https://en.defence-ua.com/analysis/shahed_136_really_cost_20_50k_iran_sold_them_to_russia_for_200_300k_in_2022_actual_price_far_higher-17764.html . Alabuga's predicted unit cost was **$48k, rising to ~$80k by April 2024** [C] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelabuga_drone_factory .

**Shahed-238 (Geran-3)**: jet, 300–600 km/h, 50–90 kg, reported 1,000–2,500 km, with GNSS/INS, EO/IR and radar-homing (anti-radiation) variants; reported export interest **$900k–$1.4M** [C] — https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/ ; https://takshashila.org.in/content/publications/Shahed-Iran-Drone-Strategy-14042026.html . **Shahed-107** is a loitering/reconnaissance munition (~1,500 km, ~15 kg). **Mohajer-6/10** (2,000 km, 300 kg) and **Arash-2** (~1,000–1,600 km) round out exports [C] — https://www.longwarjournal.org/archives/2025/04/iran-displays-missiles-and-drones-at-baghdad-military-expo.php ; https://en.defence-ua.com/analysis/iranian_arash_2_kamikaze_drones_capabilities_and_specifications-4555.html .

**Recipients.** Russia (licensed Geran production); **Sudan** — AFRICOM/Planet Labs imagery shows **Mohajer-6 and a ground-control vehicle at Wadi Sayyidna**, "Iran is supplying weaponized drones to the SAF" [D presence, H; supplier intent C, M] — https://adf-magazine.com/2024/02/safs-use-of-iranian-drones-threatens-to-destabilize-region/ ; **Venezuela** — OFAC (30 Dec 2025): QAI Mohajer-series assembled by EANSA and re-branded **ANSU-100/ANSU-series** since 2006 [D, primary, H] — https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0347 ; Armenia (~$500M deal incl. drones) [C] — https://takshashila.org.in/content/publications/Shahed-Iran-Drone-Strategy-14042026.html . **Ethiopia** [C, L] and **Belarus** (a "Nomad" Shahed-136 shown at the July 2024 Minsk parade [C, L] — https://www.armyrecognition.com/archives/archives-land-defense/land-defense-2024/belarus-starts-local-manufacturing-of-iranian-shahed-136-drones-as-nomad ) lack primary corroboration. **Tajikistan's 2022 Ababil-2 plant is contested** — RFE/RL (Mar 2026) quotes officials saying it does not exist [L] — https://www.rferl.org/a/iran-tajikistan-drone-factory-mystery-/33719984.html . **Algeria and other secondary-list names are unverified.**

**Alabuga.** The Yelabuga plant (Alabuga SEZ, Tatarstan) **opened July 2023** under a reported **$1.75bn franchise** for 6,000 Shahed-136s by Sept 2025, sited on the Kama for Caspian transport [C, well-sourced] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yelabuga_drone_factory ; https://isis-online.org/isis-reports/alabugas-shahed-136-geran-2-warheads-a-dangerous-escalation . CNN found **>5,700 produced Jan–Sep 2024**, fully localised by 2025 — https://www.cnn.com/2025/07/25/europe/russia-secretive-drone-factory-rapid-expansion-intl . ISIS reports a near-term target of **25,000 Shahed-136/year** plus a parallel **Izhevsk (Kupol/Almaz-Antey)** line [D production, H] — https://isis-online.org/isis-reports/major-developments-at-alabuga-sez-point-to-significant-expansion-in-military-drone-production .

## 2. Tower 22 (28 January 2024)

A one-way attack drone struck Tower 22 (Rukban, NE Jordan), killing three US soldiers and injuring 41–47 [D] — https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/article/3659809/3-us-service-members-killed-others-injured-in-jordan-following-drone-attack/ . Attribution: Deputy Pentagon Press Secretary Sabrina Singh said it "has the footprints of Kataib Hezbollah… We know that Iran is behind it," declining a final assessment [C, official] (same URL); the US formally blamed the **Islamic Resistance in Iraq** on 31 January [C] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_22_drone_attack . The type was indicated as a **Shahed** by a US official, never formally released [C, M] (same).

**Interceptor failure** [C, via two anonymous US officials and the Army investigation]: the drone approached **low** while a **US drone was returning** and "may have been let pass by mistake… there was no effort to shoot down the enemy drone" — https://www.nzherald.co.nz/world/enemy-drone-that-killed-us-troops-in-jordan-was-mistaken-for-a-us-drone/T363HSGXARBKTHUJDUJZI2LLAM/ . The command investigation found the BDOC was distracted by a **Scan Eagle recovery**, dismissed southern radar tracks as "birds or trash," and assumed the southern approach safe — https://b17news.com/confusion-gripped-us-base-defenders-just-before-a-flying-bomb-struck-a-deadly-blow-at-tower-22-investigation-reveals/ . **Response:** 2 February 2024 strikes hit 85 targets at seven sites with 125 precision munitions and B-1Bs [C, official] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_22_drone_attack .

## 3. Kadhimi residence attack (7 November 2021)

Explosive-laden drone(s) attacked PM al-Kadhimi's Green Zone residence; he was unharmed, guards injured [D] — https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/world-middle-east-59195399 . **Numbers differ:** the Iraqi Armed Forces said one drone attacked and **two were shot down** (three total), while some reports cited two armed drones [contested] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi ; https://jinsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Kadhimi-Drone-Attack-NatSec-Brief-1.pdf . Iraqi agencies called them **quadcopters** [C]. **No group claimed responsibility.** On 8 November anonymous officials and militia sources told Reuters it was **Kata'ib Hezbollah or Asaib Ahl al-Haq**, using Iran-made weapons [ALLEGED, anonymous, L] — https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/drone-attack-targets-iraq-pm-who-escapes-unhurt-iraq-military-2021-11-07/ . US officials **doubted Tehran directly ordered it** while agreeing militias were likely [C] — https://www.nbcnews.com/news/world/iran-likely-did-not-order-drone-attack-iraqi-prime-minister-n1283832 . Militias circulated a false-flag claim built on a **forged image**. **Net: attack D; perpetrator I, low.**

## 4. Proxy use and Iran's direct strikes

US bases in Iraq/Syria were attacked **150–170+ times** (Oct 2023–Jan 2024) [contested: DoD ">150", Economist 160, ISW >170] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_22_drone_attack . **Islamic Resistance in Iraq vs Israel:** 312 claimed attacks (Nov 2023–Nov 2024), only **89 reached Israeli territory, 4 struck**; a 3 Oct 2024 Golan strike killed two IDF soldiers [C/D] — https://israel-alma.org/the-islamic-resistance-in-iraq-attacks-against-israel-november-2-2023-to-november-20-2024/ .

**Iran's direct strikes:** April 2024 "True Promise" used ~170 drones plus 30+ cruise and 120+ ballistic missiles; Israel claimed **99% intercepted** while Maariv reported **84%** [C, partisan] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/April_2024_Iranian_strikes_on_Israel . October 2024 "True Promise II" used ~180–200 ballistic missiles with demonstrated Nevatim/Tel Nof impacts. June 2025 Twelve-Day War opened with **100+ Shaheds** on 13 June. In the **2026 war (from 28 Feb)**, CSIS records the **UAE alone absorbing 1,422 drones + 246 missiles (1–8 March)**; drones were **~71% of all recorded strikes**; the opening wave was 867 drones/339 missiles, then 190–392 strikes/day — https://www.csis.org/analysis/unpacking-irans-drone-campaign-gulf-early-lessons-future-drone-warfare . An analyst estimate put daily drone launches falling **720→60 by 9 March** [C, L] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war . The US reportedly used **~65% of its 2,330 Patriot interceptors** Feb–Jul 2026 [C, M] (same). **All interception rates are belligerent claims with no independent battle-damage assessment.**

## 5. Reported Iranian drone activity in Europe, 2024–2026

**The drone-specific evidence is thin.** The 31 July 2025 **14-state statement** condemned Iranian intelligence attempts to **"kill, kidnap, and harass"** journalists, dissidents, Jewish citizens and officials, "increasingly collaborating with international criminal organizations" — but **does not mention drones** [primary, H] — https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2025/07/joint-statement-on-iranian-state-threat-activity-in-europe-and-north-america . German 2026 prosecutions charged suspects with **plotting to kill Jewish leaders for Iran** (arson/surveillance), not drones [CHARGED] — https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/europe/germany-charges-plotting-jewish-iran-b2981228.html . Think-tank mapping of Iranian external operations records assassinations, abductions and surveillance via criminal proxies, with no established drone pattern [I] — https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/iranian-external-operations-europe-criminal-connection . Where drones did feature — the **September 2025 Danish/Norwegian airport incursions** — attribution pointed to **Russia, not Iran** [C]. **I found no public European case of police seizing Iranian drone components in a plot context; treat such claims as ALLEGED/unverified (L).**

## 6. Exports, UN embargo and smuggling

**Legal status changed twice.** UNSCR 2231 drone/missile restrictions **lapsed 18 October 2023** — https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2023-11/news/un-missile-sanctions-iran-expire . The **E3 then triggered snapback on 28 August 2025**, and the UK FCDO confirms the **re-instatement on 27 September 2025 of Resolutions including 1929 (2010) — the arms-embargo instrument** — https://www.gov.uk/government/news/e3-joint-statement-on-iran-activation-of-the-snapback ; the US Treasury corroborates "the September 27, 2025, reimposition of United Nations sanctions on Iran" — https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0313 . **So a UN arms embargo on Iran is formally back in force as of September 2026** (asserted by the E3/US).

The separate **UNSCR 2216 Yemen embargo remains in force.** The Panel of Experts recorded **254 UAVs, 100 UAV engines and ~5 tons of precursors interdicted (Nov 2022–Jun 2023)**, with debris "consistent with… the Islamic Republic of Iran" [primary, H] — https://docs.un.org/en/s/2023/833 . Its **October 2025 report (S/2025/650)** records a June 2025 seizure of **>750 tons** including a **Shahed UAV, Ghadir anti-ship missile and Misagh MANPADS**, plus "**manuals in Farsi** and Iranian police DNA kits" [D, H] — https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?s=s/2025/650&l=en&t=pdf . Much seized materiel originated in **China** (drone kits, GPS modules), and **Shahn/Sarfayt (Oman–Yemen) land crossings** are now the primary route [D] (same). **Hezbollah** drone storage/manufacture rests on IDF claims recorded in UNIFIL S/2025/460 (Iran denies) [C, M] — https://unscol.unmissions.org/sites/default/files/s_2025_460-en.pdf . For Russia the main artery is the **Caspian route Amirabad/Anzali→Astrakhan** (~600 AIS gaps by Russian-flagged ships in three months) [D, M/H] — https://www.caspianpolicy.org/research/security-and-politics-program-spp/dark-deals-on-the-caspian-how-iran-ships-drones-to-russia . OFAC's 12 Nov 2025 action designated **32 entities** across Iran, UAE, Türkiye, China, Hong Kong, India, Germany and Ukraine supporting UAV/missile production [primary, H] — https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0313 . **Hamas/PIJ and named Iraqi-militia drone transfers were not verified to primary standard.**

## 7. Autonomy and AI: claims vs demonstration

**[D]:** GNSS/INS guidance; Shahed-238 EO/IR and radar-homing seekers; and, on Russian Gerans, **cameras including the Honpho TS130C-01 (built-in computer vision), 3G/4G telemetry with Raspberry Pi computers, and mesh-radio modems** that made the drone operator-controllable and mid-mission retaskable — https://www.csis.org/analysis/shahed-geran-how-russia-continues-reinvent-one-way-attack-drone ; https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/ . **[C]:** a **"Geran Seeker"** that "can automatically recognize, lock onto, and guide itself toward a target," with onboard AI after operator designation — asserted by Ukraine's defence-ministry adviser "Flash" (June 2026) [C, belligerent, M] — https://english.nv.ua/nation/geran-seeker-drone-variant-with-automatic-target-acquisition-revealed-50620724.html . Leaked Alabuga documents show interest in a **Shahed-236 "Seeker"** able to select targets in flight [C, leaked, M] — https://isis-online.org/isis-reports/alabugas-shahed-136-geran-2-warheads-a-dangerous-escalation . **No Iranian vendor specification substantiates "AI" on the Shahed-238 itself**; the framing comes mainly from Ukrainian official analysis of recovered Gerans. Full terminal autonomy on Iranian OWA drones remains **[I], L–M**.
