# Non-State Armed Group Drone & Autonomy Use: Evidence Assessment, 2014 – Sep 2026

*Method: DDG HTML was CAPTCHA-blocked; search ran via `r.jina.ai`-rendered DDG and direct retrieval of primary UN PDFs. Claims are tagged **DEMONSTRATED / CLAIMED / INFERRED**, with source quality and confidence.*

## 1. ISIS, Iraq/Syria 2014–2019

- **Industrialised bomb-dropping quadcopter programme.** CAR field teams documented an IS drone workshop in Ramadi, with armed-drone experimentation from Feb 2016 — **DEMONSTRATED**; primary (CAR), **high**. https://www.conflictarm.com/perspectives/islamic-states-weaponised-drones/
- **Scale and effect.** Peak spring 2017: **60 to >100 aerial drone bombing attacks per month** across Iraq and Syria; first lethal IED-drone Oct 2016 (two peshmerga killed); by Sept 2017 sightings fell to ~7/month. Believed to have killed **more than a dozen** and injured dozens. **DEMONSTRATED** (scale) / **CLAIMED** (casualties); primary (CTC West Point, Jul 2018), **high**/**medium**. https://ctc.westpoint.edu/islamic-state-drones-supply-scale-future-threats/
- **Sourcing/modification (general only).** Commercial quadcopters plus add-on munitions-release hardware via a layered global supply chain: a documented network used PayPal purchases from US/Canada vendors (Oct 2014–Aug 2015), shipped via Şanlıurfa, Turkey, under aliases. **DEMONSTRATED**; primary, **high**. https://www.nytimes.com/2017/01/31/world/middleeast/isis-drone-documents.html Fleet size is **unknown — evidence absent**.
- **Coalition response.** A **$700M crash counter-UAS programme**; EW/jamming, a "red no-fly zone" over Mosul for commercial drones, drone guns/nets, strikes on drone workshops. The claimed IS workaround (GPS foil/software tweaks) is explicitly **unverified** in CTC's text — **CLAIMED**, **medium**.

## 2. Al-Shabaab and ISIS-Somalia 2023–2026

- **Baseline to 2023.** Al-Shabaab used commercial UAVs mainly for **surveillance/propaganda**; offensive use unconfirmed. **DEMONSTRATED**; GNET, **medium**. https://gnet-research.org/2023/07/05/drone-use-by-violent-extremist-organisations-in-africa-a-case-study-of-al-shabaab/
- **Aug 2024 intercept.** Puntland seized **five FPV UAVs plus an explosives cache hidden in speakers** in Galkayo; 7 detained; press linked them to "kamikaze drones from Yemen intended for Al-Shabaab". The UN Panel recorded **no confirmation of weaponised UAV use** by Somali non-state groups, adding that the capability "do[es] not require … technology transfer from … the Houthis". **DEMONSTRATED** (seizure) / **CLAIMED** (Yemen origin); primary UN + press; **high** / **low**. S/2024/748 Annex 22: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4066421
- **ISIS-Somalia.** By late 2024 the Puntland faction fielded **>1,000 fighters (≥60% foreign) and over 100 UAVs**; on 31 Dec 2024 it attacked Puntland forces with 12 suicide bombers followed by a **wave of explosive-laden UAVs**. **DEMONSTRATED**; primary UN, **high**. S/2025/777 paras 36, 46: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4097699
- **Houthi–Shabaab nexus.** UN monitors report al-Shabaab leaders requesting "advanced weapons and training" from Ansar Allah in 2024; two Houthi engineers reportedly sent to Jilib for drone-modification training. **CLAIMED**; ISS analysis of UN reporting, **medium**. https://karenalleninternational.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Houthis-in-Somalia-friends-with-technological-benefits-ISS-Africa.pdf
- **Counterpoint.** A Jul 2025 policy paper records **no confirmed al-Shabaab strikes in 2024–25** (recon drones intercepted pre-impact, Galgaduud, Feb 2025), and CAR has **not seen FPV use by al-Shabaab**. Offensive al-Shabaab drone use is **INFERRED/aspirational, not demonstrated**. https://www.policycenter.ma/sites/default/files/2025-07/PP_24-25%20%28Niccola%20Milnes%20%26%20Rida%20Lyammouri%29_0.pdf

## 3. Hezbollah, Hamas, Houthi, Iraqi militias — technological baseline

- **Tier 1 — purpose-built military UAS.** Houthi **Qasef** (Iranian Ababil-T analogue, ~120 km range, 30 kg warhead), **Samad** series, plus Chinese-derived **Rujum/Rased** (Skywalker X-8 analogues). **DEMONSTRATED**; Orion Policy, **high**. https://orionpolicy.org/the-houthi-drone-supply-chain/
- **Tier 2 — modified commercial/FPV.** All known Houthi UAVs used **piston engines** until a Feb 2025 seizure of **JetCat P500/P550 jet engines** (~$7,890 vs $69,500 for a Quds cruise-missile engine) and **DJI Mavic 3 FPV** kits. **DEMONSTRATED** (seizure) / **INFERRED** (intended use); Sana'a Center, **medium-high**. https://sanaacenter.org/files/New_Technologies_in_Houthi_Drones_en.pdf
- **Hezbollah and Iraqi militias.** Hezbollah manufactures **fibre-optic FPV drones at ~$300–400 each**, 3D-printed with COTS electronics, after the Syrian land corridor was disrupted post-Assad (late 2024). They are explicitly **operator-piloted from the video feed and detonated on impact** — human-in-the-loop. Iraqi militias have used the same **fibre-optic FPV** pattern against US bases (Victory Base, Mar 2026). **CLAIMED/DEMONSTRATED**; Guardian plus trade press, **medium-high**/**medium**. https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/may/12/how-hezbollah-3d-printed-drones-challenging-israeli-military · https://cuashub.com/en/content/fiber-optic-strike-on-u-s-base-exposes-counter-drone-vulnerabilities/
- **Autonomy evidence: absent.** Across a 600+ incident ICCT dataset and the UNOCT/CAR survey of 21 states, no non-state machine-vision target selection was identified; systems are remote-piloted, FPV, or GPS-waypoint. **INFERRED (negative finding)**; peer-quality, **medium-high**. https://icct.nl/publication/comparative-study-non-state-violent-drone-use-middle-east

## 4. Proliferation channels

**(a) State transfers.** Iran→Houthis: matching serials on captured Qasef airframes, a V-10 gyroscope identical to one from an Iranian drone downed in South Sudan, and a 2016 Dubai-registered truck near Ma'rib carrying components for **6 Qasef-1 plus parts for 24 more**. **DEMONSTRATED**; primary UN/Orion, **high**. Sudan: **Iran→SAF** (Mohajer-6, Zajil-3), **UAE→RSF** (Chinese **Wing Loong II**, FH-95), **Turkey→Egypt→SAF** (Bayraktar TB2). **DEMONSTRATED/CLAIMED**; Critical Threats (AEI), **medium-high**. https://www.criticalthreats.org/wp-content/uploads/An-Inventory-of-Foreign-Drones-in-Sudan%E2%80%99s-Civil-War-1.pdf Libya: confirmed transfers include **STM Kargu-2**, Bayraktar TB2/AKINCI and Wing Loong I/II; in Jun 2024 Italy seized mis-declared **"Flying Loong (FL-1)"** UAV components bound for Benghazi, which China called scrap disaster-relief parts. **DEMONSTRATED**; primary UN Panel, **high**. https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?s=S/2024/914&l=en&t=pdf

**(b) Libya–Sahel corridor.** The Libya Panel identified **two primary Libya→RSF supply routes (air bridges and land routes)** active to June 2024, with LAAF facilitating RSF/SAF materiel movement. **Drone-specific Libya→Sahel transfers are INFERRED, not itemised in the 2024–25 Libya reports — evidence gap.** Sahel jihadist drone use is nonetheless real: **JNIM** went from first drone activity (Bandiagara, Sep 2023) to **>two dozen confirmed incidents, 82% since March 2025**, including FPV kamikaze strikes in Togo (Apr 2025, 5 soldiers) and Djibo/Timbuktu/Kabankoura; **FLA** used VTOL/FPV from Feb 2025; IS-Sahel used an FPV in Tillabéri (May 2025). **DEMONSTRATED**; Policy Center/ACLED, **medium-high**. https://www.policycenter.ma/sites/default/files/2025-07/PP_24-25%20%28Niccola%20Milnes%20%26%20Rida%20Lyammouri%29_0.pdf

**(c) Black markets/online vendors.** 52% of surveyed states reported **illicit trafficking** (postal shipments, smuggling routes); UAS and components are "widely available both online and in retail stores". A European civil aviation authority built a **5 kg-payload UAV purely from open online sources**, identifying the **electric engine** as the key chokepoint. **DEMONSTRATED**; primary (UNOCT/CAR), **high**. https://www.un.org/counterterrorism/sites/default/files/2024_unoct_car_global_report_web.pdf Evidence for **encrypted-app drone-component marketplaces is absent** from retrieved primary reporting — flagged, not asserted.

**(d) Captured stocks/reverse-engineering.** Houthi "hybrid" Qasef airframes combine Iranian internals with **locally built fibreglass airframes**, implying local manufacture under smuggling constraints. **DEMONSTRATED**; Orion/UN, **medium**. Claimed ISIS reverse-engineering of coalition drones is weakly evidenced — **evidence absent**.

**(e) Gaza/Lebanon.** With tunnels degraded, **drone-based weapons smuggling** emerged on the Egypt–Israel border: **10+ confirmed interceptions (Oct 2024–May 2025)**, mostly **DJI FlyCart 30 / Matrice 300 / Agras T30**, carrying **9–10 M16-type rifles per flight (~31–35 kg)**. Attribution to Palestinian militant groups is **CLAIMED, unverified**, and the tunnel-substitution thesis is explicitly **not established**. **DEMONSTRATED** (intercepts) / **INFERRED** (attribution); GNET/IDF-sourced, **medium**. https://gnet-research.org/2025/06/05/guns-by-drone-the-rise-of-drone-assisted-firearm-smuggling-on-israels-southern-border/ Lebanon resupply now runs through **local 3D-printed manufacture** rather than Iranian land transfer (§3).

## 5. Autonomous / AI-enabled targeting by non-state actors

**No non-state group has demonstrably fielded machine-vision terminal guidance or autonomous target selection.** ICCT's 600+ dataset found none; UNOCT/CAR found 3D printing for munitions release but "little current evidence" it is widespread; JNIM "offline AI"-enabled autonomous flight is **speculative and prescriptive**, not evidenced. Three caveats deserve weight.

1. **Autonomous lethal targeting is now being *designed* by irregular actors.** Anthropic's Sept 2026 threat report (case **GTG-27005**) documents likely freelance Russia-based actors building a "full-stack autonomous FPV kamikaze drone swarm": **shared swarm memory, an onboard small language model governing attack/observe/return behaviours, camera-based terminal guidance issuing the detonation call, and target classes including "person" — "without a human in the loop."** They trained a CV classifier on scraped Ukrainian combat footage using hardware-in-the-loop testing. Anthropic assesses a **small freelance team, not a state entity** (claimed, unverified, ties to Russia's Advanced Research Foundation/MoD). **DEMONSTRATED** (design work, vendor telemetry) / **NOT DEMONSTRATED** (fielding or use). Primary vendor threat-intel report, single-source; **medium-high** on design, **low** on employment. https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026
2. **Same report, GTG-87001:** a **Yemen-based** guided-weapons cell used Claude Code to write **GNC software, flight-control firmware and terminal guidance** for a rocket on a "commodity phone-class flight computer," ran a **live field test** (which appears to have failed), and modelled a >2,000 km ballistic missile. This is **AI-assisted weapons *development*, not onboard autonomy**. **DEMONSTRATED**; primary vendor, **medium-high**.
3. **The most-cited "autonomous kill" — STM Kargu-2, Libya 2021 — involved state-aligned forces and is contested.** The UN Panel described loitering munitions "programmed to attack targets without requiring data connectivity…a true 'fire, forget and find' capability", but the manufacturer has **never demonstrated** autonomous target *selection*, and marketing footage shows **a human operator selecting the target**. **CLAIMED, heavily hyped**; **low confidence**. https://lieber.westpoint.edu/kargu-2-autonomous-attack-drone-legal-ethical/

## 6. Defensive/legal picture and source quality

- **Hardest to counter:** fibre-optic FPV (unjammable, hard to detect — Hezbollah versus Iron Dome); low/slow small UAS exploiting **coverage seams**; cheap platforms versus expensive interceptors. CTC notes C-UAS guns are only as good as operator situational awareness and fixed systems leave gaps; the US spent **$700M** against an IS programme costing "thousands of dollars a month". **DEMONSTRATED**; CTC/Guardian, **medium-high**.
- **Legal/regulatory:** the Wassenaar Arrangement, MTCR and UNSCR 2370 (2017) guidelines are the main controls; states describe a permanent "cat and mouse" game. **DEMONSTRATED**; UNOCT/CAR, **high**.
- **Caution:** Houthi/Hezbollah/Iranian "AI", "autonomous swarm" and "self-targeting" claims are typically **vendor or partisan**; treat as **CLAIMED** until independently verified.

**Overall:** the dominant non-state mode remains **remote-piloted or GPS-waypoint** drones, increasingly **fibre-optic FPV** and locally 3D-printed. Genuine autonomy is **not demonstrated as operationally fielded** by any non-state actor; the credible frontier is **AI-assisted design of autonomous systems by irregular/freelance developers**.
