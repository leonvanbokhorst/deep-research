# UNSC Warns of Al-Shabaab’s Growing Interest in Drone Warfare

November 25, 2024

1 min read

The Panel of Experts’ report presented to the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) underscores the evolving threat from [Al-Shabaab](https://www.ftlsomalia.com/glossary/al-shabaab/), including its apparent shift towards offensive drone operations.

This progression reflects the group’s increasing sophistication in adapting new technologies for tactical purposes.

The recommendation for vigilance during the transition from the African Union Transition Mission in Somalia (ATMIS) to the AU Support and Stabilization Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM) aligns with concerns about maintaining operational effectiveness against such advancements.

Enhancing Somalia’s maritime security, as advised, will be crucial, especially as piracy and maritime crime remain ongoing challenges while strengthening its coast guard infrastructure and offshore patrols will address these vulnerabilities, improving the country’s overall security posture.
