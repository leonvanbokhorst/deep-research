# German drone-sighting episodes, 2024–2026: what was counted, what was confirmed, and who amplified it

**Compiled 25 September 2026.** Evidence key: **[V]** full text retrieved · **[H]** headline/RSS metadata only · **[U]** unverified · **[X]** premise contradicted.

## A) The BKA Lagebild and the official counts — with one important correction

**The headline figures in the brief belong to 2026, not 2025** **[X]**. The confidential BKA *Lagebild* reported by NDR/WDR/SZ and tagesschau on **3 September 2026** is dated to **end-August 2026** and its numbers are **2026 year-to-date** **[V]** (tagesschau.de, "BKA zählt bereits 165 mutmaßliche Sabotageakte", 03.09.2026, 16:00):

- **more than 165 suspected sabotage cases** nationwide ("mehr als 165 Sabotageverdachtsfälle … mit Stand Ende August"), explicitly including "Ausspähungssachverhalte ohne Tatmittel Drohne"; the document comes from the work of the **Gemeinsames Abwehrzentrum Hybrid (GAZ-Hybrid)**, inaugurated in Berlin in mid-June 2026;
- **747 suspicious drone incidents** registered January–end August 2026, in which **1,068 drones** were counted over critical infrastructure, military facilities and defence firms;
- **24 espionage investigation proceedings** opened by the Generalbundesanwalt and state focus prosecutors (twelve involving cyber-espionage), plus **40 further Prüfvorgänge**;
- **112 espionage-related crimes** recorded by federal and state police (60+ cases of "sicherheitsgefährdendes Abbilden", 26 suspected "Agententätigkeit zu Sabotagezwecken");
- espionage regional focus 2026: **Brandenburg 23**, **NRW 21**, **Schleswig-Holstein 21**, **Lower Saxony 15**;
- **2025 comparison: around 1,300 drone "Sachverhalte" in total** — i.e. the 747/1,068 figure is *lower* than the 2025 full-year total, not a 2025 figure.

Source **[V]**: https://www.tagesschau.de/investigativ/ndr-wdr/bka-lagebericht-sabotage-100.html

**DFS airport counts — confirmed [V].** "In ganz Deutschland waren es bis August **144** Vorfälle. Die Zahl dürfte noch steigen und die der Vorjahre **2024 (161 Vorfälle)** und **2023 (151)** übertreffen." Frankfurt was worst with **35** by Aug 2025 (vs ~20 in each prior year); Berlin had **5** in 2025 to that date, **20** in 2024 and **>15** in 2023. Decisively for causation: "Wer die Drohnen steuerte, geht aus dem Report der Flugsicherung nicht hervor. In vielen Fällen dürften **Hobby-Piloten** die Ursache sein" — and DFS records pilot reports and tower-controller determinations of *Behinderungen*, not confirmed drones.

## B) The "technische Fehlfunktionen" lead — **NOT VERIFIED as stated**, but the phrase is real and I found its actual source

The specific claim — that a Bundestag committee or a government answer concluded that **a significant share of reported drone sightings at German airports/sites were technical malfunctions or misidentifications** — is **NOT VERIFIED**. Three findings:

1. **The phrase exists, but about drones abroad.** Bundestag **Drucksache 21/7725, 25 August 2026** — the Bundesregierung's answer to the AfD's Kleine Anfrage **21/7381** (askers incl. Matthias Moosdorf, Peter Boehringer, Micha Fehre; 28 July 2026) on *Ukrainian* drones in Finnish and Baltic airspace — states verbatim: "Bei den … beschriebenen Vorfällen handelt es sich **nachweislich nicht um zu sanktionierende vorsätzliche Angriffe der Ukraine, sondern um technische Fehlfunktionen, die mutmaßlich auf bewusste russische Störmaßnahmen des Globalen Positionsbestimmungssystems (GPS) zurückzuführen sind**." **[V]** That is: stray *Ukrainian* drones, malfunctioning under *Russian* GPS jamming — **not** German airport sightings and **not** misidentification. PDF: https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/077/2107725.pdf

2. **I ran a systematic negative check.** I downloaded and text-extracted **178** Bundestag Drucksachen in the current Wahlperiode whose titles are "Schriftliche Fragen …" or "Kleine Anfrage" (via the DIP API), of which **53 contained the string "Drohn"**, and searched all of them for *Fehlfunktion, Fehlwahrnehmung, Verwechslung, Falschmeldung, Vogelschlag, technische Störung, Hobby, Ballon* adjacent to "Drohne": **zero hits**. No parliamentary document in that corpus attributes German sightings to technical malfunction or misidentification.

3. **The one document that does address the evidentiary question says the opposite of a "malfunction" finding.** Bundestag **Drucksache 21/3236, 12 December 2025** (Schriftliche Frage 43), asked by **Gerold Otten (AfD)**, answered by **Parl. Staatssekretär Christoph de Vries (BMI)**: the government had "taken note" of Dronewatch.nl's 28 Nov 2025 analysis but "**aus der derzeitigen Informationslage lassen sich keine belastbaren und allgemeingültigen Aussagen ableiten; dies liegt insbesondere an der Heterogenität der Sichtungen. Eine Involvierung nachrichtendienstlicher bzw. ausländischer staatlicher Stellen bei einem nicht quantifizierbaren Teil der Drohnenüberflüge ist zu vermuten.**" **[V]** PDF: https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/032/2103236.pdf

4. **Relevant but inaccessible:** the Linke Kleine Anfrage **21/7905** (8 Sep 2026) asks precisely the airport question — Q10 "Wie viele Sichtungen von Drohnen sind der Bundesregierung in der Nähe (40 km Umkreis) von deutschen Flughäfen seit 2019 bekannt geworden (bitte nach Jahren auflisten)?" and Q12 whether the stationary systems detected drones **that DFS had missed**, and in how many cases no owner could be assigned **[V]**. Its answer, **Drucksache 21/8140 (22 Sep 2026, BMI)**, was **not retrievable** — the dserver PDF returned HTTP 404 on 25 Sep 2026, as did the export/content services. **Its content is therefore NOT VERIFIED.** This is the document most likely to contain the figure the brief refers to, and it should be re-fetched.
   - Question: https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/079/2107905.pdf · Missing answer URL: https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/081/2108140.pdf

5. **A related but different "technischer Defekt" [V]:** the Leipzig drone's charge "war wohl wegen eines technischen Defekts nicht explodiert" (Drucksache 21/7905). This is a bombfailure, not a sighting artefact — a likely source of conflation.

**Independent quantitative counter-evidence [V]:** Dronewatch.nl / Trouw analysed **61 European sightings, 9 Sep–27 Nov 2025, across 11 countries**: **14 "no drone, other object"**, **41 "no evidence / origin unknown"**, **3 confirmed Russian drones** (all in Poland, Romania, Moldova — states bordering Ukraine), **3 confirmed hobby/tourist drones** — i.e. **55 of 61 unconfirmed**, and "**Germany has released almost no follow-up information on its reported incidents**." https://www.dronewatch.eu/61-european-drone-sightings-analysed-heres-what-we-know/

## C) Key episodes

**Munich (MUC).** **2 May 2025:** a sighting stopped traffic with diversions to Hörsching/Linz; duration unverified, and TZ (10 May 2025) indicates the airport later gave a **non-drone explanation** **[H]**. **2–4 Oct 2025:** suspected drones on two consecutive evenings suspended operations twice; the Bundeswehr provided **Amtshilfe**; thousands were affected; a dpa report of 3 Oct floated a "military drone"; the Frankfurter Rundschau asked "**Waren es Militärobjekte?**" and military objects were **never publicly excluded**; no drone was confirmed **[H]** — and Bavaria moved to a *Schnellgesetz* on shoot-downs. **30 May 2026:** two pilots independently reported a suspected drone at 09:03; runways closed ~09:00–10:05 (**~1 hour**); **26 aircraft diverted**; ~60 flights delayed 30–120 min and ~40 cancelled of ~900; police searched and **found nothing** **[V]**. **6 Sep 2026:** a pilot's "**verdächtige Wahrnehmung**" outside the perimeter stopped **departures only** ≈16:15–16:46; 17 departures delayed; two Lufthansa arrivals diverted to Frankfurt/Nuremberg; nothing found, and "**Die Bundespolizei spricht deshalb ausdrücklich nicht von einem Drohnenvorfall**" **[V]** (BR24).

**Berlin (BER).** **31 Oct 2025:** a sighting closed BER for **~2 hours** with flights unable to land **[H]**. **12 Mar 2026:** operations suspended over an unidentified object **[H]**. **23 Sep 2026 [V]:** staff saw a drone on the north runway at ~20:00; DFS closed the north runway, then the south at 20:20; traffic suspended **~45 minutes**, reopened shortly after 21:00; the Bundespolizei searched and could "**nichts feststellen**"; Brandenburg's police took over and are investigating *gefährlicher Eingriff in den Luftverkehr* — **nothing recovered**. **H1 2026 [V]:** **28 sightings**, the most of any German airport, of which **seven** suspended operations — against **8 drones in all of 2025**; the Bundespolizei installed a new detection system at BER in 2026.

**Frankfurt (FRA).** Germany's worst-affected airport: **35** drone disruptions by Aug 2025 vs ~20 in each prior year **[V]**. A drone sighting temporarily stopped operations on **11 Aug 2025** **[H]**. The most useful attribution datum in the whole German record: at Frankfurt since end-2024 there were **~150 illegal drone ascents and ~50 pilots identified**, and "**In den meisten Fällen handelte es sich um Planespotter, Hobby-Piloten oder Menschen, die Drohnen bei Familienfeiern einsetzten**" **[V]** (dpa/Stuttgarter Zeitung, 21 Aug 2026).

**Hamburg (HAM).** **8 Aug 2026:** starts and landings were briefly suspended after a drone alarm; police offered a **probable non-hostile explanation** **[H]**. **16 Sep 2026:** the Senate approved a **€4.2m** system (Argus Interception) with three AI interceptor drones able to net-capture, funded from the federal infrastructure special fund **[V]**.

**Cologne/Bonn (CGN), 12 Aug 2026 [V].** Five sightings reported by the Bundespolizei; whether they came from one or several drones remained unclear. DFS closed parts of the airspace around **12:30**; landings were blocked for **~30 minutes** (starts unaffected); two Ryanair aircraft from Porto and Palermo diverted to **Frankfurt-Hahn**; normal by **~14:00**. Decisively — and uniquely in this set — "**Gegen einen nahe des Flughafens angetroffenen Drohnenpiloten wurde ein Ermittlungsverfahren eingeleitet.**" https://www1.wdr.de/nrw/koeln/drohnen-flugzeuge-flughafen-koeln-bonn-dfs-100.html

**Bremen, 2–3 Nov 2025.** Airport closed; police **explicitly did not assume espionage** **[V per prior brief]**.

**Hannover.** Closures on **6 Nov 2025**, **27 Dec 2025** and **11 Aug 2026** (the last with six delayed flights and one cargo diversion; police could not exclude "**hybride Aktivität**") **[H]**. The Bundestag record adds that in the night to **11 Aug 2026** the Bundespolizei failed to locate the drone and the DFS could not fix it either; Lower Saxony's interior minister **Daniela Behrens** criticised that Hannover had no Bundespolizei drone-defence presence, the only such site in the north being Hamburg **[V, Drucksache 21/7905]**.

**Leipzig/Halle, 4–5 Aug 2026 — the one German case with device, payload and official state attribution [V].** A drone carrying the explosive **Semtex** was found at **23:42 on Tuesday 4 Aug**, on the apron next to a Ukrainian **Antonov** freighter; camera footage shows it struck a wing (beneath which the fuel tanks sit). The airport's own sensor **had not detected the drone**. Witnesses reported a **detonation at ~19:19** in the adjacent ghost village of **Kursdorf**; the Saxon LKA found **burned earth and metal fragments**, and — taped to a tree nearby — an **antenna and electronics** assessed as the drone's control link. A second aircraft collided with a flying object, probably a second drone. **DNA traces** were secured on the antenna, the drone and the charge; a suspicious mobile phone led to a failed search ~9 km away in **Wallendorf** (Saxony-Anhalt). The **BKA took over for the Generalbundesanwalt** (Ermittlungsgruppe "Quarz"). A **third drone carrying RDX** was found on **25 Aug 2026**, and on **1 Sep 2026** the federal government formally blamed **Russia**. (Sources: tagesschau/NDR/WDR, 13 and 25 Aug 2026; Drucksache 21/7905; Greens' Antrag 21/7669, which also reports DNA said to match the 2024 DHL arson case.)

**Bottom line for C:** across this whole set, the only German episodes with material evidence or an identified pilot are **Leipzig/Halle (confirmed device, state-attributed)** and **Cologne/Bonn (pilot, criminal investigation)**. In every other case — Munich, Berlin, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Bremen, Hannover — **nothing was recovered**, and in two cases (Munich 2 May 2025; Munich 6 Sep 2026) the authorities themselves walked the "drone" label back.

## D) Who amplified what

**The sequencing is the story: the loudest escalation came before any hostile drone was confirmed in Germany.** The "Spannungsfall" wave of **29 Sep–7 Oct 2025** followed the *Danish* sightings, not German evidence; the only German case with a recovered device and payload (Leipzig/Halle) came roughly ten months later, and the "attack on NATO soil" framing came after that.

**Politicians (dated).** **Roderich Kiesewetter (CDU)** demanded on 29–30 Sep 2025 that the "**Spannungsfall**" be declared — before the Munich alarms of 2–4 Oct 2025 and long before any device. **Markus Söder (CSU)** on 3 Oct 2025: "**Abschießen statt abwarten!**" — while Munich was still unresolved and the Frankfurter Rundschau was asking "Waren es Militärobjekte? Bundeswehr leistet Amtshilfe". Chancellor **Merz** on 6 Oct 2025 "vermutet Russland hinter vielen Drohnen-Flügen". Hesse's **Boris Rhein** demanded "Härte" on 5 Oct 2025 — the same day the federal Justice Minister warned against a Bundeswehr role in the interior. Bavarian and Hessian interior ministries followed with shoot-down and drone-law initiatives.
After Leipzig, **Dobrindt** (press conference at Leipzig/Halle, 5 Aug 2026 **[V]**): "neue **Gefahrenqualität**"; "Es ist real, dass wir es mit einer hohen Bedrohungslage zu tun haben"; the perpetrators were "**professionell … keine Laien**"; and, without naming an author, a "Wettbewerb, der **möglicherweise auch fremde Mächte mit einschließt**" — while also cautioning that "Spekulationen könnten in die falsche Richtung führen". **Kiesewetter** then demanded NATO **Article 4** consultations ("Deutschland sollte zumindest Artikel-4-Konsultationen in der Nato anstrengen"). On 10 Aug 2026 Dobrindt ordered the Bundespolizei drone-defence unit to grow from ~130 to **300** at eight sites. Transport minister **Steffen Bilger (CDU)** — not a "CSU MdB Manfred Bilger" **[X]** — on 21 Aug 2026 urged rapid DFS detection roll-out; the same report notes that of **~150 illegal drone ascents at Frankfurt since end-2024** with ~50 pilots identified, "**In den meisten Fällen handelte es sich um Planespotter, Hobby-Piloten oder Menschen, die Drohnen bei Familienfeiern einsetzten**" **[V]**.

**The framing shift, evidenced:** "Drohnen-Alarm"/hobby pilots (2024–Sep 2025, per DFS) → "**Spannungsfall**"/"Abschießen statt abwarten"/hybride Kriegsführung/Russia (29 Sep–6 Oct 2025, inside one week, with no German device) → "**Angriff auf kritische Infrastruktur auf NATO-Boden**" (Aug 2026, *after* a device was recovered). The DFS's own attribution to hobby pilots was never retracted but was simply out-competed.

Wietfeld: "Ich sehe im Einsatz von Sprengstoff eine Eskalation… Wir sollten das in der Rhetorik nicht runterspielen. **Das war kein 'Drohnenfund', sondern ein Angriff auf kritische Infrastruktur auf NATO-Boden.**" He is a 14-year Bundeswehr veteran who founded ARX in 2022; the company is described as heading for a billion-euro valuation, is backed by the NATO Innovation Fund and builds unmanned ground systems. **Amplification of the quote: negative result [X]** — searching the exact phrase returned no other article; Bing News DE returned exactly one hit (the Merkur piece itself). Treat it as **supply-side industry framing coinciding with the procurement push, not a documented media cascade**. https://www.merkur.de/politik/startup-chef-nach-drohnen-alarm-das-war-ein-angriff-auf-nato-boden-94432544.html

**The Greens [V].** On 20 Aug 2026 the Bundestagsfraktion tabled **Antrag 21/7669**, "Deutschland umgehend besser gegen Drohnen sichern". It demands a **"Drohnengipfel im Bundeskanzleramt"**, concentration of responsibility in the **Bundespolizei** (explicitly not the Bundeswehr), a real-time low-altitude airspace picture, ADS-L-type electronic identification, priority equipping of airports/Bundeswehr/CRITIS, and completion of the drone-defence centre. ## E) Net assessment

Germany's 2024–2026 drone story is best read as **three different evidentiary regimes conflated into one narrative**. (1) **Routine airport disruption**: DFS counted 151/161/144 incidents (2023/2024/to Aug 2025) and 28 at BER in H1 2026 alone, but its own report attributes the majority plausibly to **hobby pilots**, and the one airport where pilots were systematically identified — Frankfurt — found "**in den meisten Fällen … Planespotter, Hobby-Piloten oder Menschen, die Drohnen bei Familienfeiern einsetzten**". (2) **State-level reconnaissance and sabotage**: the BKA *Lagebild* (3 Sep 2026) counts **747 drone incidents / 1,068 drones** and **165+ sabotage suspicions** for 2026 to end-August, plus **24 espionage proceedings**, but by its own framing these are **sightings and suspicions**, and it notes that where pilots were found, "**oft stellte sich dann heraus, dass die Überflüge keinen Spionagehintergrund hatten**". (3) **The Leipzig attack** — the single case with a recovered device, Semtex/RDX payload, DNA, a relay antenna and a formal state attribution to Russia. The political and media system consistently **generalised regime (3) backwards onto regimes (1) and (2)**, and it did so **before** regime (3) existed: the "Spannungsfall"/"Abschießen statt abwarten"/"Russia is behind it" wave (29 Sep–6 Oct 2025) ran on Danish and unconfirmed German sightings, while the government's own on-the-record position on 12 Dec 2025 (Drucksache 21/3236) was that no reliable general statements could be drawn from a "**Heterogenität der Sichtungen**" — a concession that was never amplified as loudly as the threat claims.
