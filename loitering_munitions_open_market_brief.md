## 2. Export availability

- **Switchblade:** approved for **Ukraine, France, the UK "and additional allies"**; Taiwan FMS (720 × SW300); Greece selected. **No known sale to a non-Western/non-NATO state.**
- **Hero:** US (Army/Marines), **Argentina** (Janes, Dec 2022); Rheinmetall licensed production for NATO (Nov 2025).
- **Warmate:** Poland, Ukraine, **UAE**, **Georgia** (licensed plant) — **has reached non-NATO buyers**.
- **Harop/Harpy:** India; **Azerbaijan** (combat use 2016 and 2020) — clearest non-NATO combat-proven export.
- **SkyStriker:** **Turkmenistan** (unveiled at the Sep 2021 parade — Oryx, 4 Jan 2022: https://www.oryxspioenkop.com/2022/01/replicating-success-turkmenistans.html), **Azerbaijan**, an unnamed European state (2023), Germany (Diehl partnership, Jun 2026).
- **FireFly:** **Vietnam** local-production deal (Defense Post, 9 Feb 2026 — https://thedefensepost.com/2026/02/09/vietnam-spike-firefly/).
- **Kargu:** Türkiye; **Libya (LNA)** per the 2021 UN Panel of Experts; Japanese interest (2023).
- **Lancet:** Russia only. Iran unveiled a Lancet-like munition (Forbes, 29 Apr 2024) — resemblance **[C]**, no proven transfer. Russia now uses Ukraine-war drone types in Africa (France 24, 2 Jul 2026) — **[C]**, M.
- **Shahed-136:** Russia (Geran-2/Alabuga), **Ethiopia, Sudan, Venezuela**, plus non-state recipients (below).
- **Low-cost analogues:** China markets the **Feilong-300D at ~$10,000** (Defense Post, 7 Nov 2025 — https://thedefensepost.com/2025/11/07/china-feilong-300d-drone/) — **[C]**, vendor/press, M. Turkish **"Mosquito"** (1,000 km) seen in **Azerbaijani** service (defensemirror, 24 Sep 2026) — **[C]**. North Korea mass-producing a Harop clone (Sep 2026) — **[C]**.

## 3. Non-state acquisition (priority)

**(i) State-supplied purpose-built loitering munitions — DOCUMENTED [D].** This is the real, evidenced channel:
- **Hamas:** Ababil-2T *"Shihab"* LM (2021); *"Al-Zawari"* LM (2022).
- **Hezbollah:** Ababil-2T *"Mirsad-1"* LM; used in the Oct 2024 attacks on Israel.
- **Houthis:** *Samad-2/3*, *Shihab*, **Shahed-136 *"Wa'aed"***, *Khatif-1/2*.
- **Iraqi militias:** Shahed-131 *"Murad-6"* (2021), *Murad-5* (2020).

Source: Oryx, "Expanding Sphere Of Influence: Iranian UAV Exports," 27 Nov 2022 — https://www.oryxspioenkop.com/2022/11/expanding-sphere-of-influence-iranian.html — corroborated by DIA findings and UN Yemen Panel reporting (S/2024/731; S/2025/650). Operational proof: **Tower 22** (Jordan, 28 Jan 2024), a one-way-attack drone that killed three US soldiers, attributed to Islamic Resistance in Iraq / Kataib Hezbollah (France 24, 31 Jan 2024).

**(ii) Capture and reuse of purpose-built Western/Israeli munitions — WEAK/CLAIMED.** Russia claims reverse-engineering captured Switchblade 300s into the **BAS-80** (Newsweek, 19 Sep 2023) — **[C]**, Russian-state-media origin, L–M. Ukraine has captured intact **Lancet** airframes (Army Recognition, 8 Apr 2024) — **[D]**, but state-on-state. **No documented case of a non-state group capturing and reusing a Western/Israeli purpose-built loitering munition was found.**

**(iii) Improvised FPV/commercial-drone munitions — a DIFFERENT category.** Hezbollah's FPV strike on a Merkava (May 2026) and ISIS/al-Shabaab IED-drones are improvised or commercial, not purpose-built LMs.

**Closest non-state case of a purpose-built OWA:** In **August 2024 Puntland (Somalia) announced interception of "kamikaze drones" shipped from Yemen and intended for al-Shabaab** (Horn Observer, 27 Aug 2024 — https://hornobserver.com/articles/2840/Puntland-Intercepts-Kamikaze-Drones-from-Yemen-Intended-for-Al-Shabaab ; Garowe Online; BBC Somali). **[C]** — single-sourced official claim; type and count unverified. Reporting through 2025–26 documents Houthi–al-Shabaab smuggling/technology cooperation.

**EVIDENCE ABSENT:** No case was found, 2020–Sep 2026, of a non-state armed group **independently purchasing a purpose-built loitering munition (Switchblade, Hero, Harop, Warmate, Kargu) on the open market.** Access runs through state supply (Iran → proxies) or improvised/commercial drones. Treat "terrorists bought Switchblades" claims as unsupported.

## 4. Dual-use supply chain

**Commercially available off-the-shelf:** flight controllers and hobby autopilots (PX4 / ArduPilot / Pixhawk), FPV video links, EO/IR camera modules, GNSS receivers, telemetry radios, servos, small engines, and edge-AI compute (Jetson-class). **Export-controlled or threshold-controlled:** high-end EO/IR and GNSS/IMU, ITAR USML Category VIII, and items listed under the **EU dual-use Regulation 2021/821** and the **Wassenaar Arrangement**.

**Enforcement evidence:**
- **BIS, 8 Oct 2025:** added **15 Chinese companies** (10 in China) to the Entity List for facilitating purchase of **US-origin electronic components found in weaponized UAVs operated by Hamas and the Houthis** — including Arrow Electronics (Hong Kong) subsidiaries and an IRGC-QF procurement network. Reuters — https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/us-targets-chinese-companies-over-drone-components-used-by-hamas-houthis-2025-10-08/
- **Chinese dual-use flow to Iran/Russia:** GPS units, motors, engine parts, batteries, fibre-optic cable, chips; WSJ reported ~**1,300 shipments** of dual-use components to Iran's defence ministry in H1 2026 (via Scripps News — https://www.scrippsnews.com/world/asia/chinese-dual-use-electronics-are-helping-iran-and-russia-build-deadlier-drones-despite-u-s-sanctions). **[D]/[C]**, reputable journalism, M.

## 5. The low-cost end

- **FPV one-way attack ~$400–$2,000.** Ukraine's **Vyriy** FPV reportedly flew 110 km at a **$500** unit price, down from ~$5,000 (Euromaidan Press, 11 Jul 2026; TechRadar, 15 Jul 2026) — **[C]**, press, M.
- One **$24M Tor** ≈ **14,000 FPV drones (~$1,700 each)** (defence-blog, Nov 2023, via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loitering_munition) — **[C]**, M.
- **Lancet ~$35k** and **Shahed/Geran $20k–$80k** define the OWA tier.
- **China's Feilong-300D (~$10,000)** and Turkish analogues are collapsing the fixed-wing OWA price point — **[C]**, vendor/press, M.
- **E-commerce / encrypted markets:** 2022 reporting alleged Western arms supplied to Ukraine appearing on darknet markets; **no robustly verified case of a purpose-built loitering munition sold via e-commerce or encrypted markets was found. EVIDENCE ABSENT / LOW CONFIDENCE.**

## Caveats

Vendor marketing inflates capability and circulates aspirational prices (notably the long-quoted ~$6,000 Switchblade 300 figure, unsourced in current primary material). **Contract ceilings are routinely misreported as unit costs**, and non-Western per-unit figures are wide-band analyst estimates, not procurement-grade data.
