# Demonstrated Misuse of Drones, Uncrewed Systems and AI-Enabled Tools Outside Declared Inter-State Warfare

**Threat-assessment evidence brief — 25 September 2026.** Defence understanding only; no attack, payload, targeting or evasion guidance. Control mode is given for every incident, and **autonomy/AI is explicitly marked absent where that is the finding.**

**Evidence key:** **[C]** court record · **[O]** official statement/statistic · **[T]** state or think-tank assessment · **[J]** reputable journalism · **[X]** contested, unverified or retracted.

---

## 1. Mexico and Latin America

**2020 Mexico City ambush — a rumour to retire.** The **26 June 2020** attack on police chief **Omar García Harfuch** (Lomas de Chapultepec, attributed to CJNG) was a firearms ambush by roughly 28 gunmen using a .50-calibre rifle and fragmentation grenades, with 400+ impacts and three dead. BBC Mundo and Milenio reconstructions contain **no drone**, and the resulting convictions (**316 years for twelve defendants; 86 years for two**) include **no drone element**. The claim is best read as a conflation with the later CJNG drone-IED wave and with an unrelated August 2025 US drone overflight of Valle de Bravo. **[C]** — contradicted by the court record.

**CJNG "Operadores Droneros."** The CJNG does **not** build drones; it adapts **commercial off-the-shelf** models (e.g. DJI Mini 3, ~US$419). By 2026 InSight Crime reported the unit active in **Michoacán, Guanajuato, Chihuahua, Jalisco and Baja California**; **Cárteles Unidos** and **Los Ardillos** also use drones. A CJNG drone-borne IED combined with gunfire hit a **Mexican Army helicopter at Apatzingán, 27–28 January 2025** (InSight Crime-confirmed). By contrast, a **Chihuahua helicopter incident on 12 September 2026 was gunfire-only, with no drone**. [J/T]

**Correction — the chemical-payload claim is officially denied.** The alleged **chemical drone attack at Coahuayana, Michoacán (3–4 April 2024)** was **officially denied on 10 April 2024 by Michoacán's Segob**: state police hazmat units and SEDENA found **no trace** and there were **no hospital admissions**. InSight Crime continues to repeat the claim uncritically; it should be treated as **disputed**, not established. [C/X]

**Documented incidents** (all **remotely piloted**): Aguililla, **Apr 2021** — drone drop injured two police; Tepalcatepec, **May 2021** — explosive drones against residents; Michoacán, **Aug 2024** — drone attacks in CJNG–Caballeros Templarios clashes; **Tijuana, 15–16 Oct 2025** — drones struck the FGE anti-kidnapping offices, and the FGE opened a **terrorism file** (sources disagree on one versus two-to-three drones); **Jalisco/Zacatecas, 10 Mar 2026** — explosive drones injured a woman officer; **Guerrero, Apr–Jul 2026** — attacks in Tecoanapa and Chilpancingo; **Los Ardillos, 6–9 May 2026** — 8+ hours of drone bombardment displacing 800+ families (community denunciation, **not** officially confirmed). [J/W]

**Strongest official Mexican data (SEDENA).** Drone attacks rose from **5 (2020) → 107 → 233 → 260 (H1 2023), 605 in total to mid-2023**; the **NCITE** database records **221 (2021–25)**. Drone **seizures** rose from **12 (2024) to 139 (2025)**, with 46 in H1 2026, concentrated in **Sinaloa (63), Michoacán (53) and Tamaulipas (36)**. [O]

**Other groups and regional reach.** **Los Viagras**: arrests in September 2023 (eight Colombians, drone-IED workshop) and September 2024 ("El Tigre", an ex-Colombian soldier) are **official-record** evidence of **foreign drone expertise transfer**. **Brazil**: Comando Vermelho dropped bombs on police in Rio on **28 October 2025**, and a detained Navy sailor had built release attachments and trained members. **Peru**: La Pampa mining drone explosives rest on a **single witness — weak**. **Chile**: no offensive attack found. **Argentina/Paraguay**: **no documented case found** — an evidence gap, not a cleared negative.

**Legal and policy.** On **20 February 2025** the US designated six Mexican cartels as FTOs (Sinaloa, CJNG, Cártel del Noreste, La Nueva Familia Michoacana, Cártel del Golfo, Cárteles Unidos), reaching **eight** with Cártel de Juárez and Los Viagras on **16 July 2026**. Mexico's Senate approved a **2024 reform criminalising illicit drone use** (penalties proposed at 10–60 years), and on **17 August 2026** the US and Mexico signed a declaration of intent on drone and counter-drone cooperation. **[O]**

**Scale.** ACLED recorded **~670 armed-drone events in Latin America since 2018, over 72% between January 2025 and August 2026**; Colombia rose from **38 attacks (2024) to 149 (2025)** across 41 municipalities. A US assessment (24 Sep 2026) says cartels seek "greater autonomy and payload capacity" and study jamming evasion — **this concerns control links and payloads, not machine autonomy**. **Autonomy is confirmed absent**: CNN reported (Sept 2026) expert consensus that there are **no regional reports of kamikaze drones by organised crime**, and Crisis Group states drones "don't have too much autonomy". El País's "vuelo autónomo" refers to **GPS/waypoint flight, not machine vision**; InSight Crime cites AI-guided drones only as a **Ukraine/Russia precedent**; and a widely shared "AI drone" claim concerns **fraud and TikTok recruitment, not flight autonomy**. Control is human, via video downlink and manual release, and jamming works. [T/J/O]

## 2. Colombia and Ecuador

**Colombia.** A Colombian military intelligence report (updated 7 Sep 2026) records **690 drone attacks since 21 July 2023 — 119 in 2024, 277 in 2025 and 292 in Jan–Sep 2026, already exceeding all of 2025** — affecting **487 people** (22 soldiers, 3 police, 6 civilians killed). Attribution 2025–26: **382 to "Iván Mordisco"/EMC dissidents, 155 ELN, 30 "Calarcá", 4 Comandos de la Frontera**; Mindefensa separately attributes 88% overall to Mordisco's structures. Targets include police stations, military units, a marine battalion, and **two air-navigation radars** — **Cerro Santana (25 Apr 2026)** and **Candelaria, Valle (16 Sep 2026)**, which the Defensoría called an IHL violation. All documented cases were **remotely piloted**; on 20 Feb 2026 the Army **downed an ELN drone and captured its operator**. Other named incidents: **Teorama, 13 May 2025** — ELN drones killed a soldier; **El Castillo, 18 Feb 2026** — seizure of **190 grenades adapted for drone release**, 88 anti-personnel mines, 273 detonators; **Jamundí, 7–13 May 2026** — repeated police-substation attacks, **17 devices over several days**, four wounded; **Tibú, 19 Jun 2026** — a drone-released explosive killed an **11-year-old**; **5 Sep 2026** — an ELN drone attack on a military post **killed three soldiers**; **Aguachica, 25 Sep 2026 (today)** — drone and explosives attack on a police station. [O/J]

**Statistics — read carefully.** Mindefensa's headline **"8,395 drone incidents in 2025, only 4% effective"** refers to **incidents**, not attacks; the attack figure is the 277 above. Both series are official. The 4% ratio remains the best single evidence that most drone use is **ineffective**.

**Pipelines — NOT a drone campaign.** Mindefensa recorded **8 pipeline bombings in H1 2026 versus 21 in H1 2025 (−62%)**, all **conventional**. The only drone-specific oil events were two failed explosive drones at **Caño Limón and Banadía, Arauca (22–23 Jul 2026)**, unattributed; **no drone attacks on Putumayo, OTA or Amerisur–Geopark** were found in 2024–26. PAres logged 11 drone events and 24 victims in the Catatumbo in H1 2026, where drones function mainly as **intimidation and extortion leverage**. [O/J]

**Policy — announced ≠ delivered.** The **Escudo Nacional Antidrones** (announced 10 Jan 2026, COP 6.3 bn) **stalled**: only **1 of 119 bidders** qualified, the Contraloría flagged "critical risks", it was unfunded at COP 800,000 m by 16 Jul 2026, and the enabling directive was **annulled on 8 Sep 2026**. **No law criminalises weaponised drone use** — only Aerocivil civil rules (RAC 100/91) — and the bills of Jul 2025 and Aug 2026 are not in force. **No Colombian conviction** for weaponised-drone use was identified. [O]

**Ecuador — prisons, not police.** Verified: **La Roca prison bombing, 12 Sep 2023**; Machala overflight 19 Nov 2025; an attack on Machala's prison director, 4 Mar 2026; **Monte Sinaí, 15 Aug 2026** — a drone, charges, a **remote-activation system and a printed plan of the Penitenciaría del Litoral** seized; and the Interior Minister's statement that **~600 organised-crime-linked drones attempted to reach Cárcel del Encuentro**. Offensive use outside prisons is **rare** (Los Lobos drone-bombing of four homes, El Oro, 24–25 Jun 2024). **ACLED logged only 8 Ecuadorian drone incidents (Sep 2023–Jul 2026) with zero drone-caused deaths**, and **no confirmed gang drone attack on Ecuadorian police or military** exists; there was **no drone role in the 9 Jan 2024 TC Televisión attack**. [O/J]

**Autonomy: absent.** Reported fibre-optic tethering (Jan 2026) is a **physical link to a human pilot** — the opposite of autonomy. "Swarm" reports describe simultaneous launches by several pilots, and pilot recruitment from Ukraine transfers **human** skill.

## 3. Contraband and prison smuggling by drone

**Every documented case is a remotely piloted commercial quadcopter with a release mechanism. No autonomous contraband delivery was found anywhere.**

- **UK — best official series.** HMPPS/MoJ: **1,712 incidents** in the year to March 2025, **up 43%** (from 138 in 2020/21, ~+1,140%); **£900,000** for counter-drone work; **nine arrests**. A seven-person gang got **22 years combined** (Harrow, 3 Mar 2026) after **50+ drops in three months**; 13,000 windows were fitted with steel grilles. [O/C/J]
- **Netherlands — court-established.** ~**70 flights (Apr 2021–Feb 2024)** into prisons in NL, Belgium and Luxembourg for ~€30,000; on **22 Apr 2026**: **120 hours' community service, €10,000 fine (half suspended), four-month suspended sentence**, proved on **drone flight-log and cell-tower data**. The OM wants phone-dropping reclassified from *overtreding* to *misdrijf*; prisons gained jam/disable powers on **1 July 2026**. [C/O]
- **Ireland.** **384 deliveries in 2025** at three Dublin prisons; seizures rose to **1,647 phones (+33%)**, **1,325 drugs (+28%)**, **441 weapons (+70%)**; **€5m** of mesh cut Wheatfield incursions **73%**. [O]
- **Belgium.** Haren worst affected; a **multi-prison supply network dismantled (Apr 2026)**; FPS Justice bought an **autonomous "drone-in-a-box" system — state-side autonomy only**. [C/J/O]
- **Brazil / US.** Ceresp Betim (Aug 2026): drone, **remote control and cargo-release device** seized, two charged — **PCC attribution remains unverified**. US: **Deivon Waller, 240 months** (10 Sep 2026) for a Georgia prison drone conspiracy (2019–2024; 20+ charged); a dozen indicted 24 Jun 2026. **No national US dataset exists.** [O/C]
- **Nordics.** Only isolated cases (Skänninge, Jan 2026); **no Nordic prison statistical series**.

**Gap:** EU SOCTA 2025's published summary makes **no mention of drones or prison contraband**; Europol's *Unmanned Future(s)* (2025) describes criminal UAS use as "opportunistic, **remote-controlled**, commercially available".

## 5. Terrorist and extremist experimentation, including AI

- **Islamic State (2016–2019).** IS ran a drone unit for reconnaissance, propaganda and grenade dropping using **crude, commercially derived airframes**; its coordinated multi-drone attack on Russia's **Hmeimim air base (Jan 2018) was defeated**. A 2017 Pentagon forecast that a non-state actor would fly a drone across the Atlantic within five years **has not materialised**. [J/T]
- **Propaganda and manuals.** The Dutch **DTN (Dec 2025)** records that ISIS propaganda increasingly calls for attacks using **arson, explosives and drones**, with **extensive explosives-manufacturing instructions**, and that ISKP uses **AI to mass-produce propaganda**. Europol's **TE-SAT 2026** confirms terrorist actors "regularly featured UAV systems in their propaganda" in 2025. [O]
- **Foiled and prosecuted plots.** **Esslingen, Germany (19 Jun 2024)** — Mahmoud A. charged over an **explosive drone** with a car-key detonation method; **UK 2023** — Mohamed Al Bared convicted over a **3D-printed drone** for IS; **UK 2019, Whitefield/Bury** — drone plot, jury hung, retrial; **Michigan, May 2025** — a National Guard **drone-surveillance** plot for IS; **France, 21 Mar 2025** — a man arrested having bought **a drone and explosives materials**; **Belgium, Oct 2025** — the De Wever plot above. TE-SAT 2026 also records a **12 Sep 2025** Belgian case where two IS-linked men making explosives were detected via their **internet searches**. The most advanced non-state capability remains **Hezbollah/Hamas fibre-optic FPV drones** — immune to jamming, but **tethered to a human pilot**. [O/J/C]
- **Jihadist manuals.** IS has published a **"Modern Warfare" drone-manual series since April 2024** via "The Kitchen" on encrypted platforms, gated behind one-to-one contact since October 2024; **GNET assesses the manuals do not bridge the intent–capability gap**. **[J/T]**
- **Right-wing and lone-actor cases.** Two of the first US domestic drone plots: **Skyler Philippi** (Nashville substation; **pleaded guilty Sept 2025**) and **Marvin Jalo** (Phoenix Pride, Oct 2024). Both **manually flown**. **[C]**
- **AI-enabled planning — two documented cases.** **Jaswant Singh Chail, Windsor Castle (25 Dec 2021)** entered the grounds with a crossbow intending to kill the Queen after being encouraged by a **Replika AI chatbot**; he was **jailed for nine years (5 Oct 2023)**. The AI was an **encouraging companion, not a planning or weapons tool** — "the AI planned it" framings are exaggerated. **Matthew Livelsberger, Las Vegas (1 Jan 2025)** detonated a Cybertruck outside the Trump hotel having **used ChatGPT** to research targets and explosive effects (confirmed by LVMPD and OpenAI); **no drone was involved — this is AI as an information tool, not autonomy**. [C/O]
- **Machine vision exists in the laboratory only.** Andon Labs showed that publicly available AI models could write code letting a ~US$100 consumer drone stalk a person using **facial recognition** (Aug 2026) — a **research benchmark**, with no seizure or criminal case following. [J]

**Unverified claims to avoid carrying:** a distinct **December 2022 "lone wolf drone plot"**, a distinct **2019–2021 France drone plot**, and a separate **"drone-borne explosive plot"** could **not** be verified in English, German, French or Dutch date-restricted searches. No **Europol, UN or UNICRI report quantifying AI-enabled terrorist attack planning** was found.

**Bottom line:** drones and AI feature in extremist **propaganda, aspiration and foiled plotting**; there is **no confirmed case anywhere of a fully autonomous drone in a terrorist attack outside inter-state war**, and "AI-enabled" currently means **chatbot encouragement or information retrieval, not autonomy**.

## 6. Airports and critical infrastructure

Only **three strands are evidentially solid**; two flagship cases collapsed.

- **Gatwick, 19–21 Dec 2018.** Runway closed **33 hours**; ~**1,000 flights and 140,000 passengers** affected. Police logged **170 reports, 115 deemed credible**, but **no photograph or video of a drone was ever produced**; two recovered drones were ruled out; **Paul Gait and Elaine Kirk were released without charge**, and Sussex Police paid **£200,000** (£55,000 damages plus costs, June 2020). The investigation closed **27 Sep 2019** after **£790,000** and no charges; the independent review remains **unpublished eight years on**, and a 2024 FOI found the National Police Air Service recorded **no drone sightings at Gatwick in the period**. **[O/X]** — unresolved; official "malicious" attribution contested.
- **Copenhagen, 22 Sep 2025.** Flights suspended nearly four hours; the PM called it "the most serious attack on Danish critical infrastructure to date". On **25 June 2026 Copenhagen Police concluded they could neither prove nor deny** drones were present, conceding "**we were not clear enough with our reservations**" about the earlier "capable actor" framing. **Norway's PST** found **no established foreign-state link** (Feb 2026). **[O/X]** — ambiguous; official framing retracted.
- **Madrid-Barajas.** Telemadrid reported (7 Oct 2025) that the **Guardia Civil believes the drone that forced the closure never existed** — replicating Gatwick's collapse. **[J/X]**
- **Dublin, February 2023.** Two confirmed sightings disrupted flights; **two men were charged** under the Air Navigation and Transport Act. Together with **Cologne/Bonn, 12 Aug 2026** (pilot detained, investigation opened), these are the **only airport strands with a court/prosecution record — and both point to careless or illegal flying, not hostility.** **[C]**
- **Vilnius, 13 Sep 2026.** The airport closure was caused by **birds misidentified as drones** — not a drone at all. **[O]**
- **Germany (unattributed).** Recurrent alarms at **Frankfurt** (2019–2025), **Munich** (2–3 Oct 2025 with Bundeswehr assistance; again 30 May 2026), **Berlin** (31 Oct 2025; 12 Mar and 23 Sep 2026), Bremen and Hamburg. A classified **BKA** report recorded **1,072 incidents involving 1,955 drones in Germany in 2025**. Across Europe, IISS counted **144 UAV incidents (Aug 2024–Feb 2026): ~48% over military facilities, 18% over airports, 26% over other critical infrastructure**. **[O/T/J]**
- **Leipzig/Halle, 4–5 Aug 2026 — the one confirmed malicious airport attack.** An FPV drone with **~800 g of explosive** struck an **An-124** near Ukrainian cargo aircraft and **failed to detonate**; a second was struck by a Boeing 757 on go-around; a third with **50 g RDX** was found on 14 Aug. The drone used **two SIM cards and a 5G router**, invisible to counter-drone systems as a "phone". **On 1 Sep 2026 Germany formally blamed Russia** and raised its threat level. **[O]**
- **Nuclear and energy sites.** Doel (3 drones, 9 Nov 2025), Kleine Brogel, **Volkel** (Dutch military opened fire, 22 Nov 2025, no wreckage), France's **Île Longue** SSBN base (5 UAVs, 4 Dec 2025) and Brunsbüttel LNG (from Aug 2024; prosecutors: "espionage for sabotage purposes") — **unresolved in nearly every case**. Widely cited "Swedish nuclear plant drones" date from **January 2022**. No verified drone incident at a water utility or North Sea wind farm was found.

## 7. Organised crime in the Netherlands and Belgium

- **Prisons dominate** (§3). Dutch DJI logged **298 detections in 2023, only 55 suspicious** — a low signal-to-noise ratio that is itself a caution. Belgian prisons rely on **state-side autonomous "drone-in-a-box"** systems.
- **Ports.** Antwerp saw "**dozens**" of unregistered drones within weeks (Nov 2025); Belgian customs reported drones over sensitive customs sites **four times since Nov 2025**, "possibly linked to organised crime", raising staff-safety concerns; the port CEO publicly acknowledged vulnerability. Rotterdam criminals use drones per Platform Veilig Ondernemen, but **no Dutch prosecution specifically for criminal drone overflight was found**. [O/J]
- **Reconnaissance of rivals or police: public evidence is thin.** No documented NL/BE case of a criminal drone watching a rival, a police station or an officer's home; the closest are the **terrorism** plot of Oct 2025 and a Brussels magistrate's Aug 2026 proposal to use **police** drones over drug hotspots and police stations.
- **Official assessments.** **NCTV/DTN (June 2026):** there has **never been a successful terrorist drone attack in Western Europe**; several plots were foiled; effective drone attacks still require substantial technical knowledge; **simple means such as knives or vehicles remain more likely**. Leiden University's *Tussen mogelijkheid en realiteit* (June 2026) counted **only 34 drone-related attack incidents in 2010–2025**, over half never executed, with **"criminal organisations and state actors play[ing] hardly any role"**. **[O/academic]**
- **Evidence gap:** no dedicated Politieacademie, NSCR, TNO or Het CCV study on organised-crime drone use was located. **Autonomy is explicitly absent on the criminal side**; machine vision appears only in **state detection systems**.

## 8. Hybrid and covert state activity inside NATO countries

- **Poland, 9–10 Sep 2025.** **19–23 drones** entered from the Belarus direction; up to four were shot down (**mostly by Dutch F-35s**); Warsaw, Modlin, Rzeszów and Lublin airports closed; **NATO Article 4** was invoked and **Operation Eastern Sentry** launched (12 Sep). Lublin prosecutors classified the recovered drones as **unpowered decoys with no explosives**. [O]
- **Germany, 2026 — strongest attribution.** The **Leipzig drone attack (5 Aug 2026)** was formally blamed on **Russia** on 1 Sep 2026; a suspect with Belarusian/Lithuanian and Russian passports is reportedly DNA-linked to the device. In Aug–Sept 2026 explosives/incendiaries were found near the **Jänschwalde** substation, with a second grid-sabotage attempt at **Bergheim**. [O]
- **September 2026 airspace incidents (to date).** **15 Sep, ~00:20:** an **Italian Eurofighter** on NATO Baltic Air Policing shot down a drone near **Kaunas, Lithuania** after it entered from Belarus; Lithuania's president said it was a Gerbera-2 **targeting Ukraine**, while Italy's defence minister said it "most probably" came from Russia. **14–15 Sep:** Denmark's military said a **Russian warship fired two flares at a Danish helicopter**, and Denmark summoned the Russian ambassador. **24 Sep (yesterday):** Poland declared a fire at the **Starlink ground station at Wola Krobowska**, serving Ukrainian military communications, an act of **sabotage**; the same day **Danish DDIS** warned Russia could attack NATO territory within months. Most other 2026 Baltic incursions were **Ukrainian strike drones driven off course by Russian electronic warfare**, not Russian attack drones — a distinction Baltic governments themselves stress. [O/X]
- **Sabotage networks — court-tested.** The **DHL parcel-bomb plot (July 2024)** ignited at Leipzig/Halle and a Birmingham warehouse; Eurojust announced the network's disruption in March 2026. Convictions exist in Germany (Munich, **Dieter S., six years**, Oct 2025; Stuttgart, Aug 2026), Lithuania, Poland and Czechia. The common model is **Telegram recruitment of proxies paid in cryptocurrency**. [C]
- **Undersea infrastructure — no drones involved.** In **every** attributed Baltic cable or pipeline incident the mechanism was a **ship's anchor dragged across the seabed** (Balticconnector 2023; *Yi Peng 3* 2024; *Eagle S*/Estlink 2, retrial under way; *Vezhen*, ruled an accident). **No authority has ever claimed a drone or uncrewed device cut a Baltic cable.** [O]
- **The shadow-fleet thesis is contested.** IISS (2 July 2026) judged it "**highly likely**" the Kremlin ran a UAV campaign over Europe, with ≥144 sorties possibly launched from shadow-fleet vessels; **Dronewatch** called the report dependent on "assumptions and debunked sightings". Crucially, **IISS itself notes no European government has publicly attributed a UAV sighting to Russia**. Norway's PST director said most reported cases were **stars, weather, aircraft or helicopters**; Belgian VRT *Pano* (Apr 2026) found **558 reports yielded no physical evidence** — the minister's "large drone" footage was a police helicopter; UK MoD Police closed the Lakenheath/Mildenhall inquiry in Feb 2026 with no suspects. **[T/X]**

## 9. Machine vision and autonomy — the central negative finding

**No verified seizure, court case, forensics report or official assessment substantiates machine-vision target recognition, facial recognition, geofencing defeat or autonomous engagement by a criminal, terrorist or insurgent drone.** Documented misuse is (i) hand-flown FPV with a human watching a live feed, (ii) manual line-of-sight/radio control, or (iii) pre-programmed GPS/waypoint flight.

What is reported as "autonomy" is almost always **jamming resistance** — fibre-optic tethering or cellular control — which **requires a human operator**. "Swarm" reporting describes **several drones flown simultaneously by several human pilots**. A Colombian FPV pilot told the Financial Times that cartels still **lack encrypted pilot–drone communications and correct payload mounting**: the frontier is **piloting and payload engineering**, not autonomy. Genuine autonomy exists **only in inter-state warfare** — Russia's **V2U** drone (Nvidia computer, computer-vision target selection, external comms removed in late-2025 variants) and Ukrainian AI terminal-guidance modules; even CSIS (June 2026) holds full autonomy is **"not yet a battlefield reality"**.

**Why the gap persists:** control-link advantage beats autonomy; FPV airframes cost ~US$600–1,200 versus a robust machine-vision stack; recruitment targets **pilots**, not AI engineers; and counter-UAS economics currently favour the defender.

## 10. Countervailing evidence

- **Failed attacks.** Tijuana (15–16 Oct 2025): drones struck the FGE anti-kidnapping offices with **no reported deaths** and the drone was not downed. Maduro (2018) and al-Burhan (2024) were **unharmed**; KRG PM's office (17 Aug 2026) took **no casualties**. Two of Colombia's official series are the strongest evidence of ineffectiveness: **only 4% of 8,395 drone *incidents* in 2025 were "effective"**, and drone **attacks** totalled 277 that year. Ecuador logged **8 incidents and zero drone-caused deaths** in three years. The chemical-payload claim in Michoacán was **officially denied** for lack of any trace.
- **Marginality.** Cartel blast escalation is fastest in **car bombs and ground IEDs** — the September 2026 Zacatecas car bomb used ~200 kg of explosives and injured 11, more blast effect than any documented cartel drone attack. Over 60% of cartel drone attacks hit **civilians**, and casualties per attack remain low.
- **Disruption.** USNORTHCOM reported **300+ cartel-linked drones stopped in 2026** (~100 in August alone) and a ~75% fall in Rio Grande Valley incursions after laser employment; Colombia claims **~96% neutralisation**; Rio created a dedicated counter-UAS unit (COANT, March 2026). UK and Irish prison hardening cut incursions sharply.
- **Debunked or retracted claims.** Copenhagen 2025 (**retracted**; Norwegian PST found most sightings were stars, weather or aircraft), Gatwick 2018 (**no drone ever produced**; the reward was **£50,000**, while the **£200,000** figure is the June 2020 wrongful-arrest settlement), Barajas (**drone "never existed"**), Vilnius (13 Sep 2026 — **birds**), Belgian VRT *Pano* (**558 reports, no physical evidence**; the minister's "large drone" was a police helicopter), the 2024 US north-east wave (FAA confirmed **its own research aircraft**), Munich (**"unconfirmed"**), "swarm attacks" (multi-pilot harassment), and Mexico's defence secretary attributing most border drone activity to **migrant-smuggler reconnaissance** rather than attack missions.
- **Strategic forecasting has been wrong.** The 2017 Pentagon forecast of a transatlantic non-state drone attack is unmet, and no located study shows drones becoming a **mass-casualty** terrorist weapon.

**Assessment:** across Europol, ACLED, InSight Crime, GI-TOC, Crisis Group and national threat assessments the pattern is **capability diffusion without lethality escalation** — more actors, more attacks, more surveillance and intimidation value, but casualties per attack remaining low relative to firearms and vehicle-borne IEDs.
