# Drone Incursions at Critical Infrastructure (Non-Airport), 2024 – September 2026

**Compiled 25 September 2026. Defensive threat research only: incidents, official statements, attributions and policy responses.**

## Method and evidence legend

Search engines were largely unusable here, so this rests on news-index retrieval plus full-text primary documents — chiefly the IISS dataset of **144 UAV incidents, August 2024 – February 2026** (*Russia's UAV Campaign Over Europe*, July 2026): **~48% over military facilities, 18% over civilian airports, 26% over critical infrastructure** (ports, energy, industry).

Evidence key: **[O]** official record · **[T]** think-tank/state intelligence assessment · **[J]** journalism only · **[U]** unresolved/contradicted.

## 2. Energy infrastructure

- **Brunsbüttel, Germany** — UAVs over the industrial park holding an **LNG facility and floating LNG terminal** from **8 August 2024**, approaching from the sea. Prosecutors treated the flights as "**espionage activity for sabotage purposes**" [O]. The **Heide oil refinery** was also overflown; platform type unconfirmed [T].
- **Jänschwalde and Bergheim substations, Germany** — explosives/incendiaries near a Jänschwalde substation and a second grid-sabotage attempt at Bergheim, **31 Aug – 2 Sept 2026**. Germany publicly blamed Russia for the related **Leipzig airport drone (5 Aug 2026)**; the EU's Kallas cited "**hallmarks of state-sponsored terrorism**" [O].
- **Estonia** — a stray drone from Russian airspace struck a **power station**, March 2026 [J/O]. **Lithuania** is hardening the future **Gizai substation** near Kaliningrad against Shahed-type drones, September 2026 [O]. **Romania** — F-16s and divers destroyed marine drones near the **Neptun Deep offshore gas project**, August 2026; Russia blamed by the defence ministry [O].
- **Offshore wind** — no verified North Sea wind-farm incursion found (absence of evidence, not evidence of absence).

## 3. Water utilities and telecom

- **Water**: **no verified drone incident found**, 2024–Sept 2026 — a genuine gap in open reporting.
- **Telecom**: a fire at Poland's state-owned **Starlink station at Wola-Krobowska on 23 September 2026** destroyed its power system and backup generator; Deputy PM Gawkowski called it **sabotage** and Poland "strongly suspects Russia" [O]. IISS also documents Russian-linked vessels loitering over **North Sea submarine telecom cables** (*Mulan*, Dec 2024–Jan 2025) and Swedish telecom-network attacks.

## 4. Military bases and training areas

Germany: **Ramstein**, **Ingolstadt-Manching**, **Neuburg AB**, **Schwesing** (six overflights in early 2025 at a base training Ukrainian Patriot crews), **Eckernförde** and **Sanitz**. A classified **BKA** report recorded **1,072 incidents involving 1,955 drones in Germany in 2025** [O/T]. Denmark: Aalborg, Skrydstrup, Karup, and **Køge port (3 Jan 2025 — up to 20 large UAVs in formation, police-confirmed)**. Norway: **Ørland** (27 Sept 2025), **Brønnøysund** (28 Sept 2025). UK: **~340 incidents at or near MoD sites in the year to Sept 2026** [J].

## 5. Official assessments of intent

The authoritative judgement is IISS (July 2026): it is "**highly likely that the Kremlin conducted a UAV campaign over Europe**," and "**likely**" that Russian-linked shadow-fleet vessels acted as launch/recovery platforms. Stated aims: probing air-defence response times and decision thresholds; **mapping vulnerabilities around critical infrastructure, including facilities tied to allied nuclear deterrence**; imposing economic and psychological costs; and **normalising low-level airspace violations** below the collective-response threshold.

Crucially, IISS cautions that **"none [of the European governments] have, to date, publicly attributed a UAV sighting to Russia"**, that not every sighting was Russian-directed or even a UAV, and that the aggregate pattern alone cannot be explained by misidentification or hobbyism. ECFR likewise finds "**definitive attribution to Russia or any other actor remains elusive**."

Corroborating official positions: Danish PET chief Finn Borch Andersen (25 Sept 2025) — the incidents "resemble a model of hybrid warfare," with Russian espionage **and** sabotage risk assessed "**high**." Denmark's **Defence Command (June 2026)** found observations ranged from probable aircraft to confirmed drones, with **no validated baseline of normal drone activity**; **Copenhagen Police (25 June 2026)** could **neither prove nor deny** drones were over Copenhagen Airport on 22 Sept 2025 — a significant correction to early "capable actor" claims. Sweden's military assessed the February 2026 Malmö UAV as Russian-origin, launched from the spy ship *Zhigulevsk*. Europol (March 2025) linked rising sabotage to Russian use of criminal networks; Denmark's DDIS (24 Sept 2026) expects further hybrid escalation.

**Counter-evidence:** some incidents are careless/recreational — Antwerp police seized two drones and fined pilots with invalid permits in the week before the Doel sighting, and the FAA confirmed the 2024 US north-east wave was its own research aircraft.

## 6. Where uncertainty remains

1. **No European government has formally attributed a critical-infrastructure drone sighting to Russia**; IISS's "highly likely" is a think-tank judgement, not a state finding.
2. Danish incidents are **partially retracted** — 2025 reporting should not be treated as settled.
3. **Swedish nuclear-plant overflights in 2024–2026 are unverified**; the confirmed case is 2022. **Water-sector and offshore-wind incidents are wholly unverified.**
4. Shadow-fleet launch rests on AIS/SAR correlation and two detained Russian PMSC contractors, **not** a judicial finding.
