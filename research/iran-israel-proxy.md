# Iranian Drone Strikes on Israel and Proxy Drone Attacks, 2023–Sept 2026

**Method note.** Every URL below was fetched in this task. Where a figure sits inside a Wikipedia article that itself cites a wire service, I flag it **[via citation]** — I fetched the Wikipedia revision, not the wire copy. Reuters (401), AP (403), Times of Israel (Cloudflare CAPTCHA) and CENTCOM (403) blocked direct retrieval. Interception percentages issued by a belligerent are marked **partisan**.

## 1. Iran's direct strikes on Israel

**1a. 13–14 April 2024 — "True Promise."** The barrage was "around 170 drones, over 30 cruise missiles, and more than 120 ballistic missiles." https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=April_2024_Iranian_strikes_on_Israel&action=raw — **CLAIMED**, **high** on order of magnitude.
- Interception: "Israel said that the coalition, whose defensive efforts were codenamed Iron Shield, destroyed 99 percent of the incoming weapons" (same URL, citing AP/Reuters/CNN) — **CLAIMED, partisan**, **medium**.
- **Contested:** the same article's infobox records "99% per Israel" against "84% intercepted per Maariv." Two allied-belligerent figures for one event; the true rate is unknown between ~84–99%.
- US role: CENTCOM reportedly said US forces destroyed 80 drones and 6 missiles launched from Iran and Yemen — **CLAIMED [via citation]**, Reuters headline only; I could not fetch Reuters.
- Leakers: "A senior U.S. official stated that five Iranian ballistic missiles struck the Nevatim Airbase... Additionally, four other ballistic missiles impacted the Ramon Airbase" (same URL) — **CLAIMED**, **medium**; overall damage "minimal."

**1b. 1 October 2024 — "True Promise II."** "Iran launched about 200 ballistic missiles at targets in Israel, in at least two waves." https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=October_2024_Iranian_strikes_on_Israel&action=raw — **CLAIMED**, **high** (outlets cluster at ~180–200; its citations give the IDF-derived "181 missiles").
- Leakers: "The area of the Nevatim Airbase in the Negev was hit by 20 to 32 missiles… Several other missiles hit the Tel Nof Airbase, a school in the nearby town of Gedera, and an area north of Tel Aviv" — **DEMONSTRATED** (satellite imagery, NPR), **high**. Analysts judged this more successful at saturating defences than April 2024, yet it caused no extensive damage.

**1c. June 2025 — Twelve-Day War ("True Promise III").** https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Twelve-Day_War&action=raw
- 13 June: "more than 100 Shahed drones" (IDF Brig. Gen. Effie Defrin) plus an IDF-estimated "around 150 ballistic missiles… in two waves"; 14 June: "Iran launched 200 ballistic missiles overnight" — **CLAIMED**, **medium-high**.
- "IDF says some 950 Iranian drones destroyed before being launched at Israel" — **CLAIMED, partisan**, **low-medium**; a pre-emption claim, not an interception count, and unverifiable.
- 23 June 2025, Al Udeid (Qatar): "fourteen ballistic missiles being launched at the base… with thirteen of them having been intercepted" (same domain: https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Attacks_on_US_bases_during_the_Middle_Eastern_crisis_(2023%E2%80%93present)&action=raw) — **CLAIMED**, **medium-high** (13/14 = 93%).

**1d. 2026 Iran war (from 28 February 2026).** https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2026_Iranian_strikes_on_Israel&action=raw
- Volume: "In the 40 days from the start of the war through the 8 April ceasefire, there were a total of 650 missile attacks from Iran to Israel… About 16 of the missiles that made it past Israeli missile defenses carried conventional warheads, while 50 had cluster munitions. The attacks killed a total of 24 people, all of them civilians, and resulted in more than 7,000 injuries." (Times of Israel) — **CLAIMED/DEMONSTRATED**, **medium-high**.
- Leaker rate: 16 conventional + ~50 cluster incidents out of 650 ⇒ roughly **90% did not penetrate populated areas**; the IDF assessed "about half of the roughly 300 ballistic missiles Iran has launched at Israel" by day 10 carried cluster warheads — **CLAIMED, partisan**.
- **Best drone-specific figure:** "Iran's ballistic missile launch rate has fallen approximately 92%… collapsing from 480 launches on February 28 to just 40 on March 9. **Drone launches have followed the identical curve, down 92% from 720 to 60.**" (Jerusalem Post) — **CLAIMED**, **medium**.
- Gulf states (Qatar, https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=2026_Iranian_strikes_on_Qatar&action=raw): "66 missiles were fired in total and there were over 100 reports of falling shrapnel," with 16 injured by shrapnel from interceptions (28 Feb, Qatari Interior Ministry) — **CLAIMED**, **medium**. Confirmed impacts: two Iranian drones struck Ras Laffan and Mesaieed (2 Mar); a later strike on Ras Laffan disrupted ~17% of Qatari LNG exports for 3–5 years; of three Iranian cruise missiles at Qatar on 1 April, "two missiles were intercepted while the third one hit an oil tanker" — **DEMONSTRATED**, **high**.

## 2. Islamic Resistance in Iraq (IRI) drone attacks on Israel, 2024–2026

Best-quantified source is the Israeli Alma Research and Education Center (partisan-adjacent think tank, not primary):
- "Since November 2, 2023, the Islamic Resistance in Iraq has claimed responsibility for **312 attacks against Israel**. Of these, **89 reached Israeli territory**… Reports indicated that **4 attacks struck targets within Israel**." https://israel-alma.org/the-islamic-resistance-in-iraq-attacks-against-israel-november-2-2023-to-november-20-2024/ — **INFERRED/CLAIMED**, **medium**. Also: "The vast majority of the attacks (70%)… do not actually reach Israeli territory, and there is no further verification other than their claim of responsibility."
- Earlier snapshot: "**167 attacks**… UAVs carried out **75%**… in **41 incidents (24%)** the IDF reported it intercepted the threat." https://israel-alma.org/the-islamic-resistance-in-iraq-september-2024/ — **medium**.
- **Demonstrated leakers:** 3 Oct 2024 Golan Heights base drone strike killed 2 soldiers and wounded 23–24; 25 Sept 2024 an Eilat port drone impact wounded 2; 26 June 2024 the IDF "confirmed it failed to intercept the drone." https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Islamic_Resistance_in_Iraq&action=raw — **DEMONSTRATED**, **high**.
- Jordan: "Jordan said it shot down dozens of Iranian drones" (April 2024); the Royal Jordanian Air Force also intercepted Iraqi drones in June 2025 — **CLAIMED** (Jordanian/Israeli), **medium**.
- 2026: IRI "claimed responsibility for 23 drone strikes on US assets in Erbil" (2 Mar); Kataib Hezbollah "launched a drone that breached the air defenses of the Camp Victory base and managed to strike it" (14 Mar) — **CLAIMED** / **partly DEMONSTRATED** (Al Jazeera video), **medium**.

## 3. Kataib Hezbollah / IRI attacks on US bases, 2023–24

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Attacks_on_US_bases_during_the_Middle_Eastern_crisis_(2023%E2%80%93present)&action=raw
- **Volume (contested):** "more than **170 attacks** on US military bases and assets in Syria, Iraq, and Jordan" from 17 Oct 2023 (ISW, 28 Jan 2024). The Tower 22 article counts instead "**160 occasions**" of missile/rocket fire between 7 Oct 2023 and 27 Jan 2024 (The Economist). https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Tower_22_drone_attack&action=raw. The widely quoted "~165+" falls between two different counting methods; the discrepancy is unresolved.
- **Tower 22, 28 Jan 2024:** a one-way drone struck the Rukban, Jordan outpost, killing 3 US soldiers and injuring 47. A US official indicated a **Shahed drone** was used; the Pentagon said the attack bore Kata'ib Hezbollah's "footprints"; the US formally blamed the IRI on 31 January. Iran denied involvement. — **DEMONSTRATED** (casualties, **high**); attribution **CLAIMED**, **medium**.
- **US retaliation, 2 Feb 2024:** strikes "hit **85 targets** across seven facilities, three in Iraq and four in Syria, using **125 precision-guided missiles**," with two B-1B bombers; "more than 80 were destroyed or rendered inoperable." Deaths 54–55 (35 in Syria per SOHR; 16–17 in Iraq). On 7 Feb a US drone strike in Baghdad killed KH commander Abu Baqir al-Saadi. — **CLAIMED** (US), **high** for the package, **medium** for battle damage. KH suspended operations on 30 Jan 2024; attacks halted after the February strikes.

## Caveats
1. **All interception rates are belligerent claims**; no independent assessment validates the 99% (April 2024) or ~93% (Al Udeid 2025) figures.
2. **Claim-vs-impact gap dominates proxy drone warfare:** ~70–82% of IRI's *claimed* attacks never reached Israeli territory (Alma).
3. **2026 drone data is thin.** The only precise figure found (720 → 60 daily launches) rests on one Jerusalem Post analysis seen via Wikipedia; treat as **low-medium**. I found **no** overall 2026 Iranian drone-launch total against Israel/Gulf states and did not guess one.
4. **Not retrieved:** primary CENTCOM April 2024 tally (80 drones/6 missiles) exists only as a Reuters headline in a citation list; Reuters/AP/ToI were inaccessible.
