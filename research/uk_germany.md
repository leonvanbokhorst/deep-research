# UK and German National Institutional Assessments: Drones/UAS, AI and Autonomous Capabilities as an Internal Threat

**25 September 2026.** Primary documents retrieved directly (gov.uk, mi5.gov.uk, bka.de, verfassungsschutz.de, bundesregierung.de, dserver.bundestag.de); search engines were largely blocked, so gov.uk's search API, sitemaps, the Bundestag Drucksachen route and Google News RSS were used for discovery. **Labels:** [P] PRIMARY (agency document/official speech/parliamentary source); [S] SECONDARY (journalistic). Quotations are verbatim. No operational or attack guidance.

## A. UNITED KINGDOM

**A1. CONTEST — no 2025/26 refresh exists.** The strategy remains **CONTEST 2023** (22 Sept 2023); gov.uk shows "Last updated: 18 July 2023" and no 2024–26 edition exists. AI appears as a **hinged future warning** (para 48): *"Artificial Intelligence (AI) is one of the fastest growing deep technologies in the world… with the potential for terrorist activity to become more sophisticated with less effort."* Drones appear as **capability**, not threat (annex, para 19): *"We have developed the first UK police counter-drone capability to address the increasing potential for small drones to be used as weapons or to facilitate crime"*; the UK co-leads a GCTF initiative *"to counter terrorist use of Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS)."* **NOT VERIFIED:** the promised *"refreshed UK Drone Security Strategy"* was never located. [P]

**A2. MI5.** Ken McCallum's **16 Oct 2025** annual threat update [P] is the clearest UK AI statement: *"Would-be terrorists already try to harness AI for their propaganda, their weapons research, their target reconnaissance. State actors exploit AI to manipulate elections and sharpen their cyber attacks"*, plus *"potential future risks from non-human, autonomous AI systems which may evade human oversight and control."* He explicitly caps the alarm: *"I am not forecasting Hollywood movie scenarios… Artificial intelligence may never 'mean' us harm. But it would be reckless to ignore the potential for it to cause harm."* Drones appear **once**, as protective-security advice only — *"whether the threat is from sabotage, theft or drones"* — and MI5 makes **no** claim of drone-enabled UK terrorism. State threats: *"a 35% increase"* in state-threat investigations and *"more than twenty potentially lethal Iran-backed plots"* in a year. On **1 May 2026** JTAC raised the threat level SUBSTANTIAL → **SEVERE** citing Islamist and Extreme Right-Wing threat, with **no drone or AI nexus**. On **9 July 2026** McCallum welcomed the **National Security (State Threats) Act 2026**, citing *"arson, sabotage and physical violence"* and *"the growing threat from proxy organisations."* [P]

**A3. CT Policing and the Independent Reviewer.** No drone-specific public statement exists on counterterrorism.police.uk (sitemaps searched) — **NOT VERIFIED / apparently absent.** [P] Jonathan Hall KC (Independent Reviewer of Terrorism Legislation) is the most substantive official voice. In his **22 Oct 2024** speech *"Generative AI, Drones, and Terrorism"* [P]: *"Drones have been adopted by criminals – for example, to fly contraband into prisons – and will no doubt be adopted further by terrorists"* — an **official warning about the future**, not evidence of current use. He urges regulatory restraint: *"If the law does not keep pace with technologies, then moral panic will set in… in the absence of laws, then terrorists will be emboldened to use drones; and society may end up overreacting, and miss out on their benefits."* His **"Terrorism Acts in 2024"** report (laid 15 July 2025) [P] frames drones as policy tension: *"Drones as an aspect of future economic growth are in tension with drones as an aspect of terrorist (and hostile state) threat."*

**A4. National Crime Agency.** *National Strategic Assessment 2026* [P]: *"Technology is no longer simply a tool that criminals use. It is reshaping crime itself"*; drones are named among prison drug-smuggling methods (*"drones flying drugs into prisons"*), and on AI: *"AI adds complexity and volume, and algorithms accelerate and normalise harm."* No NSA 2025 located — **NOT VERIFIED**.

**A5. Prisons — the best-evidenced UK drone threat, and it is criminal, not terrorist.** MoJ/HMPPS, **31 July 2025** [P]: *"there were 1,712 drone incidents between April 2024 and March 2025 at prisons across England and Wales. This is an increase of 43 per cent compared to the previous 12 months."* Operations at HMP Manchester and Wandsworth yielded **nine arrests**; a recovered drone had *"a value of £6,000… and the ability to hold four loads at one time."* Follow-ups: **16 Jan 2026** — £6.5m UKRI anti-drone R&D to *"learn from Ukraine's expertise"*, asserting *"prison drones pose a direct threat to national security"* and repeating a **770%** rise (*"between 2019 and 2023"*); **11 June 2026** — £35m to grille 13,000 cell windows at 17 high-risk prisons and *"over 200 arrests linked to drones smuggling contraband"*; **3 Feb 2026** — £1.85m UKDI competition noting *"kinetic interceptors or wide-area jamming, are often unsuitable in custodial and urban environments."* **SI 2023/1101** created drone-only Restricted Airspace around closed prisons from 25 Jan 2024. [P]

**A6. Airports and counter-UAS policy.** The **2019 UK Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Strategy** [P] names Gatwick as the trigger: *"The drone disruption to UK airports over the 2018 Christmas period was a wake-up call"*, and records **284 / 319 / 168** prison drone incidents for 2016/17/18. The **Air Navigation Order 2016** was amended (Feb 2019, in force 13 Mar 2019) to extend airport restriction zones to the ATZ plus 5km×1km runway extensions. Current procurement is **Ukraine-driven**: MoD/UKDI (15 Dec 2025) announced *"over £142 million"*, including *"around £30 million… into counter-drone technology… in the face of increasing Russian-linked drone incursions across Europe"*; UKDI (27 Mar 2026) sought solutions against **fibre-optic-controlled UAS**, *"immune to conventional countermeasures."* The FCDO's **11 Sept 2025** OSCE statement condemned *"violations of Polish sovereign airspace by Russian military drone assets"* that closed *"key civilian airports."* **No 2025/26 Heathrow or Gatwick drone closure was verified**; Sept 2026 UK airport disruption was an **air traffic control failure** — Heathrow drone-closure claims are **NOT VERIFIED**. [P]

**A7. UK figures.** Prison incidents 2016/17/18: 284/319/168 [P]. Apr 2024–Mar 2025: **1,712** (+43%) [P]. 2019→2023 growth: **+770%** [P]. Arrests for prison drone smuggling: **200+** [P]. Drone incidents at UK military sites, yr to Aug 2026: *"over 340"* — **[S]** (Telegraph/Independent, 18–19 Sept 2026), no primary release found → **NOT VERIFIED**.

## B. GERMANY

**B1. BfV — explicitly warns against over-attribution.** *Verfassungsschutzbericht 2025* (published 30 June 2026) [P] treats drone overflights as a driver of uncertainty, not a proven campaign: *"die Meldungen über mutmaßliche Drohnenüberflüge verdeutlichen die Notwendigkeit einer genauen Untersuchung aller Vorfälle… um die aktuelle hybride Bedrohungslage realistisch einzuordnen"* ("reports of **suspected** overflights… require precise examination of all incidents to classify the hybrid threat realistically"). It names the risk of **"Überattribution"** and *"eine Überschätzung der Fähigkeiten des russischen Staates"*, and states: *"Gleichwohl gibt es bisher keine Beweise dafür, dass russische staatliche Stellen Extremisten in Deutschland instrumentalisiert und für Sabotage eingesetzt haben"* ("**there is so far no evidence** that Russian state bodies have instrumentalised extremists in Germany for sabotage"). Hostile drone activity is documented under **Islamism** (Hizb Allah drone-component procurement, Salzgitter arrest 14 July 2024), plus a section *"KI-Nutzung durch Islamisten."* The **2024** report (10 June 2025) [P] has **no** substantive drone-overflight content — the wave postdates it.

**B2. BKA — the clearest official brake on the AI narrative.** *Bundeslagebild Organisierte Kriminalität 2025* [P], first structured survey of "spezielle Tatmittel": *"der Einsatz von künstlicher Intelligenz (1 EV)"* — **AI in exactly one case**, against 43 for concealed vehicles — before generalising forward: *"Künstliche Intelligenz kann beispielsweise zur Tatplanung und -ausführung… verwendet werden."* Drones appear only as a future risk via veterans' skills *"im Umgang mit Technologien wie Drohnen."* *Bundeslagebild Cybercrime 2025* [P] gives the sharpest honesty marker: *"Bislang liegen keine polizeilichen Kennzahlen zum konkreten KI-Einsatz in Cybercrimedelikten vor"* ("**to date there are no police metrics on concrete AI use** in cybercrime").

**B3. Federal government and BMVg — assessments, presumptions and admitted legal gaps.** The Bundesregierung **FAQ, 2 Sept 2026** [P] is the central official statement: *"die Zahl [illegaler Drohnenflüge] ist besonders seit Beginn des Angriffskrieges Russlands gegen die Ukraine angestiegen. Viele dieser illegalen Vorfälle werden mutmaßlich auch von ausländischen staatlichen Akteuren… gesteuert"* — note **"mutmaßlich"** (presumed). It also warns against alarmism: *"Viele Drohnenflüge zielen nicht primär auf direkte Angriffe, sondern auf Verunsicherung"* ("many drone flights are not aimed primarily at direct attacks but at causing uncertainty") — an explicit **influence-operations framing**. BMVg admitted a legal gap (12 Sept 2025): *"Für den Bereich von zum Beispiel Kleinstdrohnen… hat die Bundeswehr in diesem Höhenband nicht die Befugnis und die Wirkungshoheit"*, and set the constitutional limit (6 Oct 2025): *"Eine kinetische Wirkung ist bei diesen Vorfällen im Innern durch das Grundgesetz aber eben nicht vorgesehen."* The **Chancellor's attribution was presumptive**: *"Unsere Vermutung ist, dass Russland hinter den meisten dieser Drohnenflüge steckt."* After an explosive-laden drone was found near a Ukrainian cargo aircraft at **Leipzig/Halle (4–5 Aug 2026)**, the government **attributed it to Russia on 1–4 Sept 2026**. Countervailing, Antwort **21/7725** (25 Aug 2026) [P] classified comparable Ukrainian airspace intrusions as *"technische Fehlfunktionen, die mutmaßlich auf bewusste russische Störmaßnahmen des… GPS zurückzuführen sind"* — **not an "bewaffneter Angriff"** under Art. 5 NATO / Art. 42(7) EUV.

**B4. Legislation and procurement.** The **Zweites Gesetz zur Änderung des Luftsicherheitsgesetzes** (in force **17 Mar 2026**) [P] lets the *Streitkräfte* use *Waffengewalt* *"als letztes Mittel"* in *Amtshilfe*, moving the decision to the BMVg ministry and dropping *Benehmen* with the BMI. The **Bundespolizeigesetz** reform [P] adds powers *"zur Detektion und Abwehr von Drohnen"* plus *"automatisierte Erkennung von Gefahren."* The **Gemeinsames Drohnenabwehrzentrum** opened 17 Dec 2025 [P]; the **Bundespolizei Drohnenabwehreinheit** began with 130 staff and €100m, expanding (10 Aug 2026) to **300** staff and 8 locations. **Contested:** Grüne Drucksache **21/7669** [P] calls the GDAZ *"Symbolpolitik"* and doubts its *"tatsächliche[r] Arbeits- und Einsatzfähigkeit."*

**B5. German figures.** Suspicious drone flights registered by BKA in 2025: **">1,000"** — quoted inside [P] Drucksachen 21/7243 and 21/7669, but both footnote tagesschau, so the figure itself is **[S]** (BKA President Münch, Dec 2025 — who also cautioned *"Nicht jede Drohne ist ein 'Liebesgruß aus Moskau'"*). Airport drone incidents: 144 to Aug 2025; 166 flight objects H1 2026, **+64% y/y** [S]. NRW cases 232 (2024) → 351 (2025) [S]. Sabotage suspicion cases 2025: 321, *"oft mit Drohnen verknüpft"* [S]. Bundeswehr incidents since Feb 2022: *"mehrere Hundert"* [S]. **DFS per-year official figures and drone-specific arrest/prosecution statistics: NOT VERIFIED.**

## C. Disagreements and thin evidence

1. **BfV vs executive attribution (Germany) — the sharpest disagreement.** The BfV (30 June 2026) speaks of *"mutmaßliche"* overflights, warns of **"Überattribution"**, and states there is **no evidence** of Russian state tasking of extremists. The Chancellor spoke of a *"Vermutung"*, and the government attributed the Leipzig drone to Russia in September 2026. German media interrogated the public evidence base (deutschlandfunk, 3 Sept 2026: *"Wie viele Beweise muss die Politik öffentlich vorlegen?"*).
2. **Same government, opposite classification.** Drucksache 21/7725 (25 Aug 2026) treated comparable 2026 intrusions as **technical malfunctions** from GPS jamming, not armed attacks — a brake on generalising from incursions to attacks.
3. **BKA data vs political salience.** AI appeared in **one** organised-crime case in 2025 and the BKA has **no police metrics** on AI in cybercrime. German AI-threat language is therefore **official warning about the future**, not measured prevalence.
4. **MI5 vs media framing (UK).** McCallum's AI passage is deliberately hedged and MI5 claims no drone-enabled UK terrorism; the more assertive drone language comes from the **Independent Reviewer** (a legal oversight role) and prison-security policy, where evidence is genuinely strong.
5. **The best UK evidence is the wrong category for counter-terrorism.** Prison drones — 1,712 incidents, +770%, 200+ arrests, multiple convictions — are an **organised-crime** problem, and drones are absent from the May 2026 SEVERE rationale.
6. **UK statutory vacuum.** No CONTEST refresh since 2023, and the promised *"refreshed UK Drone Security Strategy"* was never located.
7. **Both states now justify counter-UAS procurement via Ukraine-derived lessons and Russian grey-zone incursions** rather than domestic terrorist use — a shift in the stated evidentiary basis.
8. **German competence dispute.** BMVg held kinetic counter-UAS inside Germany constitutionally unavailable; the March 2026 law answered part of this, but the opposition alleges the instruments are symbolic and no independent effectiveness evidence is public.
