# Drone-caused disruption at German airports and official responses, 2023 – September 2026

**Scope:** incidents and official responses only; no operational or counter-UAS method detail.
**Evidence key:** **[V]** full text retrieved · **[H]** headline/RSS only · **[U]** unresolved.

## 1. Aggregate scale (DFS / BDL / BKA)

[V] DFS via dpa (26 Sep 2025): **151** drone incidents at German airports in **2023**, **161** in **2024**, **144** by Aug 2025. Frankfurt was worst (**35** disruptions by Aug 2025, up from ~20 each prior year). DFS records observations, not intent; perpetrators "usually remain unknown," hobby pilots "frequently assumed."

[V] **H1 2026:** BER logged **28 sightings** (seven causing suspension) — the most of any German airport; full-year 2025 was only **8**, and a 24 Sep 2026 dpa package reports **37 since January**.

[V] **BDL**: drone disruption cost **€60m in 2025**, **€160m with follow-up** (ZEIT, 26 Aug 2026). [V] A confidential **BKA Lagebild** (end-Aug 2026) counted **165+ suspected sabotage cases**, **747 suspicious drone incidents** (**1,068 drones**) and **24 espionage investigations** (tagesschau/NDR/WDR/SZ, 3 Sep 2026).

## 2. Frankfurt (FRA)

[V/H] **11 Aug 2025** — a drone sighting "temporarily stopped" flight operations (hessenschau headline verified; body withdrawn). [H] WELT named FRA the "Spitzenreiter" for sightings (4 Oct 2025); Hesse's premier promised tougher defence and Interior Minister Poseck moved to tighten drone law (5/9 Oct). [V] **7 Aug 2026** — Bundespolizei warned drone pilots via social media against entering the FRA flight-restriction zone.

**[U] No individual FRA drone closure in July 2024 or 2023 could be substantiated.** Aggregate reporting confirms repeated 2023 impact (DIE ZEIT, 21 Apr 2024) and 75 *nationwide* incidents in H1 2024 (FAZ/Spiegel, 9 Jul 2024). The July 2024 FRA event remains **unconfirmed**.

## 3. Munich (MUC) — premise correction

**The Bundeswehr deployment belongs to October 2025, not May 2025.**

[V] **3–4 Oct 2025 (Fri–Sat):** suspected drones on **two consecutive evenings**; operations suspended **twice**; dozens of flights diverted or cancelled; thousands affected. [H] Frankfurter Rundschau/FAZ framed it as *"Waren es Militärobjekte? Bundeswehr leistet Amtshilfe"*; dpa (3 Oct) said a **military drone** may have caused the shutdown. [V] A 2026 Merkur interview with Arx Robotics CEO Marc Wietfeld confirms Bundeswehr, airport and political actors brought specialists in overnight. **[U] Attribution ambiguous** — German military objects were never publicly excluded. Bavaria then planned a *Schnellgesetz* on shoot-downs and a Polizeiaufgabengesetz amendment.

[H] **2 May 2025:** a separate sighting stopped traffic, diverting aircraft to Hörsching/Linz (MeinBezirk.at); duration **unverified**, and TZ (10 May) indicates the airport later gave a non-drone explanation. [V] **30 May 2026:** two pilots independently reported a suspected drone at 09:03; runways closed ≈09:00–10:05 (**~1 hour**); **26 aircraft diverted**; ~60 flights delayed 30–120 min, ~40 cancelled of ~900; police searched and **found nothing**. [V] **6 Sep 2026:** a "verdächtige Wahrnehmung" closed **departures only** ≈16:15–16:46 (**~30 min**); 17 departures delayed, two Lufthansa arrivals diverted; nothing found, and Bundespolizei **explicitly declined to call it a drone incident**.

## 4. Berlin (BER)

[V] **23 Sep 2026:** staff sighted a drone on the north runway at 20:00; DFS closed the north runway, then the south at 20:20; traffic suspended **~45 minutes**, reopened 21:05. Bundespolizei reported "keine Feststellung" — **nothing recovered**. Brandenburg police took over, investigating *gefährlicher Eingriff in den Luftverkehr*. **[U] unresolved.** [H] **31 Oct 2025:** a sighting closed BER **~2 hours** (Tagesspiegel), prompting calls for better defence. [H] **12 Mar 2026:** BER suspended operations over an unidentified object. [V] Bundespolizei installed a new detection system at BER in 2026.

## 5. Hamburg (HAM) and Cologne/Bonn (CGN)

[H] **8 Aug 2026:** HAM starts and landings briefly suspended after a drone alarm; police offered a probable non-hostile explanation. [V] **16 Sep 2026:** Hamburg's Senate approved a **€4.2m** system (Argus Interception) with three AI interceptor drones able to net-capture, funded from the federal infrastructure special fund. [V] **12 Aug 2026 (CGN):** **five** sightings; DFS closed airspace parts ~12:30; **landings blocked ~30 min**; two Ryanair flights diverted to Frankfurt-Hahn; normal by ~14:00. A **drone pilot found near the airport** became the subject of an **Ermittlungsverfahren** — the clearest single attribution here.

## 6. Context

[H] Hannover closed **6 Nov 2025**, **27 Dec 2025** and **11 Aug 2026** (the last with six delayed flights and one cargo diversion; police noted "hybride Aktivität" could not be excluded). Erfurt-Weimar closed **22 Oct 2025**. Bremen, **2–3 Nov 2025**: police explicitly **did not** assume espionage. [V] **Leipzig/Halle, 4–5 Aug 2026**: an explosive-laden drone was found among Ukrainian Antonov freighters, apparently striking a wing without detonating. The **Bundesanwaltschaft** took over; the government blames Russia, which denies it; a third drone was found 25 Aug 2026.

## 7. Attribution, prosecution and policy

[V] Innenminister **Dobrindt**: Leipzig showed a "new danger quality" and **professional** perpetrators, implying "fremde Mächte"; investigation ongoing. Routine sightings are mostly **[U] unresolved**; where pilots are identified, espionage usually proves absent (BKA). Arrests: a man in Hesse (Oct 2025); a Moldovan in Bavaria (July 2026) over alleged spying on defence firm KNDS for Russian state bodies.

[V/H] Dobrindt directed the Bundespolizei drone-defence unit to grow from ~130 to **300 specialists at eight sites** (10 Aug 2026); DLR opened a **German Centre for Drone Security in Cochstedt** (18 Aug); Verkehrsminister **Bilger** pushed rapid **interim DFS detection** and corridor coverage (21 Aug); interior ministers agreed to equip **eight airports** (Frankfurt, Munich, Berlin, Düsseldorf, Hamburg, Stuttgart, Leipzig, Cologne); the **Greens demanded a "Drohnengipfel" in the Chancellery** (20 Aug); the joint **GAZ-Hybrid** centre opened in Berlin in June 2026. Bundeswehr deployment in the interior remains contested.
