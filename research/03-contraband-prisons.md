# Drone-Based Contraband and Prison Smuggling — Evidence Assessment

**Compiled:** 25 September 2026 · Threat/defence assessment only.
**Cross-cutting finding:** every documented contraband case below used a **remotely piloted** drone (commercial quadcopter with release mechanism, or a thrown package). No verified autonomous contraband delivery was found; autonomy appears only in *prisons' own* systems (Belgium) and a Europol 2035 scenario.

---

## Belgium

- **8 Dec 2023, Belgium/France** — an organised crime group ran drone supply of drugs and cigarettes into French and Belgian prisons; gang dismantled. Remote. Journalism ([VRT](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqAFBVV95cUxPMDByUlo4QmxUb0s4SFJiaVlMY1RoLWx6bzJkbUZLdlBfM0trLVJremZNTzBlb24xSXFKRVJJQm1hdVQ1aEJlaGxySEtYcndkaEN6UG5kNmdxeVVMTGh2REc0eVYta1M3cGtjaTdCY19RRW8wV212OWJ1UXlITEZnaGNmTUNMbWVSVUhqUE1HTmxnWTJ6ZUxvZWpFWHktSU1Yaml1ZG9RWTE)).
- **Apr–Aug 2025** — a drone-enabled escape attempt failed and prisons bought detection systems; the administration then began using **autonomous guard drones** against air smuggling — the only autonomy found, and a state countermeasure ([Nieuwsblad](https://www.nieuwsblad.be/binnenland/plan-om-gedetineerde-te-laten-ontsnappen-met-drone-mislukt-gevangenissen-krijgen-detectiesysteem/53944705.html), [GVA](https://www.gva.be/binnenland/belgische-gevangenissen-zetten-autonome-drones-in-tegen-smokkel-via-de-lucht/87227624.html)).

## Netherlands

- **Apr 2021 – Feb 2024, Veenhuizen / Almelo / Krimpen aan den IJssel** — a 34-year-old and two accomplices flew **~70 flights** delivering phones and drugs onto exercise yards, earning ~€30,000; one inmate caught a phone through his cell window. Remote; proved by drone flight data and cell-tower records ([NOS](https://nos.nl/artikel/2611582-taakstraf-en-boete-voor-man-die-met-drones-drugs-dropte-in-gevangenissen), [Rijnmond](https://www.rijnmond.nl/nieuws/2185084/taakstraf-voor-afleveren-iphones-en-drugspakketjes-in-gevangenis-via-drones)).
- **22 Apr 2026 sentence:** 120 hours' community service, €10,000 fine (half suspended), four months suspended, via *procesafspraken*. The OM called drones "an increasingly serious threat to order and safety in penitentiary institutions" and wants phone-dropping reclassified from an **offence** to a **crime**, which currently caps sentencing and blocks wiretaps. A follow-up links the pilot to a *Mocro Maffia* associate ([Rijnmond](https://www.rijnmond.nl/nieuws/2185610/met-zijn-drone-dropt-mark-34-drugs-en-mobieltjes-in-gevangenissen-ook-voor-handlanger-mocro-maffia)).
- **1 Jul 2026** — Dutch prisons, including EBI Vught, gained power to **take down drones themselves** ([Parool](https://www.parool.nl/nederland/gevangenissen-mogen-vanaf-woensdag-zelf-drones-neerhalen-criminelen-zoeken-voortdurend-naar-nieuwe-manieren~bd15a654/)).

**Gap:** no DJI/NCTV statistical series; the June 2026 NCTV threat review does not cover this.

## United Kingdom

**Official statistics (HMPPS/MoJ, 31 Jul 2025):** incidents hit a record **1,712 in the year to March 2025**, up **43%** from 1,196, versus **357 (2021/22)** and **138 (2020/21)** — about five sightings daily, a **1,140%** five-year rise. MoJ added **£900,000** for counter-drone work on top of **£40m** for netting and reinforced windows; **nine arrests** followed operations at HMP Manchester and Wandsworth ([Standard/PA](https://www.standard.co.uk/news/crime/drones-wandsworth-ministry-of-justice-ian-jones-b1240992.html), [Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/crime/prison-drugs-crisis-justice-committee-drones-b2855449.html)).

- **3 Mar 2026** — a seven-person drone gang sentenced to a **combined 22 years** after **50+ drops in three months** targeting Wormwood Scrubs, Brixton, Pentonville, Wandsworth, Norwich, Leicester, Onley and Bedford ([Met](https://news.met.police.uk/news/drone-gang-behind-majority-of-london-prison-drops-jailed-506687)).
- **18 Sep 2026** — three jailed over a drone plot at HMP Manchester ([ITV](https://www.itv.com/news/granada/2026-09-18/three-people-jailed-over-drone-plot-to-smuggle-drugs-into-prison)).
- **31 Oct 2025** — the Justice Committee called the drugs crisis "endemic" (136 drug deaths, Dec 2022–2024), recommended "Sky Fence" systems, and noted throwovers and visits remain more common.

Sky News reported a further **40%+ rise** ([31 Jul 2025](https://news.sky.com/story/number-of-drones-incidents-in-prisons-rises-by-more-than-40-figures-show-13404812)); **13,000 windows** were being gridded (Jun 2026). All cases remote-controlled.

## Ireland

**Official statistics (Irish Prison Service, 2025):** **384 drone deliveries detected**, including **212 at Wheatfield, 58 at Cloverhill, 49 at Mountjoy** (the published breakdown does not sum to the total). Seizures rose: **1,647 phones (+33%)**, **1,325 drugs (+28%)**, **441 weapons (+70%)**; recognised gangs rose **80% to 34**. The IPS spent **€5m** on metallic mesh; at Wheatfield incursions fell **73%** (12 deliveries in the first four months of 2026), and **20 arrests** followed in 2026, up 56%. Minister Jim O'Callaghan said netting "was proving effective" ([Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/crime-law/2026/05/04/despite-battle-against-high-tech-drone-deliveries-prison-contraband-is-on-the-rise/)).

## European-level evidence

- **Europol Innovation Lab, *The Unmanned Future(s)* (2025, ISBN 978-92-9414-064-7):** criminals increasingly use drones to smuggle illicit goods; malicious use "mostly involves opportunistic, **remote-controlled**, commercially available and easily obtainable UAS", is "particularly prevalent in rural and uninhabited border areas", and includes pilots selling services online. Its "prison-yard extractions" line is a **2035 scenario**, not documented fact.
- **EU SOCTA 2025 summary:** no drone or prison-contraband mention — a significant **evidence gap**.
- **Europol** warns drones pose a growing threat to civil society ([Sveriges Radio, 13 Apr 2026](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqwFBVV95cUxNbXk0T2MwbUNEbjRUREMzRHdndE1GT3N4SGpLZk80QnVTTU5oOWRqbVRxWGZ0dkQ5dUxKb3FhUkwzQlk0dzJTVkkwRnNHYnBjZmFnb1d6UVNMU1F1ek1wemF6dDVPajVRZUlTSGRTX09XWHlyazVVVUVfc3NFQW9jVGozNTd0R1h1X05qVmpJZnpyaHFSWi1wMkVlRV9LV0MxTzgxVElLNzBObWs)).

## Evidence quality and gaps

- **Strongest:** UK (MoJ/HMPPS counts, named sentences), Ireland (IPS counts, spend, arrests), US (federal prosecutions).
- **Moderate:** Netherlands (court outcome, no DJI aggregate), Belgium (court reporting, no sighting series), Brazil (police/SEAP seizures, sparse aggregates).
- **Weak:** Sweden/Denmark (individual cases only), EU (thematic report, no quantified baseline).
- **Judgement:** present in all surveyed states, growing where measured (UK, Ireland), reliant on **remotely piloted commodity hardware** rather than autonomy, and met with hardening, detection and new counter-drone powers. The main gap is a comparable European statistical baseline.
