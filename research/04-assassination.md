## 1. Caracas, 4 August 2018 — Maduro

At 17:41, during a live broadcast of the Guard's anniversary parade, drones exploded near the presidential stage. Interior Minister Reverol said two DJI M600s carried about 1.8 kg of C-4, one meant to detonate above Maduro and one in front of him; Communication Minister Rodríguez said three drones were used and jammers deflected them. Seven to nine people were hurt, including a girl in the Don Eduardo building — resident testimony and Telemundo footage corroborate a device striking civilian property. **No autonomy is alleged.**

**Staged or real?** A real explosive event is well supported; authorship is not. No independent investigation was permitted and journalists were detained. Bellingcat judged two drones "likely" armed but said attribution was impossible. **"Soldados de Franelas"** claimed responsibility in a Twitter statement read in Miami by exiled journalist Patricia Poleo within hours, alongside a competing "Operación Fénix" claim — the speed, overlap and ambiguity weaken both. Former police chief **Salvatore Lucchese** told Reuters he largely organised the operation, then partly retracted. Convictions rested on admissions the opposition says were obtained under drugging and torture; Requesens got 8 years, 17 others 30/24/20/16/5 years. Fernando Albán, later arrested over the plot, died in SEBIN custody; the official suicide finding is disputed.

## 2. Baghdad, 7 November 2021 — al-Kadhimi

Two days after pro-Iran crowds clashed with security forces in the Green Zone, an explosive-laden quadcopter struck the PM's residence; the Iraqi Armed Forces said two more drones were shot down. The garage and parts of the house were damaged and six guards injured; Kadhimi was unhurt. **No group claimed responsibility.** Reuters, citing regional officials and militia sources, attributed it to Shi'ite militias with Iranian-made weapons, while US officials doubted direct Iranian ordering. Kata'ib Hezbollah and Asa'ib Ahl al-Haq called it an "inside job", and the Washington Institute documented militia disinformation including a **forged** aircraft image. Evidence is official-statement-plus-sourcing, not court-grade.

## 3. September 2025 — María Corina Machado and Latin America

**No drone element was found.** Checks of English and Spanish Wikipedia and news indexes for a September 2025 attempt on Machado returned nothing credible; that month's record shows US military pressure and Machado in hiding. Venezuela's 6 October 2025 claim of a "false flag" plot against the US embassy in Caracas is an uncorroborated state claim, not a drone case. Comparators: **Haiti, 7 July 2021** (the Miami Herald reported grenades were also dropped from drones during President Moïse's assassination; firearms were the main means) and **Colombia 2025** (Senator Uribe Turbay — firearms only).

## 4. NATO and allied countries

**Belgium, 9 October 2025** is the clearest NATO-soil case: three arrested in Antwerp over an alleged jihadist-inspired plan to attack PM Bart De Wever and other politicians **by drone**, interdicted pre-attack. **United States, 15 June 2026**: a DOJ affidavit alleged a cell planned to attack the White House UFC event with snipers **and bomb-equipped drones**, targeting Trump, Vance and Netanyahu. **Poland, April 2024**: an alleged Russian sleeper agent ("Paweł K.") was tasked with intelligence on Rzeszów–Jasionka airport and, per the SBU, with killing President Zelenskyy using **an FPV drone or a sniper rifle**; indicted May 2025. Also noted: Turkey (Iran-linked "drone plans") and Lithuania (2026 GRU-linked plots).

**Gaps:** no credible drone-borne attempt on a politician was found in Germany, France, the Netherlands, Spain or Italy; reported Iranian and Russian plots there used firearms, arson, acid or criminal proxies.

## 5. Butler, Pennsylvania — 13 July 2024

**No drone was used in the attack.** Crooks fired eight rifle rounds from a roof. The drone element is reconnaissance only: at about **3:50 p.m.**, roughly two hours before the shooting, he flew a drone for about **11 minutes**, capturing the terrain behind his eventual firing position (BBC, 27 July 2024; NYT timeline, 28 July 2024; consistent with the FBI's recovered drone). No weaponised or autonomous drone was involved.

## 6. Africa, Asia and the Middle East outside declared inter-state war

Beyond Kadhimi: **Sudan, 30 July 2024** — two drones struck the Jubayt ceremony attended by Gen. al-Burhan, killing five; the SAF blamed the RSF, which blamed Islamist elements, leaving attribution unresolved. **Israel, September 2025** — Shin Bet said it foiled a Hamas plot to kill Minister Ben-Gvir with explosive drones. **Jordan, 15 April 2025** — 16 arrested over rocket and drone plots. **Cambodia, February 2025** — Hun Sen claimed a drone plot was foiled; self-reported and single-sourced, treat as unverified. No credible documented drone attempt on a leader was found for **India, Pakistan, Somalia or Ethiopia**.

## Assessment

Every case reviewed involved **remotely piloted, short-range quadcopters or adapted consumer platforms** (DJI M600, generic quadcopters, FPV types). **No source alleged autonomous target selection, loitering-munition behaviour or AI-enabled engagement.** Claims of success or foiling rest disproportionately on governments or security services that controlled the scene and, at Caracas, restricted independent access — the category's central evidentiary weakness.
