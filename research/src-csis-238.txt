Title: Shahed-238

URL Source: https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/

Markdown Content:
**The Shahed-238** is a jet-propelled one-way attack unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) designed and built domestically by Iran. First officially unveiled in 2023, Iran has used Shahed-238 UAVs against targets in Israel and Gulf states, and Russia has widely employed the Shahed-238 in its offensive campaign against Ukraine.

## Shahed-238 at a Glance

Originated from Iran Possessed by Iran, Russia Alternate name Geran-3 Class UAV Length 3.5 m Wingspan 3 m Payload 50 – 90 kg Status Operational Weight 250 – 370 kg Speed 300 – 600 km per hour

## Shahed-238 Development

Shahed Aviation Industries developed the Shahed-238 as a jet-powered variation of the Shahed-136, continuing Iranian UAV development efforts since the end of the Iran-Iraq War. Iranian state media has claimed that jet propulsion on other one-way-attack UAVs could enable higher speeds and agility. Iranian Ashura Aerospace University of Science & Technology first unveiled the Shahed-238 during a visit by former Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei to show recent achievements in the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Aerospace Forces.[1](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-1-11042 " Vadim Kushnikov, “Iran Officially Unveils Shahed-238,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, November 20, 2023, <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/en/news/iran-officially-unveils-shahed-238/\">https://militarnyi.com/en/news/iran-officially-unveils-shahed-238/.</a> ")

There are several varieties of the Shahed-238 UAV, and the range, speed, payload, altitude, and other specifications are interdependent and likely to vary based on modifications. Although sources vary, Shahed-238 UAVs are more expensive than their Shahed-131 or Shahed 136 counterparts due to the use of jet engines. Even though this does not necessarily reflect the price of production, some reporters have indicated that Russia is interested in purchasing Shahed-238 UAVs at $900,000 – $1.4 million per unit, depending on the variant.[2](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-2-11042 " Oded Yaron, “Gold for Drones: Massive Leak Reveals the Iranian Shahed Project in Russia,” <em>Haaretz</em>, February 21, 2024, <a href=\"https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/security-aviation/2024-02-21/ty-article-magazine/gold-for-drones-massive-leak-reveals-the-iranian-shahed-project-in-russia/0000018d-bb85-dd5e-a59d-ffb729890000\">https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/security-aviation/2024-02-21/ty-article-magazine/gold-for-drones-massive-leak-reveals-the-iranian-shahed-project-in-russia/0000018d-bb85-dd5e-a59d-ffb729890000.</a> ")

## Specifications

Similar to previous Shahed variants, the Shahed-238 relies on several reverse-engineered or commercially available foreign components. The Shahed-238 reportedly relies on TJ150 jet engines manufactured by the Czech company PBS Velká Bíteš and has a speed of approximately 600 km per hour.[3](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-3-11042 " Olivia Sapwood, “Czech engine and Western electronics: components of the Shahed-238 drone revealed,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, February 8, 2024. <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/en/news/czech-engine-and-western-electronics-components-of-the-shahed-238-drone-revealed/\">https://militarnyi.com/en/news/czech-engine-and-western-electronics-components-of-the-shahed-238-drone-revealed/</a> ") The Russian variation, known as the Geran-3, is powered by a Chinese Telefly JT80 engine and has a speed of approximately 300 to 370 km per hour.[4](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-4-11042 " David Hambling, “Russia’s New Jet-Powered Shahed Revealed: What It Means For Ukraine,” <em>Forbes</em>, September 18, 2025, <a href=\"https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidhambling/2025/09/18/russias-new-jet-powered-shahed-revealed-what-it-means-for-ukraine/\">https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidhambling/2025/09/18/russias-new-jet-powered-shahed-revealed-what-it-means-for-ukraine/.</a> ") Other reports note that the Geran-3 may also use the TJ150 engine or its Iranian copy, the Tulou-10 turbojet engine.[5](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-5-11042 " Dmytro Shumlyanskyi, “Реактивний «Шахед»: не дешева ерзац-крилата ракета та нова загроза для України,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, July 21, 2025, <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/\">https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/</a>; “Shahed 238 UAV Engine Engine №10089982,” <em>War and Sanctions</em>, January 6, 2024, <a href=\"https://war-sanctions.gur.gov.ua/en/components/4438\">https://war-sanctions.gur.gov.ua/en/components/4438.</a> ") This engine may provide the Geran-3 a flight speed of 550 to 600 km per hour and a range of 2,500 km.[6](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-6-11042 " Dmytro Shumlyanskyi, “Реактивний «Шахед»: не дешева ерзац-крилата ракета та нова загроза для України,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, July 21, 2025, <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/\">https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/.</a> ") However, the reported range of the Shahed-238 varies; some sources approximate the range to be 1,000-2,000 km and a maximum altitude of 9,000 meters.[7](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-7-11042 " Dmytro Shumlyanskyi, “Реактивний «Шахед»: не дешева ерзац-крилата ракета та нова загроза для України,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, July 21, 2025, <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/\">https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/</a>; Howard Altman, “Russia’s Jet Powered Shahed Kamikaze Drone Is A Big Problem For Ukraine,” <em>The War Zone</em>, July 31, 2025, <a href=\"https://www.twz.com/news-features/russias-jet-powered-shahed-kamikaze-drone-is-a-big-problem-for-ukraine\">https://www.twz.com/news-features/russias-jet-powered-shahed-kamikaze-drone-is-a-big-problem-for-ukraine.</a> ")

The Shahed-238 has a length of approximately 3.5 meters, a wingspan of 3 meters, and a maximum take-off weight of between 250 and 370 kg.[8](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-8-11042 " Dmytro Shumlyanskyi, “Реактивний «Шахед»: не дешева ерзац-крилата ракета та нова загроза для України,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, July 21, 2025, <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/\">https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/.</a> ") The Shahed-238 can carry a payload of 50 kg, though some Russian variants can carry a payload of 90 kg.[9](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-9-11042 " Dmytro Shumlyanskyi, “Реактивний «Шахед»: не дешева ерзац-крилата ракета та нова загроза для України,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, July 21, 2025, <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/\">https://militarnyi.com/uk/articles/reaktyvnyj-shahed-ne-desheva-erzats-krylata-raketa-ta-nova-zagroza-dlya-ukrayiny/</a>; Howard Altman, “Russia’s Jet Powered Shahed Kamikaze Drone Is A Big Problem For Ukraine,” <em>The War Zone</em>, July 31, 2025, <a href=\"https://www.twz.com/news-features/russias-jet-powered-shahed-kamikaze-drone-is-a-big-problem-for-ukraine\">https://www.twz.com/news-features/russias-jet-powered-shahed-kamikaze-drone-is-a-big-problem-for-ukraine.</a> ") The Shahed-238 is also reportedly capable of intercepting air targets, including the MQ-9 Reaper drone.[10](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-10-11042 " “Leaked Documents Reveal Iranian New Shahed-238 Power Jet Drone Can Intercept US MQ-9 Reaper,” <em>Army Recognition Group, </em>August 27, 2025, <a href=\"https://www.armyrecognition.com/archives/archives-land-defense/land-defense-2024/leaked-documents-reveal-iranian-new-shahed-238-power-jet-drone-can-intercept-us-mq-9-reaper\">https://www.armyrecognition.com/archives/archives-land-defense/land-defense-2024/leaked-documents-reveal-iranian-new-shahed-238-power-jet-drone-can-intercept-us-mq-9-reaper.</a> ") Some Shahed-238 variations also maintain a black paint scheme to increase the difficulty of detection at night.[11](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-11-11042 " David Hambling, “Russia’s New Jet-Powered Shahed Revealed: What It Means For Ukraine,” <em>Forbes</em>, September 18, 2025, <a href=\"https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidhambling/2025/09/18/russias-new-jet-powered-shahed-revealed-what-it-means-for-ukraine/\">https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidhambling/2025/09/18/russias-new-jet-powered-shahed-revealed-what-it-means-for-ukraine/.</a> ")

Variants of the Shahed-238 rely on different guidance systems. Reportedly, some versions rely on inertial navigation systems and global positioning systems, while others use infrared, optical, or radar guidance systems to strike heat-contrast targets and military equipment in the rear.[12](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-12-11042 " Vadim Kushnikov, “Iran Officially Unveils Shahed-238,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, November 20, 2023, <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/en/news/iran-officially-unveils-shahed-238/\">https://militarnyi.com/en/news/iran-officially-unveils-shahed-238/.</a> ") Some analysts assess that the radar-homing variants may be used as anti-radiation missile targeting radars to support the suppression of enemy air defense missions.

The Shahed-238 is capable of being launched from an accelerated car, a truck-mounted rail, or a stationary platform using a solid fuel booster.[13](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-13-11042 " Vadim Kushnikov, “Iran Officially Unveils Shahed-238,” Militarnyi, November 20, 2023, https://militarnyi.com/en/news/iran-officially-unveils-shahed-238/; “Russia’s jet-powered Shahed-238 drones introduce new challenges to Ukraine’s air defenses,” <em>Army Recognition Group</em>, August 2, 2025, <a href=\"https://www.armyrecognition.com/focus-analysis-conflicts/army/conflicts-in-the-world/russia-ukraine-war-2022/russias-jet-powered-shahed-238-drones-introduce-new-challenges-to-ukraines-air-defenses\">https://www.armyrecognition.com/focus-analysis-conflicts/army/conflicts-in-the-world/russia-ukraine-war-2022/russias-jet-powered-shahed-238-drones-introduce-new-challenges-to-ukraines-air-defenses.</a> ")

Kyiv.[17](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-17-11042 " Howard Altman, “Russia’s Jet Powered Shahed Kamikaze Drone Is A Big Problem For Ukraine,” <em>The In September 2025, a Shahed-238 was found in Iraq’s Basra province, which, according to Iraqi sources, was conducting a reconnaissance flight to monitor activity along the Iran-Iraq border.[18](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-18-11042 " Roman Pryhodko, “Iranian Shahed-238 Drone Detected in Iraq Border Region,” <em>Militarnyi</em>, September 29, 2025, <a href=\"https://militarnyi.com/en/news/iranian-shahed-238-drone-detected-in-iraq-border-region/\">https://militarnyi.com/en/news/iranian-shahed-238-drone-detected-in-iraq-border-region/.</a> ") During the first weeks of Operation True Promise IV, Iran employed thousands of one-way-attack UAVs and Shahed-238 UAVs in its retaliatory campaign to shorten warning times and complicate air defense engagement cycles.[19](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-19-11042 " Kateryna Bondar, “Unpacking Iran’s Drone Campaign in the Gulf: Early Lessons for Future Drone Warfare,” <em>Center for Strategic and International Studies</em>, March 10, 2026, <a href=\"https://www.csis.org/analysis/unpacking-irans-drone-campaign-gulf-early-lessons-future-drone-warfare\">https://www.csis.org/analysis/unpacking-irans-drone-campaign-gulf-early-lessons-future-drone-warfare.</a> ") On February 28, 2026, a Shahed-238 struck a forecourt area in front of a hotel in Dubai.[20](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/shahed-238/#easy-footnote-bottom-20-11042 " “Iran: Unlawful Strikes Across Gulf Endanger Civilians,” <em>Human Rights Watch,</em> March 17, 2026, <a href=\"https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/03/17/iran-unlawful-strikes-across-gulf-endanger-civilians\">https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/03/17/iran-unlawful-strikes-across-gulf-endanger-civilians.</a> ")
