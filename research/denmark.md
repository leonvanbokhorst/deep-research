# Denmark — PET assessments, drone incidents and counter-UAS (PRIMARY-source evidence)

## PET terror assessments
**2026 edition** — *Vurdering af terrortruslen mod Kongeriget Danmark 2026* (PDF created 28 May 2026, 25 pp, ISSN-2795-1332). **I retrieved it (HTTP 200, 24 MB)**; the reported 404 was not reproducible here. Overall threat stays **"alvorlig"** (serious); militant Islamist "alvorlig", right-wing "generel", left-wing "begrænset", anti-authority "minimal". Iran is singled out ("brød ud den 28. februar 2026").

§7.3 "Droner" repeats earlier barrier language but **adds** the closest Danish official text linking drone overflights to terrorism:
> "Den forstyrrende effekt, som droner kan have ifm. kritisk infrastruktur, kan potentielt bringes til anvendelse i sammenhæng med handlinger, der efter de konkrete omstændigheder kan udgøre en terrorhandling i straffelovens forstand."

An **OFFICIAL WARNING ABOUT THE FUTURE**, immediately self-qualified: such flights "vil kræve en dronepilot med teknisk viden og en grad af planlægning" (require technical knowledge and planning).

**Countervailing official view (2025 & 2024, §6.3/7.3):** "Det kræver økonomiske midler, teknisk viden og adgang til eksplosiver at våbengøre en drone ifm. terrorangreb i Vesten," and "CTA har ikke kendskab til, at der har været gennemført terrorangreb med anvendelse af våbengjorte droner i Vesten." **No weaponised-drone terror attack in the West**; barriers remain but fall *gradually* ("gradvist lettere og billigere").

**AI:** 2026 frames it as a reach/radicalisation multiplier — "Bots og kunstig intelligens øger ekstremisters rækkevidde i onlinemiljøer." 2024 was more cautious: "for tidligt at vurdere de mere specifikke effekter." **No PET text says AI lowers barriers to attack, sabotage or assassination.**

**Espionage:** the index lists only 2023 and 2022 editions — no 2024–2026 assessment found (**NOT VERIFIED**). The live page says the threat "udgår primært fra Rusland, Kina og Iran."

## Russia sabotage
PET's sabotage page: "Truslen fra russisk sabotage mod Danmark er høj. PET har set konkret russisk planlægning og forberedelse af sabotage rettet mod Danmark," adding that Russia has "planer for sabotage af kritisk infrastruktur i Danmark, hvis den sikkerhedspolitiske situation forværres." The 5 Sep 2026 item carries an express caveat: "Det betyder ikke, at PET har oplysninger om et konkret forestående angreb på et bestemt sted eller tidspunkt." **Flag: HØJ rests on planning/reconnaissance evidence, not an imminent plot.**

## September 2025 airport shutdowns
Official numbers: Rigspolitiet 26 Sep 2025 — "mere end 500 anmeldelser … på tværs af alle politikredse"; Københavns Politi 24 Sep 2025 — "mere end 100 anmeldelser om droner og dronelignende genstande" in 24h. NOST escalated to operationsberedskab (25 Sep), but framed intent narrowly: "det er myndighedernes fortsatte vurdering, at droneflyvningerne primært har til hensigt at skabe utryghed og forstyrre vores infrastruktur," and Rigspolitiet: "Politiet vurderer ikke, at der på noget tidspunkt i forløbet har været risiko for civilbefolkningen."

**NOT VERIFIED:** a reported 25 June 2026 Københavns Politi press conference (Inspector Søren Thomassen) said to have concluded the force could neither prove nor disprove drones over Kastrup, with "we were not clear enough with our reservations" about the "capable actor" claim. Found only via Wikipedia citing media (SECONDARY). If accurate it weakens the Russia-attribution narrative — **verify directly.** Conclusive Russia attribution was **not found**.

## Counter-UAS policy
**No Danish counter-drone legislation, procurement or national security strategy provision was verified (NOT VERIFIED).** Verified activity is administrative: police temporary drone restriction zones (e.g. Copenhagen/Amager 16–20 Sep 2026), NOST coordination, and SAMSIK guidance urging citizens to film and report sightings — resilience advice, not capability.
