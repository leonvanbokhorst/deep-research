# Hybrid and covert state activity inside NATO: drones, sabotage, parcel bombs and undersea infrastructure

**Evidence brief — compiled 25 September 2026.** Threat research for defence understanding; no attack, sabotage or evasion methods described. Grades: [C] court record · [O] official statement · [I] intelligence assessment · [J] journalism · [X] contested.

---

## 1. Poland, 9–10 September 2025

19–23 unarmed drones entered eastern, central and northern Poland from the Belarus direction between 23:30 CEST on 9 September and 06:45 on 10 September. Polish F-16s, Dutch F-35s, Italian AEW and a Belgian tanker responded; up to four drones were destroyed, mostly by Dutch F-35s — the first time a NATO state downed Russian assets in its airspace since 2022. A Gerbera was still being recovered on 12 March 2026 near Galczyce, ~170 km west of Warsaw. [O][J]

Warsaw, Modlin, Rzeszów and Lublin airports closed; Poland invoked NATO **Article 4**; the UN Security Council held an emergency briefing on 12 September. NATO SG Rutte called it the largest concentration of airspace violations yet seen but did not call it an armed attack; President Trump suggested it "might be a mistake" [X]. Lublin prosecutors found the drones were unpowered decoys with no explosives. NATO launched **Operation Eastern Sentry** on 12 September 2025.

## 2. Denmark and Norway, September 2025 — and the 2026 reversal

On **22 September 2025** Copenhagen Airport closed for nearly four hours after two to three "large drones"; Oslo Gardermoen closed briefly the same night. PM Frederiksen called it "the most serious attack on Danish critical infrastructure to date"; police described a "capable actor", possibly launching from a ship. Within a week drones were reported at Aalborg, Billund, Esbjerg, **Skrydstrup** (F-16/F-35 base), Karup and Kastrup airbase. Norway reported objects at **Ørland** (27 Sept) and Brønnøysund (28 Sept). [O][J]

**2026 reversal — key opposite evidence:** on **25 June 2026 Copenhagen Police concluded it could neither prove nor disprove** drones over CPH that night, conceding officers had not been "clear enough with our reservations" about the "capable actor" line — though the Armed Forces separately concluded drones *had* overflown Danish military installations that month. **Norway's PST** found no established foreign-state link in February 2026, its director noting most cases were stars, weather, aircraft or helicopters. [O][I]

## 3. Baltic states and Finland, 2026

Most 2026 incursions were **Ukrainian strike drones driven off course by Russian electronic warfare**, not Russian attack drones. Ukrainian drones crashed in Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia on 23–25 March (one hit the Auvere power-station chimney); an AN-196 with a live warhead came down near Kouvola, Finland, on 29–30 March. On 7 May a drone exploded at an oil depot in Rēzekne, Latvia, prompting Defence Minister Sprūds to resign and the Siliņa cabinet to fall. Baltic Air Policing aircraft downed stray drones over Estonia (19 May, Romanian F-16) and Latvia (June and August, French and Italian jets). On 2–4 September a **Russian-made Geoscan 701** was found near Porvoo. [O]

The three Baltic foreign ministers jointly rejected Russian claims that their airspace was used to strike Russia (10 April 2026). [O]

## 4. The state of play today, 25 September 2026
- **15 September:** an **Italian Eurofighter** on Baltic Air Policing from Šiauliai shot down a drone over Pratkunai near Kaunas after it entered from Belarus. President Nausėda said it was a **Gerbera-2 targeting Ukraine**; Italy's defence minister said it "most probably" came from Russia. [O][X]
- **14–15 September:** Denmark said a **Russian warship fired two flares at a Danish helicopter** and summoned the Russian ambassador; Russia accused the helicopter of dangerous manoeuvring, and Denmark accelerated a $275m aid package. [O]
- **24 September:** Poland declared a fire at a **Starlink ground station at Wola Krobowska** serving Ukrainian military communications an act of **sabotage**; Denmark's DDIS warned Russia could attack NATO territory. [O][X]

## 5. Russian intelligence-linked sabotage, 2024–2026

- **July 2024 DHL parcel-bomb plot:** incendiaries concealed in massagers and cosmetics, posted from Vilnius, ignited at the **Leipzig/Halle DHL hub** and at Minworth, Birmingham. Eurojust announced on **6 March 2026** that a joint investigation team had disrupted the network; Reuters reported **22 suspects and GRU responsibility**. [O][J]
- **Verdicts:** Stuttgart **convicted a Ukrainian man on 18 August 2026**; Lithuania charged 15 in September 2025 (trial of five opened 17 April 2026, no verdict). In Munich, **Dieter S. received six years on 30 October 2025** for scouting US bases in Bavaria. [C][J]
- **Poland:** a C-4 IED struck the Warsaw–Lublin line on 16 November 2025; two Ukrainian nationals are accused and fled to Belarus. A Russian couple were jailed on **9 July 2026**, and a Russian opposition activist and his wife on **7 September 2026**. Recruitment runs through **encrypted/Telegram channels paying proxies in cryptocurrency**, directed from Russia via cut-outs. [C][O][I]

## 6. Undersea infrastructure — no drones were involved

**In every attributed Baltic cable or pipeline incident the damage mechanism was a ship's anchor dragged across the seabed. No authority has claimed a drone cut a Baltic cable.**

| Date | Asset | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| 7–8 Oct 2023 | Balticconnector + EE-S1 | *NewNew Polar Bear* anchor recovered 24 Oct 2023; mechanical, not explosive; China called it an accident, Finland's NBI investigated "intentional damaging"; HK captain charged, trial expected 2027 |
| 17–18 Nov 2024 | BCS East-West Interlink, C-Lion1 | *Yi Peng 3*; **no one charged**; US intelligence assessed the cuts as not deliberate [X] |
| 25 Dec 2024 | Estlink 2 + 4 telecom cables | *Eagle S*; alleged gross sabotage; Helsinki dismissed for lack of jurisdiction, but the **Court of Appeals ruled on 27 Aug 2026 that Finland has jurisdiction**; retrial began September 2026 |
| 31 Dec 2025 | Elisa Finland–Estonia cable | *Fitburg*; anchor drag confirmed (March 2026); charges June 2026, trial began September 2026 |
| 26 Jan 2025 | Latvia–Gotland cable | *Vezhen* — Sweden ruled an **accident** [O] |

NATO responded with **Baltic Sentry** (14 January 2025), plus the JEF's **Nordic Warden** and **Arctic Sentry** (February 2026). The drone-at-sea story is separate: the **IISS report of 2 July 2026** alleged 144 incidents and "likely" shadow-fleet launch platforms, contested by Dronewatch as resting on "debunked sightings" [I][X].

## 7. Drone reconnaissance over bases and critical infrastructure

- **Germany:** up to ten drones over **Manching** and Neuburg in Dec 2024–Jan 2025; Munich prosecutors opened an espionage probe and recovered nothing, with sightings also around Ramstein, Brunsbüttel and Rheinmetall sites. Munich Airport closed on 2–3 October 2025 over **"unconfirmed drone sightings"**. On **4–5 August 2026 an explosives-carrying FPV drone struck an An-124's wing at Leipzig/Halle without detonating**; **Germany formally blamed Russia on 1 September 2026**. [O][J]
- **Belgium and the Netherlands:** drones over Kleine Brogel, Brussels, Liège, Antwerp and the Doel nuclear plant (2–9 Nov 2025), and the Dutch military **fired on drones over Volkel Air Base** on 22 November 2025 without recovering wreckage. **Both contested:** VRT's *Pano* (April 2026) found 558 reports yielded no physical evidence and the minister's "drone" footage was a police helicopter; NOS found no hard evidence, and the MIVD/AIVD 2025 report does not attribute the overflights. [O][J][X]
- **United Kingdom:** drones over RAF Lakenheath, Mildenhall and Fairford, 20–26 November 2024; **no operator identified; the MoD Police inquiry closed in February 2026 with no suspects.** [J][X]

## 8. Opposite and contested evidence

1. **Copenhagen:** police could neither prove nor disprove drone presence (June 2026); the "capable actor" framing was walked back. **Norway's PST** found no foreign-state link (February 2026).
2. **Belgium and the Netherlands:** national investigations found no physical evidence; a ministerial "drone" was a helicopter.
3. **Undersea:** *Vezhen* ruled accidental; US intelligence assessed the 2024 cuts as accidents; **no drone involvement**.
4. **2026 Baltic incursions:** predominantly **Ukrainian** strays; Russia's alleged role is jamming, not launch.
5. **IISS shadow-fleet drone report** contested; a German search of the vessel *Hav Dolphin* found no launch link. The FAA confirmed the 2024 US north-east drone wave was its own research aircraft; academics attribute much of the broader panic to misidentification.

## 9. Assessment

Court-tested attribution exists only for the **sabotage and parcel-bomb networks** (Germany, Poland, Lithuania, UK, Czechia) and for **anchor-drag cable damage**. Official state-level attribution to Russia exists for the Leipzig drone attack, Polish railway sabotage, the Starlink fire and Finnish vessel seizures; Sweden's Øresund case names the launching platform (February 2026). The **September 2025 Copenhagen/Oslo wave, the UK November 2024 sightings and the Belgian and Dutch cases remain unattributed or actively contested**, and the 2026 Baltic incursions were largely Ukrainian strays. No consolidated NATO or Europol attribution report had been published by 25 September 2026.
