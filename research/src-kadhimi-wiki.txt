On 7 November 2021, the [Iraqi Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Armed_Forces "Iraqi Armed Forces") reported that [Prime Minister of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Iraq "Prime Minister of Iraq")[Mustafa Al-Kadhimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi "Mustafa Al-Kadhimi") survived an [assassination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination "Assassination") attempt that morning. An [explosive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosive "Explosive")-laden [drone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_combat_aerial_vehicle "Unmanned combat aerial vehicle") attacked his [Baghdad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad "Baghdad") residence while two were shot down. Several members of his security personnel were injured.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:12-3) No one has claimed responsibility, although it was generally believed that it was perpetrated by pro-Iran militias.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:14-4)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:15-5)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:16-6)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:17-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:18-8)[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-9)[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:11-10)[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-11)[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:5-12)

On 8 November, Kadhimi's office released a statement that the perpetrators were the same people who assassinated Nibras Farman, an agent for the [Iraqi National Intelligence Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_National_Intelligence_Service "Iraqi National Intelligence Service").[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:19-13)

Since the [American invasion of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003_invasion_of_Iraq "2003 invasion of Iraq"), Iraq has been embroiled in a [sectarian conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sectarian_violence_in_Iraq "Sectarian violence in Iraq") between [Sunni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunni_Islam "Sunni Islam") militants and [Shi'a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam "Shia Islam") militias, deepening tensions between the two communities. The conflict flared up after [IRGC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Revolutionary_Guard_Corps "Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps") commander [Qasem Soleimani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qasem_Soleimani "Qasem Soleimani")[was assassinated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Qasem_Soleimani "Assassination of Qasem Soleimani") by the US army, leading to numerous attacks on "US troops, [Iraqi armed forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_armed_forces "Iraqi armed forces") and US-aligned forces",[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:8-14)[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-15) including [a missile strike on Erbil International Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Erbil_rocket_attacks "2021 Erbil rocket attacks").[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:8-14)

On October 10, 2021, parliamentary elections were held which [resulted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Iraqi_parliamentary_election "2021 Iraqi parliamentary election") in the [Sadrist Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadrist_Movement "Sadrist Movement") winning most of the seats and certain pro-Iran parties losing seats. The latter parties alleged that there was [electoral fraud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_fraud "Electoral fraud"),[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:9-16) leading to a [constitutional crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_crisis "Constitutional crisis"). Just two days before the attack occurred, supporters of pro-Iranian groups [tried to storm the Green Zone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Baghdad_clashes "2021 Baghdad clashes"), [throwing stones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone_throwing "Stone throwing") at the local security forces, who responded by firing tear gas at them and shooting in the air to disperse the crowd (resulting in the death of one to two demonstrators),[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:10-17)[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-18) heating up tensions between Iraq's political movements.[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-19)[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-20)

According to Matthew Zais of the [Atlantic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Council "Atlantic Council"), Iraqi warlord and the head of Asaib Al-Haq, [Qais Khazali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qais_Khazali "Qais Khazali"), had "described previous attacks against Americans and similarly warned the Iraqi Prime Minister" the day before the drones struck.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-21)

On 7 November 2021, according to reports by the [Iraqi Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Armed_Forces "Iraqi Armed Forces"), an [explosive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosive "Explosive")-laden [drone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_combat_aerial_vehicle "Unmanned combat aerial vehicle") attacked the then [Iraqi Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Iraq "Prime Minister of Iraq"), [Mustafa Al-Kadhimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi "Mustafa Al-Kadhimi")'s, [Baghdad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad "Baghdad") residence while two were shot down. Several members of his security personnel were injured.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:12-3) No one has claimed responsibility, although it was generally believed that it was perpetrated by pro-Iran militias.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:14-4)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:15-5)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:16-6)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:17-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:18-8)

On 8 November, a pair of anonymous regional officials and some (also anonymous) militia sources told [Reuters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reuters "Reuters") that the attack was the fault of [Shi'te militias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_militias "Iraqi militias") such as [Kata'ib Hezbollah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kata'ib_Hezbollah "Kata'ib Hezbollah") or [Asaib Ahl al-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asa'ib_Ahl_al-Haq "Asa'ib Ahl al-Haq"), also alleging that the weapons used by the perpetrators were made in [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran "Iran").[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:1-22)[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-23)

On 8 November, Kadhimi's office released a statement that the perpetrators were the same people who assassinated Nibras Farman, an agent for the [Iraqi National Intelligence Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_National_Intelligence_Service "Iraqi National Intelligence Service").[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:19-13)

### Damage inflicted by the attack

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Damage inflicted by the attack")]

Many parts of Kadhimi's home, including his [garage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garage_(residential) "Garage (residential)"), were damaged by the blast, and at least one [explosion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explosion "Explosion") was documented.[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:7-24) Western diplomats reported hearing the noise of gunfire and explosions inside the Green Zone.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:10-17)[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-25)

Iraqi government agencies described the perpetrators as using "[quadcopter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadcopter "Quadcopter")" drones; the same type used by [Saraya Awliya al-Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saraya_Awliya_al-Dam "Saraya Awliya al-Dam") in the [2021 Erbil rocket attacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Erbil_rocket_attacks "2021 Erbil rocket attacks"). Drones are usually used in the region by pro-Iran militias, since they are hard to be attributed to and allow larger militias and Iran to deny responsibility.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-26)

[Hamdi Malik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamdi_Malik?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hamdi Malik (page does not exist)"), who has studied the presence of Shi'te militias in Iraq, described it as a "clear message of 'We can create chaos in Iraq, - we have the guns, we have the means'".[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:1-22)

[Tallha Abdulrazaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallha_Abdulrazaq?action=edit&redlink=1 "Tallha Abdulrazaq (page does not exist)"), writing for [TRT World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TRT_World "TRT World"), wrote that "the attack can be characterized as a failed coup attempt, and as a pointed and explosive message to Kadhimi and anyone else deemed to be an impediment to the Shia militia's gangster rule in Iraq," arguing that it was a warning sign to scare off Iraq's anti-Iran movement.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:5-12)

Two former and one current United States Defense officials expressed doubt that the assassination attempt was directly the fault of Iran, but did agree the attack was most likely committed by Shi'ite militias.[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-27)

Two writers for the [Jean Jaures Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fondation_Jean-Jaur%C3%A8s "Fondation Jean-Jaurès") expressed worries that the strike could "plunge back" Iraq "into chaos in a highly flammable regional and internal context".[[28]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-28)

![Image 2](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f6/Flag_of_Iraq.svg/40px-Flag_of_Iraq.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)[Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq "Iraq"): [The President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Iraq "President of Iraq") of the [Republic of Iraq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraq "Iraq"), [Barham Salih](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barham_Salih "Barham Salih") tweeted that "We [Iraqis] cannot accept that Iraq will be dragged into chaos and a coup against its constitutional system."[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:10-17)

Many [Shi'a](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam "Shia Islam") clerics and politicians, including [Muqtada al-Sadr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muqtada_al-Sadr "Muqtada al-Sadr"), denounced what they termed to be a "terrorist attack". Sadr, who had himself gained significant progress during the October elections,[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:9-16) called for the [Popular Mobilization Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Mobilization_Forces "Popular Mobilization Forces") to "disband," expressing condemnation of efforts by both Iran and the United States to involve themselves in Iraq's affairs. [Abu Ali al-Askari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Ali_al-Askari?action=edit&redlink=1 "Abu Ali al-Askari (page does not exist)"), a prominent figure in [Kata'ib Hezbollah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kata'ib_Hezbollah "Kata'ib Hezbollah"), charged Sadr with hypocrisy for failing to disarm his own group, [Saraya al-Salam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_Companies "Peace Companies").[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-29)[[30]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-30)

A few weeks later, Sadr announced he had just dissolved one of his own factions, the [Promised Day Brigade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promised_Day_Brigade "Promised Day Brigade").[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-31)

[Amar al-Hakim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammar_al-Hakim "Ammar al-Hakim") and [Haider al-Abadi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haider_al-Abadi "Haider al-Abadi") were among the Shi'ite leaders who condemned the attack.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:11-10)

Several prominent Shi'ite militia leaders, including Askari and [Qais Khazali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qais_Khazali "Qais Khazali"), dismissed the attack as a fraud, arguing that it was an [inside job](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_job "Inside job") by the Iraqi government.[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:4-32)[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-33)[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-34)[[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-35)

The spokesman for [Asaib al-Haq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asaib_al-Haq "Asaib al-Haq"), Mahmoud al-Rubaie, tweeted that it was "a fabricated explosion… with the aim of covering up [yesterday's crimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Baghdad_clashes "2021 Baghdad clashes") and preoccupying public opinion".[[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-36)

The Patriarch of the [Chaldean Catholics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaldean_Catholics "Chaldean Catholics") and head of the [Chaldean Catholic Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaldean_Catholic_Church "Chaldean Catholic Church"), [Louis Raphaël I Sako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Rapha%C3%ABl_I_Sako "Louis Raphaël I Sako") called for prayers for Iraq and the safety of Prime Minister Mustafa Al-Kadhimi, after the assassination attempt.[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-37)

[Masoud Barzani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masoud_Barzani "Masoud Barzani"), former president of [Iraqi Kurdistan region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iraqi_Kurdistan "Iraqi Kurdistan") expressed his solidarity with Kadhimi in a telephone call.[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:4-32)

Since the drone strike, various media channels and movements have encouraged [conspiracy theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conspiracy_theory "Conspiracy theory") related to the assassination attempt.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:11-10)[[69]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:13-69)

An Iraqi commentator accused U.S. forces of "purposefully deactivating the Green Zone's [anti-aircraft system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-aircraft "Anti-aircraft") so the attackers could get away with the strike."[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:11-10)

Other Shi'a militias released a dossier on [Telegram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telegram_(software) "Telegram (software)") arguing that it was a [false-flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_flag "False flag") strike and that an image of a drone used was actually a "French drone", despite the fact that the [munition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammunition "Ammunition") found at the attack had no traces to France whatsoever. The dossier also puts forth a [fake photo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forgery "Forgery") showing the traces of an American plane, the [MC-12W Liberty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beechcraft_C-12_Huron "Beechcraft C-12 Huron"), but scholars discovered that the image was forged.[[69]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attempted_assassination_of_Mustafa_Al-Kadhimi#cite_note-:13-69)
