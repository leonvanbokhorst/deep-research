# Colombia 2024–2026: Drones vs. Oil/Energy Infrastructure & Anti-Drone Policy and Law

Compiled 25 September 2026. `web_search` was non-functional this session; findings rest on Google News RSS, Bing News RSS and direct outlet retrieval. All URLs retrieved unless flagged "not resolved".

**Framing:** Colombia's pipeline statistics count **conventional explosive charges (voladuras)**, not drones. Drone attacks on **oil** infrastructure are rare; both July 2026 Arauca cases failed to detonate. Colombia's drone problem is overwhelmingly aimed at the **Fuerza Pública and civilians** (Cauca, Catatumbo/Norte de Santander, Nariño, Chocó, Antioquia, Huila). Do not conflate the datasets.

---

## A. Drone attacks on oil / pipeline infrastructure

### A1. Caño Limón and Banadía, Arauca — 22–23 July 2026
- **22 Jul 2026, Caño Limón–Coveñas field.** A homemade "dron artesanal" with explosives entered the field and crashed short of target in bad weather; charge did **not** activate. Intended target: a ~100,000 bbl crude tank. ~200 workers normally present. EOD ("Grupo Marte", Octava División) responded. Semana: the device "no puede ser detectado por los sistemas de seguridad"; such drones "no tienen precisión, ya que su fabricación no es militar y son adaptados para que carguen explosivos." **Actor: ELN** (Semana's journalistic attribution; not officially confirmed). **Control: remotely piloted/improvised — no autonomy alleged.** Semana 23 Jul (https://www.semana.com/nacion/articulo/eln-intento-atacar-con-dron-explosivo-tanques-de-petroleo-en-arauca-habia-200-personas-en-la-zona/202619/); Vanguardia 23 Jul (https://www.vanguardia.com/colombia/2026/07/23/dron-con-explosivos-cayo-en-campo-petrolero-de-cano-limon-y-ejercito-evito-una-tragedia/); Caracol 23 Jul (https://caracol.com.co/2026/07/23/hallan-dron-cargado-con-explosivos-cerca-de-tanques-del-complejo-petrolero-de-cano-limon/). **Reputable journalism**, unnamed security sources.
- **23 Jul 2026 night, Banadía complex, Saravena — second drone in <24h.** Found inside the complex; it "alcanzó a impactar varias de las torres" but did not detonate. **Actor: unidentified**, no official attribution. El Tiempo 23 Jul (https://www.eltiempo.com/justicia/delitos/segundo-dron-con-explosivos-es-hallado-en-un-complejo-petrolero-de-arauca-en-menos-de-24-horas-3573463), which itself flags the 100,000-bbl target as *uncorroborated*.
- **Contested:** Caracol — authorities could not tell whether the Caño Limón drone *crashed during an attempt* or was *placed deliberately*. Vanguardia — no official attribution; the adjacent military unit, not oil infrastructure, may have been the target. Treat both as **failed attacks of unconfirmed attribution**.
- **Origin: 21 Jul 2023**, vereda Laureles, rural **Puerto Guzmán, Putumayo** — first-ever Colombian drone+explosives attack, by "Comandos de Frontera"; military target, not oil.

### A2. Putumayo / Oleoducto Transandino (OTA) / Amerisur–Geopark
**Negative finding: no drone attack on Putumayo oil infrastructure in 2024–2026.** Zero incidents at Orito, the OTA or Amerisur/Geopark. OTA coverage concerns the Corte Constitucional's Oct 2025 suspension over Awá impacts and a Jan 2025 statement it would not be dismantled.

### A3. Official drone counts and attribution (intelligence report, Sept 2026)
Military intelligence, updated **7 Sep 2026**, via El Tiempo 11 Sep 2026 (https://www.eltiempo.com/justicia/conflicto-y-narcotrafico/las-cifras-del-mas-reciente-informe-de-inteligencia-sobre-los-ataques-con-drones-en-colombia-asi-escalo-la-amenaza-3585299). **Official intelligence document reported by reputable journalism.**
- **690 drone actions since 21 Jul 2023.** 2023: 2; **2024: 119; 2025: 277; 2026: 292** (to 7 Sep — under nine months already exceeds all 2025; 2026 ≈ 42% of the series).
- Attribution 2025–26: **'Iván Mordisco' dissidents 382** (237 in 2025; 145 in 2026 — over half the total); **ELN 155** (36; **119**); **'Calarcá' faction 30** (4; 26); **Comandos de Frontera 4**.
- **487 people affected.** Military wounded 2024–26: **342** (31/166/145); police wounded **59** (36/7/16); civilians wounded **55** (28/15/12). Deaths: **22 military** (15/6/1), **3 police**, **6 civilians** (sub-register noted; others count >10).
- **Capability milestone: first "kamikaze" attack 24 Jan 2026**, El Estanquillo, Patía, Cauca, attributed to 'Carlos Patiño' — the airframe carries the explosive to the target rather than releasing it. Still one-way attack, **not autonomy**.
- Evolution: drones began as ISR, then were adapted to carry explosives — enabling attack "a distancia, sin que quien opera el aparato tenga que permanecer en el punto de impacto."

**Pipeline statistics (MinDefensa, conventional):**
- **Jan–Jun 2026: 8 pipeline bombings vs 21 in Jan–Jun 2025 → −62%.** H1 series: 2015:48, 2018:74, 2019:35, 2020:31, 2021:14, 2022:18, 2023:28, **2024:3**, 2025:21, 2026:8 (El Tiempo 23 Jul 2026).
- **Jan–Jun 2025: 21 vs 3 in Jan–Jun 2024 = +600%**, most attributed to the **ELN**; concentrated in Arauca/Santander; targeting Caño Limón–Coveñas (Sierracol concession; Cenit/Ecopetrol-owned), 30° API crude feeding Barrancabermeja (El Tiempo 20 Jul 2025, https://www.eltiempo.com/justicia/conflicto-y-narcotrafico/ataques-a-oleoductos-aumentaron-600-en-el-primer-semestre-de-2025-autoridades-atribuyen-mayoria-de-atentados-al-eln-3473708).
- **Full-2024: 43 vs 37 in 2023 (+16.2%). Jan–Aug 2025: 25 vs 13 (+92%). Arauca = 92% of 2025 cases; Saravena = 56.5%** within Arauca (Portafolio 5 Oct 2025, https://www.portafolio.co/energia/4-1-millones-de-barriles-de-petroleo-se-dejaron-de-producir-en-2024-a-causa-de-atentados-641397). Ministry definition = line rupture **by explosive device activation**.
- Industry (ACP/Campetrol) **claims**, attributed: 4.1 m bbl not produced 2024 (vs 3 m in 2023); >40 pipeline attacks and >180 illicit connections in 2024; Caño Limón–Coveñas idle ~10 months, OTA ~19; royalties −14.9% H1 2025.
- **The pipeline threat through mid-2026 is overwhelmingly conventional.** July 2026 Arauca is the only drone-specific oil-infrastructure case located — and it failed.

### A4. PAres and Indepaz
**PAres, 3 Jul 2026, "El uso de drones en el Catatumbo"** (NGO mapping from community reports and media): **11 drone actions Jan–Jun 2026**; 6 (54.5%) against military assets, 5 (45.5%) affecting civilians/property in confrontations; **24 victims — 4 soldiers killed, 13 wounded, 3 civilians killed, 4 wounded**; **Tibú (5) + El Tarra (4) = 81.8%**, plus Sardinata (1), Teorama (1); 4 events Jan–Apr vs **7 (63.6%) in May–June**; ELN signalled against the Fuerza Pública, civilian harm mainly from **ELN vs Frente 33 (EMBF)** clashes; 101,883 displaced in 17 months. PAres urges stronger **detection and jamming (inhibición)**. https://www.pares.com.co/el-uso-de-drones-en-el-catatumbo-una-nueva-amenaza-para-la-poblacion-civil/
**PAres, 29 Aug 2025** (Mindefensa data): 560 affectations Jan–Jul 2025 (Jan 53; Feb–Jul 507); Ejército 352, Policía 204, Armada 4; Cauca 126, Antioquia 67, Norte de Santander 64, Valle 50, Nariño 45, Bolívar 43. Frames IEDs/drones as a **low-exposure repertoire** serving control of corridors and rents (coca, mining, contraband, extortion). https://www.pares.com.co/explosivos-y-drones-el-repertorio-de-violencia-que-reconfigura-las-afectaciones-a-la-fuerza-publica/
**Indepaz:** no published Indepaz report or dataset on drones or pipeline attacks verified. Director **Leonardo González** is quoted (RCN, Sept 2026) calling drones "una nueva estrategia, de los últimos cinco o seis años… incluso, con enjambres de drones," in Cauca, Catatumbo and Sierra Nevada — a **spokesperson statement**.

### A5. Illegal mining / extortion
Evidence **thin**. Clearest: a drone attack attributed to FARC dissidents at a **mine in Segovia, Antioquia** (La FM 3 Aug 2026; not verified at source). ELN ran **15 drone attacks in Chocó in the first week of July 2026** (https://caracol.com.co/2026/07/06/eln-ha-lanzado-15-ataques-con-drones-cargados-con-explosivos-en-choco-en-la-primera-semana-de-julio/) — a gold-mining department, but attributed to a general offensive. Defensoría reporting links drones with displacement and 10% gold "vacunas" in nororiente antioqueño. **No source retrieved evidences drones used for extortion collection or surveillance — unproven.**

## B. Anti-drone policy: "Escudo Nacional Antidrones"

**Announced 9–10 Jan 2026.** MinDefensa-led; stated total **>COP 6.3 trillion ("billones")**; Petro ordered **~COP 1 trillion for phase one in 2026** (https://caracol.com.co/2026/01/10/con-inversion-superior-a-63-billones-mindefensa-liderara-el-proyecto-escudo-nacional-antidrones/). Vanguardia/El Colombiano say **"cerca de 6.2 billones"** — flag the discrepancy. The Army cited **">400 attacks since April 2024."** **Contracting model (official):** government-to-company, only with **manufacturers or legal representatives** — no intermediaries, embassy-supported; info session 16 Jan; FF.MM./Policía team plus the Transparency Group; trials in **real operational environments**. Public reporting **did not confirm site numbers, radar types or jam/intercept inventory** — treat capability counts as unconfirmed.

**Timeline:** 2 Feb arrival announced in **Barrancabermeja**; 14–16 Mar trials announced, starting **23 Mar**; 6 Apr contract due in May after presidential order (CAMBIO); **11 Apr only one company passed trials**; 17 Apr government turns abroad after **119 bidders failed**; 23 Apr Sánchez announces **international trials in Asia, Europe and North America** and asks Congress for a drone law; ~30 Jun El Colombiano calls it a "papa caliente" for the incoming government; **15 Jul Semana/Contraloría — the Army's Catatumbo anti-drone system "no sirve", contract COP 80,000 million, "riesgos críticos"**; **16 Jul still unfunded, MinDefensa awaiting COP 800,000 million for phase one** (https://www.eltiempo.com/justicia/conflicto-y-narcotrafico/escudo-nacional-antidrones-sigue-sin-recursos-mindefensa-espera-aprobacion-de-800-000-millones-para-iniciar-la-primera-fase-3571646); 21 Aug incoming government calls it **insufficient**, ex-minister denies; **29 Aug El Colombiano — "nunca despegó: 12 viajes y millones evaporados en 3 continentes"**; 2 Sep Axon expects reactivation; **8 Sep the Sánchez directive on anti-drone purchases annulled**, Army preparing a **COP 30,000 million** buy after the **Codaltec** deal failed (https://www.elcolombiano.com/colombia/pedro-sanchez-directiva-antidrones-compra-sistemas-codaltec-HK40795277).
**Assessment: COP 6.3 trn is a stated multi-year project value, not an executed budget.** Reported funded/contracted sums (COP 800,000 m sought; COP 80,000 m Catatumbo; COP 30,000 m new buy) are an order of magnitude smaller. By Sep 2026 the programme had failed to deliver and was under audit.

### B7. Counter-UAS procurement
- **Confirmed:** **Axon** (US) "ya tiene desplegados sistemas antidrones en bases del Ejército y la Fuerza Aérea" — country manager Fernando Vegas Cueto (Infobae and El Colombiano, 3 Sep 2026). The only named in-country C-UAS deployment verified.
- **8 Oct 2025:** MinDefensa **halted** an anti-drone buy for the **Casa de Nariño** after US-supplied equipment was withdrawn (Infobae).
- **Unverified single-source:** Infodefensa, 3 Jun 2025, "Colombia adquiere sistemas antidron por 25 millones de dólares" — headline only; no supplier, no corroboration. Do not cite as fact.
- **Do not conflate:** **Barak MX** (Dec 2025) is air defence, not C-UAS; **MQ-9A Reaper** transfers (Aug 2026) are ISR/strike. Colombia **blocked Israeli firms from Expodefensa 2025** (Infodefensa, 28 Jul 2025).
- Other: a Colombian firm developing a remote anti-drone station (Infobae, 31 Jan 2026); a 70+ company proposal (Telemedellín, 29 Apr 2026); Army's first **drone battalion** in Boyacá (Oct 2025); **105 drone operators** graduated (9 Apr 2026).

### B8. Law — civil aviation regulation vs criminal law
**Civil aviation — exists.** Drones governed by Aerocivil (UAEAC) under **RAC 100** (UAS); **RAC 91** covers manned aviation. Aerocivil ran **"Vuela Legal, Vuela Seguro"** (30 Mar 2026) and publicised **sanctions up to COP 20 million**; it issued **temporary drone flight bans** around elections and the 7 Aug 2026 inauguration. This is **administrative regulation** — registration, certification, authorisation, fines — **not criminal law**.
**Criminal law — no dedicated offence found.**
- **30 Jul 2025:** MinDefensa filed a bill to regulate drone use, citing **162 drone attacks** that year (El Tiempo).
- **6 Aug 2026** (one day before the government change): Sánchez filed **four bills**. One creates a **legal framework for supervision and control of UAS**, jointly with MinTIC, MinComercio and MinTransporte, to prevent illegal/unauthorised use. It also sets rules for **exceptional use of counter-drone countermeasures, limited exclusively to surveillance and private-security activities** under specific conditions. The others cover a statute for ~86,200 professional soldiers/infantes de Marina, Veterans Law reform, and reform of the Military Disciplinary Code (Ley 1862 de 2017). https://www.eltiempo.com/justicia/conflicto-y-narcotrafico/mindefensa-radica-cuatro-proyectos-de-ley-para-regular-drones-reformar-el-regimen-militar-y-fortalecer-beneficios-de-la-fuerza-publica-3576593
- ~21 Sep 2026: Senate Comisión Segunda scheduled drone-regulation debate. **Passage status as of 25 Sep 2026 unconfirmed.** 12 Sep 2026: government moved to **restrict entry of drones, chemical precursors and yellow machinery** — trade/executive control, not criminal statute.
- **Explicit finding: no Colombian law was found that specifically criminalises weaponised drones or creates a stand-alone drone-terrorism offence.** Prosecutions rely on pre-existing terrorism, rebellion, arms-trafficking and explosives provisions. The 2025–26 bills **regulate**; they do not criminalise. Any claim that Colombia criminalised weaponised drones in 2024–26 should be rejected absent the enacted text. Ley 2107/2021 concerns weaponised *autonomous* systems internationally, not domestic drone criminalisation.

### B9. Official statements and counts
- **Defensoría del Pueblo (Sep 2026):** explosive drone attacks **+146% Jan–May 2026**; attacks against **14 homes, 3 schools, 2 service stations**; victims in **Cauca, Catatumbo, sur de Bolívar, Caquetá, Nariño, Huila, Chocó, Magdalena**; risk now **urban**; SAT issued **17 alerts** in 2025–26; **47 drone attacks in 8 Cauca municipalities** in one week; 7 wounded (four minors aged 9–16) in **Asnazú, Suárez, Cauca**. https://www.infobae.com/colombia/2026/09/03/los-ataques-con-drones-explosivos-aumentaron-146-y-ya-amenazan-zonas-rurales-y-urbanas-de-colombia-advirtio-la-defensoria-del-pueblo/
- **Army 20 May 2026:** **>20 explosive drone attacks per month**. **17 Sep 2026:** **173 attacks against the FF.MM. in 2026** (Blu Radio); a retired FAC major: "no hay sistema antidrones 100% efectivo," attacks dating from **2018**. **4 Sep 2026 (CAMBIO):** **418 attacks** against military, **25 dead**. **5 Sep:** threat affects **>14 departments**; an ELN drone strike on a garrison killed ≥3 soldiers.
- **16 Sep 2026:** a drone attack **hit a 3D radar of Aerocivil** in **Candelaria, Valle del Cauca**; the Defensoría said it violated IHL (El Universal; Infobae 18 Sep) — the most significant infrastructure-targeted drone strike located. A FAC helicopter was reportedly hit by drones in Ocaña, Norte de Santander (Infobae, 5 Sep 2026).
- Earlier: **>300 attacks / 215 victims** (El Tiempo, 26 Sep 2025); **18 dead, 252 wounded**, one attack "every day and a half" (La FM/El Tiempo, 7 Nov 2025); **393 attacks in under two years** (El Colombiano, 19 Dec 2025). A separate Infobae report said the Army **"neutralised almost 9,000"** drone events in 2025 — almost certainly drone **flights/incursions**, not explosive attacks; do not interchange.
- **22 Sep 2026 — FIP (NGO):** attacks on the Fuerza Pública **+62% between 2024 and 2025**, drones included (EFE, Expreso). Attribute to FIP.
- Sept 2026 intelligence alleged Mordisco ordered the recruitment of minors to operate explosive drones — an **intelligence claim**, not a court finding.

## Gaps and cautions
1. **No autonomy evidence anywhere.** Every incident located is improvised, adapted, remotely piloted, poor precision. The 24 Jan 2026 "kamikaze" case is one-way attack, not autonomous targeting. "Enjambre" headlines = coordinated multi-drone, not autonomous swarming.
2. **Drone vs conventional separation is essential** for pipeline data.
3. **Putumayo/OTA: negative finding** for oil infrastructure.
4. **Indepaz: no published drone/pipeline dataset verified** — only its director's quoted statement.
5. **C-UAS procurement: only Axon confirmed in-country**; the USD 25 m claim is unverified.
6. **No dedicated criminal offence for weaponised drones confirmed as enacted.**
7. El Espectador, Razón Pública, Infodefensa and Defensoría primary sources were located by headline but not retrievable at source; flagged above.
