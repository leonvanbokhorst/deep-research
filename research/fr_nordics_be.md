# Drones, uncrewed systems, AI and autonomy as internal threats: French, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian and Belgian national security institutions

**Compiled 25 September 2026. Defence/understanding research only; no operational content.**

**Method.** `web_search` is broken here and every general search engine tested (Brave, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Mojeek, Startpage, four SearXNG instances) is IP-blocked or CAPTCHA-walled. Findings come from **direct primary-source retrieval** — agency sites, agency PDFs converted with `pdftotext`, parliamentary report PDFs and open-data APIs, and the Wayback Machine where Cloudflare/Anubis blocked access. Unretrievable items are marked **NOT VERIFIED**, not inferred. **PRIMARY** = agency/official document; **OFFICIAL ASSESSMENT** = present-tense claim; **OFFICIAL WARNING (FUTURE)** = forward-looking official judgement; **TESTIMONY** = expert witness recorded in an official report. Quotes are given in the original; translations only where the meaning is not evident.

## A) FRANCE

**1. SGDSN / plan VIGIPIRATE (PRIMARY, PDF dated 22 June 2026)** — the clearest French **OFFICIAL ASSESSMENT**, in subsection **5.2.4 "Que faire en cas de survol de drones ?"**:

> "Devenus accessibles et simples à mettre en œuvre, les drones constituent une menace sérieuse et en plein essor… des personnes malveillantes peuvent utiliser ces nouveaux modes d'action pour collecter des informations en vue de la préparation ou de la conduite d'un acte terroriste. De plus, un drone peut représenter une arme du fait de sa capacité d'emport (grenade, arme chimique ou biologique, etc.)."
> *(drones are accessible and simple to operate; a serious and rapidly growing threat; usable to gather information to prepare or conduct a terrorist act; can be a weapon via its payload — grenade, chemical or biological weapon)*

It lists drone photography of targets as a reconnaissance method, assigns counter-air terrorism to **PIRATAIR-INTRUSAIR**, and cautions that an overflight "doit être considéré comme potentiellement malveillant" while possibly being mere negligence. **Notable: VIGIPIRATE contains zero occurrences of "intelligence artificielle" or "IA".**

**2. JOP-24 counter-drone operation (PRIMARY).** Ministère des Armées bilans (19 and 27 Aug 2024): **"plus de 2400 aviateurs ont été engagés… avec 88 interceptions d'aéronefs et 64 télé-pilotes de drones interpellés."** Systems included PARADE, BASSALT, DRONE BLOCKER, L2AD, MILAD, RADIANT and BADA rifles, two **G1X** small-object radars, RAF **ORCUS** (detected 9 drones near a Paris Olympic site), German HP47 jammers and a Spanish NASAMS radar, run through **Centres opérationnels de lutte anti-drones (COLAD)**, with **Opération Sentinelle** as sensor and effector.

**3. Counter-UAS law — the brief's premise is wrong (PRIMARY, corrected).** **CSI art. L. 114-2 is not the counter-drone provision**: it sits in Livre I, Titre Ier, Ch. IV *Enquêtes administratives* and governs **background checks** for transport-security staff. **Loi n° 2021-998 du 3 août 2021 contains no drone article** (verified against the full code and Conseil constitutionnel n° 2021-822 DC). The real basis is **CSI art. L. 213-2** (*Ch. III bis : Protection contre les menaces résultant d'aéronefs circulant sans personne à bord*), version *LOI n° 2026-791 du 16 août 2026*:

> "Les services de l'Etat … peuvent utiliser des dispositifs désignés par arrêté du Premier ministre destinés à rendre inopérant ou à neutraliser un aéronef circulant sans personne à bord, en cas de menace imminente, pour les besoins de l'ordre public, de la défense et de la sécurité nationale…"

Measures must be "adaptées, nécessaires et proportionnées". **The statute authorises only neutralising/disabling — "detect/identify/jam" is not statutory language.** Paragraph II extends it to **OIV operators**. Authorisation is regulatory (*décret n° 2024-221 du 12 mars 2024*): the **Prime Minister**, or on delegation the Minister for the Armed Forces or prefects; authorisation "ne peut être délivrée pour une durée excédant trois ans"; jamming additionally needs an ANFR frequency impact study. **The regime itself has no sunset** (permanent since loi n° 2016-1428); only individual authorisations expire.

**4. Parliamentary material.** **Sénat n° 812 (30 June 2026)** supplies the only verified domestic counter-UAS numbers: coverage now extends to "des aéroports, des établissements pénitentiaires, des infrastructures énergétiques"; **gendarmerie = 51 systems** (9 jammer vehicles + 42 kits, target 71 kits end-2026); **police = OSLAD, 10 vehicles, 52 kits**; and **"Le nombre de missions de lutte anti-drones de la police nationale est passé de 96 en 2023 à 1 434 en 2024 … puis à 866 en 2025."** Threat framing includes "la livraison, notamment en prison, de téléphones, d'armes ou de stocks de stupéfiants." **Sénat n° 861 (7 July 2026, drones / lutte anti-drones / IA)** **self-excludes the domestic angle** ("Les dispositifs relevant des forces de sécurité intérieure n'entrent donc pas dans son champ d'analyse"), so its only usable content is **TESTIMONY** (Thierry Berthier, 9 June 2026): **"La barrière à l'entrée pour l'attaquant est très faible… dissémination vers des groupes paramilitaires, vers des narco cartels mexicains, vers des groupes terroristes."** This is the corpus's strongest barrier-lowering statement — but it is expert testimony, **not** an agency intelligence assessment, and makes no sabotage or assassination claim.

**5. DGSI — no public threat assessment exists (NEGATIVE FINDING).** Its public site, reconstructed from current and archived snapshots, has no "publications", "études" or "rapport d'activité" section; navigation is *Découvrir la DGSI*, *La DGSI à vos côtés* (terrorisme / contre-espionnage / cyberdéfense), podcasts, careers. No public page carries drone, AI or sabotage threat text. **The premise that DGSI publishes a public annual activity report is NOT VERIFIED and appears false.**

**6. AI governance: INESIA (PRIMARY).** Created 31 January 2025 under SGDSN, it addresses "risques systémiques" and "les risques potentiels que les modèles et systèmes d'IA peuvent faire peser sur les intérêts fondamentaux de la Nation" — AI as **systemic security risk**, not a terrorism or drone enabler.

**7. Incident figures — mostly NOT VERIFIED.** **Nuclear:** OPECST n° 267 (29 Jan **2015**) on the Oct–Nov 2014 wave records the CEA confirming overflights of nuclear sites, an **OFFICIAL ASSESSMENT** by F. Rol-Tanguy ("ne constituent pas un risque supplémentaire… Mais le risque zéro n'existe pas"), and **recorded dissent** from a senator: "il me semble hâtif d'écarter d'emblée l'hypothèse d'une démarche terroriste." No official aggregate count exists. **Prisons:** four Sénat written questions exist (n° 05873 réponse 23/03/2023; n° 01926 24/10/2024; n° 03654 03/11/2022; n° 09873 en attente 27/08/2026) but bodies are JavaScript-rendered → **counts NOT VERIFIED; do not quote a figure.** **Airports and SNRP: NOT VERIFIED** — do not expand the acronym.

## B) SWEDEN

**1. Säpo (PRIMARY).** *Årsbok* 2024–2026 is **NOT VERIFIED** (site returns HTTP 247 Anubis challenge; Wayback holds *årsbok* only to 2021). The successor series **Säkerhetspolisens lägesbild 2025–2026** (~Mar 2026) **was retrieved via Wayback**: "**Sabotagehotet från Ryssland är reellt**… Särskilt prioriterade är mål som påverkar västs möjlighet att stödja Ukraina." Drones appear as reported anomalies — "många incidenter och avvikelser som till exempel kabelbrott, **drönarflygningar** och cyberangrepp anmälts till Polismyndigheten" — with Säpo assessing whether foreign states are behind them; it cites "de ryska drönare som kränkte polskt luftrum i september 2025". **No incident counts are given.**

**Strong official counter-evidence to hype:** "**Inget fysiskt sabotage har än så länge kunnat knytas till främmande makt**"; "Än så länge har Sverige inte varit utsatt för omfattande försök till angrepp eller traditionella sabotage"; "Sverige är i dagsläget inte det primära målet". On sightings: "det är mycket svårt att med blotta ögat … avgöra vad som är en drönare och vad som är något annat", and the public should "inte dra förhastade slutsatser och utgå från att Ryssland ligger bakom allt som händer." The terrorism threat level was **lowered 4→3 on 23 May 2025**, threat mainly lone actors using "förhållandevis enkla medel". Säpo's only barrier-lowering claim concerns **online material, not drones or AI**: propaganda "syftar till att normalisera våldet och därmed **sänka tröskeln för våldsanvändning**". **No AI mention was found in the Säpo pages retrieved.**

**2. Counter-UAS policy (PRIMARY, regeringen.se).** Dir. 2026:45 (12 June 2026) commissions an inquiry on civil-defence drone use and protection against hostile drones, to propose which civilian actors may use detection plus "elektroniska, kinetiska och andra medel" (report due 8 Oct 2027); the accompanying release is titled "Sverige behöver ett starkare försvar mot fientliga drönare". Also: 2 Apr 2026 — **SEK 8.7bn GUTE II** counter-drone/air-defence contracts (Saab, BAE Bofors, deliveries 2027–28); 9 Mar 2026 — inquiry on remote drone identification; 12 Jan 2026 — **>SEK 4bn** for unmanned systems including loitering munitions.

**3. SOU 2025:57, *Polisiär beredskap i fred, kris och krig* (19 May 2025, PRIMARY).** Proposes military support to police to neutralise drones — "oskadliggöra obemannade farkoster (drönare m.m.) … i samband med insatser mot brott för vilket det är föreskrivet fängelse i två år eller mer" — with Armed Forces personnel "under direkt ledning av Polismyndigheten eller Säkerhetspolisen" and **no right of appeal**. It calls for capability to "upptäcka, identifiera och oskadliggöra" drones near "flygplatser, fängelser och andra skyddsområden", and records drones as an intrusion vector. Its only AI statement is ambient: AI "kan också användas för att sprida propaganda och desinformation".

**4. Swedish drone incident figures 2024–2026: NOT VERIFIED** (no official quantitative series from Polisen, LFV, MSB, Försvarsmakten or Säpo).

## C) DENMARK — PET

PET's *Vurdering af terrortruslen mod Danmark* carries a standing drone subsection in both the **March 2024** and **2025** editions (2025 based on the situation as of 1 March 2025). Both are **OFFICIAL ASSESSMENTS** stating barriers **remain**:

> "Det kræver økonomiske midler, teknisk viden og adgang til eksplosiver at våbengøre en drone ifm. terrorangreb i Vesten. CTA vurderer dog, at den fortsatte teknologiske udvikling på området betyder, at det bliver **gradvist lettere og billigere** at tilegne sig og anvende droner…"

Plus an explicit evidence limit: "**CTA har ikke kendskab til, at der har været gennemført terrorangreb med anvendelse af våbengjorte droner i Vesten**" — no completed weaponised-drone attack in the West, though there have been **prevented** plots. On **AI**, PET 2024 was deliberately cautious: "**Det er dog på nuværende tidspunkt for tidligt at vurdere de mere specifikke effekter**"; AI appears only as a reach/radicalisation and disinformation multiplier. On **sabotage**, PET rates the Russian threat *høj* with "konkret russisk planlægning og forberedelse", but its own 5 Sept 2026 item caveats that this does not mean "oplysninger om et konkret forestående angreb på et bestemt sted eller tidspunkt".

**Sept 2025 airport drone wave — official numbers (PRIMARY, independently re-verified).** Rigspolitiet, 26 Sept 2025 — **"mere end 500 anmeldelser … på tværs af alle politikredse"** in 24 hours. Københavns Politi, 24 Sept 2025 — **"mere end 100 anmeldelser om droner og dronelignende genstande"**. NOST moved to **operationsberedskab** (25 Sept 2025), but the same statement is the corpus's clearest official **de-escalation**: **"Det er myndighedernes fortsatte vurdering, at droneflyvningerne primært har til hensigt at skabe utryghed og forstyrre vores infrastruktur."**

**Flag:** a delegated researcher reports a **PET 2026** edition adding drone text ("Den forstyrrende effekt, som droner kan have ifm. kritisk infrastruktur, kan potentielt bringes til anvendelse i sammenhæng med handlinger, der … kan udgøre en terrorhandling…", self-qualified as requiring "en dronepilot med teknisk viden og en grad af planlægning"). **I could not reproduce this: the PET media URL returned HTTP 302 → /Error Pages/404 on three tests on 25 Sept 2026.** Treat as **UNCONFIRMED**. Also **NOT VERIFIED**: reported 2026 attribution walk-backs (Wikipedia-only) and **any Danish counter-drone legislation, procurement or strategy provision**.

## D) NORWAY — PST

**Sabotage warning (NTV 2026, 6 Feb 2026, PRIMARY).** Norway is in "den mest alvorlige sikkerhetspolitiske situasjonen siden andre verdenskrig"; **"Russisk etterretning kan se seg tjent med å utføre sabotasjeaksjoner mot mål i Norge i 2026"**, likely targets "eiendom og logistikkinfrastruktur knyttet til" Ukraine support, "men også sivil infrastruktur kan rammes" (attributed to proxies; PST notes "en liten nedgang i antall hendelser i 2025").

**Drones — asymmetric and low-salience.** PST nowhere links drones to state sabotage. Drones appear only in terrorism chapters: Islamists show "noe interesse for bruk av droner til både angreps- og rekognoseringsformål"; right-wing — **"Droner har til nå vært lite benyttet, men kan være nyttige til rekognoseringsformål."** An official statement that the barrier has **not** fallen — the mirror image of VIGIPIRATE.

**AI.** NTV 2026: "Kunstig intelligens (KI) øker statlige cyberaktørers evne"; KI usable "i alle fasene av et datainnbrudd"; AI chatbots may serve "angrepsplanlegging". **NTV 2025 supplies the key counterweight:** "**KI gir betydelige muligheter til å forsvare seg mot trusler. Det er fremdeles vanskelig å vurdere om effekten vil være størst for trusselaktørene eller de som skal beskytte seg.**" NTV 2025 is also the *stronger* drone-warning document — tech may raise "økt interesse for 3D-printede skytevåpen og droner som angrepsmiddel… kan være til inspirasjon for angrepsplanlegging i Norge" — so **PST's drone posture softened between 2025 and 2026**.

**Police walk-backs of the autumn 2025 wave (PRIMARY, three districts — the strongest official corrective in this corpus).** Ørland flystasjon, 15 Oct 2025 — "svært lite sannsynlig at droneflyvning har funnet sted"; observations spanned 04:58–07:20 and "ikke uvanlig at stjerner og planeter forveksles med droner". Sleipner oil platform, 13 Oct 2025 — observations "kan knyttes til skipstrafikk". Oslo Gardermoen, 6 Nov 2025 — could neither confirm nor deny drones; closed for lack of evidence.

**Counter-UAS.** Politidirektoratet, 17 Dec 2025, created a national **kompetansesenter mot ulovlig droneflyvning** at Taraldrud "i løpet av 2026". Avinor states a 5 km no-fly zone around all airports. *Norges dronestrategi* (**2018 — not current policy**) recorded the then-legal gap: "ikke noe klart hjemmelsgrunnlag for politiet til å iverksette mottiltak mot droner som utgjør en trussel". **No aggregate national incident count found → NOT VERIFIED.**

## E) BELGIUM — OCAM/OCAD and Defence

**OCAM threat figures, 6 Feb 2026 (PRIMARY).** 157 threat reports in 2025, **down 26%** from 213; severity 59% level 1, 36% level 2, **5% level 3, none level 4** ("Aucune menace n'a été jugée très grave et imminente"); jihadist 55%→39%, far-right 5%→13%, far-left 1%→4%; 75% lone actors; minors 13%. Cited modus operandi are "les explosifs, la violence physique et les armes à feu" — **drones are not among OCAM's cited means.**

**Rapport annuel 2025 (May 2026, PRIMARY):**

> "Une augmentation des « opérations de reconnaissance ennemies », du vandalisme et des tentatives ou actes de sabotage imputables au régime russe sur le territoire de l'UE et de l'OTAN est perceptible depuis 2023. Tant les infrastructures sensibles, vitales et critiques (pipelines, aéroports, etc.) que les bases militaires peuvent constituer des cibles. … Fin octobre et début novembre 2025, ce sont surtout les violations de l'espace aérien européen qui ont retenu l'attention."

**On AI:** the 2025 report mentions it once — "le recours croissant à l'intelligence artificielle par les acteurs extrémistes" — describing extremist propaganda practice, **not** a barrier-lowering claim; the word is **absent from the 2024 report**. **The word "drone" is absent from both**; the closest OCAM comes to the 2025 wave is the generic "violations de l'espace aérien européen". OCAM's site search `?s=drone` returns one unrelated hit.

**Divergence with Defence (PRIMARY).** mil.be, 16 Apr 2026, answering a VRT/PANO broadcast, admits drones "ont pu être observés au-dessus du quartier militaire d'Elsenborn en octobre 2025" and that Defence "ne disposait pas des moyens et des possibilités nécessaires pour détecter les drones en survol et, après leur détection, pour intervenir. **Les capacités très limitées en matière de moyens anti-drones** ne permettaient pas de faire face … à cette **menace hybride aiguë**." Procurement: 23 Dec 2025 — 40 Piorun MANPADS, SAAB Giraffe 1X radar, DroneShield kits, an adapted SX4 rifle, 100+ COBBS drone guns; 16 Apr 2026 — an urgent C-UAS package under art. 25 of the law of 13 Aug 2011 (COBBS *dronehunter BLAZE*, Senhive RF antennas), due "au plus tard fin juin 2026". The Crisis Centre (29 June 2026) launched **Plan SHIELD**, the first National Defence Plan since the Cold War, stating it "est une mesure préventive, et non une réaction à une menace imminente."

**NOT VERIFIED:** Belgian drone incidents at Kleine-Brogel, Brussels Airport or Peutie; the legal authority to neutralise drones (police vs military) and any 2024–2026 counter-drone law; any OCAM thematic drone note.

## Cross-cutting analysis

**1. On "barriers" — a three-way split, not a consensus.** The **strongest wording is the weakest evidence**: Sénat n° 861's "La barrière à l'entrée pour l'attaquant est très faible" is **expert testimony** in a report that expressly excludes internal security. VIGIPIRATE (June 2026) is genuine agency doctrine and uniquely names a drone as a weapon. PET states the cost/knowledge/explosives barrier and says it erodes **"gradvist"**. Against these: **PST** states drones "har til nå vært lite benyttet", confined to reconnaissance; **OCAM never writes the word at all**; **Säpo** reports drone flights as anomalies but says "Inget fysiskt sabotage har än så länge kunnat knytas till främmande makt". There is **no convergent French–Nordic–Belgian official consensus that drones transform the internal threat**.

**2. On AI — no institution claims it lowers barriers to terrorism, sabotage or assassination.** The most robust cross-institutional result. Across VIGIPIRATE (absent), PET ("for tidligt at vurdere de mere specifikke effekter"), PST (cyber capability, but also "betydelige muligheter til å forsvare seg"), Säpo (no AI mention retrieved), the Swedish SOU (propaganda/disinformation), OCAM (extremist propaganda) and SGDSN/INESIA (systemic model risk), **AI is consistently framed as a reach, cyber, reconnaissance or defensive multiplier — never as a barrier-lowering enabler of kinetic terrorism, sabotage or assassination.** The one barrier-lowering claim with official standing concerns **online propaganda lowering the threshold for violence** (Säpo), not AI.

**3. Official cautions against over-reading evidence deserve equal billing with the warnings.** NOST (Sept 2025): the Danish flights were intended "primært … at skabe utryghed og forstyrre vores infrastruktur", with no assessed risk to the public. Three Norwegian police districts publicly **retracted** autumn-2025 sightings. Säpo warns it is "mycket svårt att med blotta ögat … avgöra vad som är en drönare" and urges the public not to assume Russia is behind everything. PET caveats its own *høj* sabotage rating. Sweden's SOU pairs countermeasures with the observation that an overflight may be negligence. OCAM reports **falling** threat-report numbers and **zero** level-4 threats, while Plan SHIELD insists it is not a response to imminent threat.

**4. Transparency asymmetry is itself a finding.** DGSI publishes no threat assessment; SGDSN publishes detailed doctrine. PET publishes rich quantified assessments; OCAM publishes statistics but omits drones; PST is forward-looking and specific; Säpo's *årsbok* is unreachable while its *lägesbild* is retrievable only via archive. A comparative threat picture is shaped as much by **publication practice** as by threat perception.

**5. Thin evidence to flag.** The PET 2026 text and the reported Danish attribution walk-back are unconfirmed. **No primary document located establishes state attribution for the autumn-2025 drone wave.** French prison, airport and nuclear incident counts are unverified, and the only substantive French nuclear-drone evidence (OPECST n° 267) is from **2015**. No official Swedish or Norwegian aggregate drone-incident series exists publicly. The brief's legal premise (L. 114-2 / loi 2021-998 as the French counter-drone basis) is **affirmatively wrong**.
