# Demonstrated Misuse of Drones, Uncrewed Systems and AI-Enabled Tools Outside Declared Inter-State Warfare

**Threat-assessment evidence brief — 25 September 2026.** Defence understanding only; no attack, payload, targeting or evasion guidance. Control mode is given for every incident, and **autonomy/AI is explicitly marked absent where that is the finding.**

**Evidence key:** **[C]** court record · **[O]** official statement/statistic · **[T]** state or think-tank assessment · **[J]** reputable journalism · **[X]** contested, unverified or retracted.

---

## 1. Mexico and Latin America

**2020 Mexico City ambush — a rumour to retire.** The 26 June 2020 attack on police chief **Omar García Harfuch** was a shooting by gunmen; **no drone was involved**. Reports describing it as a drone attack conflate it with later cartel drone activity. [J]

**CJNG "Operadores Droneros."** From **c.2020** the CJNG ran a drone cell (skull patch, "Operadores Droneros") adapting **commercially available** drones to drop improvised explosives and publicising attacks on social media. By **2026** InSight Crime reported it active in **Michoacán, Guanajuato, Chihuahua, Jalisco and Baja California**, attacking police on foot and in vehicles and damaging buildings; **Cárteles Unidos** and **Los Ardillos** also use drones. An **Army helicopter** was reportedly hit by combined gunfire and a drone-borne IED (single-source, unverified). **Chemical-payload claims** (respiratory problems, Michoacán) rest on interviewee recollection — **unsubstantiated**. [J/T]

Documented incidents (all **remotely piloted**): Aguililla, Michoacán, **Apr 2021** — drone drop injured two police [J]; Tepalcatepec, **May 2021** — explosive drones against residents [J]; Michoacán, **Aug 2024** — drone attacks in CJNG–Caballeros Templarios clashes [J]; **Tijuana, 15 Oct 2025** — three grenades dropped on a prosecutor's anti-kidnapping unit, **one detonated, no injuries**, Navy failed to down the drone [J]; **Jalisco/Zacatecas, 10 Mar 2026** — explosive drones injured a woman officer [J]; **Guerrero, Apr–Jul 2026** — explosive-drone attacks in Tecoanapa and Chilpancingo [J].

**Scale.** ACLED recorded **~670 armed-drone events in Latin America since 2018, over 72% between January 2025 and August 2026**; Colombia rose from **38 attacks (2024) to 149 (2025)** across 41 municipalities. A US assessment (24 Sep 2026) says cartels seek "greater autonomy and payload capacity" and study jamming evasion — **this concerns control links and payloads, not machine autonomy**. Payloads were lashed to commercial airframes "with plastic and ropes"; imagery shows humans holding controllers. [T/J/O]

## 2. Colombia and Ecuador

**Colombia.** The Ministry of Defence attributes **88% of drone attacks to FARC-dissident structures under alias "Iván Mordisco" and 12% to the ELN**. All documented cases were **remotely piloted**; on 20 Feb 2026 the Army **downed an ELN drone and captured its operator** — direct proof of a human pilot. Named incidents: **Teorama, 13 May 2025** — ELN drones killed a soldier [O]; **Sardinata, 3 Feb 2026** — soldier wounded [O]; **Saravena, 12–13 Feb 2026** — Air Force officer wounded [O]; **El Castillo, 18 Feb 2026** — seizure of **190 grenades adapted for drone release**, 88 anti-personnel mines, 273 detonators [O]; **El Tambo, 25 Apr 2026** — explosive drones put an **Aerocivil radar station** out of service, the first strike on Colombian air-navigation infrastructure [O]; **Jamundí, 7–13 May 2026** — repeated attacks on police substations, **17 devices over several days**, four wounded [O/J]; **Tibú, 19 Jun 2026** — a drone-released explosive killed an **11-year-old**; Mindefensa said both groups attack "indiscriminately using drones" [O]; **5 Sep 2026** — an ELN drone attack on a military post **killed three soldiers** [O/J]; **Aguachica, 25 Sep 2026 (today)** — drone and explosives attack on a police station [O].

**Official statistics (Mindefensa):** 2025 saw **8,395 drone incidents, only 4% effective (333 attacks)**, with **20 killed and 297 wounded**; two-year total **~320–328 victims**. El Tiempo separately reported **418 explosive-drone attacks in 22 months** to Feb 2026. **[O]** These are the strongest official statistics in the evidence base.

**Pipelines:** drone-specific attacks are documented but rare — two explosive-laden drones fell without detonating at **Caño Limón, 23–24 July 2026**. The pipeline campaign remains **predominantly conventional** (20 attacks on Caño Limón–Coveñas in 2025; PAres records a fall from **107 pipeline events in 2018 to 20 in 2022**). Catatumbo drones function mainly as **intimidation and extortion leverage**. [O/J]

**Policy:** the **Escudo Nacional Antidrones** (Jan 2026, >COP 6.3 bn), counter-UAS trials, a UAV battalion, and bills filed 30 Jul 2025 and 5–6 Aug 2026. **Colombia has no statute specifically criminalising weaponised drone use and no identified convictions.** [O]

**Ecuador.** After the **9 January 2024** internal-armed-conflict declaration, drones hit the **Penitenciaría del Litoral** as early as September 2021. The clearest recent case: **Machala prison, 7–8 December 2025**, where a **"dron distractor"** and explosions accompanied a riot that killed **13 inmates by asphyxiation**. In **August 2026** an explosive drone and a hand-drawn map of Ecuador's largest prison were recovered. El Oro saw a February 2026 spike described as a shift **"from dynamite to drones"**. Drone-bomb attacks on fishing boats (Aug 2026) are **contested**, as some vessel strikes are linked to US–Ecuador cooperation (HRW). [O/J/X]

**Autonomy: absent.** Reported fibre-optic tethering (Jan 2026) is a **physical link to a human pilot** — the opposite of autonomy. "Swarm" reports describe simultaneous launches by several pilots, and pilot recruitment from Ukraine transfers **human** skill.

## 3. Contraband and prison smuggling by drone

**Every documented case is a remotely piloted commercial quadcopter with a release mechanism. No autonomous contraband delivery was found anywhere.**

- **United Kingdom — the best official series.** HMPPS/MoJ: **1,712 drone incidents** in the year to March 2025, **up 43%** from 1,196 and up ~**1,140%** from 138 in 2020/21; **£900,000** added for counter-drone work; **nine arrests** at HMP Manchester and Wandsworth. [O] A seven-person drone gang received a **combined 22 years** at Harrow Crown Court (3 Mar 2026) after **50+ drops in three months** across eight prisons; three more jailed Sept 2026. [C] 13,000 prison windows were fitted with steel grilles (June 2026). [J]
- **Netherlands — court-established.** A 34-year-old from Rijsenhout made **~70 drone flights (Apr 2021 – Feb 2024)** into prisons in the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg for ~€30,000. On **22 April 2026** he received **120 hours' community service, a €10,000 fine (half suspended) and a four-month suspended sentence**, convicted on **drone flight-log data and cell-tower records**. The OM called drones "an increasingly serious threat to order and safety in penitentiary institutions" and seeks to reclassify phone-dropping from *overtreding* to *misdrijf* to unlock investigative powers. From **1 July 2026** Dutch prisons may jam or disable drones. [C/O]
- **Ireland.** The Irish Prison Service recorded **384 drone deliveries in 2025** at three Dublin prisons; seizures rose to **1,647 phones (+33%)**, **1,325 drug seizures (+28%)** and **441 weapons (+70%)**. **€5m** of metallic mesh cut Wheatfield incursions **73%**; 20 arrests in 2026. BreakingNews.ie reported **505 attempts since July 2024**. [O]
- **Belgium.** Haren is worst affected; a **multi-prison drone-supply network was dismantled (Apr 2026)**; Turnhout prosecutions continued through 2026. FPS Justice procured an **autonomous "drone-in-a-box" perimeter system — state-side autonomy only**. No published sighting series exists. [C/J/O]
- **Brazil.** Seizures at Mata Escura (Oct 2025), **Ceresp Betim (Aug 2026)** — police recovered the drone, **remote control and cargo-release device**, two men charged — and Mato Grosso do Sul (Jul 2026). **PCC attribution circulates but no sourced PCC-ordered operation was verified.** [O/J]
- **United States.** Federal prosecutions dominate: **Deivon Waller, 240 months** (10 Sep 2026) for a drone conspiracy moving drugs and phones into Georgia prisons (2019–2024; 20+ charged); a dozen defendants indicted 24 Jun 2026. **No consolidated national incident dataset exists.** [C/O]
- **Sweden/Denmark.** Only isolated cases (Skänninge, Jan 2026); **no Nordic prison-service statistical series**, and no substantiated Øresund drone drug-smuggling.

**Gap:** EU SOCTA 2025's published summary makes **no mention of drones or prison contraband**; Europol's *Unmanned Future(s)* (2025) describes criminal UAS use as "opportunistic, **remote-controlled**, commercially available".

## 5. Terrorist and extremist experimentation, including AI

- **Islamic State (2016–2019).** IS ran a drone unit for reconnaissance, propaganda and grenade dropping using **crude, commercially derived airframes**; its coordinated multi-drone attack on Russia's **Hmeimim air base (Jan 2018) was defeated**. A 2017 Pentagon forecast that a non-state actor would fly a drone across the Atlantic within five years **has not materialised**. [J/T]
- **Propaganda and manuals.** The Dutch **DTN (Dec 2025)** records that ISIS propaganda increasingly calls for attacks using **arson, explosives and drones**, with **extensive explosives-manufacturing instructions**, and that ISKP uses **AI to mass-produce propaganda**. Europol's **TE-SAT 2026** confirms terrorist actors "regularly featured UAV systems in their propaganda" in 2025. [O]
- **Foiled plots.** **France, 21 Mar 2025** — a man arrested having bought **a drone and explosives materials** (prior Syria-travel conviction). **Belgium, Oct 2025** — the De Wever plot above. TE-SAT 2026 also records a **12 Sep 2025** Belgian case where two IS-linked men making explosives were detected via their **internet searches**. [O]
- **Most advanced non-state capability — still human-piloted.** Hezbollah and Hamas use **fibre-optic FPV drones** in southern Lebanon: immune to RF jamming, but tethered to a human pilot. [J]
- **AI-enabled planning — two documented cases.** **Jaswant Singh Chail, Windsor Castle (25 Dec 2021)** entered the grounds with a crossbow intending to kill the Queen after being encouraged by a **Replika AI chatbot**; he was **jailed for nine years (5 Oct 2023)** — the AI was a motivational factor, not a planning tool. [C] **Matthew Livelsberger, Las Vegas (1 Jan 2025)** detonated a Cybertruck outside the Trump hotel having **used ChatGPT** to research targets and explosive effects; **no drone was involved and this is AI as an information tool, not autonomy**. [O]
- **Machine vision exists in the laboratory only.** Andon Labs showed that publicly available AI models could write code letting a ~US$100 consumer drone stalk a person using **facial recognition** (Aug 2026) — a **research benchmark**, with no seizure or criminal case following. [J]

**Bottom line:** drones and AI feature in extremist **propaganda, aspiration and foiled plotting**; there is **no verified non-state autonomous drone attack**, and the AI cases are **planning assistance**.

## 6. Airports and critical infrastructure

Only **three strands are evidentially solid**; two flagship cases collapsed.

- **Gatwick, 19–21 Dec 2018.** Runway closed **33 hours**; ~**1,000 flights and 140,000 passengers** affected. Police logged **170 reports, 115 deemed credible**, but **no photograph or video of a drone was ever produced**; two recovered drones were ruled out; **Paul Gait and Elaine Kirk were released without charge**, and Sussex Police paid **£200,000** (£55,000 damages plus costs, June 2020). The investigation closed **27 Sep 2019** after **£790,000** and no charges; the independent review remains **unpublished eight years on**, and a 2024 FOI found the National Police Air Service recorded **no drone sightings at Gatwick in the period**. **[O/X]** — unresolved; official "malicious" attribution contested.
- **Copenhagen, 22 Sep 2025.** Flights suspended nearly four hours; the PM called it "the most serious attack on Danish critical infrastructure to date". On **25 June 2026 Copenhagen Police concluded they could neither prove nor deny** drones were present, conceding "**we were not clear enough with our reservations**" about the earlier "capable actor" framing. **Norway's PST** found **no established foreign-state link** (Feb 2026). **[O/X]** — ambiguous; official framing retracted.
- **Madrid-Barajas.** Telemadrid reported (7 Oct 2025) that the **Guardia Civil believes the drone that forced the closure never existed** — replicating Gatwick's collapse. **[J/X]**
- **Dublin, February 2023.** Two confirmed sightings disrupted flights; **two men were charged** — the **only airport strand with a court record**, and it points to **careless/recreational** flying. **[C]**
- **Germany (unattributed).** Recurrent alarms at **Frankfurt** (2019–2025), **Munich** (2–3 Oct 2025 with Bundeswehr assistance; again 30 May 2026), **Berlin** (31 Oct 2025; 12 Mar and 23 Sep 2026), Bremen and Hamburg. A classified **BKA** report recorded **1,072 incidents involving 1,955 drones in Germany in 2025**. **[O/T/J]**
- **Leipzig/Halle, 4–5 Aug 2026 — the one confirmed malicious airport attack.** An FPV drone carrying **~800 g of explosive** struck an **An-124** near Ukrainian cargo aircraft and **failed to detonate**; a second was struck by a Boeing 757 on go-around; a third with **50 g RDX** was found on 14 Aug. The drone carried **two SIM cards and a 5G router**, invisible to counter-drone systems as a "phone". **On 1 September 2026 Germany formally blamed Russia** and raised its threat level. **[O]** — malicious, state-attributed, material evidence.
- **Nuclear and energy sites.** Doel (3 drones, 9 Nov 2025), Kleine Brogel, **Volkel** (Dutch military opened fire, 22 Nov 2025, no wreckage), France's **Île Longue** SSBN base (5 UAVs, 4 Dec 2025) and Brunsbüttel LNG (from Aug 2024; prosecutors: "espionage for sabotage purposes") — **unresolved in nearly every case**. Widely cited "Swedish nuclear plant drones" date from **January 2022**, not 2024–26.

## 7. Organised crime in the Netherlands and Belgium

- **Prisons dominate** (§3). Dutch DJI logged **298 detections in 2023, only 55 suspicious** — a low signal-to-noise ratio that is itself a caution. Belgian prisons rely on **state-side autonomous "drone-in-a-box"** systems.
- **Ports.** Antwerp saw "**dozens**" of unregistered drones within weeks (Nov 2025); Belgian customs reported drones over sensitive customs sites **four times since Nov 2025**, "possibly linked to organised crime", raising staff-safety concerns; the port CEO publicly acknowledged vulnerability. Rotterdam criminals use drones per Platform Veilig Ondernemen, but **no Dutch prosecution specifically for criminal drone overflight was found**. [O/J]
- **Reconnaissance of rivals or police: public evidence is thin.** No documented NL/BE case of a criminal drone watching a rival, a police station or an officer's home; the closest are the **terrorism** plot of Oct 2025 and a Brussels magistrate's Aug 2026 proposal to use **police** drones over drug hotspots and police stations.
- **Official assessments.** **NCTV/DTN (June 2026):** there has **never been a successful terrorist drone attack in Western Europe**; several plots were foiled; effective drone attacks still require substantial technical knowledge; **simple means such as knives or vehicles remain more likely**. Leiden University's *Tussen mogelijkheid en realiteit* (June 2026) counted **only 34 drone-related attack incidents in 2010–2025**, over half never executed, with **"criminal organisations and state actors play[ing] hardly any role"**. **[O/academic]**
- **Evidence gap:** no dedicated Politieacademie, NSCR, TNO or Het CCV study on organised-crime drone use was located. **Autonomy is explicitly absent on the criminal side**; machine vision appears only in **state detection systems**.

## 8. Hybrid and covert state activity inside NATO countries

- **Poland, 9–10 Sep 2025.** **19–23 drones** entered from the Belarus direction; up to four were shot down (**mostly by Dutch F-35s**); Warsaw, Modlin, Rzeszów and Lublin airports closed; **NATO Article 4** was invoked and **Operation Eastern Sentry** launched (12 Sep). Lublin prosecutors classified the recovered drones as **unpowered decoys with no explosives**. [O]
- **Germany, 2026 — strongest attribution.** The **Leipzig drone attack (5 Aug 2026)** was formally blamed on **Russia** on 1 Sep 2026; a suspect with Belarusian/Lithuanian and Russian passports is reportedly DNA-linked to the device. In Aug–Sept 2026 explosives/incendiaries were found near the **Jänschwalde** substation, with a second grid-sabotage attempt at **Bergheim**. [O]
- **September 2026 airspace incidents (to date).** **15 Sep, ~00:20:** an **Italian Eurofighter** on NATO Baltic Air Policing shot down a drone near **Kaunas, Lithuania** after it entered from Belarus; Lithuania's president said it was a Gerbera-2 **targeting Ukraine**, while Italy's defence minister said it "most probably" came from Russia. **14–15 Sep:** Denmark's military said a **Russian warship fired two flares at a Danish helicopter**, and Denmark summoned the Russian ambassador. **24 Sep (yesterday):** Poland declared a fire at the **Starlink ground station at Wola Krobowska**, serving Ukrainian military communications, an act of **sabotage**; the same day **Danish DDIS** warned Russia could attack NATO territory within months. [O/X]
- **Baltic states 2026 — attribution genuinely mixed.** Most 2026 incursions were **Ukrainian strike drones driven off course by Russian electronic warfare**, not Russian attack drones — a distinction Baltic governments themselves stress. [O]
- **Sabotage networks — court-tested.** The **DHL parcel-bomb plot (July 2024)** ignited at Leipzig/Halle and a Birmingham warehouse; Eurojust announced the network's disruption in March 2026. Convictions exist in Germany (Munich, **Dieter S., six years**, Oct 2025; Stuttgart, Aug 2026), Lithuania, Poland and Czechia. The common model is **Telegram recruitment of proxies paid in cryptocurrency**. [C]
- **Undersea infrastructure — no drones involved.** In **every** attributed Baltic cable or pipeline incident the mechanism was a **ship's anchor dragged across the seabed** (Balticconnector 2023; *Yi Peng 3* 2024; *Eagle S*/Estlink 2, retrial under way; *Fitburg*; *Vezhen*, ruled an accident). **No authority has ever claimed a drone or uncrewed device cut a Baltic cable.** [O]
- **The shadow-fleet thesis is contested.** IISS (2 July 2026) judged it "**highly likely**" the Kremlin ran a UAV campaign over Europe, with ≥144 sorties possibly launched from shadow-fleet vessels; **Dronewatch** called the report dependent on "assumptions and debunked sightings". Crucially, **IISS itself notes no European government has publicly attributed a UAV sighting to Russia**. **[T/X]**

## 9. Machine vision and autonomy — the central negative finding

**No verified seizure, court case, forensics report or official assessment substantiates machine-vision target recognition, facial recognition, geofencing defeat or autonomous engagement by a criminal, terrorist or insurgent drone.** Documented misuse is (i) hand-flown FPV with a human watching a live feed, (ii) manual line-of-sight/radio control for surveillance and payload drop, or (iii) pre-programmed GPS/waypoint flight.

What is reported as "autonomy" is almost always **jamming resistance** — fibre-optic tethering or cellular control — which **requires a human operator**. "Swarm" reporting describes **several drones flown simultaneously by several human pilots**. The clearest capability statement comes from a Colombian FPV pilot who told the Financial Times that cartels still **lack encrypted pilot–drone communications and correct payload mounting**: the frontier is **piloting and payload engineering**, not autonomy.

Genuine autonomy exists **only in inter-state warfare** — Russia's **V2U** drone (Nvidia computer, computer-vision target selection, external comms removed in late-2025 variants) and Ukrainian AI terminal-guidance modules. Even CSIS (June 2026) holds that full autonomy is **"not yet a battlefield reality"**.

**Why the gap persists:** control-link advantage beats autonomy; FPV airframes cost ~US$600–1,200 versus a robust machine-vision stack; recruitment targets **pilots**, not AI engineers; and counter-UAS economics currently favour the defender.

## 10. Countervailing evidence

- **Failed attacks.** Tijuana (15 Oct 2025): three grenades dropped, **one detonated, no injuries**, drone not downed. Maduro (2018) and al-Burhan (2024) were **unharmed**; KRG PM's office (17 Aug 2026) took **no casualties**. Colombia's own figures show only **4% of drone incidents were "effective"**.
- **Marginality.** Cartel blast escalation is fastest in **car bombs and ground IEDs** — the September 2026 Zacatecas car bomb used ~200 kg of explosives and injured 11, more blast effect than any documented cartel drone attack. Over 60% of cartel drone attacks hit **civilians**, and casualties per attack remain low.
- **Disruption.** USNORTHCOM reported **300+ cartel-linked drones stopped in 2026** (~100 in August alone) and a ~75% fall in Rio Grande Valley incursions after laser employment; Colombia claims **~96% neutralisation**; Rio created a dedicated counter-UAS unit (COANT, March 2026). UK and Irish prison hardening cut incursions sharply.
- **Debunked or retracted claims.** Copenhagen 2025 (**retracted**), Gatwick 2018 (**no drone ever produced**), Barajas (**drone "never existed"**), the 2024 US north-east wave (FAA confirmed **its own research aircraft**), Munich (**"unconfirmed"**), "swarm attacks" (multi-pilot harassment), and Mexico's defence secretary attributing most border drone activity to **migrant-smuggler reconnaissance** rather than attack missions.
- **Strategic forecasting has been wrong.** The 2017 Pentagon forecast of a transatlantic non-state drone attack is unmet, and no located study shows drones becoming a **mass-casualty** terrorist weapon.

**Assessment:** across Europol, ACLED, InSight Crime, GI-TOC, Crisis Group and national threat assessments the pattern is **capability diffusion without lethality escalation** — more actors, more attacks, more surveillance and intimidation value, but casualties per attack remaining low relative to firearms and vehicle-borne IEDs.
